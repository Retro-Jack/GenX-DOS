STATEMENT OF INTENT & SCOPE

This repository is a composite work, and the original material in it is under
TWO licences.

  CC BY-NC 4.0 ....... the DOS prompt and its filesystem, the AMIBIOS POST
                       animation, the menu tree, the home page and feature
                       article, the wiki, the game instruction pages, and the
                       artwork and prose throughout.

  GPL-3.0-or-later ... the emulator-facing glue, being
                         systems/_shared/genx-*.js
                         systems/_shared-ejs/genx-*.js
                         systems/*/play.html
                       Every file above carries an SPDX header saying so, and
                       the licence text is in COPYING at the repo root.

WHY THE SPLIT: those glue files load and drive GPL-licensed emulator engines in
the same browser context, so they are best treated as part of a combined work
with those engines. The GPL does not permit further restrictions to be imposed
on such a work, and NonCommercial is a further restriction. Putting the glue
under GPL-3.0-or-later removes the conflict at the only place it arises. It
costs nothing that matters: the glue is small and specific to this project,
while the work worth protecting — the prompt, the writing, the artwork and the
several hundred instruction pages — stays NonCommercial.
