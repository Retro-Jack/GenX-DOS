This one needs a keyboard

GenX-DOS is a desktop DOS terminal and 8-bit emulator suite — you drive every machine from a physical keyboard, so a touchscreen can't run it. Open it on a desktop or laptop and the whole museum is open to you.

What you're looking at

A museum that boots

Behind each number on the menu sits a working replica of the real machine — faithful enough that the games can't tell the difference. Forty-year-old software actually runs.

Nothing installed, nothing tracked

The whole thing arrives in one go and runs from a single place. No downloads while you play, no accounts, no phoning home.

It has opinions

Not "the 8-bit era" — the 8-bit feel. Ten games per system, sorted by year, every control checked against the original manuals. Curated, not dumped.
