Removal upon request

If you hold rights in anything here, or you legally represent someone who does, and you raise an identified copyright or intellectual-property concern, the material comes down immediately. Not debated, not defended, not delayed pending a formal process.

This page is the whole procedure. It is short on purpose.

What it covers

Everything on genx-dos.fun and everything in the GitHub repository — not only games: software titles, system ROMs, BIOS and firmware images, emulator code, artwork, fonts, sound, documentation, trade marks and names.

The games, and the firmware we supplied for the machines that need it, are served by genx-dos.fun and are not in the repository at all, so for those the site is where removal happens.

What a request needs

Three things, so that nothing is pulled on an anonymous say-so:

  1. Who you are — a name, and the company or firm if you act for one.
  2. Your standing — that you hold the rights, or who you represent and in what capacity.
  3. What and why — the specific title, file or asset, and the basis of the concern.

That is the whole bar. No notice format, no particular form of words, no registration certificates, no chain-of-title documents, no legal citations. A few sentences covering those three points are taken in good faith and acted on. You do not need a lawyer to write to us, and sending one changes nothing about the outcome.

Where to send it

  • Email admin@genx-dos.fun. This is the shortest route and needs no account anywhere. A few sentences are enough.
  • Or open an issue, if you would rather it were public: https://github.com/Retro-Jack/GenX-DOS/issues — there is a Content removal request template, though a plain issue, or an email saying the same thing, is equally fine.

What happens then

The material is removed from the site as soon as the request is read, and from the repository in the same pass. You get a reply saying what was taken down. GenX-DOS is one person's hobby project, so "as soon as it is read" means hours rather than minutes — but it is not conditional on anything, and there is no counter-argument waiting for you.

Nothing is asked in return: no proof beyond the three points above, no agreement to terms, and no request that you reconsider.

What we can and cannot reach

We control the site and the repository, and those are cleaned properly — including the repository's history, not just its current state.

Two things are outside our reach, and it is better to say so than to imply otherwise:

  • Other people's copies of the repository. Two forks were taken before September 2026 and still hold the history as it was then. We can ask their owners to delete them; we cannot make them.
  • Web archives, caches and mirrors run by third parties. Those have their own removal routes, and we will point you at the right one if it helps.

If you are not a rights holder

Tell us anyway if something looks wrong — a mis-credit, a licence we have misread, an attribution that names the wrong person. The same addresses work, and corrections are welcome from anyone.
