Boxer

Acornsoft, 1984. Two-fighter side-view boxing — sprite-flicker low, animation crisp, more cerebral than the era's arcade slug-fests.

System help — the Acorn Electron controls

A single boxing round on a single screen. You and the opponent face off; move around the ring, punch, and dodge. Three knock-downs (or running down the round clock with a points lead) wins the match.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. Choose 1-player or 2-player at the next prompt.
  3. Round 1 begins; the AI starts cautious and gets more aggressive as you advance.

Controls

This game supports a USB gamepad — the D-pad or left stick moves, A punches and B jumps. Pick 1- or 2-player on the keyboard at the title.

On a PC keyboard the Electron *: key lands on the apostrophe — this emulator maps it to ', so it's listed as ' below.

Scoring

  • Clean punch through guard: 5 points
  • Knock-down: 50 + 10-count interval
  • KO (3 knock-downs or stay-down): match ends, full round bonus
  • Points decision: highest score at bell wins, no bonus

Strategy

  • Slip-then-counter. The AI throws a 1-2 combo most exchanges. Dodge both, then punch the recovery window.
  • Don't trade. Punching while taking a hit costs more health than the swap of damage suggests.
  • Step into a missed swing. An opponent who misses recovers slow — close the gap and land a free combo.
  • Manage stamina. Throwing punches uses energy; pacing matters across a long round.
