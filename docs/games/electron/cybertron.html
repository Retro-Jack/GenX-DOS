Cybertron Mission

Micro Power, 1983 — Matthew Bates. Top-down multidirectional shooter through maze-like rooms; one of Micro Power's flagship Electron titles.

You roam a maze of rooms — sixteen per level, laid out on a 4×4 grid — hunting the treasures scattered through them. Collect the number required (one more each level, up to six), then find the safe to drop to the next level. Four maze layouts cycle as you descend, recoloured to look fresh. Electrified walls, robot guards, and a wall-phasing creature called the Spook — which turns up if you dawdle — all want you dead.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start — on a gamepad that's Start.
  2. Move in eight directions: A up, Z down, and the ,/. keys (the game's </>) for left/right.
  3. Shoot in the direction you're facing with M — a shot also briefly stuns the Spook.
  4. Walk over treasures to collect them; once you have enough, reach the safe to advance.

Controls

This game supports a USB gamepad — the D-pad or left stick moves, any face button fires, and Start begins the game at the title. You shoot in whichever of the eight directions you're facing, and holding two directions together moves (and fires) diagonally.

The game labels left/right </> — those are the comma and full-stop keys, so press , and ..

Strategy

  • Draw a map. The rooms are deliberately disorienting — pencil-and-paper mapping is the classic advice.
  • Don't dawdle. Linger in a room and the Spook appears — fast, and it passes through walls. A shot only stuns it, so keep moving.
  • Mind the walls. Electrified walls kill on contact — thread the gaps.
  • Know your enemies. Spinners don't shoot; Clones fire rapidly on later levels; Cyberdroids learn your movements. The mix gets nastier as the four levels repeat.
