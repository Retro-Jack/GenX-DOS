Donkey Kong

Coleco, 1982 — pack-in title for the ColecoVision launch. Port of Nintendo's 1981 arcade classic.

Climb a series of stage girders to rescue Pauline (the Lady) from Donkey Kong at the top. Donkey Kong hurls barrels, fireballs, and live coals down the stage; Jumpman (later Mario) ducks, jumps, and climbs ladders to reach her. The ColecoVision port preserves three of the four arcade stages: 25m (barrels), 50m (cement), and 100m (rivets).

Getting started

  1. At the title, press 1 or 2 on the keypad to select 1- or 2-player.
  2. Start at the bottom of the first girder stage. Reach the top to advance.
  3. Three lives. Lose them all and the game ends.

Stages

StageHazard
25m (barrels)Donkey Kong rolls barrels down the girders. Jump them or climb to break the chain.
50m (cement)Conveyor belts of cement tubs. Time movements to the belt's direction.
100m (rivets)Remove the eight rivets by walking over each — Donkey Kong falls when the last is gone.

Strategy

  • Jump barrels at the apex. Jump too early or too late and the barrel catches you on the descent.
  • Grab the hammer. The hammer powerup destroys hazards for a few seconds — chain hits before it expires. You can't climb while holding it.
  • Climb full ladders. Partial climbs leave you hanging mid-girder if a barrel rolls past.
  • The rivet stage is precise. Each rivet must be walked over completely; touching it is not enough.

ColecoVision controls

Standard ColecoVision controller. Donkey Kong uses only the joystick and the left fire button — the right button and keypad are unused during play.
