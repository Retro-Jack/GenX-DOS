Mr. Do!

Coleco, 1983 — port of Universal's 1982 arcade dig-and-dodge classic

A clown digs tunnels through coloured fruit and ground, eating cherries and crushing creatures along the way. Push apples down on top of pursuers, throw your bouncing power ball to clear paths, and either eat every cherry or knock out all the creatures to clear the level. Spell EXTRA across the title by collecting the four bonus letters that drop.

Getting started

  1. Press 1 or 2 on the keypad at the title.
  2. Mr. Do digs tunnels by walking. Cherries appear in clusters of eight; eating a full cluster gives bonus points.
  3. Each level ends when you eat all cherries, kill all monsters, or spell EXTRA.

Tactics

ElementEffect
Power ballBounces forward through tunnels — kills any creature it hits. Returns to you. Don't throw it into space (you're without it briefly).
ApplesDrop when you dig the ground beneath them. Crush creatures below, or trap Mr. Do if you misjudge.
EXTRA lettersSpecial creatures appear with letters E-X-T-R-A; collect all four for a bonus level + extra life.
Diamond bonusRare — appears in the centre after a cherry cluster is eaten. Big points.

Strategy

  • Don't throw your ball blindly. While the ball is away, you're defenceless. Reserve it for cleared sight lines.
  • Crush, don't fight. Pushing an apple onto a pursuing creature is safer than throwing the ball at it.
  • Eat cherry clusters whole. Each cluster of eight pays a bonus only if you eat them all in sequence.
  • EXTRA-creature timing. The EXTRA-letter monsters appear briefly and disappear — don't ignore them, but don't die chasing them either.

ColecoVision controls

Joystick + left fire button. The power ball follows the tunnels you've dug — it doesn't bore through fresh ground, so plan dig paths to set up shots.
