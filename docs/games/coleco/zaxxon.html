Zaxxon

Coleco, 1982 — port of Sega's 1982 arcade isometric shooter

System help — the ColecoVision controls

An isometric scrolling shooter — your fighter flies diagonally into the screen over an asteroid base. You must manage altitude as well as left/right position: dip too low and you crash into a wall; fly too high and the anti-aircraft fire shifts to track you. The altitude bar on the side of the screen is as important as the playfield itself.

Getting started

  1. Press 1 or 2 on the keypad at the title.
  2. Your fighter flies forward automatically. The joystick steers left/right and adjusts altitude.
  3. Watch the altitude indicator (the vertical bar on the side) — there's no other reliable way to tell how high you are over the terrain.

Strategy

  • The altitude bar is the boss. Most deaths are flying-into-a-wall, not enemy fire. Always glance at the side gauge before nosing down.
  • Refuel constantly. Fuel tanks are scattered through the base — shooting them adds to your tank. Run out and you crash.
  • Stay low in the asteroid base, high in space. Each section has a different "safe" altitude — adjust on the transition.
  • The Zaxxon boss is a sponge. Multiple hits to bring it down — keep firing through its return fire.

ColecoVision controls

Joystick + left fire button. The diagonal scrolling makes the joystick feel rotated; line up shots by tracking the altitude bar rather than the apparent screen direction.
