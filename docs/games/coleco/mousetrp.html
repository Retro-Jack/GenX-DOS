Mouse Trap

Coleco, 1982 — port of Exidy's 1981 arcade maze game

A maze game in the Pac-Man tradition, but you can lock and unlock doors mid-chase. Eat the cheese, dodge the cats, and trigger door-colour buttons at the screen edges to seal cats behind you. The hidden hook: occasionally pick up the bone powerup and turn into a dog, then chase the cats yourself.

Getting started

  1. Press 1 or 2 on the keypad at the title.
  2. The maze has three colours of door (red, yellow, blue). Each colour is opened/closed by its own keypad button — Numpad 1 / Numpad 2 / Numpad 3 — from anywhere in the maze, not by walking up to a wall switch.
  3. Eat all the cheese to clear the level.

Strategy

  • Doors aren't just defense. Sealing a chasing cat behind a closed door is the core mechanic — use the colour buttons aggressively, not just when cornered.
  • Save bones for cat clusters. Eating the bone turns you into a dog for a few seconds; one isolated cat isn't worth it. Wait for two or three together.
  • Learn the keypad-to-colour mapping. Numpad 1/2/3 each control one door colour — knowing which number seals the door between you and a cat without looking down is what saves lives.
  • Hawks ignore doors. The hawk that appears in later levels flies over walls — there's no maze trick for it, just dodge.

ColecoVision controls

Joystick to move; the keypad drives the doors. Numpad 1/2/3 open and close the three colours of door from anywhere on the screen, and Numpad 5 turns the mouse into a dog while you hold a bone. The fire buttons aren't used.
