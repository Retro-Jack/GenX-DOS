Smurf: Rescue in Gargamel's Castle

Coleco, 1982 — original by Coleco's in-house team (also released for the 2600 and Atari 8-bit)

System help — the ColecoVision controls

A scrolling platformer: Smurf treks across forest, fence, and cave screens to reach Gargamel's castle and rescue Smurfette. Each screen has its own hazards — spiders, snakes, bats, the river — and Smurf jumps fences, ducks bats, and times runs through narrow gaps. Lives are measured in Smurfs; lose three and the rescue fails.

Getting started

  1. Press 1 or 2 on the keypad at the title.
  2. Smurf walks right by default. Reach the right edge of each screen to advance to the next.
  3. Jump hazards with the fire button; some sections need both walk and jump in sequence.

Strategy

  • Don't sprint blind. Each new screen has different hazards in fixed positions — pause briefly to assess.
  • Jump heights matter. Smurf's jump arc is fixed; some fences require a running start to clear.
  • The river crossing. Wait for the floating log to align with your bank, then jump as it drifts.
  • Smurfette is at the right edge. The castle is the final screen — once you reach her, the screen pauses for the rescue.

ColecoVision controls

Joystick + left fire button. The keypad is unused during play.
