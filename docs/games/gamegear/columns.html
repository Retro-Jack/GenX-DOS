Handheld · Sega Game Gear (1991)

Columns

Sega's gem-matching puzzler and a Game Gear pack-in — the colour handheld's answer to Tetris, and a perfect fit for the format.

System help — the Sega Game Gear controls

Coloured jewels fall three at a time in a vertical column. Land them so that three or more of the same colour line up — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — and they vanish, dropping everything above into fresh combos. The drop speeds up as you go; survive and chain for the big scores.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, press Enter (Start); pick Arcade (endless) or Flash mode.
  2. Cycle the three gems in a column and slot them where colours will match.

Strategy

  • Diagonals are free combos. Build so a single placement completes a vertical, a horizontal *and* a diagonal at once.
  • Keep the stack low and flat — a tall, jagged pile leaves no room to set up matches.
  • Chain reactions (clears that trigger more clears as gems fall) are where the score really climbs.
  • Watch the next piece and pre-plan its landing column before it drops.
