Handheld · Sega Game Gear (1992)

Defenders of Oasis

A proper turn-based RPG on the Game Gear, wrapped in an Arabian Nights setting — one of the handheld's hidden gems for anyone who wants a real adventure on the go.

System help — the Sega Game Gear controls

A prince must save his desert kingdom from the demon Ahriman. Explore towns and dungeons, talk to townsfolk, and fight random encounters in classic menu-driven turn-based battles — attack, magic, items, flee. Recruit companions (including a genie), level up, gear up, and work towards the final confrontation.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, press Enter (Start); begin a new game.
  2. Explore, talk to everyone, and expect random battles between locations.

Strategy

  • Talk to everyone. Town NPCs gate progress with hints and key items — wandering off without them stalls the story.
  • Grind a little before bosses. A few extra levels and better gear make the demon fights far kinder.
  • Save often (menu → Save); battery save persists in the browser, and it's a long quest.
  • Keep healing items stocked before heading into a new dungeon — supply runs back to town are tedious.
