Handheld · Sega Game Gear (1992)

Aerial Assault

A solid horizontal/vertical scrolling shoot-'em-up — fly a prototype fighter to take down the eco-terrorist group EDF before they wreck the planet.

Six stages of side-scrolling shooting: blast waves of enemy craft and ground installations, grab power-ups to upgrade your main gun and add options, and survive a screen-filling boss at the end of each. A good-looking, good-playing GG shooter that punches above the handheld's weight.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, press Enter (Start); you launch straight into stage 1.
  2. Shoot the carriers/power-up pods and collect the icons they drop.

Strategy

  • Keep your weapon level up. Dying drops you back to the pea-shooter — grab every power-up and play it safe once upgraded.
  • Stay mobile. The small screen gives little reaction time; weave constantly rather than parking in one lane.
  • Save the bomb for boss patterns or screen-clearing emergencies, not stray enemies.
  • Learn the boss approaches — each end-of-stage boss telegraphs an opening if you don't panic.
