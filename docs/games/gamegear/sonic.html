Handheld · Sega Game Gear (1991)

Sonic the Hedgehog

The Game Gear's flagship — a ground-up 8-bit Sonic (distinct from the Mega Drive game), built by Ancient/Aspect, with its own zones and a famous trick to the Special Stages.

Blast through six zones of loops, springs and badniks as Sonic, collecting rings and freeing animals. The 8-bit Sonic is tuned for the small screen — a bit more deliberate than the Mega Drive blueprint, with platforming that rewards looking before you leap. Collect the Chaos Emeralds (one hidden in each zone's first act) for the good ending.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, press Enter (Start) past the title.
  2. Run right; jump with Z. Keep at least one ring to survive a hit.

Strategy

  • Rings are your shield. Take a hit with rings and you just scatter them (grab a few back); take one with zero rings and you're dead.
  • The Chaos Emeralds are hidden in Act 1 of each zone, not in special stages — explore the first act of every zone to find them.
  • Don't over-run blind. The small screen hides pits and spikes; momentum is good but look ahead.
  • Roll down slopes to build speed through loops and gaps without holding run into danger.
