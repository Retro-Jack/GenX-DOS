Handheld · Sega Game Gear (1992)

Streets of Rage

Sega's flagship beat-'em-up, squeezed onto the Game Gear (released as Bare Knuckle in Japan). Clean up a crime-ridden city with your fists, feet and the odd dropped weapon.

Pick an ex-cop and walk right, thumping wave after wave of street thugs across eight stages to a final showdown with crime boss Mr. X. Punch, kick, grab and throw; pick up pipes and bottles; and call in a one-off special police backup when you're swamped. The Game Gear cut is trimmed but keeps the swagger and the music.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, press Enter (Start); choose your fighter.
  2. Walk right, beat everyone on screen, advance to the next group.

Strategy

  • Use the depth. Step up/down out of an enemy's line, then strike from the side — don't stand in a crossfire.
  • Grabs and throws clear space fast and can knock one thug into another.
  • Save the special for when you're surrounded; it's your panic button, not a combo opener.
  • Grab weapons and food — pipes hit hard, and roast chicken refills your health.
