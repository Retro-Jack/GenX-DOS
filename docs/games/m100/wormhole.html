Worm Hole (Snake)

A Snake/Tron-style survival game from David Busch's 25 Games for your TRS-80 Model 100 (TAB Books, 1984).

System help — the TRS-80 Model 100 controls

Your worm crawls across the screen and never stops, leaving a solid trail behind it. Steer with the arrow keys to weave around the scattered obstacles — and, as the trail grows, around your own body. You score a point for every step you survive; the only way it ends is a crash.

Getting started

  1. Answer Instructions? with Y or N.
  2. Set the speed, 1 (fast) to 9 (slow), then the difficulty, 1 (easy) to 9 (hard) — difficulty controls how many obstacles litter the screen.
  3. The worm sets off on its own. Turn it with the arrows to stay clear of everything.

Controls

KeyboardAction
Arrow keysTurn the worm up / down / left / right

You only change direction — the worm moves by itself at the speed you set, so plan your turns early.

Crashing

Run into an obstacle, your own trail or the edge and it's over. The game shows how many points you racked up and offers another go — decline and it returns you to the Model 100 menu.
