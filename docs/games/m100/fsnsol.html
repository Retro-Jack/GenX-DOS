Four Seasons Solitaire

From The Model 100 Companion — a tokenized-BASIC rendering of the classic Four Seasons patience (also called Corner Card or Vanishing Cross), with machine-code graphics and sound squeezed onto the 240 × 64 LCD.

System help — the TRS-80 Model 100 controls

A single-deck patience laid out as a cross. You build one suit up from each foundation and shuffle cards around a five-pile cross to free the ones you need — a compact, thinky game that suits the Model 100's screen and its unhurried, take-it-anywhere character.

Getting started

  1. The game loads into the Model 100 and starts on its own — you drop straight into the deal.
  2. Free the four aces (one per suit) up onto the foundations, then build each foundation up in suit to the King.
  3. Use the cross piles to stage cards; when you're stuck, turn the stock for the next card.

The layout

  • The cross — five tableau piles arranged as a plus sign. You may build these down regardless of suit and move cards between them to dig out what you need.
  • Four foundations — one corner for each suit's ace, built up in suit (A 2 3 … K).
  • The stock — the rest of the deck, turned up a card at a time when you have no other move.

Controls

Four Seasons is played from just a few keys. It opens by asking "Keep cumulative scores?" — press Y or N — then initialises and shuffles the deck. From then on the bottom line prompts PLAY? and shows how many cards are left in the stock alongside the current card:

KeyAction
MMove the current card onto a cross pile
HSend the current card Home to a foundation
QQuit the game

Any other key just re-shows the Please type <M>, <H> or <Q> reminder, so you can't go wrong.

Winning

You win when all four foundations are complete — every suit built up from ace to King. As with most single-deck patiences, not every deal is winnable; the skill is in seeing the order to unstack the cross before the stock runs dry.
