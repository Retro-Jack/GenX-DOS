Skydiver

An arcade timing game from David Busch's 25 Games for your TRS-80 Model 100 (TAB Books, 1984) — a gentler cousin of the old bomber games, dropping parachutists instead of bombs.

System help — the TRS-80 Model 100 controls

A plane tracks across the top of the screen while a landing pad waits below. Release your parachutist at the right moment and he drifts down on a diagonal — time it so he settles onto the pad. Nail it for ten points; miss and he crashes for a penalty. Then the plane comes round again.

Getting started

  1. Answer Instructions? with Y or N.
  2. Pick a difficulty from 1 (hard) to 4 (easy) — the number sets how wide the landing pad is.
  3. Watch the plane cross the top and tap any key to release the chute. Judge the diagonal so it lands on the pad.

Controls

KeyAction
Any keyRelease the parachutist (while the plane is flying)
QAt the between-rounds prompt, quit to the Model 100 menu

Scoring

A safe landing on the pad is worth ten points; a crash costs you one. There's no finish line — it's pure score-attack, so keep landing to build your total, and press Q when you've had your fill.
