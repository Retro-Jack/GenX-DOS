Bowling

A full ten-frame game from David Busch's 25 Games for your TRS-80 Model 100 (TAB Books, 1984) — proper strikes, spares and running totals, using the width of the LCD as the lane.

System help — the TRS-80 Model 100 controls

A ball tracks up and down the left edge of the screen while the pins wait across the lane. Release the ball when it's lined up where you want it, and watch it roll across and scatter the pins with a beep. Two balls a frame, ten frames, scored just like the real thing — a strike borrows the next two balls, a spare the next one.

Getting started

  1. Answer Instructions? with Y or N.
  2. Set the speed, 1 (fast) to 9 (slow) — this is how quickly the ball tracks up and down, so it sets the difficulty of your timing.
  3. When the ball reaches the height you want, press any key to release it. It rolls across the lane into the pins.

Controls

KeyAction
Any keyRelease the ball (while it's tracking up and down)

Where the ball is on the left when you let go decides which pins it hits — aim high or low to work the corners.

Scoring

Standard ten-pin scoring across ten frames, totals posting frame by frame along the bottom. Knock them all down with the first ball for a strike, or clear the rest with the second for a spare. After the tenth frame it's game over — play again, or decline to drop back to the Model 100 menu.
