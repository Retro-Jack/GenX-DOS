Paper, Rock, Scissors

The playground classic against the computer, from David Busch's 25 Games for your TRS-80 Model 100 (TAB Books, 1984).

You and the Model 100 each pick paper, rock or scissors; the winner takes a point. The computer chooses at random after you've committed — the book cheerfully assures you it isn't peeking — so there's no strategy to it, just the timeless standoff. First to fifteen points wins.

Getting started

  1. Answer Do you want instructions? with Y or N.
  2. Each round the running score shows, then you're asked to Select. Press your weapon; the computer reveals its own and the point is awarded.
  3. Hit any key to move to the next round. First to fifteen takes the match.

Controls

KeyChoice
PPaper — wraps rock
RRock — breaks scissors
SScissors — cut paper

Upper or lower case both work. Matching weapons is a tie — no points either way.

Winning

The first of you to reach fifteen points wins the match, and it offers a rematch. Decline and it drops you back to the Model 100 menu.
