Slot Machine

A pocket one-armed bandit from David Busch's 25 Games for your TRS-80 Model 100 (TAB Books, 1984) — the author cheerfully admits he rigged the odds so you'll go bust.

System help — the TRS-80 Model 100 controls

You start with $200 and feed the machine one silver dollar a spin. Three fruits roll up — cherries, lemons or oranges. Match three, or land one of each, and you're paid; anything else and your dollar's gone. The stated object of the game? To see how long you can keep pressing keys before the money runs out.

Getting started

  1. Answer Instructions? with Y or N.
  2. Press any key to pull the handle. The three reels roll and settle.
  3. Your balance shows after each spin. Keep going until the machine cleans you out.
