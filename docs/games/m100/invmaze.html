Invisible Maze

A timed race through a maze you can't see, from David Busch's 25 Games for your TRS-80 Model 100 (TAB Books, 1984).

Your cursor starts at the top-left of the screen; the exit is the right-hand edge. Between them lies a maze of invisible walls. Feel your way across with the arrow keys — hit a wall and you're buzzed and stopped — and do it as fast as you can, because the clock is running.

Getting started

  1. Answer Do you want instructions? with Y or N.
  2. Pick a difficulty from 1 (easy) to 9 (hard) — higher means more walls in your way.
  3. Steer the * from the left edge to the right edge. Reach it and the game tells you your time.

Controls

KeyAction
Arrow keysMove the cursor up / down / left / right
DPeek — flash the whole maze on screen briefly
QQuit

Peeking with D costs you time but shows every wall — memorise what you can before it vanishes again.

Winning

Get from the left edge to the right edge and it reports how many minutes and seconds you took. Faster is better — so the real game is deciding how often you dare peek.
