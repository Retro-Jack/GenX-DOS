Shinobi

Sega, 1987 — a ninja rescuing hostages, with shuriken and a limited magic.

System help — the arcade controls

Joe Musashi crosses five missions freeing hostages before facing each boss. Reaching every hostage is not optional: miss one and the stage does not open. Ninjutsu clears the screen, once per life.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Every hostage must be freed. The exit does not open until they are all released — missing one means the stage cannot be completed at all.
  • Ninjutsu is once per life. It clears the screen, and dying is the only way to get another. Treat it as a single card per life.
  • Crouch to change your firing line. Standing and crouching shots cover different heights, and most enemies commit to one.
  • The bonus round is 10,000. Worth more than most of a stage, and reached by freeing the hostages rather than by fighting.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
