Arcade · 1981 Namco

Bosconian

Namco, 1981 — a free-scrolling shooter with a radar and a countdown.

System help — the arcade controls

Fly a scrolling starfield destroying enemy bases before the fleet arrives. The radar and the spy ship warnings matter more than the shooting: the map is far bigger than the window on it.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The radar is the game. The field is far larger than the screen. Everything worth destroying is found on the radar first and shot second.
  • Bases die from the core. Shooting the cannons is optional; destroying the centre destroys the station.
  • The spy ship is announced. A synthesised voice tells you it has arrived. It is worth chasing, and it is worth more the sooner you catch it.
  • Clear the field before the fleet. Take too long and a formation arrives to hunt you across the whole map.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
