Vigilante

Irem, 1988 — Vigilante, a beat-'em-up on Kung-Fu Master's frame with a nunchaku.

System help — the arcade controls

A vigilante walks east through five stages of a wrecked city to find Madonna. The nunchaku is a pick-up with reach and speed the fists do not have, and you lose it when you take a hit.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The nunchaku is temporary. It has reach and speed the fists do not, and you drop it when you are hit — which makes taking a hit doubly expensive.
  • It is Kung-Fu Master’s frame. Same rhythm: crowds from both sides, crouch for the low attacks, a boss at the end of each stage.
  • Do not get surrounded. There is no screen-clearing move; being flanked is how runs end.
