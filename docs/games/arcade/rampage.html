Arcade · 1986 Midway

Rampage

Midway, 1986 — three people turned into monsters, demolishing 128 cities.

System help — the arcade controls

George, Lizzie and Ralph punch buildings down floor by floor while the army shoots at them. Eating what is inside the buildings restores health, and some of it is poison.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Vehicles are worth fifteen soldiers. Punching a taxi or a police car pays far more than fighting the people shooting at you.
  • Eat to heal, but check what you are eating. Food inside buildings restores health; some of what looks like food does the opposite.
  • Buildings come down floor by floor. A building is a series of holes, each worth 200, so demolition is steady income rather than one payout.
  • Three monsters, one screen. In multiplayer you can punch each other, and a stunned monster is one that is not scoring.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
