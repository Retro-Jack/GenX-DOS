Sinistar

Williams, 1982 — 'Beware, I live!', and a boss that hunts you across the map.

System help — the arcade controls

Mine crystals from asteroids to build sinibombs while the enemy builds Sinistar from the same field. When it is finished it comes for you, and only the sinibombs will stop it.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The real cabinet used an analogue stick, so hold a direction rather than tapping it — a tap is often too brief to register.

Strategy

  • Mine before it wakes. Crystals become sinibombs, and sinibombs are the only thing that will stop Sinistar. Building the stock is the first half of the game.
  • It is assembled from the same field. The enemy builds Sinistar out of the asteroids you are mining, so a slow game is one where it finishes first.
  • Listen. It announces itself before it arrives. That warning is the cue to stop mining and start running.
  • Momentum wraps. Space here carries you; there is no braking, only turning and burning fuel.
