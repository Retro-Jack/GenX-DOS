Arcade · 1985 Sega

Hang-On

Sega, 1985 — Yu Suzuki's superbike racer, and the first of Sega's ride-on cabinets.

System help — the arcade controls

Ride a course against the clock, leaning into corners and passing traffic. The original cabinet was a motorcycle you sat on and tilted; here the lean is on the movement control.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The real cabinet used an analogue stick, so hold a direction rather than tapping it — a tap is often too brief to register.

Controls

No buttons at all — the control panel was an analogue stick and nothing else.

Strategy

  • Lean into the corner. The bike steers by leaning; a corner taken upright will run wide whatever the throttle is doing.
  • Checkpoints are the only clock. Reaching one extends the run. There is no finish to reach, only the next gate.
  • Traffic is scenery until it is not. The other bikes do not race you; they exist to be in the way at the wrong moment.

No point values are documented for this cabinet.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
