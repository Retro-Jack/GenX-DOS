Ghosts'n Goblins

Capcom, 1985 — Ghosts'n Goblins, famous for being merciless and for costing you your trousers.

System help — the arcade controls

Arthur crosses a graveyard, a forest and a castle to reach the Princess. Two hits kill you: the first takes your armour and leaves you in your underwear, the second takes you. Finishing the game once is not finishing it.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Two hits and you are done. The first takes your armour, the second takes you. The armour is the health bar.
  • The lance is the reliable weapon. It travels straight and fast; several of the others arc or drop and will get you killed while you learn them.
  • Learn where things spawn, not where they are. Enemies appear at fixed points as you scroll, so the danger is the position you walk into.
  • Finishing once is not finishing. The game requires a second run through before it ends properly.
