Operation Wolf

Taito, 1987 — a light-gun game with a mounted machine gun and six missions.

System help — the arcade controls

Rescue hostages from a jungle camp, shooting soldiers while avoiding the hostages themselves. The cabinet had a bolted-down gun; here you aim with the movement control.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The real cabinet used a light gun, so hold a direction rather than tapping it — a tap is often too brief to register.

Strategy

  • Vehicles are worth twenty soldiers. A helicopter at 2,000 or a HIND at 7,000 dwarfs the infantry. Clear the aircraft first and the ground second.
  • Never shoot a hostage. They appear among the soldiers and hitting one costs you directly — the game is as much about not firing as firing.
  • Ammunition and magazines are pickups. Both are finite and both are dropped in the field; running dry mid-wave is the usual way missions fail.
  • Mission completion is 10,000. More than any single target, so finishing matters more than farming.

Arcade controls
