Blaster

Williams, 1983 — a first-person shooter through space, from the Robotron team.

System help — the arcade controls

Fly into the screen through waves of enemies towards a distant paradise. Extremely fast, extremely hard, and one of the first games to charge more than a quarter to play.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The real cabinet used an analogue stick, so hold a direction rather than tapping it — a tap is often too brief to register.

Running into an enemy scores, but costs shields; the magic arches score nothing at all.

Strategy

  • Consecutive kills escalate. Ground robots pay 100 through to 500 for an unbroken run, so a clean sequence is worth three times a broken one.
  • Ramming works and hurts. You can score by collision, but it comes out of your shields — a deliberate trade rather than an accident.
  • The arches are not targets. They score nothing and cost shields. Fly through the gaps.
  • It is extremely fast. This was a game that charged more than a quarter because runs were short. Reading the wave ahead matters more than aim.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
