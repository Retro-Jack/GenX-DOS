Out Run

Sega, 1986 — a Ferrari, a passenger, and a choice of road at every junction.

System help — the arcade controls

Drive west across Europe against the clock, choosing left or right at each fork so no two runs are the same. Five endings, three radio stations, and no other cars to race — only the clock.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The real cabinet used an analogue stick, so hold a direction rather than tapping it — a tap is often too brief to register.

Strategy

  • Choose the road. Every stage ends in a fork, and the five endings come from those choices. The right-hand routes are generally harder and shorter.
  • The clock is the only opponent. There are no rivals to beat, only checkpoints to reach, so a clean line matters more than a fast one.
  • Off-road costs more than it looks. Leaving the tarmac scrubs speed badly and the recovery takes longer than the avoidance would have.
