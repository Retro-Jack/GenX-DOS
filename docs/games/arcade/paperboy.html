Paperboy

Atari, 1984 — a week's paper round down Easy, Medium and Hard Street.

System help — the arcade controls

Ride down the street delivering to subscribers and not to anyone else. A paper through a subscriber's letterbox scores; a paper through a non-subscriber's window scores more but is vandalism, and vandalising a subscriber loses you the customer. Survive the street and the BMX obstacle course follows.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The cabinet used handlebars; hold a direction to steer and to speed up or slow down.

Strategy

  • The mailbox pays more than twice the doorstep. It is a harder throw and worth taking.
  • Vandalism only pays on non-subscribers. The same window costs you the customer if they take the paper — and a lost customer is gone for the rest of the week.
  • Keep papers in hand. Bundles are only 50 points but running dry mid-street ends the round's earnings.
  • The training course is where the safe points are. No traffic, no dogs, and the time bonus rewards finishing rather than surviving.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
