Qix

Taito, 1981 — draw boxes to claim territory, without being touched while drawing.

System help — the arcade controls

Claim 75 percent of the screen by drawing rectangles, while the Qix drifts inside the unclaimed area and Sparx run the borders. Slow draws are worth double and are far more dangerous.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Scoring

ActionPoints
Each area captured1 point per pixel
Captured with a slow draw throughout2 points per pixel

The target is 75 percent of the screen; a slow draw is worth double but leaves you exposed for twice as long.

Strategy

  • Slow draws pay double and cost time. That is the entire risk-reward of the game: the same box is worth twice as much if you never speed up while drawing it.
  • Cut the Qix into a corner. Splitting the free area traps it in a smaller space, which makes every later claim safer.
  • The Sparx own the borders. You are safe on a completed edge only until they arrive along it.
  • An unfinished line kills you. Stopping mid-draw is not neutral — the line you are standing on is lethal to touch until it is closed.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
