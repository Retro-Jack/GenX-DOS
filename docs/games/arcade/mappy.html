Mappy

Namco, 1983 — a police mouse, a mansion of cats, and a great many trampolines.

System help — the arcade controls

Mappy recovers stolen goods from Nyamco and his cats across a side-on mansion, bouncing between floors on trampolines. Doors are the weapon: opened at the right moment they flatten whatever is behind them.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Two of each item per mansion, and collecting a matching pair consecutively multiplies the second — the multiplier grows to six for the last pair, so a perfect mansion taken cheapest-first is worth about 8,500.

Strategy

  • Collect in pairs, cheapest first. The consecutive-pair bonus grows through the mansion, so the safes should be last, when the multiplier is highest.
  • Doors are the weapon. A door opened as a cat reaches it flattens the cat. That is the only offence Mappy has.
  • Trampolines wear out. Each has a limited number of bounces before it breaks and drops you. Watch the colour change.
  • Microwave doors clear a corridor. They send a wave down the floor that catches anything in it — worth saving for a crowd.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
