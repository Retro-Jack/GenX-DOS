Arcade · 1984 Atari

Marble Madness

Atari, 1984 — Mark Cerny's isometric race, and the first arcade game with a stereo FM soundtrack.

System help — the arcade controls

Roll a marble down six isometric courses against the clock. There are no lives and no enemies you can shoot — only the clock, the edges, and creatures that shove, flatten or eat you. Time left over is carried into the next race.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. Hold a direction rather than tapping it — the real cabinet had a trackball, and a tap is too brief to register as a roll.

Controls

This cabinet had no buttons at all — the trackball was the whole control panel.

Level scores are paid as you cross the flags; the deductions and the Ultimate Level bonus are settled at the end of the game.

Strategy

  • Leftover time carries forward. Finishing a course early does not just score — it buys you the seconds you will need on the next one.
  • Falling costs a thousand and the clock. The clock keeps running while the marble is replaced, which is usually the worse half.
  • Hold the direction, do not tap it. This was a trackball; short taps barely register, and the marble carries momentum either way.
  • There is nothing to shoot. Everything on the course is avoided, not fought — the only opponent is the clock.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
