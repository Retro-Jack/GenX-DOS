Donkey Kong

Nintendo, 1981 — Miyamoto's breakthrough, and the first appearance of Mario.

System help — the arcade controls

A carpenter climbs four stages of girders, lifts, conveyors and rivets to reach the ape at the top. Barrels roll, fireballs chase, and the hammer is on a timer you cannot control.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

These are the values from the cabinet’s own instruction manual.

Strategy

  • Jumping two barrels doubles the pay. A single jump is 100 and a double is 200, so letting two bunch up before you jump is worth doing when the gap allows.
  • The hammer is on a timer you cannot stop. It cannot be put down and it cannot be carried up a ladder, so take it only when there is a run of barrels in front of you.
  • The accessories are 300 each. Olive’s hat, umbrella and purse are worth three barrels apiece and are usually somewhere awkward.
  • Rivets pay per rivet. The last stage is scored by removal, not by speed — every one is 100 whether you rush or not.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
