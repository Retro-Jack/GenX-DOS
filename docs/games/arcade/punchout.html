Arcade · 1984 Nintendo

Punch-Out!!

Nintendo, 1984 — two screens, a wireframe boxer, and a queue of opponents with patterns.

System help — the arcade controls

You box from behind a translucent fighter, watching the opponent for the tell that precedes each punch. Every fighter has a pattern; the game is learning it before your health runs out.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

This cabinet used 3 buttons. Buttons not listed here do nothing on this machine.

Scoring

ActionPoints
First punch of a combination40
Following punches80 to 200
Hook300
Uppercut500
Any punch that knocks the opponent down500

The bonus starts at 30,000 and falls by 135 points a second, so the fight itself is a countdown.

Strategy

  • The clock is worth more than the punches. At 135 a second the bonus dwarfs the individual hits — a fast knockout scores far better than a long, tidy one.
  • The hook does more damage than the uppercut. It is worth fewer points but lands more easily and hits harder, so chase the knockdown rather than the 500.
  • Every opponent telegraphs. There is a tell before each punch. The game is entirely about learning it, not about reflexes.
  • Watch through your own fighter. Your boxer is translucent for a reason; the information is behind him.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
