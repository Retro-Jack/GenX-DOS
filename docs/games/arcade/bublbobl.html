Arcade · 1986 Taito

Bubble Bobble

Taito, 1986 — two dragons, a hundred rooms, and a secret ending.

System help — the arcade controls

Bub and Bob trap enemies in bubbles and pop them. The true ending requires two players and a long list of hidden conditions, which is why the game has been picked over for forty years.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

Strategy

  • Bubbles are platforms as well as traps. You can stand on your own bubbles to reach ledges the jump will not.
  • Pop them in groups. Several enemies trapped and burst together are worth considerably more than the same enemies one at a time.
  • The clock brings a chase. Take too long on a room and something arrives that cannot be trapped.
