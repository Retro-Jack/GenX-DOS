Bubbles

Williams, 1982 — you are a bubble cleaning a kitchen sink.

System help — the arcade controls

Clean the sink by absorbing crumbs and grease while ants, sponges and razor blades try to burst you. Growing makes you stronger but also a larger target.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

No buttons at all — the control panel was the stick and nothing else.

Strategy

  • Work the drain. Everything is worth double taken around it, which makes the most dangerous part of the sink the most profitable.
  • Growing is a trade. A bigger bubble absorbs more but is a bigger target, and the enlargement bonus only pays if you survive it.
  • The Cleaning Lady is a weapon. Collecting her gives you the sweeping ability that deals with Greasies and Roaches, which you cannot otherwise touch.
  • Ants and razors do not chase equally. Learn which hazards track you and which merely occupy space; only the first need answering.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
