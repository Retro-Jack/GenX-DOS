Arcade · 1983 Atari

I, Robot

Atari, 1983 — Dave Theurer's third entry here, and the first arcade game with filled 3-D polygons.

System help — the arcade controls

You are Interface Robot, hopping across floating playfields towards Big Brother's eye. Red squares are lethal until the eye closes; jump only when it is shut. Clear the field, survive the untitled space between levels, and do it again.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The cabinet used an analogue stick, so hold a direction rather than tapping.

Controls

The panel had two buttons, but the emulator’s own data marks the second as unused, so only one does anything here.

Strategy

  • The bonus is a clock. It starts between 3,000 and 7,000 and falls the whole time the field is up, so hesitation costs more than mistakes.
  • Jump only when the eye is shut. Red squares are lethal while it is open, and it is watching for exactly that.
  • The space sections are free points. Nothing there is required — shoot what you can reach on the way through.
  • Ten jewels, 999 cap. Collecting all ten scores more than the section will pay out, so there is no reason to take a risk for the last one or two.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
