Battlezone

Atari, 1980 — Ed Rotberg's first-person tank battle, and the first 3-D vector game.

System help — the arcade controls

A first-person tank duel across a vector landscape. Enemy tanks, supertanks and missiles approach from all sides; the radar at the top of the screen shows where. The volcano and the blocks are scenery, but the blocks will stop a shell — yours and theirs.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

Battlezone has two sticks, one per tread, and both are mapped. Push both the same way to drive; push one only, or push them opposite ways, to turn on the spot. Fire is the one button that matters.

Strategy

  • Read the radar, not the screen. The wedge at the top shows everything around you, including what is behind. Most deaths are something you never turned to look at.
  • Aerial fighters always come from the front. They open fire from close range and are hard to dodge once there, so shoot them on the approach rather than manoeuvring.
  • Turn away from an incoming shell, then edge forward. Doing both hard at once tends to swing you back into its path.
  • The blocks are cover. Scenery stops a shell either way — put one between you and a tank you are not ready to face.
  • The saucer is free points. It does not shoot. It is worth more than anything else on the field.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
