Robotron: 2084

Williams, 1982 — Robotron: 2084, the game that made two sticks a genre.

System help — the arcade controls

One stick moves, the other fires, independently — so you can retreat while shooting the way you came. Rescue the last human family from waves of robots on a single screen with no cover at all.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

No buttons at all — the control panel was two sticks and nothing else.

Strategy

  • Spheroids are worth ten Grunts. And they are transports: killing one before it opens denies you a screenful of Enforcers as well as paying 1,000.
  • Kill Brains before they reach a human. A Brain converts humans into Progs that hunt you. Every one it reaches is a rescue lost and a pursuer gained.
  • Hulks cannot be killed, only pushed. Shooting one shoves it. Do not spend the wave trying to clear something that will not clear.
  • Fire away from where you are going. That is what the second stick is for — retreating and shooting back down your own path is the core skill.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
