Arcade · 1988 Irem

Image Fight

Irem, 1988 — Image Fight, a vertical shooter that will send you back to do it again.

System help — the arcade controls

A combat simulator that grades you on the way through: fail the accuracy requirement in the training stages and you are made to fly a penalty stage before continuing. The pods orbiting your ship can be locked in eight directions.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

The emulator lists four buttons but Irem’s own flyer names two, and those are the two the game uses.

Strategy

The mechanics below are from Irem’s own sales flyer for the cabinet.

  • The first five stages are graded. Each one reports an accuracy percentage when you finish it. Average below 90 percent across the five and you are sent to a supplementary training stage before you may continue.
  • Only a 90 percent pilot reaches the real fight. Clear all five at 90 or better and stage six — the actual combat, rather than the simulation — opens.
  • Speed is a four-level control. The B button steps through four speeds, shown at the bottom of the screen, rather than accelerating continuously.
  • Pick your pods deliberately. A red pod turns opposite to the stick and fires in sixteen directions; a blue pod is fixed forward. Up to three can be carried, and taking a fourth recolours all three.
  • Fire the pods with both buttons. With pods equipped, pressing A and B together launches them as a shot. The forepart can only be replaced once the one you have is destroyed.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
