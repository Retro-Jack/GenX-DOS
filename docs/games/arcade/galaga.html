Galaga

Namco, 1981 — the follow-up to Galaxian, and the captured fighter.

System help — the arcade controls

Aliens fly in, form up, then dive. The boss can capture your ship with a tractor beam — and if you shoot it while it holds your fighter, you get the ship back and fly two abreast with double fire.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Shoot them on the way down. A diving enemy is worth about twice a stationary one, so patience during the formation phase pays.
  • A flagship with two escorts is worth 1,600. Killing the escorts first throws away three quarters of the value. Take the whole group as it dives.
  • Losing a ship to the tractor beam is not losing it. Let it be captured, then shoot the flagship holding it and you fly two ships abreast with double the fire.
  • Do not shoot your own captured ship. It is flying alongside the enemy formation and it is still yours.

Arcade controls
