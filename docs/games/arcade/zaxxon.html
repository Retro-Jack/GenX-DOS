Zaxxon

Sega, 1982 — the first isometric shooter, and the first game to show a shadow.

System help — the arcade controls

Fly an axonometric fortress at a height you control as well as a position. The shadow beneath your ship is the only way to judge that height, which is why it is there.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Fuel tanks are survival, not score. Fuel drains continuously and only the tanks refill it, so the 300 points are a side effect of staying airborne.
  • Fly by the shadow. It is the only indication of your altitude, and altitude is what decides whether a shot passes over a wall or into it.
  • Radar towers are worth five planes. At 1,000 apiece they are the best target on the fortress and are usually placed awkwardly.
  • Height is a weapon. Most gun emplacements cannot elevate. Crossing high over a battery is safer than shooting through it.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
