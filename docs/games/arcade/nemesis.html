Arcade · 1985 Konami

Nemesis

Konami, 1985 — the first Gradius, and the power-up bar that every shooter copied.

System help — the arcade controls

A horizontal shooter where capsules do not upgrade you directly: they advance a selector along a bar, and you choose when to spend. Speed early, missiles later, and the Option drones last.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The bar is a budget, not an upgrade. Capsules move a selector along; the choice is when to spend, and spending early on speed costs you the Options later.
  • Options are the real weapon. The trailing drones double and quadruple your fire and cannot be destroyed. Most of a good run is built around getting two.
  • Too much speed will kill you. Each speed-up is permanent until you die. Three is usually more than the corridors allow.
