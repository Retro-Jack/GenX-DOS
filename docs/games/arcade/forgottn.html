Arcade · 1988 Capcom

Forgotten Worlds

Capcom, 1988 — Forgotten Worlds, a horizontal shooter where you aim independently of where you fly.

System help — the arcade controls

Two mercenaries float through a ruined future, turning to face any direction while drifting. The cabinet used a rotary stick for the aim; here that rotation is on the movement control, and the shop between stages sells the weapons you will need.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The real cabinet used a spinner, so hold a direction rather than tapping it — a tap is often too brief to register.

Strategy

  • Aim and fly are separate. You rotate to face any direction while drifting, so retreating while firing forward is the basic manoeuvre.
  • The shop is between stages. Money from kills buys the weapons, and the later stages assume you have been buying.
  • Two players share the screen, not the aim. Each rotates independently, which makes covering angles the co-operative part.

No point values are documented for this cabinet.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
