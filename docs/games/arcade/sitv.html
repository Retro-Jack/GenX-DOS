Space Invaders

Taito, 1978 — Space Invaders in Taito's own release, the one Japan played.

System help — the arcade controls

Fifty-five aliens descend in formation and speed up as you shoot them — an accident of the hardware that became the defining feature. Four bases give cover until you or the invaders destroy them.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • They speed up because there are fewer of them. The machine draws what is left each frame, so a thinning formation runs faster. That acceleration is the difficulty curve.
  • Clear a column, not a row. Taking one side down first buys you room at the edge the formation is heading towards.
  • Your own fire destroys the bunkers. Shooting through a shield erodes it from underneath. Step out to fire if you want the cover to last.
  • The top row is worth three of the bottom. And the bottom row is the one that reaches you first, which is the tension the whole game runs on.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
