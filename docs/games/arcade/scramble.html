Scramble

Konami, 1981 — the first side-scrolling shooter with distinct, ordered stages.

System help — the arcade controls

Fly a jet through six terrains to a base at the end. Fuel drains constantly and is only replenished by bombing the fuel dumps below you, so shooting what is in front and bombing what is beneath are the same job.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Fuel tanks are not optional. Fuel drains the whole time and the tanks are the only refill, so the 150 points are incidental — bombing them is how you stay airborne.
  • Bomb ahead of the target. The bomb travels forward as it falls, so a tank directly beneath you is already behind the drop.
  • The cavern sections punish speed. Height is the only control that matters there, and the ceiling arrives faster than the floor.
  • Time in the air is money. Ten points a second means a careful run scores more than a fast one, up to the point the fuel runs out.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
