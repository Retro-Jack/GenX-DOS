Sky Skipper

Nintendo, 1981 — a biplane rescuing animals from a gorilla, released alongside Donkey Kong.

System help — the arcade controls

Fly a biplane over a scrolling landscape, dodging the gorilla's attacks and freeing captured animals. Nintendo's other 1981 arcade game, and much less remembered than its stablemate.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Altitude is the control. The biplane is steered mostly by height; the gorilla’s attacks are aimed at a line you can simply be above or below.
  • Fuel and animals both run out. The rescue is against a limit, so a slow, safe circuit can still fail.
  • Land carefully. Recovering the animals means putting the plane down, which is where most runs end.

No point values are documented for this cabinet.
