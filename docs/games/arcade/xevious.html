Xevious

Namco, 1982 — two weapons, two planes of enemies, and hidden ground targets.

System help — the arcade controls

A vertical shooter where the air and the ground are separate: the zapper hits aircraft, the blaster bombs installations, and the reticle shows where the bomb will land. Some ground targets are buried and invisible.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Air and ground are different weapons. The zapper only hits aircraft and the blaster only hits the ground. Firing the wrong one at a target does nothing at all.
  • The reticle is ahead of you. The bomb lands where the marker is, not where you are, so ground targets are aimed at before you reach them.
  • Some ground targets are buried. Bombing apparently empty terrain is not wasted — parts of the map hide installations that only a blind bomb finds.
  • The mothership is worth four ordinary kills. It appears in areas 4, 9 and 14, and the value rises the more of its core you destroy.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
