Pac-Man

Namco, 1980 — Toru Iwatani's maze game, in its original Japanese release.

System help — the arcade controls

Clear the maze of dots while four ghosts hunt you, each with its own pursuit logic. The power pills turn the hunt around for a few seconds, and the whole game is spent managing those seconds.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

No buttons at all — the control panel was the stick and nothing else.

The ghost values only escalate within a single power pill — eating one ghost per pill scores 200 every time.

Strategy

  • Chain the ghosts. Four ghosts on one pill is 3,000 points; four ghosts on four pills is 800. Almost all of the scoring difference between players is here.
  • The tunnels are yours. Ghosts travel slowly through the side tunnels and you do not, which makes them the reliable way to break a pursuit.
  • Watch what the ghosts do, not where they are. They turn away from you when a pill is eaten. That reversal is the signal the vulnerable period is starting or ending.
  • The fruit is stationary here. It appears under the centre box and stays put — unlike Ms. Pac-Man, you can plan a route to it.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
