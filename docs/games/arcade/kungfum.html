Kung-Fu Master

Irem, 1984 — Kung-Fu Master, the game that started the side-scrolling beat-'em-up.

System help — the arcade controls

Thomas climbs five floors of a temple to reach Sylvia, punching and kicking through grabbers, knife throwers and a boss on each floor. Everything after this genre owes it something.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Five floors, five bosses. Each floor ends with a fight, and the run up to it is a war of attrition on your health bar.
  • Crouch to punch low. The grabbers come in at waist height; standing punches miss them entirely.
  • Grabbers must be shaken off. Waggling free costs health over time, so avoiding the grab is worth more than escaping it quickly.
  • Every enemy has a weak spot. The manual is explicit that different foes are hurt most by different blows, and that striking without a reason is how a run ends.
  • No single blow kills you. Each one drains energy, though — so the danger is accumulation, not any one hit.
  • Do not fight snakes or spinning bombs. They cannot be beaten profitably; the manual’s own advice is to stay out of their way.

Scoring

No point table has been found for the cabinet, but the manual for the home conversion states that an extra life is won at 75,000 points.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
