Arcade · 1982 Midway

Tron

Midway, 1982 — four sub-games from the film, chosen from a wheel.

System help — the arcade controls

Light cycles, tanks, the MCP cone and the grid bugs, each a self-contained game, played in an order you pick. The cabinet glowed blue; the spinner steers and the stick moves.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The real cabinet used a spinner, so hold a direction rather than tapping it — a tap is often too brief to register.

Time left on the clock is added when you reach the I/O Tower. Level one starts with 500 on the timer; every level after it starts with 1,000.

Strategy

  • The timer is score, not just a limit. Whatever is left when you reach the I/O Tower is added, so finishing fast pays directly.
  • Choose the order. The four sub-games are picked from a wheel. Taking the ones you are good at while the clock is generous is a real decision.
  • Clear every grid bug. The completion bonus is worth twenty of them.
  • Tanks take three hits. And each hit is worth more than the last, so a tank you finish is worth 900, not 500.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
