Arcade · 1983 Midway

Tapper

Midway, 1983 — a barman working four bars at once, against the clock.

System help — the arcade controls

Slide drinks down four counters to advancing customers, catch the empties they slide back, and collect the tips. Miss a glass and it smashes; miss a customer and he throws you over the bar.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

An extra barman at 20,000 points. The four bars repeat on a thirteen-level cycle, with more customers each time round.

Strategy

  • Empty mugs are worth more than saloon customers. At 100 apiece, catching the returns is not tidying up — it is the better half of the income.
  • The bars get more valuable as they get harder. An alien-bar patron is worth three times a saloon one, so the later cycles pay for the difficulty.
  • Serve the far end first. A drink slides the whole counter; starting with the nearest customer wastes the length of the bar.
  • The bonus level is 3,000 for one correct answer. It is worth three completed levels and costs nothing to attempt.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
