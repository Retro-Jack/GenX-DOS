Popeye

Nintendo, 1982 — the licence Nintendo wanted before it invented Donkey Kong.

System help — the arcade controls

Popeye collects hearts Olive Oyl drops from the top of the screen while Bluto chases him. The spinach can is on a platform somewhere, and reaching it reverses the chase for a few seconds.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Complete the level after hitting Bluto but before he lands in the water and the 3,000 is not paid. An item that reaches the bottom must be collected within about ten seconds or it breaks and costs a life.

Strategy

  • Catch high, not low. The same heart is worth 500 at the top and 50 at the bottom — ten times the value for reaching it a second or two sooner.
  • Let Bluto land before you finish. The 3,000 is only paid once he is in the water. Finishing the level first throws it away.
  • Spinach scores nothing. It is time, not points. Take it when Bluto is between you and something you need, not for the score.
  • A dropped item is a life. Anything that reaches the bottom is on a ten-second fuse; that is a harder deadline than Bluto is.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
