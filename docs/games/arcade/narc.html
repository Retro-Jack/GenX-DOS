Narc

Williams, 1988 — a drug raid, digitised actors, and a great deal of red.

System help — the arcade controls

Two agents walk right through a city arresting or destroying dealers, with a machine gun and rockets. Notorious in its day for how much of it was on fire.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Rockets are for groups. The machine gun handles individuals; the rockets are worth saving for a crowd or a vehicle.
  • Arresting pays differently from destroying. The game offers both, and they are not equivalent — the choice is part of the scoring.
  • Cover exists. It looks like a straight walk right, but the scenery can be used and the enemies do use it.

Scoring and settings

The service manual gives no point table, but it does state what a game starts with:

Lives per play31 (hardest) to 7 (most liberal)

From the cabinet’s own operator’s manual. These are the factory settings; an operator could change them, so a machine in the wild might not match.
