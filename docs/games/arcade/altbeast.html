Altered Beast

Sega, 1988 — Altered Beast, and 'Welcome to your doom.'

System help — the arcade controls

A resurrected centurion walks right through the underworld collecting spirit orbs. Three orbs transform you into a beast — werewolf, weredragon, werebear — and only the beast can finish the stage.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Three orbs, then fight properly. You are weak until transformed. The white wolves carrying orbs are the priority over everything else on screen.
  • The beast form is the stage. Each level gives a different creature with a different attack; the human sections are the preamble.
  • Do not miss the third orb. Two orbs is a partial transformation and will not carry you through the boss.
  • Bumping costs you. Contact damage is the main threat — spacing matters more than combos here.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
