Ninja Spirit

Irem, 1988 — Ninja Spirit, a side-scrolling action game with four weapons and a shadow.

System help — the arcade controls

A ninja crosses feudal Japan with sword, shuriken, chain-sickle and bombs, switching between them constantly. Collecting a companion gives you a shadow that mirrors your moves a beat behind.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Four weapons, constantly. Sword, shuriken, chain-sickle and bombs each answer a different range. Sticking with one is the common mistake.
  • The shadows fight with you. The companion pick-up mirrors your moves a beat behind, which doubles your output and your reach.
  • Crouch and jump change the weapon arc. Every weapon behaves differently from each stance.
