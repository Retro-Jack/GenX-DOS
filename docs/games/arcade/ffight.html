Final Fight

Capcom, 1989 — Final Fight, the beat-'em-up that defined the form for a decade.

System help — the arcade controls

Haggar, Cody or Guy walk east through Metro City hitting everyone in the way. Health comes from food found by breaking the scenery, and the special move that clears a crowd costs you health to use.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Food restores health rather than scoring: fruit a quarter, pizza or a hamburger a half, and a barbecue all of it.

Strategy

  • Break everything. The scenery holds both the treasure and the food, and a diamond is worth 10,000 — more than a stage of ordinary fighting.
  • The special costs health. It clears a crowd around you but takes a slice of your own bar, so it answers being surrounded, not being outnumbered.
  • Pick your character for reach. Haggar is slow and strong, Guy is fast and short-ranged, Cody sits between them. The difference is spacing, not damage.
  • Do not eat at full health. Food does not carry over; walking past a barbecue you cannot use is the right call.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
