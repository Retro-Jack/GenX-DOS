Radar Scope

Nintendo, 1980 — the game whose unsold cabinets became Donkey Kong.

System help — the arcade controls

A fixed shooter with a perspective starfield and a tractor-beam enemy. It sold badly in America, and the leftover cabinets were converted — which is why Donkey Kong exists at all.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The tractor beam is the threat. The enemy that pulls rather than shoots is the one that ends runs; break away early rather than firing through it.
  • The perspective is not decoration. The starfield conveys depth, and enemies arrive along it — distance is a real dimension here.
  • One shot at a time. Fire discipline matters more than rate; a wasted shot leaves you unarmed until it clears.
