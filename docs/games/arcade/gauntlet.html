Arcade · 1985 Atari

Gauntlet

Atari, 1985 — Ed Logg's four-player dungeon crawl, and the machine that made "Wizard needs food, badly" a catchphrase.

System help — the arcade controls

Warrior, Valkyrie, Wizard or Elf, descending through a dungeon of generators, ghosts and demons. Health drains continuously and food restores it; when health hits zero you are dead. Shoot the generators, not the monsters — the generators are what keep making them.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Shoot the generators, not the monsters. Generators produce the monsters indefinitely. Clearing the room means clearing the sources.
  • Health is a clock. It drains continuously whether you are fighting or not, so hesitation costs life directly and food is the only refill.
  • Do not shoot the food. A stray shot destroys it, which is why the warning exists.
  • Fight hand to hand where you can. The manual is explicit that melee scores more than shooting — moving into a monster beats standing off it, health permitting.
  • Transporters go to the nearest one you can see. If two are equally close it picks at random, so position before you step in.
  • Some walls crumble. The older-looking ones give way to repeated shots, which opens routes the map does not advertise.
  • The four characters are genuinely different. The Valkyrie survives, the Wizard hits hardest with magic, the Elf is quickest and the Warrior hits hardest in melee.
