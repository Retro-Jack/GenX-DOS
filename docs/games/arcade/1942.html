Capcom, 1984 — Yoshiki Okamoto's Pacific-theatre shooter, and the game that established Capcom in the arcade.

System help — the arcade controls

You fly a P-38 north through twenty-two stages towards Tokyo. The loop is a vertical scroller, but the loop-the-loop is the point: you have a limited number of rolls that take you out of the plane of fire entirely.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The loop is the defence. You have a limited number of rolls and they take you out of the plane of fire completely — they answer situations that dodging cannot.
  • Formations pay together. Clearing a whole squadron rather than picking at it is what releases the power-ups.
  • Green Zeros swarm. Individually trivial at 30 points, dangerous in numbers because they loop unpredictably.
  • Twenty-two stages is a marathon. Rolls are the resource that has to last, so spending them early is what ends runs.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
