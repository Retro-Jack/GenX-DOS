Arcade · 1983 Atari

Star Wars

Atari, 1983 — the Death Star trench run, in colour vector graphics, with digitised speech from the film.

System help — the arcade controls

Three phases on a loop: destroy the TIE fighters, strafe the Death Star's surface towers, then fly the trench and put a shot down the exhaust port. Do it and the station explodes and the loop restarts, harder.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The cabinet used a flight yoke; here the movement keys aim the crosshair, and any of the four fire buttons shoots.

Controls

This cabinet used 4 buttons. Buttons not listed here do nothing on this machine.

Strategy

  • Shoot the towers, not the surface. The Death Star run only requires the towers; everything else is scenery you are wasting shots on.
  • The trench is about height, not aim. Once inside, survival is staying off the walls. The exhaust port shot is the easy part by comparison.
  • Fire continuously. There is no ammunition limit and no penalty for missing. Holding fire is strictly better than aiming carefully.
  • It loops harder. Destroying the station restarts the three phases at a higher difficulty rather than ending the game.
