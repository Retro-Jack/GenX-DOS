R-Type

Irem, 1987 — R-Type, and the Force pod that made it famous.

System help — the arcade controls

A horizontal shooter through the Bydo empire. The Force is an indestructible drone you attach to the front or back of your ship, or detach to fight on its own — cover, weapon and puzzle piece in one.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

The mechanics below are from Irem’s own sales flyer for the cabinet.

  • The Force is indestructible. Irem’s own description: a diamond-armoured cannon with three attacking modes and three power levels. Nothing destroys it, which is what makes it cover as well as a weapon.
  • Attach it front or back. Button B joins and disjoins it, and it can be locked to either end of the craft. Rear-mounted it answers what is chasing you; front-mounted it clears what is ahead.
  • Charge the beam and let go. Holding the fire button fills the beam meter along the bottom of the screen; releasing fires a wave proportional to what you accumulated.
  • Weapons come out of the POW armours. Shoot those and they release the air-to-ground laser, the reflective laser, the air-to-air laser, the heat-seeking missile, the speed-up and the Bit drones that guard you.
  • Shoot the POW armours. At 200 points they are unremarkable, but they are what drops the laser units — the manual says outright that is where your weapons come from.
  • Match the unit to the stage. The manual’s advice: use the laser best suited to the stage and enemy in front of you rather than keeping a favourite.
  • Eight stages, each with its own boss. The stages are fixed and tight; survival comes from knowing what is next rather than reacting to it.
