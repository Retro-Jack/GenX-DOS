Black Tiger

Capcom, 1987 — Black Tiger, a side-scrolling fantasy hack-and-slash with a shop.

System help — the arcade controls

A barbarian descends through caverns to fight dragons, buying armour, weapons and potions from old men chained up along the way. Money is dropped by everything you kill, and choosing what to spend it on is most of the game.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Pick the money up immediately. Zenny drops disappear after a second or two, so a kill you walk away from is a kill you were not paid for.
  • Buy armour before weapons. The old men sell both; survival extends the run further than damage does.
  • Two currencies at once. Monsters give points and money on different scales, so the most valuable thing to kill is not always the most valuable thing to score.
  • Keys open more than doors. Some chained old men are behind locks, and they hold the better stock.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
