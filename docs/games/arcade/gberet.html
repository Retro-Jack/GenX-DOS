Green Beret

Konami, 1985 — Green Beret, a side-scrolling run through a prison camp.

System help — the arcade controls

You cross four stages with a knife, picking up flamethrowers, mortars and RPGs that last only a few shots. The knife never runs out, which is why you will finish most fights with it.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The knife never runs out. Everything you pick up is temporary and rationed; the knife is what you will finish most of the game with.
  • Crouch to change your line. Standing and crouching are two different attack heights, and most enemies commit to one of them.
  • Save the rockets for the groups. A flamethrower or an RPG has a handful of shots — spending one on a lone soldier wastes it.
