Elevator Action

Taito, 1983 — a spy in a building of thirty floors and a great many lifts.

System help — the arcade controls

Descend a building collecting documents from red doors, using lifts and escalators, with agents in pursuit. Doors can be slammed on them, and the getaway car is at the bottom.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Scoring

ActionPoints
Shooting an agent100
Jumping on an agent150
Dropping a light on an agent300
Collecting a secret document500

A level bonus of 1,000 times the level number, rising to a flat 10,000 per level after the tenth. Three to six men to start, with a spare at 10,000, 15,000, 20,000 or 25,000 depending on the cabinet.

Strategy

  • Lights are worth three shots. Shooting the ceiling light above an agent scores 300 against 100 for shooting the agent, and takes out anything under it.
  • The red doors are the objective. Documents are 500 each and the lift will not release you at the bottom without them.
  • Lifts are weapons. An agent caught by a descending lift is dealt with for nothing, and the shaft is the safest way down.
  • The bonus grows with the level. Depth pays: after the tenth floor every completed level is worth a flat 10,000.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
