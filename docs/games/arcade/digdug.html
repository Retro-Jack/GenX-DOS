Dig Dug

Namco, 1982 — dig tunnels, inflate monsters until they burst, drop rocks on them.

System help — the arcade controls

You tunnel through soil hunting Pookas and Fygars. The pump inflates them in stages and they can escape if you stop; dropping a rock is faster and scores more, but you only get two per level.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Scoring

Pooka, layer 1 to 4200 / 300 / 400 / 500

Deeper is worth more, and a Fygar is worth twice a Pooka at the same depth.

Strategy

  • Depth is money. The same monster is worth two and a half times as much on the bottom layer as the top. Digging down first is a scoring decision.
  • Rocks beat the pump. Dropping a rock is faster and scores more than inflating, but there are only two per level, so choose the moment.
  • An inflated monster deflates. Stop pumping and it recovers. Commit to the kill or do not start it.
  • Fygars breathe along the tunnel. Approach one from above or below rather than along its line, where the flame reaches furthest.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
