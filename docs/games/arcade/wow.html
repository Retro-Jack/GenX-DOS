Wizard of Wor

Midway, 1980 — Wizard of Wor, a maze shooter for two players at once.

System help — the arcade controls

Two Worriors clear a dungeon of Burwors, Garwors and Thorwors, with the Wizard himself appearing to teleport around and shoot at you. Players can shoot each other, which is the point.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The Worluk is worth more than it looks. Killing it doubles everything in the following dungeon, so the dungeon after a Worluk is where a run is really scored.
  • The radar shows what the dark does not. Later dungeons hide the invisible monsters; the map at the bottom is the only way to know where they are.
  • Doorways are chokepoints. A corridor mouth lets you fire down one line instead of watching four.
  • You can shoot the other player. It is worth 1,000, and in two-player it is entirely permitted — which is the joke the cabinet was built around.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
