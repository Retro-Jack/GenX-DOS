Arcade · 1982 Williams

Splat!

Williams, 1982 — two sticks, and a great deal of mess.

System help — the arcade controls

A two-stick game in the Robotron mould: move with one hand, act with the other, across screens that fill with obstacles. Rare in its day and rarely seen since.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

No buttons at all — the control panel was two sticks and nothing else.

Strategy

  • Two sticks, as with Robotron. One hand moves and the other acts, independently. The habit of aiming where you walk has to be unlearned.
  • The screen fills up. Obstacles accumulate rather than clearing, so early tidiness pays for late survival.
  • Keep to the open ground. With no way to clear what has built up, the only defence is not being enclosed by it.
  • Neither the emulator nor any guide names its buttons. The cabinet had none — the two sticks were the whole panel.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
