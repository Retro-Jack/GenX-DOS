Yie Ar Kung-Fu

Konami, 1985 — Yie Ar Kung-Fu, an early one-on-one fighter with a roster.

System help — the arcade controls

Oolong fights his way through eleven opponents, each with a different weapon and reach — a chain, a staff, a fan, a flying stance. Sixteen moves, and matching the right one to the opponent is the whole game.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

The harder the move is to land, the more it pays — a jumping high attack is worth four ordinary ones.

Strategy

  • The jumping attacks are worth four times the rest. At 2,000 each they are most of a high score, and they are the hardest to land, which is the trade.
  • Every opponent has a reach. The chain, the staff and the fan all outrange your fists. Match your move to their weapon rather than repeating a favourite.
  • Crouching beats high attacks. A crouching mid-level punch is worth 900 and passes under most of what is thrown at you.
  • Eleven opponents, eleven answers. There is no single move that works throughout — the roster is the puzzle.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
