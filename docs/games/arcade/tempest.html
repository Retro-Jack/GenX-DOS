Arcade · 1980 Atari

Tempest

Atari, 1980 — Dave Theurer again, this time a tube shooter in colour vector graphics.

System help — the arcade controls

You are a claw at the rim of a geometric tube. Enemies climb the lanes towards you; you slide around the rim and shoot down them. Reach the top and the Superzapper clears the tube — once per level, and a second use only kills one enemy.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. Hold left or right to slide around the rim — the cabinet used a spinner, so the claw moves while the key is held.

Starting on a higher level pays a bonus that grows the further in you begin — 6,000 at level 3, and rising steeply from there.

Strategy

  • The Superzapper is once per level. The first use clears the tube; a second use in the same level kills exactly one enemy. Treat it as a single card.
  • Shoot down the lanes, not at the rim. Anything you kill low in the tube cannot flip up at you later.
  • Spikes are the tax on a slow level. Spikers build them the whole time they live, and you have to fly through whatever is left when the level ends.
  • Flippers are the ones that reach you. Everything else has to become something else first.
  • Keep moving round the rim. A stationary claw is a target in a lane; a moving one is a target in none.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
