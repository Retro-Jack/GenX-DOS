Inferno

Williams, 1984 — two sticks again, this time moving and aiming through a burning maze.

System help — the arcade controls

Move with one stick and fire with the other through rooms of a hostile complex, rescuing what can be rescued. Never widely released, which is why almost nobody has played it.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Move with one stick, fire with the other. The same grammar as Robotron, in rooms rather than an open field.
  • Rooms are chokepoints. Doorways decide the fight; holding one is easier than crossing open floor.
  • Rescue is the objective. Clearing a room is not the goal in itself — what you get out matters more than what you kill.
