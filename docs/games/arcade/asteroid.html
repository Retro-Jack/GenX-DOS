Asteroids

Atari, 1979 — Lyle Rains and Ed Logg's vector shooter, and the machine that out-earned Space Invaders in the United States.

System help — the arcade controls

You are a ship adrift in an asteroid field. Shoot a large rock and it breaks into two mediums; shoot those and they break again. Clear the screen and a denser field replaces it. Flying saucers arrive between waves — the large one fires wildly, the small one aims at you.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Four shots at a time. That is the limit on screen at once, which at close range is enough to take a large rock all the way down to nothing in a single pass.
  • Everything wraps. Your shots leave one edge and return on the other — and so do the saucers', so an empty screen edge is not cover.
  • Ten thousand points changes the game. Below it the large saucer fires at random and you can hold the middle of the screen. Above it the small saucer is always around, and it aims; standing still stops working.
  • Thrust is momentum, not steering. There is no brake. Tap it, or you will spend the next ten seconds crossing your own screen backwards.
  • Hyperspace is a last resort. It puts you somewhere random, which is as often worse as better. Keep it for the moment when there is genuinely no gap left.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
