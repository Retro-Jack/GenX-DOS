Arcade · 1983 Konami

Gyruss

Konami, 1983 — a tube shooter that spirals inward from Neptune to Earth.

System help — the arcade controls

You circle the rim of the screen firing towards the centre while enemies spiral outward at you. The route home is the solar system, two warps a planet.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • You orbit; they spiral out. Movement is around the rim only. Position is an angle, not a place, and the shortest way to a target is sometimes the long way round.
  • The bonus stages are worth 20,000. That is more than most of a normal stage, and they are free of risk beyond wasted time.
  • Double fire is worth protecting. The two-shot upgrade changes the clear rate substantially, and you lose it when you die.
  • Two warps a planet. The route from Neptune to Earth is fixed, so you always know how much game is left.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
