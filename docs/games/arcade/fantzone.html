Fantasy Zone

Sega, 1986 — a bright, cheerful shooter with a shop and a boss on every stage.

System help — the arcade controls

Opa-Opa flies left or right through pastel levels, destroying the bases that hold each one together and then fighting the boss. Money from kills buys speed and weapons between stages.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Destroy the bases, not the enemies. Each stage is held together by a set number of bases; clearing them is what summons the boss.
  • Money is the upgrade path. Kills drop coins and the shop between stages sells the engines and weapons. Saving for a big weapon is a real choice.
  • Speed is the best purchase early. Opa-Opa is slow by default, and most deaths are being unable to leave a space in time.
