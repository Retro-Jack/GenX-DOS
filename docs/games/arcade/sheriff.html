Arcade · 1979 Nintendo

Sheriff

Nintendo, 1979 — an early maze shooter, and one of Shigeru Miyamoto's first credits.

System help — the arcade controls

A sheriff defends a town from a ring of bandits closing in from all sides. One stick moves and the other aims, which in 1979 was an unusual thing to ask of a player.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Two sticks: move and aim separately. One hand walks, the other points. Backing away while firing at the ring is the whole defence.
  • The bandits close in a circle. There is no safe edge — the threat is from every side at once, which is why the second stick exists.
  • Keep the centre. Cornering yourself removes half the directions you can retreat into.
