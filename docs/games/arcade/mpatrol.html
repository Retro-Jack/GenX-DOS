Arcade · 1982 Irem

Moon Patrol

Irem, 1982 — Moon Patrol, credited with introducing parallax scrolling to the arcade.

System help — the arcade controls

A moon buggy drives right across a pitted landscape, jumping craters and shooting rocks and the ships overhead. The background rolls at three different speeds, which in 1982 was the thing people came to look at.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Jump and shoot are one manoeuvre. Craters need the jump and the rocks need the gun, and they arrive together.
  • The checkpoints are letters. The course is divided alphabetically, which is how you judge progress and how far a death sets you back.
  • Speed is a choice. You can slow down. Most of the difficulty comes from players who do not.
  • Beat the checkpoint time and keep the difference. Every second under the target is another hundred points, so the run is scored on pace rather than on what you destroy.
  • Dodge the UFO bombs by changing speed. Irem’s own advice: accelerate or brake rather than trying to shoot your way out.
  • Cross the minefields on the throttle. Speed control, not the gun, is what gets you through them.

Scoring

The course is lettered, and five of those letters are timed checkpoints. Reach one inside its target and you are paid a flat bonus plus a hundred points for every second you saved.
