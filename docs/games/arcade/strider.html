Strider

Capcom, 1989 — Strider Hiryu, all cartwheels, plasma sword and impossible slopes.

System help — the arcade controls

A ninja runs, climbs and slides through a future Russia. Hiryu clings to any surface and attacks while moving, so the game is built around never stopping — standing still is what gets you hit.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

This cabinet used 2 buttons. Buttons not listed here do nothing on this machine.

Strategy

  • Never stand still. Hiryu attacks while moving and clings to almost any surface. Stopping is what gets you hit.
  • The sword has reach and an arc. It covers above and in front on the swing, so height differences are less of a problem than they look.
  • Slopes and ceilings are floor. If it is a surface, you can be on it; routes that look impossible usually are not.
