Arcade · 1981 Midway

Omega Race

Midway, 1981 — a vector shooter with a spinner, and Midway's only vector game.

System help — the arcade controls

You rotate and thrust around wrapping arenas shooting drones and mines. Momentum carries, the screen wraps, and the spinner turns you faster than any joystick would.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The real cabinet used a spinner, so hold a direction rather than tapping it — a tap is often too brief to register.

Strategy

  • Momentum never stops. There is no brake. Thrust is a commitment, and the wall is the only thing that will change your mind.
  • The screen wraps and so do the mines. Clearing an area does not mean it stays clear; anything you left drifting comes back round.
  • The spinner turns faster than a stick. Rotation is quick and continuous here, so aiming is cheap and moving is expensive — turn and fire rather than reposition.
  • Mines are the reliable income. Ships are worth more but move; mines sit still and add up.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
