Joust

Williams, 1982 — knights on flying ostriches, and the best two-player argument of 1982.

System help — the arcade controls

Flap to gain height and joust: whoever is higher at the moment of contact wins. The eggs left behind must be collected before they hatch, and the pterodactyl arrives if you take too long.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

First egg on a wave250

Egg collection climbs by 250 for each egg gathered without dying on that wave, so an uninterrupted sweep is worth several times a scattered one.

Strategy

  • Height decides the joust. Whoever is higher at the moment of contact wins. Everything else is manoeuvring to be the one above.
  • Collect the eggs in one run. The value climbs 250 at a time and resets when you die, so a clean sweep is worth far more than the same eggs taken piecemeal.
  • Eggs hatch. Left alone they become new riders — and a hatched rider is worth less than the egg was.
  • The pterodactyl is a timer. It arrives when a wave runs long. It is worth 1,000 but it is telling you to hurry.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
