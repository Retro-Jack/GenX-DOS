Rastan

Taito, 1987 — a barbarian, a sword with real reach, and a lot of stairs.

System help — the arcade controls

Rastan crosses six areas of a fantasy kingdom. The sword's arc is long and slow, so the game is about spacing rather than speed, and the pick-up weapons change that spacing entirely.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The enemies are endless. More will always come, so clearing an area before moving on is impossible — keep going rather than fighting.
  • Spacing beats speed. The sword’s arc is long and slow. Standing at the edge of its reach is safer than closing.
  • Attack downward from a jump. A jumping thrust covers ground the standing swing cannot and is the answer to anything that follows you.
  • Pick-up weapons change your reach. The axe, mace and fire sword each alter the spacing the game is built on.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
