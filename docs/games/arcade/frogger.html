Frogger

Konami, 1981 — five lanes of traffic, a river, and no way back.

System help — the arcade controls

Get five frogs home. The road wants to flatten you and the river wants to drown you, because a frog that touches water dies even though the logs floating on it are safe.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

No buttons at all — the control panel was the stick and nothing else.

Strategy

  • Never go backwards. Only forward hops score, so a retreat costs you the ten points and the seconds both.
  • The river kills, the logs do not. A frog that touches water dies even though everything floating on it is safe — the hazard is the gap, not the current.
  • Fill the outer homes first. The middle bays stay reachable longest; the corners get harder as the traffic thickens.
  • The fly and the lady frog are worth four hops each. Both are worth 200 and both are optional, so take them only when the lane is already safe.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
