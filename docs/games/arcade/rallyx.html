Rally-X

Namco, 1980 — a scrolling maze race with a smoke screen and a fuel gauge.

System help — the arcade controls

Drive around a maze larger than the screen collecting ten flags while rival cars chase you. Smoke stalls a pursuer but burns fuel, and running dry ends the round.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

One extra car, at 20,000 points. Fuel is refilled when you lose a car — and a board can still be cleared after the fuel runs out.

Strategy

  • Flags are worth more the later you take them. The tenth flag is worth ten times the first, so the route matters more than the speed.
  • Find the S flag early. It doubles everything remaining in the level, which means taking it before the flags rather than after.
  • Smoke costs fuel. It is the only defence, but fuel is also points at the end of the round, so every screen you lay is score you are spending.
  • The maze is bigger than the window. The radar is the only view of the whole board; drive by it rather than by what is on screen.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
