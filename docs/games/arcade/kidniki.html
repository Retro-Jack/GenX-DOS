Arcade · 1986 Irem

Kid Niki

Irem, 1986 — Kid Niki: Radical Ninja, a platformer with a spinning sword.

System help — the arcade controls

A ninja crosses seven stages to rescue Princess Margo. The sword spins in front of you rather than stabbing, which makes range and timing a different problem from most platformers of the period.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The sword spins. It covers a circle rather than a line, so the timing is about when to start the spin, not where to point it.
  • Momentum carries into the jump. Kid Niki does not stop dead; approach speed decides where you land.
  • Seven stages, seven bosses. Each has a pattern that is learned rather than out-fought.
