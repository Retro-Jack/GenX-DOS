Rolling Thunder

Namco, 1986 — Rolling Thunder, and the idea that you should take cover.

System help — the arcade controls

An agent moves through the Geldra hideout with a pistol and finite ammunition, ducking behind crates and vaulting between two levels of the screen. Standing in the open and firing is how you die.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Cover is the mechanic. Crates and doorways stop bullets. Standing in the open to trade shots is how the game kills you.
  • Ammunition is finite. The pistol runs dry and the resupply doors are the only refill, so shots have to be aimed rather than sprayed.
  • Use both levels. Vaulting between the upper and lower walkway breaks a firing line more reliably than running does.
