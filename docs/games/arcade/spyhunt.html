Arcade · 1983 Midway

Spy Hunter

Midway, 1983 — a spy car with oil slicks, smoke screens and Peter Gunn.

System help — the arcade controls

Drive south down a scrolling road, running enemy cars off it with guns, oil and smoke. The weapons van you drive into for a refit is the only way to get missiles, and the score only starts counting after the first weapon.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The real cabinet used a paddle, so hold a direction rather than tapping it — a tap is often too brief to register.

Destroying a civilian, the weapons van or a tugboat pauses your scoring for one, two or three seconds respectively — roughly 150, 300 and 450 points withheld.

Strategy

  • Distance is the base score. You are paid per screen travelled, and more on water than road, so survival is scoring even when nothing is destroyed.
  • Do not shoot the weapons van. It is how you rearm, and destroying it costs you both the refit and two seconds of scoring.
  • Civilians cost you. Blue cars are not targets; running one off the road pauses your score.
  • The Mad Bomber is the prize. At 700 it is worth more than four Switchblades.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
