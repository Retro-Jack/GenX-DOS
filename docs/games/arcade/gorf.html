Gorf

Midway, 1981 — five different games in one cabinet, with speech.

System help — the arcade controls

Gorf runs five distinct missions in sequence — an Astro Battle, Laser Attack, Space Warp, Flag Ship and the rest — and taunts you between them in a synthesised voice.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • It is five games, in order. Astro Battle, Laser Attack, Space Warp, Flag Ship and the rest each have their own rules. Treat the transitions as new games rather than new waves.
  • Catch the invaders as they launch. An invader is worth double if you hit it while the robot is still putting it out.
  • The shield is finite in a different way each mission. What protects you in one section does not exist in the next, so do not carry a habit forward.
  • The taunts are timed, not random. Gorf speaks at fixed moments; when it starts talking, something is about to change.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
