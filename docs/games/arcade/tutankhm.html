Tutankham

Konami, 1982 — a maze game in an Egyptian tomb, with a screen-clearing flash.

System help — the arcade controls

Explore a scrolling tomb for treasure and the exit, shooting left and right only. The flash kills everything visible but you get very few of them, and that rationing is the game.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Twelve thousand points if you take all six treasures on a level. Level one has only three; every level after it has six. Extra life at 30,000.

Strategy

  • Treasures escalate steeply. The sixth is worth eight times the first, so a level abandoned early throws away most of its value.
  • The flash is rationed. It kills everything on screen but you are given very few. Losing one to a single chaser is the common mistake.
  • You can only fire left and right. Anything above or below you has to be walked away from, not shot.
  • Keys are worth 1,500 in total. Five hundred to pick up and a thousand to use, so no key should be left behind.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
