HeliFire

Nintendo, 1980 — a submarine shooting upward at helicopters and downward at mines.

System help — the arcade controls

You control a sub on the surface, firing up at aircraft and down at the depth charges and mines below. Fighting in two directions at once is the whole idea.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

One button. The driver gives the stick all four directions, so the submarine moves vertically as well as along the surface.

Strategy

  • Two directions at once. Aircraft above and mines below, and both will kill you. Splitting attention is the game rather than an inconvenience.
  • The depth charges sink at a fixed rate. They can be shot on the way down, and that is easier than moving out from under them.
  • Surface position is everything. The sub moves along one line; every threat is judged by where it will cross that line.
