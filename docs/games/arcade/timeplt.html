Time Pilot

Konami, 1982 — Time Pilot, a free-scrolling dogfight through five eras.

System help — the arcade controls

You fly in any of eight directions across a sky that never ends, shooting down aircraft from 1910, 1940, 1970, 1982 and 2001. Each era ends with a mothership.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Scoring

A damaged boss that leaves the screen keeps its damage — it only repairs if you lose a life.

Strategy

  • The sky has no edges. It scrolls freely in eight directions and wraps. There is no corner to retreat into, so keep circling rather than backing away.
  • The formation bonus is on a timer. Clearing a whole wave is worth 2,000, but only if you do it before the game stops offering.
  • Bosses remember. Twenty-three hits, and damage carries over if it escapes — unless you die, which resets it.
  • Missiles score the same as aircraft. At 100 apiece there is no reason to dodge something you could shoot.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
