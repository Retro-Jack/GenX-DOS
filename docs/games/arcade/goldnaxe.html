Golden Axe

Sega, 1989 — Golden Axe, three warriors, and thieves you kick for magic.

System help — the arcade controls

Barbarian, Amazon or dwarf fight through Yuria to Death Adder. Magic comes from potions stolen off the little thieves who wander the levels, and holding out for a bigger spell is the central gamble.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

Strategy

  • Magic is stolen, not found. The little thieves who run through the levels carry the potions. Kicking them while they sleep is the only supply.
  • Hold out for the bigger spell. More potions means a stronger spell, so spending at two bottles wastes what four would have done.
  • The mounts are worth taking. A beast changes your reach entirely, and you keep it until you are knocked off.
  • Three characters, three trades. The dwarf hits hardest, the Amazon is quickest, the barbarian sits between them — and their magic differs more than their swords do.
  • Scoring is a rating, not a table. The end screen grades you on total score against lives lost rather than listing point values, so this page has no scoring section.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
