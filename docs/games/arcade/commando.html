Commando

Capcom, 1985 — a one-man run-and-gun up a vertically scrolling battlefield.

System help — the arcade controls

Super Joe walks north through enemy territory with a rifle and a finite bag of grenades. The rifle fires where you face; the grenades clear what the rifle cannot reach, including men behind cover.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Scoring

The available guide covers the enemies in prose rather than a table, so only the prisoner bonus is given outright.

Strategy

  • Free every prisoner. A thousand points each, and they hand over weapon upgrades that matter more than the score does.
  • Grenades reach what the rifle cannot. Men behind sandbags and in trenches are immune to gunfire; that is what the second button is for.
  • The rifle fires where you face, not where you walk. Which means retreating while firing needs the turn to happen first.
  • Shelters hide prisoners. The underground entrances are worth the detour they cost.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
