Ms. Pac-Man

Midway, 1981 — Ms. Pac-Man, which began as an unauthorised conversion and became the better game.

System help — the arcade controls

Four mazes instead of one, fruit that wanders rather than sitting still, and ghosts whose movement is partly random — so the pattern memorisation that beat Pac-Man does not work here.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Controls

No buttons at all — the control panel was the stick and nothing else.

Strategy

  • Patterns do not work here. The ghosts have a random element that Pac-Man’s did not, which is exactly why this game outlived it. Play the screen, not a memorised route.
  • The fruit moves. It enters through a tunnel and wanders the maze rather than sitting under the box, so it has to be chased and is worth up to 10,000 by the banana board.
  • Four ghosts per pill is 3,000. As in the original, chaining is where the score is.
  • Learn the four mazes. There are only four, and they cycle. The tunnels are in different places in each.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
