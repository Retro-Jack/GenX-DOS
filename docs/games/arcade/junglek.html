Jungle King

Taito, 1982 — Jungle King, later Jungle Hunt after a lawsuit about a certain ape man.

System help — the arcade controls

Four distinct stages: swinging on vines, swimming past crocodiles, dodging boulders, and a final rescue. Each is a different game, which was unusual for 1982.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Scoring

The timer starts at 5,000 and whatever is left when you reach the rescue is added. It runs down faster in later stages.

Strategy

  • Duck rather than jump. A ducked boulder is worth 200 and a jumped one 100 — double for the harder-looking option.
  • The timer is the score. Whatever is left is added at the rescue, so hesitation costs more than the individual points are worth.
  • Four stages, four games. Vines, water, boulders and the rescue share nothing but the timer. Do not carry habits between them.
  • Crocodiles pay double closed. Striking one with its jaws open is worth less than catching it shut.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
