Space Harrier

Sega, 1985 — Space Harrier, a into-the-screen shooter at a ferocious pace.

System help — the arcade controls

A man with a jet cannon runs and flies through a checkerboard landscape, dodging pillars and shooting dragons. There is no cover and almost no pause; it is about reading the lane ahead.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The real cabinet used an analogue stick, so hold a direction rather than tapping it — a tap is often too brief to register.

Controls

Strategy

  • There is no cover. Everything is dodged. Pillars, dragons and shots all occupy the same lane you do.
  • Read the lane, not the object. At this speed you are reacting to where a gap will be, not where a hazard is.
  • Keep moving vertically. You can fly as well as run; staying at ground level halves the space you have.

Some land creatures cannot be destroyed at all — avoiding them is the only defence. These figures come from Sega’s manual for the home conversion rather than the cabinet, so the coin-op may differ; no arcade document that states them has been found.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
