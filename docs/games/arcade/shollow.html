Satan's Hollow

Midway, 1981 — Satan's Hollow, a fixed shooter that builds a bridge.

System help — the arcade controls

Shoot down bat-like creatures and collect the girders they drop to assemble a bridge across the lake. Only when the bridge is finished do you get to cross and fight Satan himself.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The bridge is the objective, not the birds. Girders dropped by the creatures are what you are collecting; shooting without gathering makes no progress at all.
  • Shields absorb both missiles and bricks. Everything the birds drop can be stopped rather than dodged, which is usually the better option.
  • Red birds damage the bridge. Their glowing bricks destroy sections you have already built, so they are the priority target.
  • Only a finished bridge lets you cross. There is no partial credit — Satan waits until the span is complete.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
