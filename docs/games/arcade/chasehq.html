Arcade · 1988 Taito

Chase H.Q.

Taito, 1988 — a police pursuit where catching the car means ramming it.

System help — the arcade controls

Drive a Porsche after a criminal against the clock, then, having caught up, ram the target repeatedly until it stops. The turbo is limited and choosing when to burn it is the game.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

The rules below are from Taito’s own operator’s manual.

  • Catching the suspect buys you a minute. Pull alongside and the dome light goes on, the siren sounds, and the timer is extended by 60 seconds — that extension is the whole second half of the round.
  • Then you have to ram it. Crash into the target repeatedly until the damage meter down the left of the screen shows it immobilised. Catching up is not finishing.
  • Three turbo bursts a round. They are on the shift-knob button, and there are exactly three — spending one in traffic is spending one of three.
  • Five rounds, one clock. The game ends when the timer reaches zero or when you have cleared all five.
