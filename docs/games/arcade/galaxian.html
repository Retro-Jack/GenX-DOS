Galaxian

Namco, 1979 — the answer to Space Invaders, in proper colour, with enemies that break formation.

System help — the arcade controls

A fixed shooter where the aliens no longer march in lockstep: they peel off the formation in ones and twos and dive at you, firing on the way down.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Wait for the dive. Everything is worth double once it breaks formation, and the formation is not going anywhere.
  • They come in threes. A flagship leaves the convoy with two escorts. Where the group is going matters more than where it started.
  • One shot at a time. Only one of your bullets may be on screen. A miss is not a small mistake, it is the whole weapon until it clears.
  • Stay under the formation, not the divers. The convoy drifts predictably; the divers do not.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
