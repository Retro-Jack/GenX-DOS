Arcade · 1988 Taito

The New Zealand Story

Taito, 1988 — The New Zealand Story, a kiwi rescuing its friends from a walrus.

System help — the arcade controls

Tiki crosses twenty-odd levels of vertical, maze-like stages with a bow, and can steal the balloons and flying craft the enemies use. Cheerful, and much harder than it looks.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Steal their transport. Balloons and flying craft can be taken from the enemies using them, and they change how a vertical level is solved entirely.
  • The levels are mazes, not corridors. They run upward as much as sideways, and the route is rarely the obvious one.
  • The bow fires where you face. Aiming means turning, which means committing — plan the shot before you need it.
  • It is harder than it looks. The presentation is cheerful; the difficulty is not.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
