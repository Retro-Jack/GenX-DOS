Arcade · 1985 Capcom

Gun.Smoke

Capcom, 1985 — a vertically scrolling Western, aimed in three directions at once.

System help — the arcade controls

A bounty hunter walks north through outlaw country. The three buttons fire left, centre and right, and pressing two together fires diagonally — the whole game is choosing an angle before the target reaches it.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • Three buttons, eight directions. Left, centre and right fire individually; pressing two together fires diagonally. Aiming is a chord, not a stick.
  • Choose the angle before the range. You are committing to a lane well before the target arrives in it.
  • Boots, horses and guns are all pick-ups. Speed and firepower are separate upgrades and both are worth the detour.
