Missile Command

Atari, 1980 — Dave Theurer's Cold War nightmare, defending six cities from ballistic missiles.

System help — the arcade controls

Enemy missiles rain on six cities and three missile bases. You fire counter-missiles that explode where you aim; anything caught in the blast dies. Explosions chain, so a well-placed shot takes a whole salvo. The cities you save carry over — when they are all gone, so are you.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. Hold a direction to move the crosshair; it was a trackball on the real machine, so it travels while held.

Strategy

  • Aim where the missile will be. Your counter-missile takes time to arrive and then time to bloom. Fire at empty sky ahead of the trail, not at the warhead.
  • One blast can take a whole salvo. Explosions set off explosions, so a shot placed where trails converge is worth five placed on individual warheads.
  • Spend the outer bases first. Alpha and Omega reload at the end of the wave like Delta does, but Delta is the one in the middle when you are desperate.
  • The multiplier is the game. A wave-11 ICBM is worth six times a wave-1 one, so surviving deep is worth far more than clearing early waves perfectly.
  • Cities are currency. You keep the ones you save, and the wave ends when the missiles stop — not when you run out of anything.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
