Space Firebird

Nintendo, 1980 — Space Firebird, a fixed shooter with an escape button.

System help — the arcade controls

Birds dive from a formation above while you slide along the bottom. The extra button warps you out of trouble, disappearing your ship briefly rather than moving it.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The escape button is not a move. It removes you briefly rather than relocating you, so it answers being surrounded, not being aimed at.
  • The birds dive in arcs. They do not fall straight; leading them matters more than tracking them.
  • Formation first, divers second. Anything still in formation is predictable and worth clearing while it stays that way.

Each battle is 50 Firebirds, and clearing them all moves you to a harder one. A spare ship at 5,000 points, or 8,000 depending on how the cabinet was set. From Nintendo’s own operator’s manual.
