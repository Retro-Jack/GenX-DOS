Contra

Konami, 1987 — two commandos, an alien invasion, and a lot of bullets.

System help — the arcade controls

Run right, shoot everything, and pick up the letters that change your gun — the spread is the one everyone wants. One hit kills, which is why the game is remembered as hard.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The spread gun is the one to hold. It covers angles nothing else does, and you lose your weapon when you die — which makes dying expensive beyond the life.
  • One touch kills. Everything does. Position matters more than aim throughout.
  • Shoot upward on the base stages. The into-the-screen sections hide the weak point in the middle; the walls are only cover.
  • Two players is easier and harder. A second gun clears faster, but you share the lives.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
