Arcade · 1983 Irem

10 Yard Fight

Irem, 1983 — 10-Yard Fight, an early and very simplified American football game.

System help — the arcade controls

You attack up a vertically scrolling field, handing off or passing and then running for the line. Five difficulty tiers, from high school to the Super Bowl, each one only unlocked by winning the last.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Strategy

  • The hand-off is safer than the pass. A pass can be intercepted; a run cannot, and the game is short enough to grind out.
  • Five tiers, each unlocked by the last. High school through to the Super Bowl, and the defence genuinely changes between them.
  • The clock is the real defence. Running the ball is safe but slow, and the half will end whether you have scored or not.
