Arcade · 1982 Namco

Pole Position

Namco, 1982 — the first racing game with a qualifying lap, and a real circuit.

System help — the arcade controls

Qualify on Fuji Speedway, then race it. The sprite scaling and the roadside detail were the state of the art in 1982, and the qualifying lap decides your grid position rather than just gating you.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.
  3. The real cabinet used a spinner, so hold a direction rather than tapping it — a tap is often too brief to register.

Qualifying is a lap against a target time — 58.50 for pole, 60.00 for second, 62.00 for third.

Strategy

  • Qualify properly. Grid position is worth up to 4,000 points before the race even starts, and a better grid means fewer cars to pass.
  • Staying on the track is scoring. Ten points a moment sounds trivial; over a full race the tarmac is a meaningful part of the total.
  • The clock is the real opponent. Time left at the end is 200 a second. There are no positions to win, only seconds to save.
  • Touching a rival ends the run. Collisions are not a nudge here — they destroy the car outright.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
