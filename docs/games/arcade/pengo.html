Pengo

Sega, 1982 — a penguin, a field of ice blocks, and the Sno-Bees.

System help — the arcade controls

Push ice blocks to crush the Sno-Bees, or stun them by shaking the wall they are touching. Lining up the three diamond blocks scores a large bonus and is the expert's route.

Getting started

  1. Insert a coin — the coin slot at the top left, or 5.
  2. Press 1 player start — the 1P button, or 1.

Scoring

ActionPoints
Aligning the three diamond blocks away from the wall10,000
Aligning them against the wall5,000

The diamond bonus dwarfs the time bonus, so it is generally worth chasing even at the cost of a slower level.

Strategy

  • The diamond alignment is the game. Ten thousand points is worth more than a whole level cleared conventionally. Locate the three diamond blocks first and plan around them.
  • Align away from the wall. Against the wall pays half, and getting them there usually destroys other blocks on the way.
  • Stun before you crush. Shaking the wall a Sno-Bee is touching stuns it, which is far more reliable than lining up a block.
  • Blocks are finite. Every one you push into a wall to kill something is one fewer available for the alignment.

Arcade controls

Coin on 5, start on 1, and the six cabinet buttons on Z X C V A S. The arcade controls page covers the panel in full.
