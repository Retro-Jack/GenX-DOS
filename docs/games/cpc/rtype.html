R-Type

Electric Dreams, 1988 — the CPC conversion of Irem's 1987 coin-op, whose copyright the game still displays on its title screen. A horizontally scrolling shooter across eight stages, flying the R-9 fighter against the Bydo Empire.

System help — the Amstrad CPC controls

The title screen sets the job out plainly: blast off and strike the evil Bydo Empire. Your ship carries a beam weapon that builds up while you hold fire — the BEAM bar along the bottom of the screen shows the charge — and releasing it sends a shot far stronger than the ordinary rapid fire.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Let the loader run — the technical-specification screen and the logo play first.
  3. At the title screen, press fire to play.

Controls

This one is a joystick game, so a USB gamepad works directly through the emulated 9-pin port — no key-faking needed. The D-pad or left stick steers and any face button fires.

There is no fixed keyboard layout to memorise. The game offers ESC TO DEFINE KEYS at the title screen and then asks you to press a key for each direction and for fire in turn, so any keys you prefer become the controls.

Strategy

  • Hold fire between targets. The beam charges whenever you are not shooting, and a full bar clears obstacles that ordinary fire cannot.
  • Learn the stage, not the reflex. Eight stages ship on the disc, each with fixed enemy patterns — progress comes from knowing what is coming rather than from reacting to it.
  • The screen scrolls whether you are ready or not. Being caught against the left edge by terrain is as fatal as being shot.
