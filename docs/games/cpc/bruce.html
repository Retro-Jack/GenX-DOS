Bruce Lee

Datasoft / U.S. Gold, 1984 — Ron J. Fortier's original design. Bruce fights through a wizard's fortress collecting lanterns while the Green Yamo and a ninja close in.

Twenty chambers of platforms, ladders and lanterns. Each lantern Bruce claims opens the way deeper toward the wizard; the Green Yamo (the big sumo) and the stick-wielding Ninja hunt you the whole way. Best of all, the Yamo doesn't have to be computer-controlled — hand him to a friend.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. At the title, A toggles 1 PLAYER / 2 PLAYERS and B toggles the Green Yamo between COMPUTER and OPPONENT control. Enter begins.

Controls

This game is keyboard-driven — its controls live on the CPC's own keys, so a gamepad doesn't apply here. The < and > keys live on your , and . keys.

KeyAction
A / ZUp / down (climb, jump, duck)
< / >Left / right
SpacePunch / kick (with a direction held)
PPause — Enter continues
EscQuit

Strategy

  • Lanterns first, fights second. The enemies respawn — brawling them is time, not progress. Grab the chamber's lanterns and move.
  • The flying kick clears space. Jump with a direction and strike — both the Yamo and the Ninja go down long enough to slip past.
  • Let them take the hits. The Yamo and Ninja trigger the same traps you do — lure them into the hazards and let the scenery fight for you.
  • Two-player Yamo is the party mode. A second player on the sumo turns the whole game into pursuit comedy.
