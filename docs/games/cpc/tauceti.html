Tau Ceti

CRL Group, 1986 — Pete Cooke. A lone armed skimmer, a dead colony, and a fusion reactor that has to be shut down before it takes the planet with it.

Tau Ceti III was colonised in 2050 and prospered for ninety years, until plague and a meteor strike left it silent — and its robot defence systems running amok. Two expeditions were lost. You are the third: fly a ground-skimmer across more than thirty cities, gather cooling rods, and shut down the main fusion reactor in the capital, Centralis. The planet's day lasts about an Earth hour, and with no moon you will need infra-red to see anything after dark.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. At the title screen, press Fire (Space) to start.
  3. You begin docked, in ground mode. Type NAME to name your pilot, then LAUNCH and Enter to head out onto the surface.

Controls and commands below are transcribed from the CRL instruction manual; the credits and 1986 copyright are read from the game's own title screen. Every flight key can be reassigned in-game with the KEYS command.

The two modes

Docked, you talk to the skimmer's computer by typing commands (ground mode). Out on the surface you fly on single keys (flight mode).

Strategy

  • Name yourself first. NAME is meant to be used before you launch — it identifies you to the computer.
  • Watch the clock. A "spin" is roughly one Earth hour and sixteen make a day. Night falls fast and the planet has no moon, so switch on infra-red (I) rather than flying blind.
  • The rods are the mission. Cooling rods are scattered across the cities; you cannot shut down Centralis' reactor without assembling them.
  • Use the jump pads. J near a pad moves you between distant cities far faster than flying.
  • Rearm before you press on. Buildings with facilities will repair, refuel and rearm via EQUIP — cheaper than dying to a defence robot.
  • Remap what you dislike. Sixteen flight keys is a lot; KEYS lets you move any of them.
