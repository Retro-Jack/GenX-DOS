Roland on the Run

Amsoft / Epicsoft, 1984 — Said A. Abouelhassan. Launch-era Roland: ride the train, jump the traffic, make the hideouts.

Roland's escape is a moving puzzle in lanes. You control the speed of the train he's riding; from its roof he leaps to the first line of traffic, from there to the second, and finally to the hideouts at the roadside. Each lane moves at its own pace — the game is matching speeds and timing the three jumps.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. The game asks for your number of lives — any number from 1 to 255, gloriously — then a starting level from 1 to 9.
  3. Lose the lot and it's Y for another game.

Controls

This game is played on the keyboard. The inlay notes it accepts an Amsoft JY1 joystick, but its controls are five discrete keys rather than directions, so a gamepad gains you nothing here. The < and > keys live on your , and . keys. Keys below are as the game's own operating instructions list them.

KeyAction
>Accelerate the train
<Decelerate the train
ZJump from the train to the first line of traffic
XJump from the first line of traffic to the second
CJump from the second line of traffic to the road hideouts
EscHold — any key restarts play

Strategy

  • Each jump key is lane-specific — Z, X and C only work from their own lane, so there's no mashing your way across. Know which lane you're in.
  • Drive the train, don't fight it. Speed control is half the game: slow down to line Roland up with a gap before committing to the first jump.
  • Learning a level? Take 255 lives. The lives prompt is the difficulty dial — set it low only when you're ready to make level 9 mean something.
