Pole Position

Atari, 1983 — home port of the 1982 Namco arcade designed by Toru Iwatani's team. The arcade machine pioneered scaled-sprite racing; this Atari 8-bit port keeps the qualifying lap and Fuji Speedway course.

System help — the Atari 800XL controls

Qualify your Formula 1 car by completing one practice lap within the time limit, then race the Fuji Speedway against eight rival cars. Overtake, avoid roadside billboards, and don't crash — every collision costs precious time.

Getting started

  1. Click START (or press F4). Choose race length (1, 2, 4, or 8 laps).
  2. Qualifying lap begins. Beat the target time to reach the main race.
  3. Main race: green flag, all 8 cars on grid in front of you. Push past them lap by lap.

Long back straightTop speed — overtake here
Final left-right onto start straightStay outside-inside-outside

Strategy

  • Qualify clean. The qualifying lap timer is tight. One crash = no main race.
  • Shift up by the first corner. Stay in Low past ~120 km/h and the engine note flattens — you stop accelerating.
  • Overtake on straights, never in corners. Two cars on the same line at corner-entry = guaranteed crash.
  • Crashes destroy your car visually but only cost time. You respawn at the same place; the timer keeps draining.
  • Avoid billboards. Brushing a roadside sign causes a crash — same time penalty as hitting a rival.
  • Aim to extend the timer. Each completed lap adds bonus seconds — running out of time ends the race.

Atari 800XL controls

The Atari 8-bit version uses joystick + fire only — gear shifting is via the stick's up/down direction (not a separate button). The arcade's two-position gear lever maps to ↑/↓.
