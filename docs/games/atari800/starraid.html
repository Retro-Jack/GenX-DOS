Star Raiders

Atari, 1979 — designed by Doug Neubauer, written for the Atari 400/800 launch. The first 3D first-person space combat game on a home computer; arguably the killer app that sold the Atari 8-bit. ROM cartridge.

You are an Atlanta-class starship commander hunting Zylons across a 4x8 galactic grid. Switch between front view, aft (rear) view, the long-range galactic chart, and the computer attack display to track and destroy the entire Zylon fleet before they overrun your starbases.

Getting started

  1. Press F4 Start to choose a mission.
  2. Press 1–4 at the mission prompt for Novice / Pilot / Warrior / Commander.
  3. Switch to the galactic chart (G), pick a sector with Zylons (red), engage hyperwarp (H).
  4. In combat, centre the target in the cross-hairs and fire.

Strategy

  • Watch your energy. Every shot, shield-hit, hyperwarp jump, and even the long-range scan drains the energy bar. Dock at a starbase (manoeuvre into the launch bay until it captures you) to refuel.
  • Manage Zylon advance. Zylons move sector-to-sector on the galactic chart in real time. If they reach all four starbases you lose — clear the closest threats first.
  • Theta lock first. Press T to acquire a lock on the nearest enemy — the computer attack display then shows distance and bearing arrows for the rest of the combat.
  • Shields cost energy but die quick without. Run shields up only when in combat; drop them during hyperwarp and docking to conserve.
  • Hyperwarp accurately. A bad warp leaves you in an empty sector wasting energy. Use the galactic chart cursor (0–9 for X/Y) precisely before hitting H.

Atari 800XL controls

This is a keyboard-and-joystick game — the joystick handles pitch/yaw and fire, the keyboard handles every system command. No analogue paddle controllers needed.
