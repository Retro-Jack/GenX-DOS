Miner 2049er

Big Five Software, 1982 — Bill Hogue. Bounty Bob hunts the fugitive Yukon Yohan through 10 mines (later expanded to 11 on cart). One of the first home games with more than 4 distinct levels — its 10-screen scope was a marketing landmark.

Bounty Bob must walk over every floor tile of a mine to "claim" it (tiles change colour as you cover them). When every tile is claimed, you advance to the next mine. Mutant radioactive creatures patrol — touch them and you lose a life, unless you grab a power-up first.

Getting started

  1. Press F4 Start at the title screen to begin.
  2. Walk Bob across every floor section using ←/→. Jump with L Ctrl.
  3. Use ladders, slides, and transporters to reach upper levels.
  4. Touch power-ups (the moving items) to turn mutants edible briefly — chase and kill them for bonus points.

Strategy

  • Plan a path. Walk every tile, but don't backtrack — pick a route that snakes through the whole level in one continuous run.
  • Power-up timing. Grab the power-up just before mutants close in, then chase down as many as you can before it expires.
  • Jump heights vary. Hold ↑ + L Ctrl for a higher jump, sideways + jump for distance, neutral + jump for a vertical hop.
  • Falls kill above a certain height. Use slides and ladders to drop safely, never just jump off ledges.
  • The slide commits you. Once on a slide, the only escape is to ride it out — plan entries carefully.

Atari 800XL controls

Pure joystick + fire. The Atari version added the floating-platforms mine (level 8) over the Apple ][ original.
