Ballblazer

Lucasfilm Games, 1985 — David Levine, Peter Langston. The first game published by Lucasfilm Games (alongside Rescue on Fractalus!). Two-player split-screen sci-fi sport with real-time fractal-derived audio. Frequently cited as a launch milestone for the company.

Two hover-rotofoils (yours and a rival) chase a plasmorb across a grid playfield, trying to push it through goalposts at either end. The first to score 10 points or run out the clock wins. Split-screen, even in 1-player vs CPU.

Getting started

  1. Press F4 Start.
  2. Choose 1-player (vs CPU 1–9 difficulty) or 2-player split-screen.
  3. The plasmorb spawns at centre. Race to it, ram into it to grab, then drive toward your goal.

Scoring

GoalPoints
Roll the plasmorb through any goalpost1
From midfield (long shot)2
From your own half (very long shot)3

After each goal the goalposts narrow slightly — high-scoring games end with skinny gates.

Strategy

  • Let the view do the aiming. Your rotofoil automatically turns to face the Plasmorb — focus on positioning, then hit L Ctrl to fire it goalward.
  • Steal by ramming. If you hit the opponent while they have the ball, the ball pops free.
  • Long shots score more. A 3-point shot from your own half can swing a losing match — but the trajectory is hard to keep straight under fire.
  • Manage the clock. If you're ahead, hold the ball near your goal to run time — but the opponent can ram.
  • Strafe to find a lane. Left / right slide you sideways while you stay facing the ball — use it to dodge the opponent and open a clear shooting lane.

Atari 800XL controls

Joystick + fire (fire = auto-aim toggle). 2-player needs joysticks in ports 1 and 2 — the split-screen view shows both players' perspectives simultaneously, a notable technical feat for 1985 8-bit hardware.
