Bruce Lee

Datasoft, 1984 — Ron J. Fortier. Bruce Lee fights through 20 single-screen chambers of a wizard's lair to reach the Green Yamo and the final wizard. A platformer + fighting hybrid that paired perfectly with the home-computer era.

Collect every lantern in each chamber to open the next door. Two enemies stalk Bruce: the Ninja (with a stick) and the green Sumo (Yamo) who throws him. Punches and kicks dispatch them — but they respawn endlessly. Survive to the wizard's chamber.

Getting started

  1. Press F4 Start. Choose 1- or 2-player mode (in 2-player, second human controls the Sumo).
  2. Collect all lanterns in a chamber to unlock the exit.
  3. Avoid traps (exploding bushes, electric shock zones, bottomless pits).
  4. Fight or evade the Ninja and Sumo as you go.

Enemies

EnemyBehaviour
NinjaWalks toward Bruce, swings stick at close range. Easy to bait into a jump-kick.
Sumo (Green Yamo)Lumbers in, grabs Bruce, throws him. Tough to knock down — kick from behind or jump over.
Exploding bushesStatic; touch = death. Memorise positions.
Lightning zonesAnimated electric arcs; cross only when the gap appears.

Strategy

  • Jump-kicks dominate. A running jump-kick can clear two enemies and reach a high platform — your most versatile move.
  • Sumo from behind. The Sumo can't block kicks to his back. Lure him forward, jump over, kick.
  • Lantern order. Sweep lanterns from the riskiest first (close to enemies / traps), saving the safe ones for when you're hurt.
  • 2-player tip. The Sumo player can deliberately fall in pits — they respawn. Use that to camp dangerous lantern spots.
  • The wizard's chamber. Final room — no enemies, just timing. The wizard appears briefly; reach his platform before he vanishes.

Atari 800XL controls

Single joystick + fire. The Atari version is the original — every other 8-bit port (C64, Spectrum, MSX, CPC) derives from this code. Smooth 60 Hz animation made the kicks feel snappy in a way 50 Hz ports never quite captured.
