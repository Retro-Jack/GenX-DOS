Console · Atari 2600

Adventure

Atari, 1980 — designed by Warren Robinett. The first action-adventure game on console, and home of the first known video-game Easter egg.

You're a square. Roam a multi-screen kingdom looking for the Enchanted Chalice and return it to the Gold Castle. Three dragons (Yorgle, Grundle, Rhindle) want to eat you; the Bat steals and shuffles objects between rooms. Find keys (Gold, Black, White) to open castles and the magnet to retrieve lost items.

Getting started

  1. Press V (Select) to pick game variant (1 = easy, 2 = full game, 3 = random-placement).
  2. Press Enter (Reset) to start.
  3. Push the joystick to walk; Space to pick up / drop items (you can only hold one at a time).

Strategy

  • Sword + dragon = front kiss. The sword's hitbox is at the tip; charge in head-first while holding the sword, you'll kill the dragon. Walk in side-ways and you'll be eaten.
  • Bat is chaos. The Bat picks up items at random and flies them around — including a dragon corpse, which it'll drop on YOU. Drop your current item near the Bat to make it swap, then snatch a better one.
  • Bridge through walls. The bridge is just placed on a wall and you walk over. Critical for shortcuts through Black Castle.
  • The Easter egg. The Grey Dot (in the catacombs, one room east + several south of the Black Castle) — bring it to a specific wall section in the room east of the Gold Castle to unveil Warren Robinett's signature in flashing colours.
  • Magnet wins. If a dragon eats you holding an item, that item's lost inside. The magnet can extract it.

Atari 2600 controls

Single-stick + single-button. Enter (Reset) starts the game; V (Select) cycles variant. The arrow keys move; Space is the joystick fire button (drop/pick-up).
