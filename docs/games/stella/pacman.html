Pac-Man

Atari, 1982 — Tod Frye's notorious 2600 port of Toru Iwatani's 1980 arcade. The "wafers and ghosts" version with the flickering ghost — Atari's first major perceived flop.

A maze, four ghosts, dots ("wafers"), four power pills. Eat all the wafers without being touched by Inky, Blinky, Pinky, or Clyde. Power pills turn ghosts blue and edible for a short window. The 2600 port replaces fruit with "vitamins."

Getting started

  1. Press V (Select) to cycle game variant.
  2. Press Enter (Reset) to start.
  3. Walk through the maze with the joystick eating dots.

Ghost cheat sheet

ColourBehaviour (in 2600 port)
Red (Blinky)Direct chase — heads straight for Pac-Man
Pink (Pinky)Ambush — targets 4 tiles ahead of Pac-Man
Cyan (Inky)Erratic — combines Blinky's position with Pac-Man's vector
Orange (Clyde)Random — flips between chase and scatter

2600 port limitation: only one ghost is rendered per scanline, producing the iconic "flicker." Lining up multiple ghosts can momentarily hide one. Use the flicker — don't fight it.

Strategy

  • Eat power pills last. Power-up windows are short. Eat surrounding wafers first, then grab the pill, then chase the cornered ghost cluster.
  • Cornering trick. The ghosts can't reverse direction except at intersections — fake them out by turning a corner just as they commit.
  • Symmetric paths. The 2600 maze is symmetrical; both warp tunnels are usable. Run the tunnel when cornered.
  • Don't camp. The 2600 version has a "spawn timer" — wait too long in one spot and the ghosts converge on you faster.
  • Vitamin bonus. The "vitamin" (Atari's name for the cherry/strawberry) appears once per maze in the centre. Grab it for 100 pts.

Atari 2600 controls

Joystick + fire button — fire is unused in Pac-Man. Press Enter (Reset) to start. The 2600 port is widely considered the worst arcade translation of its era; play it for historical interest, then try the Coleco port for the canonical home experience.
