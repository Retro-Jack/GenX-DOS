Console · Atari 2600

Breakout

Atari, 1978 (2600 port of the 1976 arcade) — designed by Nolan Bushnell and Steve Bristow; the arcade hardware was a Steve Wozniak / Steve Jobs build. Paddle-controller pack-in classic.

A paddle bounces a ball into a wall of bricks above. Each brick clears on contact; clear two screens to win a round. The paddle shrinks halfway up the wall and the ball speeds up — managing angle of impact (centre vs. edge of paddle) is the entire skill.

Getting started

  1. Click game reset (or press Enter) to start a game, then Space to serve each ball.
  2. Steer the paddle left/right with the ← / → arrow keys.
  3. Clear the wall twice to complete the game; you get five balls per round.

Game variants

The Select switch cycles 12 variants: solo / two-player, Breakdown (rebuild bricks), Cavity (extra balls behind the wall), Steerable (control ball angle mid-flight), Foozpong, Catch (catch the ball and re-launch). Game 1 is the canonical mode.

Strategy

  • Edge English. Hitting the ball with the left or right edge of the paddle adds steep angle. Centre = bounce straight back. Master this to attack specific brick stacks.
  • Wedge. Try to tunnel the ball up the side of the wall so it ricochets along the top — pinball mode that clears bricks fast without you needing to control it.
  • Speed-up trigger. The ball accelerates after 4, 12, and 36 hits, and again when reaching the top two rows. Anticipate the jump.
  • Paddle shrinks. When the ball reaches the top, your paddle halves in width. Position matters more once you've cleared the bottom half.

Atari 2600 controls

Click the game area to enable sound and controls. Enter (the GAME RESET switch) is the start button — most 2600 games won't begin play until you press it.
