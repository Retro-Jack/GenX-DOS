Handheld · Atari Lynx (1991)

Rampart

Atari's genre-bending castle game — part artillery shooter, part real-time Tetris-y wall-building, part strategy. Unlike anything else in the Lynx library.

Each round runs in three phases: place cannons inside your walled castle, bombard the enemy ships/castles by aiming and firing those cannons, then repair — frantically drop Tetris-like wall pieces to fully re-enclose at least one castle before the timer ends. Fail to seal a castle and you're out. Survive, expand, and wipe out the enemy.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, start a game; you'll cycle through the place → fire → repair phases each round.
  2. During repair, rotate and drop wall pieces to close a complete loop around a castle.

Strategy

  • Sealing a castle is survival. Repair always comes first — a fully-enclosed castle keeps you alive even if you scored no hits.
  • Cannons must sit inside walls and you fire all of them — more cannons = more shots, so claim territory to place them.
  • Aim for the enemy's walls, not just their cannons — breaching their enclosure stops them sealing up next round.
  • Pre-plan your repair shape. Know which gaps you'll fill before the pieces start dropping; hesitation runs out the clock.
