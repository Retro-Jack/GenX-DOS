Handheld · Atari Lynx (1989)

Electrocop

A Lynx launch title — a run-and-gun action game where a cybernetic cop fights through a fortress to rescue the kidnapped President's daughter.

System help — the Atari Lynx controls

Blast through the corridors of an enemy fortress, shooting robots and dodging traps, against a ticking countdown. Between action floors you hack security terminals via quick mini-games to unlock the next area. Reach the top before time runs out.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, start; you begin at the fortress entrance with a time limit.
  2. Shoot enemies, grab power-ups, and find the terminals/lifts to climb floors.

Strategy

  • Mind the clock. The countdown is the real enemy — don't linger; learn the fastest route up.
  • The terminal hacks are timed logic puzzles; failing them costs time, so practise the patterns.
  • Collect weapon upgrades — the starting blaster is weak against the tougher robots deeper in.
  • Save-state before each new floor; the time pressure makes blind runs costly. Five save slots per game — pick 1–5 from the save or load menu.
