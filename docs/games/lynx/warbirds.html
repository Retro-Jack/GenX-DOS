Handheld · Atari Lynx (1989)

Warbirds

A Lynx launch title — World War I biplane dogfighting, again leaning on the hardware's scaling to swing enemy planes around you in a 3D-feeling sky.

Take to the skies in a WWI fighter, chasing and gunning down enemy aces across a run of missions. The view sits behind your plane; enemy biplanes scale and bank through the frame as you manoeuvre to get them in your sights. Watch your fuel and ammo, and don't fly into the ground.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, start the campaign; you launch into the first sortie.
  2. Turn to track the nearest enemy, line up, and fire in bursts.

Strategy

  • Lead your shots. Fire where the enemy is heading, not where it is — banking targets need anticipation.
  • Short bursts. Your gun overheats/empties; conserve ammo and don't spray.
  • Keep your nose up. In the heat of a turn it's easy to dive into the terrain.
  • Pick off stragglers rather than diving into a pack; isolate and kill.
