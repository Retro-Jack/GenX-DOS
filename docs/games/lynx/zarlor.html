Zarlor Mercenary

Epyx / Atari, 1990. A vertical-scrolling shoot-'em-up with a twist: you're a warship-for-hire clearing zones of enemies for bounty, then spending the takings on bigger guns at a between-mission arms bazaar.

Fly your warship up a scrolling battlefield, blasting enemy craft and ground installations and hoovering up the money they leave behind. Between missions a merchant sets up shop: cash buys a laser, a megabomb, shields and upgrades, so each contract funds the firepower for the next. It's a Lynx showpiece — up to four players over ComLynx — but solo it's a tight buy-and-blast loop.

Getting started

  1. The game boots to its title screen — click the game area to enable sound and controls, then press Z (B) to start.
  2. Fly with the D-pad (↑ forward, ↓ back, ←/→ steer) and fire your cannon with X.
  3. After a mission, visit the merchant — put the box on him and press X to buy the laser and a megabomb before the next run.

Strategy

  • Cash is the game. Money dropped by kills is the only way to afford the good weapons — clear thoroughly and sweep up every drop before it scrolls off.
  • Buy the laser first. The starting cannon is weak; the laser on Z is a big damage jump and the earliest worthwhile purchase.
  • Hoard megabombs. The Option 1 megabomb clears the screen — save them for swarms and the tougher end-of-zone pressure rather than routine fire.
  • Shop deliberately. At the merchant, drop the box on him and press X to enter buy/sell mode, then pick items; sell anything you've outgrown to fund the next tier.
  • Party trick. Ctrl+Enter (Option 2 + Pause) flips the screen 180° and reverses the controls — handy for a left-hander, chaos for everyone else.
