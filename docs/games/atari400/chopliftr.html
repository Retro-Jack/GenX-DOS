Choplifter!

Brøderbund Software (Dan Gorlin), 1982 — side-scrolling helicopter rescue across enemy lines. Land near hostage barracks, ferry survivors back to base, dodge MiGs and tanks. The Atari 8-bit cart that defined "civilian rescue" as a genre.

System help — the Atari 400 controls

Pilot a chopper from left base across 8 zones to the right. Each zone has hostage barracks — blow the door, land, watch 16 hostages run aboard, fly home. Enemy tanks ground-fire; jets and air-jets engage in dogfights. Lose all hostages or the chopper, game over. Rescuing all 64 wins the campaign.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space or fire at the title to start.

Strategy

  • Three rotation states. Facing right = forward flight + forward fire. Facing left = reverse + rear fire. Facing front (down) = stationary hover + downward fire — the only way to drop bombs straight onto barracks.
  • Land gently, run hostages. Touch ground softly next to a barracks; hostages spawn and run towards you. Take off only when 16 are aboard or no more are running — partial loads still score.
  • Tanks fire arcs. Tank shells lob, not flat — the arc means you can fly low between the muzzle and the impact point, but stay too high and the arc catches you.
  • Jets are kamikaze. Air-jets ram. Face them, fire forward, retreat backward — never let them reach contact.
  • Last-zone push. The 4th zone onward, enemies spawn faster than you can shoot. Save bomb-runs for groups, dogfight singles.
