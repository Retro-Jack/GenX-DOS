Asteroids

Atari, 1981 — Lyle Rains and Ed Logg's 1979 vector arcade ported to the Atari 8-bit. The home version replaced vectors with filled-shape bitmaps but kept the wraparound playfield and physics intact.

Your ship floats in a wraparound asteroid field. Shoot asteroids — each large one breaks into 2 mediums, each medium into 2 smalls. Clear all rocks to advance to the next wave with more, faster ones. Flying saucers appear at random and shoot at you. Hyperspace teleports you to a random spot (sometimes safe, sometimes worse).

Getting started

  1. Press F4 Start.
  2. Rotate with ←/→, thrust with ↑, fire with L Ctrl.
  3. Press ↓ for hyperspace if cornered.

Strategy

  • Tap thrust. Short bursts let you reposition; long thrusts send you careening into rocks.
  • Centre control. Drift near screen-centre — the wraparound field is most predictable from the middle.
  • Hyperspace as last resort. Random re-entry can land you in a rock.
  • Lurk on small saucers. They shoot accurately but pay 1000 each — worth chasing once you're confident.
  • Leave one small rock. Classic high-score farm — kill all but one small asteroid and grind saucers.
