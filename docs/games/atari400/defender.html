Home Computer · Atari 400

Defender

Atari, 1981 — home port of Eugene Jarvis's 1980 Williams arcade. The arcade was infamous for its 5-button + 2-axis control panel; the Atari 8-bit version remaps it all to a joystick + a few keys, becoming infinitely more approachable.

System help — the Atari 400 controls

Patrol a horizontally-scrolling alien planet, defending the humanoids on the ground from waves of Landers, Mutants, Bombers, Pods, and Swarmers. Landers grab humanoids and try to mutate them — rescue every humanoid before the planet's surface is destroyed, then the wave ends.

Getting started

  1. Click START (or press F4).
  2. Watch the radar at the top for incoming Landers (yellow).
  3. Fly towards them, shoot before they touch the ground.
  4. If a Lander grabs a humanoid, shoot the Lander mid-air and catch the falling humanoid by flying under them.

Strategy

  • Watch the radar, not the screen. The mini-map is the whole game — your screen view is just a slice of it.
  • Catch falling humanoids. Shoot the Lander, then fly underneath so the humanoid lands on your ship; carry it to the ground.
  • Smart bomb the Pod cluster. Pods become 4–5 Swarmers — better to use a bomb than try to kill them all individually.
  • Never let all humanoids die. If the planet's last humanoid is taken, the surface explodes and the screen fills with Mutants — game-over conditions.
  • Reverse-thrust to ambush. Quick stick-flip lets you fire backward at a chasing enemy.
