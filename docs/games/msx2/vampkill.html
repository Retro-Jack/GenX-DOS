Vampire Killer

Konami, 1986 — the MSX2 sibling of Castlevania, released the same week as the Famicom Disk System original. Same hero (Simon Belmont), same castle, same whip — but the level design is exploration-driven instead of linear: each stage is a maze hiding multiple doors, and you need the specific key for the locked exit.

Side-scrolling stages with locked-room mazes rather than the Famicom version's straight corridors. Defeat enemies for hearts (used as ammo for sub-weapons) and gold; gold buys keys, sub-weapons and map-room hints from in-castle traders. Reach the boss only after finding the right key for the level's exit door.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title to start Stage 1.

Controls

KeyAction
← / →Walk left / right
↑ / ↓Climb stairs
SpaceWhip / attack
↑ + SpaceThrow sub-weapon (cross, dagger, axe, holy water — costs hearts)

Strategy

  • Don't burn keys early. Most stages have several locked doors but only one exit door; check behind low-cost doors first by their proximity to coloured walls.
  • Stairs commit you. Like Castlevania, once Simon steps onto a staircase he can't reverse mid-step — bait enemies off the stairs first.
  • Holy water freezes bosses. Save heart stocks; a holy-water-cross combo on Phase 1 bosses (zombies, bats) is the cheap kill.
  • Different from the Famicom version. If you've played Castlevania (NES/FDS), don't expect the same map — Vampire Killer's stages are wider and require exploration.
