Space Manbow

Konami, 1989 — horizontal shoot-'em-up regarded as the technical pinnacle of the MSX2. Multi-layer parallax scrolling, in-stage cutscenes, and a soundtrack that pushed the FM-PAC sound chip well beyond anything earlier on the system. Pilot the Manbow fighter against the Versry Empire across seven sci-fi stages.

System help — the MSX2 controls

Not built on the Gradius/Nemesis engine — power-ups here are R-Type style: collect a fixed weapon power module that determines the entire shot pattern for the run. Multi-layer scrolling means hazards appear from foreground tiles, not just enemies — the ship has to navigate terrain through bio-mechanical corridors.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title to start.

Strategy

  • Charge shot wrecks bosses. Hold fire to charge; release on the boss's weak point for several frames' damage in one hit.
  • Speed level 2. Manbow's three speeds are slow / medium / fast; medium is the practical sweet spot for stages 1-5. Drop to slow for stage 6's narrow corridors.
  • Foreground tiles kill. Hazards aren't just enemies — the bio-mechanical walls protruding from the foreground inflict the same damage. Read the parallax depth carefully.
  • Konami's MSX2 farewell. Their last big MSX2 release before they pivoted to PC Engine; the game shows everything they'd learned about V9938 sprite multiplexing.
