F-1 Spirit: The Way to Formula-1

Konami, 1987 — top-down circuit racer; sequel to "Road Fighter" pushed up to MSX2 hardware. Career mode climbs the motorsport ladder: F3000 → Endurance → F-1, racing on real circuits (Monza, Silverstone, Suzuka, Nürburgring) against rival cars. The V9938's smooth scrolling shines.

Choose a class, then race the season schedule of named circuits. Finish positions earn points; top-tier points unlock the next class. Mid-race pit stops are an option — you trade time for tyres/fuel, the AI doesn't pit so you pay back the lost seconds with corner consistency. Damage matters: smash too many barriers and the car retires.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Select class (F3000 to start) then a circuit; press Space at the grid.

Strategy

  • Short-shift entries. Drop a gear before the corner not in it — F-1 Spirit's mid-corner gear changes break the back wheels loose.
  • Pit on lap 2 of 5. Most circuits reward an early pit: fresh tyres on lap 2 mean your last three laps are quicker than the rivals' tyre-worn finish.
  • Don't fight the wall. Wall contact slows you AND damages the car; lifting and re-aligning is faster than carrying speed into the wall.
  • F3000 to F-1 is non-linear. The Endurance class between them requires both a co-driver and 24-hour stamina (long-race format); some players skip it once they've banked points.
