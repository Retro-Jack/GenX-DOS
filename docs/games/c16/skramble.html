Skramble

Anirog, 1984 — Konami's 1981 Scramble arcade reborn on the C16. Six-stage horizontal scroller through fuel caves, mountain ranges, and the final defended fortress.

System help — the Commodore 16 controls

Pilot a jet across six side-scrolling stages — terrain, fuel depots, anti-air rockets, mountain caves, asteroid field, base run. Two weapons: forward laser (lots of ammo) and gravity bombs (drop on ground targets). Refuel by shooting yellow tanks below. Out of fuel = game over. Make the base, return to stage one at higher difficulty.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press fire at the title to start.

Strategy

  • Tap fire constantly. Each press fires laser AND drops a bomb — keeps the screen clear without exhausting either supply faster than the other.
  • Refuel even when "full". The gauge ticks down constantly; pre-empt the dry spell on rocket-heavy screens by topping up early.
  • Climb in caves. The cave roof is forgiving; the floor isn't. Stay just below the ceiling and dive to bomb depots.
  • Base run rhythm. The final corridor closes — hammer fire from entry, no aiming needed; collisions are the killer, not enemies.
