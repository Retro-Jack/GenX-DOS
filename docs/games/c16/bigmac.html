Big Mac

Atlantis Software, 1985 — single-screen platform game where Mac, a Scottish football fan in tartan, climbs ladders dodging beer cans and angry rivals on the way to the trophy. £1.99 budget classic.

Donkey Kong layout — angled girders, ladders, hazards. Reach the top to claim the trophy; falling cans, rolling kegs, and rival fans patrol the route. Pick up bonus items (haggis, whisky) for extra points. Single-screen but increasingly hazardous on each loop.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press fire at the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Run left / right
Numpad 8Climb ladder up
Numpad 2Climb ladder down
Numpad 0 / 5Jump (over cans)

Strategy

  • Jump rolling cans early. The hitbox is wider than the sprite suggests — leave a generous gap.
  • Ladders are safe from rolling hazards. When in doubt, climb up a rung — kegs roll past horizontally without descending.
  • Bonus items aren't worth dying for. The trophy is the score driver; whisky is gravy.
  • Loop difficulty. Each completion respawns Mac at the bottom with faster cans — no infinite stalling allowed.
