Xargon Wars

Anirog, 1984 — Defender-style horizontal-scrolling shooter on a single planet surface. Defend abductee humans from the alien fleet sweeping across the landscape.

System help — the Commodore 16 controls

Side-scrolling defender variant. A radar strip across the top shows the full landscape; pilot left/right wrapping at the edges, fire forward, dive to scoop falling humans before they hit the ground. Lose all humans = planet destroyed, jump to harder wave.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press fire at the title to start.

Strategy

  • Radar first, screen second. The off-screen mini-map at the top tells you where the next abductor is — react to that before it enters the main view.
  • Catch falling humans mid-air. A human dropped from too high will splatter even if you killed the abductor — get under them on the way down.
  • Wrap the planet. The map is circular; the fastest route to a far threat is sometimes the wrap-around direction, not direct chase.
  • Smart-bomb the swarm spawns. When the screen fills with abductors, retreat to a quiet patch rather than dying in the cluster.
