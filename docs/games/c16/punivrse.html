Mr. Puniverse

CRL Group (Andrew Spencer), 1986 — bodybuilding-themed single-screen platformer. Train Mr. Puniverse from a 7-stone weakling to a strongman by collecting protein/dumbbells and avoiding sand-kickers on the beach.

System help — the Commodore 16 controls

Side-on beach scene. Walk left/right, jump between platforms; collect weights and protein shakes to build up the bicep meter; avoid bullies who kick sand in your face (lose progress) and pelicans dropping fish. Hit the bicep target to complete the level and move to a new beach.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press fire at the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Walk left / right
Numpad 8Jump
Numpad 0 / 5Pick up / use item

Strategy

  • Dumbbells beat protein. Direct bicep boost — protein shakes are slower-acting and easier to spill if a bully connects.
  • Bullies have a sand-kick range. Stay outside one body-width when passing; charge them only at full size.
  • Watch the pelican shadow. Bombing-fish hits where the shadow lands — dodge the shadow, not the bird.
  • Andrew Spencer's other game. Same author as Berks (1985) — both lean on the C16's TED-chip sprite multiplexing for the busy-screen feel.
