Hustler

Bubble Bus Software, 1985 — top-down 8-ball pool simulation. Aim the cue, set power, take your shot. Solo practice or two-player alternating. The C16 budget standout for sports sims.

System help — the Commodore 16 controls

Overhead pool table with realistic-for-1985 ball physics. Rotate a directional aim line around the cue ball, set shot power, stroke. Spotted and striped balls compete for pockets; sinking the 8-ball before clearing your set is an instant loss. Two-player alternating turns or solo time-trial.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press fire at the title to start; pick 1- or 2-player.

Strategy

  • Soft for position, hard for pots. Lower-power shots leave the cue ball near the next target; max-power shots scatter the cue unpredictably.
  • The aim line is straight. No spin modelling — the line is exactly where the cue ball will go on contact.
  • Cushion banks are reliable. One-cushion bank shots are deterministic enough to call; two-cushion are coin-flip.
  • Don't scratch. Sinking the cue ball hands the next shot to your opponent with ball-in-hand.
