Fire Ant

Anirog / Mr. Micro, 1984 — climb through a multi-screen ant colony as a worker ant, ferrying food up to the queen's chamber while avoiding spiders and beetles. C16's 16K-RAM budget twist on the Mr. Robot template.

Vertical platforms span a flick-screen ant tunnel network. Pick up food items below; carry them upward to the queen's room at the top. Spiders abseil down threads; beetles patrol horizontals. One-touch death. Each delivery scores; clear the food and the screen resets at higher pest density.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press fire at the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Run left / right
Numpad 8Climb up (when on a ladder/web)
Numpad 2Climb down
Numpad 0 / 5Jump

Strategy

  • Take one item at a time. Hands full = slower turn-around; the spider density rises with screen time.
  • Watch the spider threads. Threads telegraph the drop point — wait for the spider to bottom-out before walking under.
  • Use beetles' fixed patterns. Beetles patrol predictable horizontal routes; time crossings between passes.
  • The queen's room is the safe rest. No enemies spawn in the top chamber — catch your breath there before the return run.
