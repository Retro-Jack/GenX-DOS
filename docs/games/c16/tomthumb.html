Tom Thumb

Pirate Software, 1985 — single-screen platformer based on the fairytale. The tiny Tom navigates oversized household hazards (clocks, candles, kitchen utensils) collecting items between rooms.

System help — the Commodore 16 controls

Each screen is one room of a giant's house, scaled so Tom is sprite-sized vs furniture. Hop between platforms; pick up items; dodge moving hazards (candles dripping wax, swinging pendulums, kitchen knives). Reach the room exit to advance. Limited lives.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press fire at the title to start.

Strategy

  • Watch the wax drip. Candles drip on a fixed rhythm — count one drop, then sprint past.
  • Pendulums lead. A swinging pendulum's hit-zone is its bottom arc; cross at the apex of its swing, never the bottom.
  • Tom can't fight. No attack button — everything is dodge-and-time. Patience beats reflexes.
  • Pickups gate exits. Some rooms won't open the door until you grab a specific item (key, candle, cheese).
