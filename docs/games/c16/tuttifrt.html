Tutti Frutti

Tynesoft, 1985 — single-screen maze action. Roam corridors collecting fruit while avoiding monochrome ghosts. Pac-Man derivative with a C16 fruit-platter twist.

System help — the Commodore 16 controls

Maze with fruit pickups instead of dots. Eat all the fruit to clear the level. Ghosts patrol fixed routes initially; touching one costs a life. Pick up the bonus "vitamin" (centre of the maze) to briefly reverse the chase — ghosts become edible for points.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press fire at the title to start.

Strategy

  • Pre-queue turns. Push the next direction before reaching the junction; the input buffers one move ahead.
  • Vitamin timing. Save the power-up until two ghosts are close — eating both during one window is the big-score play.
  • Wrap-around tunnels. Side edges of the maze connect — emergency escape route when cornered.
  • Last-fruit safety. Ghosts speed up as the fruit count drops; leave the safest fruit for last and clear corners first.
