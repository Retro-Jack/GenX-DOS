Metroid

Nintendo R&D1, 1987 (NA) — designed by Yoshio Sakamoto. The progenitor of the "Metroidvania" genre: non-linear exploration gated by power-ups.

Samus Aran lands on planet Zebes. Explore a maze of caverns, find missile expansions and beam upgrades, then defeat the two mini-bosses (Kraid and Ridley) and Mother Brain in the final corridor. No save system — the password (NA) records your progress for re-entry.

Getting started

  1. At the title, leave "GAME START" highlighted, press START. (Use "CONTINUE" to enter a password.)
  2. Shoot the first round door, go right, fall into the elevator to Brinstar. Find the Maru Mari (Morph Ball) — it's a few rooms left.
  3. Pick up Long Beam and missiles before going deep. Map the layout as you go — there's no in-game map.

Strategy

  • Map on paper. No in-game map. Most players grid-out Brinstar and Norfair while exploring.
  • Get Ice Beam first. Metroids (Tourian gate) are immune to Wave; only Ice + 5 missiles per Metroid clears them.
  • Bomb-jump trick. Drop a bomb in morph ball, the blast pops you up; chain bombs for vertical climbs (faster than running back for the elevator).
  • Justin Bailey. Password "JUSTIN BAILEY ------ ------" starts you with all upgrades (except Wave/Screw) and Samus unsuited.
  • Energy management. Tanks cap at six. Refill at orange enemies in lower corridors before tackling Kraid/Ridley.

NES controls

Default keyboard mapping for this bundle: arrows = D-pad, Z = B, X = A, ENTER = START, V = SELECT (beam/missile toggle).
