Castlevania

Konami, 1987 (NA) — directed by Hitoshi Akamatsu. Simon Belmont's first outing; the action-platformer that set the gothic-horror template.

System help — the NES controls

Six stages, eighteen sub-stages. Simon's main weapon is the Vampire Killer whip; sub-weapons (dagger, axe, holy water, cross, stopwatch) cost hearts to use. Reach Dracula at Stage 6 — three hits with the whip in his first form, several in his hideous second form.

Getting started

  1. Press START to begin (no character select; you're always Simon).
  2. Whip enemies with B. Climb stairs by pressing UP (to go up) or DOWN (to go down) while Simon is standing at the foot or head of a staircase.
  3. Collect candles by whipping them — hearts power sub-weapons; bigger items boost score or grant power-ups.

Strategy

  • Jumps don't curve. Commit to a jump's direction before leaving the ground — Simon's arc is fixed. Stand-still jumps for vertical, run-jumps for horizontal.
  • Knockback kills. Most deaths are bottomless-pit knockback. Approach pit edges from a crouch, not at full run.
  • Medusa Heads (Stage 4). Iconic killers. Whip them out before they sine-wave into you; the holy water locks them in place mid-arc.
  • Bosses on the easy list. Holy Water + II multiplier stunlocks Phantom Bat (1-boss), Mummies (3), Frankenstein (4), Grim Reaper (5), and Dracula form 2.
  • Whip upgrades. Two consecutive Morning Stars upgrade Vampire Killer to chain whip (longer reach). Dying drops you back to leather.

NES controls

Default keyboard mapping for this bundle: arrows = D-pad, Z = B, X = A, ENTER = START, V = SELECT.
