Tetris

Nintendo, 1989 (NA) — Alexey Pajitnov's puzzle, licensed from Elorg via Henk Rogers. The "Nintendo" version (not the Tengen one). Rotation system is NRS (the original — no wall-kicks).

System help — the NES controls

Stack tetrominoes (I, O, T, L, J, S, Z) into solid horizontal rows; cleared rows disappear and you score. Pieces fall faster each level. Game ends when the stack reaches the top of the playfield. Two modes: A-Type (endless, level → speed) and B-Type (clear 25 lines with a pre-set garbage stack).

Getting started

  1. Title → press START. Pick A-TYPE (endless) or B-TYPE (target).
  2. Choose starting level (0-9 on A; 0-9 on B) and, for B-Type, starting height (0-5 garbage rows).
  3. Rotate and slide each piece into place before it locks.

Strategy

  • Tetrises only. Single-line clears are score-suicide. Keep a clean column-1 well and wait for I-pieces to score 1200×(n+1).
  • Flat top. Avoid overhangs — NRS Tetris has no T-spins or wall-kicks; a piece that doesn't fit is lost.
  • Soft drop banks score. 1 point per cell dropped. At high levels this is meaningless; at low levels it adds up.
  • Level cap. Speed maxes around level 19 (NTSC ~1 cell every 4 frames). Heart-rate spike is the actual game.
  • B-Type rocket. Clearing all 25 lines at height 5 starting at level 9 unlocks the rocket cutscene with all 7 game characters.

NES controls

Default keyboard mapping for this bundle: arrows = D-pad, Z = B, X = A, ENTER = START, V = SELECT.
