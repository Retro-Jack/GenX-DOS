Sopwith

1984 — David L. Clark, for BMB Compuscience. A biplane, a bomb load, and someone else's buildings to put it on. The author released the source in 2000, latterly under the GNU GPL.

System help — the IBM PC controls

A side-on biplane game with a proper flight model underneath it: the aircraft stalls, it loops, and it will fly into the ground if you stop paying attention. You bomb the enemy's buildings and shoot down anything that comes up to argue. The whole thing runs in four colours at a pace that still feels quick, and it is one of the few games here whose source its own author put into the world.

Getting started

  1. Pick a game from the opening screen: S for one player, C to fly against the computer. M is for several players over a network and has nothing to answer here.
  2. The sound starts switched off. Once you are flying, press S again to turn it on — it is the same key, and it does a different job in the air than it does on the opening screen.
  3. You start on the ground. Accelerate with X until you have flying speed, then pull the nose up.
  4. Buildings are the targets. Bombs fall where physics puts them, not where you are pointing.
  5. If you get lost, H turns the plane towards home.

Controls

From the game's documentation. The three flight keys sit together under the right hand on a US layout.

KeyboardAction
,Elevator up — pull the nose up
/Elevator down — push the nose down
.Flip the aircraft over
XAccelerate
ZDecelerate
SpaceMachine gun
BDrop a bomb
HTurn towards home
SSound on or off — the game starts with it off

Pulling up is a comma and pushing down is a slash, which feels backwards for about a minute and then stops mattering. The flip is what lets you fly level in the other direction without turning.

Tips

  • Speed is lift. Most crashes are stalls: hauling the nose up at a speed that will not hold it.
  • Bombs need a run-in. They keep your forward speed when you let them go, so release early and shallow rather than diving at the target.
  • The flip is not a turn. It reverses the aircraft without the loss of height a loop costs you.
  • The ground is the most dangerous thing in the game, and it never moves.
