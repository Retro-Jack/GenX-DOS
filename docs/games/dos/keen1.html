Commander Keen: Marooned on Mars

1990 — id Software, published by Apogee. Billy Blaze, eight years old, builds a spaceship in the garage and loses its parts to the Vorticons. Episode one of three.

System help — the IBM PC controls

The first smooth-scrolling platform game on the PC, and the reason the machine stopped being thought of as the place where console games went to be worse. Four parts of Keen's ship are scattered across Mars, held by Vorticons who kill him on contact. He has a pogo stick, a raygun with limited charges, and a map screen to choose where to go next. Episode one was given away, which is how most people met it.

Getting started

  1. You arrive on a map screen. Walk to a level entrance and press Ctrl to enter it.
  2. Find the four parts of the ship: the Joystick, the Battery, the Vacuum and the Whiskey.
  3. The raygun has limited charges, so most of the game is avoiding things rather than shooting them.
  4. The pogo stick reaches places ordinary jumping cannot, and takes practice.

Controls

From the game's manual. Ctrl and Alt are the two you need, and they are pressed together to fire.

Tips

  • The pogo is the real movement. Bouncing keeps height between hops and reaches ledges a jump will not; it is the difference between finishing the game and not.
  • Charges are scarce. Shoot what blocks the route, walk around everything else.
  • Contact kills. Keen has no health bar — touching a Vorticon is the end of the life.
  • The map lets you choose. A level going badly can be left for later.
  • Saving works but does not last. Nothing here survives the tab closing.
