Digger

1983 — Windmill Software. Tunnel through the earth for emeralds and gold, with Nobbins chasing you through your own tunnels and Hobbins chewing new ones of their own.

System help — the IBM PC controls

The IBM PC's own arcade game, and still the one people name first. You drive a digging machine through soil, cutting tunnels as you go, collecting emeralds and dropping bags of gold on the things that chase you. The Nobbins follow your tunnels; the Hobbins dig their own, which is much worse. It has four colours, a single-voice speaker playing Popcorn, and more life in it than most games with a thousand times the budget.

Getting started

  1. The game starts by itself and plays a demo. Press a key to take over.
  2. Collect every emerald, or destroy every monster, to clear the level.
  3. A bag of gold pushed off an edge falls, splits open, and can be collected — or lands on something and squashes it.

Tips

  • Gold is a weapon. Dig out the soil beneath a bag and it falls; anything underneath is finished. This kills more monsters than the gun does.
  • Eight emeralds in a row is worth 250. Plan the route so you take them in unbroken runs rather than wandering.
  • Hobbins are the problem. Nobbins are stuck in your tunnels and can be out-manoeuvred; a Hobbin cuts its own and will come straight at you.
  • The cherry turns the tables. Eat it and the chase reverses, with each monster caught worth double the last.
