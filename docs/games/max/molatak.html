Mole Attack

Commodore, 1982 — whack-a-mole arcade adaptation. Moles pop out of nine holes in a fixed grid; clobber them before they retreat. Two-player alternating mode for competitive whacking.

3×3 grid of mole-holes. Each round, moles emerge at increasing frequency from random holes; aim a hammer cursor over the active hole and strike. Hits score; missed swings or letting a mole disappear lose time. Score thresholds advance to faster rounds. Two players alternate turns under separate scores.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to begin.

Controls

Strategy

  • Move first, swing last. Cursor travel time is the bottleneck — don't pre-swing while in transit.
  • Centre rest position. Park the cursor on the centre hole between waves; minimum travel to all 8 outer holes.
  • Don't multi-tap. Each hit registers once; spamming fire while a mole's down wastes the buffer.
  • Late-round chains. Top difficulty has two moles up simultaneously — pick the one closer to your cursor first, the further one re-spawns predictably.
