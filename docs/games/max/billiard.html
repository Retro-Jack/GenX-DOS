Billiards

HAL, 1983 — top-down pool sim. Aim a cue ball at coloured balls, sink them in pockets, two-player alternating.

System help — the Commodore MAX Machine controls

Overhead view of a billiard table with six pockets. A cue ball sits where the previous shot left it; you rotate an aim line, set power, and stroke. Coloured balls scatter, ricochet off cushions, and (ideally) drop into pockets. Sinking the cue ball is a foul. Two players alternate turns until the rack is cleared.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to set up the rack.

Strategy

  • Soft shots leave position. Hammering every ball scatters the cue unpredictably; soft pots leave you set up for the next pocket.
  • Cushion math. The MAX physics are simple angle-of-incidence — a one-cushion bank shot is usually accurate enough to call.
  • Don't scratch. Sinking the cue gives the opponent ball-in-hand; calculate where the cue ends up before stroking hard.
  • Aim line is your friend. The visible aim line shows initial trajectory; trace mentally one cushion past the contact point.
