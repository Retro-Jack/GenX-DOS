Jupiter Lander

HAL, 1982 — Atari-style Lunar Lander port with Jupiter's gravity. Steer a thruster-equipped lander to one of three pads of escalating point value.

A side-view rocky terrain rolls past as you steer the lander left/right, thrust up against gravity, and descend toward landing pads at the bottom. Pads are valued 1× (large flat zone), 2× (medium platform), and 3× (narrow notch). Land at < safe-velocity and the lander credits you a fuel bonus + the multiplier; touch too hard or off-pad and you explode.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to release the lander from orbit.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Sideways thrust left / right
Numpad 8Main vertical thrust
Numpad 0 / 5Some builds: fire boost

Strategy

  • Fuel is finite. Every thrust costs fuel from the gauge; you cannot refuel mid-descent. Plan minimum-burn approaches.
  • Suicide burn at the end. Free-fall as long as you can; full vertical burn just before touchdown to null vertical velocity.
  • 3× pad demands precision. Lateral velocity must be near zero — a 3× landing on the narrow pad scores triple but a glancing approach explodes.
  • Restart on success. The game loops at higher difficulty — score-chase is the long game.
