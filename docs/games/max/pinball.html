Pinball Spectacular

HAL, 1983 — single-screen pinball with bumpers, drop targets, and a centre ramp. Push left/right flippers, keep the ball alive, chase the bonus multiplier.

Vertical playfield. Spring-launch the ball into a curved top lane, then keep it in play with two flippers at the bottom. Hitting bumpers, lit lanes, and drop-target banks scores; clearing target banks lights a bonus multiplier for the next ball. Three balls per game; tilt is disabled on this cart.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to load the first ball.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4Left flipper
Numpad 6Right flipper
Numpad 0 / 5Plunger (hold to pull, release to launch)

Strategy

  • Trap, don't flap. Catching the ball on a held flipper lets you aim a controlled shot — wild flips drain centre.
  • Drop targets first. Lighting the bonus multiplier early means every later bumper hit pays more.
  • Soft plunge to skill-shot. A short plunger pull sometimes drops the ball into a top scoring lane instead of the orbit.
  • Drain prevention. The MAX's flippers are short; nudging via fast double-flip is the only "shake the cabinet" trick available.
