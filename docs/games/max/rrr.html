Radar Rat Race

Commodore, 1982 — MAX cart edition of the VIC-20 launch title. A mouse navigating a single-screen maze collecting cheese while cats and a radar-tracking rat hunt it.

Top-down maze; the screen is the level (no scrolling). Collect every wedge of cheese to clear; cats patrol fixed routes; the rat homes on your radar position. Any contact loses a life. Cleared mazes ramp the patrol speed.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to spawn.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 8 2 4 6Move up / down / left / right
Numpad 0Drop cheese (decoy)

Strategy

  • Plan the sweep before moving. Cats are deterministic — pick a route that never crosses their fixed patrol paths.
  • Lead the rat into cats. The rat tracks you; cats ignore the rat. Set up the geometry to make them collide.
  • Decoys are scarce. Save them for the final wedge when a cat blocks the path.
  • Wrap-around tunnels. Left and right edges connect — last-resort escape when both pursuers close from the same side.
