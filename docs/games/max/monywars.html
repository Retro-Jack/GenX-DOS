Money Wars

Commodore, 1982 — Pac-Man-tier action with a financial-warfare reskin. Roam a maze collecting cash bags while dodging tax-collector agents. The MAX's odd entry in the corporate-greed-as-arcade genre.

Maze layout with cash bags scattered through corridors. Move to collect them while four "agents" patrol. Touch one = lose a life. Pick up a "bribe" power-up to briefly reverse the chase (agents become collectable for bonus points). Clear all cash to advance to the next, faster maze.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 8 2 4 6Move up / down / left / right
Numpad 0 / 5Drop decoy / use power-up (varies)

Strategy

  • Pre-buffer turns. Push the next direction before hitting the junction; the input queues one move.
  • Bribe timing matters. Hold the power-up until two or more agents are close — chain captures are the score driver.
  • Wrap-around tunnels. Side edges connect; emergency escape when cornered.
  • Last-bag rush. Agent speed ramps as cash count drops; leave corners for last and clear the centre while agents are still slow.
