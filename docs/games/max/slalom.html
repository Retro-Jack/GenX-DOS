Slalom

HAL, 1982 — top-down ski race. Steer between alternating red and blue gates without missing any, against a downhill course timer.

Vertical scrolling ski course. Pairs of gates (red on the left, blue on the right) flow up the screen; you steer your skier through the centre gap. Missing a gate adds a time penalty. Reach the finish line at the bottom for a course time; faster is better.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to start the run.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Steer left / right
Numpad 2Tuck (faster, less steering control)
Numpad 8Snowplough (slower, more steering control)

Strategy

  • Tuck the straights. Lock in speed between gates; only break tuck for sharp gate-to-gate transitions.
  • Snowplough the choke points. Some gate pairs come fast back-to-back; the snowplough lets you carve the second gate without overshooting.
  • Penalty math. A missed gate usually costs more time than the half-second saved by going wide — through every gate is the faster line.
  • Three difficulty courses. Beginner / Intermediate / Expert — different gate density and rhythm.
