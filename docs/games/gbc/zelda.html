Handheld · Nintendo Game Boy (1993)

The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening

The first portable Zelda and one of the Game Boy's crown jewels — a dream-logic adventure on Koholint Island, no Hyrule or Triforce in sight.

Shipwrecked Link must wake the Wind Fish by collecting the eight Instruments of the Sirens from eight dungeons. A full top-down Zelda — overworld exploration, item-gated dungeons, trading sidequest, fishing, and a famously bittersweet ending — all on a 1993 cartridge.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, press Enter (Start), create/select a save file, name your hero.
  2. Fetch your sword from the beach (south of the village), then head for the first dungeon.

Strategy

  • Assign wisely. Only two items fit on A/B at once — you'll juggle sword/shield/feather/powder constantly. Open START often.
  • The trading quest (Yoshi doll → … → magnifying lens) unlocks key progress; talk to everyone and chase each trade.
  • Roc's Feather + sword is the combo for most combat and gaps; the Pegasus Boots add a dash later.
  • Save anywhere via Shift → Save; battery saves persist in the browser. Use this emulator's save states before tricky bosses too. Five save slots per game — pick 1–5 from the save or load menu.
