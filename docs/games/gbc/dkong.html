Handheld · Nintendo Game Boy (1994)

Donkey Kong ("DK '94")

Starts as a remake of the 1981 arcade game, then explodes into 100+ puzzle-platform stages. One of the best Game Boy titles, and an early Super Game Boy showcase.

System help — the Nintendo Game Boy controls

After the four classic arcade boards, DK grabs Pauline and the real game begins: a sprawling puzzle-platformer where Mario has an acrobatic moveset — handstands, backflips, triple-jumps and the ability to pick up and throw enemies and keys. Each stage is a self-contained puzzle: grab the key, carry it to the locked door, reach Pauline.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, press Enter (Start) at the title.
  2. Clear the four arcade stages to unlock the 90+ puzzle levels.

Strategy

  • Learn the moveset. Handstand (press ↓+A together), backflip (while running, tap the opposite direction + A), and the handstand high-jump (press A again out of a handstand) open routes plain jumps can't reach.
  • The key resets. If you leave a key too long it teleports back to its start — plan the carry route before you grab it.
  • Throw enemies into others or off ledges to clear paths; thrown keys can be tossed across gaps.
  • Super Game Boy added colour borders originally — here this emulator gives the mono original a tidy palette.
