Handheld · Nintendo Game Boy Color (2002)

Shantae

WayForward's late, legendary GBC swansong — one of the best-looking games on the system, with huge animated sprites. A platform-adventure starring the half-genie Shantae.

Released right at the end of the GBC's life (and now hugely sought-after), Shantae is a Metroidvania-ish adventure across Sequin Land. Whip enemies with your hair, learn belly-dance transformations (monkey, elephant, harpy…) to traverse the world, and chase Risky Boots through towns, deserts and dungeons. A genuine technical showpiece for the colour Game Boy.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, press Enter (Start), begin a new game.
  2. Attack with Z (hair-whip); explore Scuttle Town and head out into Sequin Land.

Strategy

  • Day/night matters. The world changes between day and night (different enemies, shops, events) — warp-dance or sleep to shift it when stuck.
  • Learn the dances. Each transformation (monkey climb, elephant smash, harpy flight…) gates new areas — a classic ability-gated map.
  • Upgrade the whip and magic in town with gathered gems before tackling the dungeons.
  • Battery save persists in the browser; it's a meaty adventure, so save before long expeditions.
