Alpiner

Texas Instruments (Jim Dramis), 1982 — Solid State Software cart. Vertical-scrolling mountain-climbing action: scale six progressively-harder peaks while dodging the hazards each mountain throws at you. Synthesized voice taunts you between climbs ("You wimp!" "You klutz!" — the voice peripheral was a selling point).

System help — the TI-99/4A controls controls

Side-on climber. The mountain scrolls down; you climb up across rocky terrain dodging boulders, animals and locals (snakes on Mt Hood, bears on Mt McKinley, gorillas on Mt Kilimanjaro, etc.). Each summit unlocks the next peak. Reach the top of all six (Hood → McKinley → Garfield → Kenya → Kilimanjaro → Everest) and the voice declares "Master climber!"

Getting started

  1. Press 2 at the TI title.
  2. Choose 1-2 players, select a mountain to start on (you can skip ahead at the cost of bonus points).

Controls

KeyboardAction
↑ / ↓Climb up / down the mountain
← / →Move left / right to dodge hazards

Strategy

  • Sidestep, don't backtrack. Descending wastes the climb's altitude bonus; sidestep over hazards horizontally.
  • Each peak has a signature animal. Memorise the hazard pattern — snakes on Hood follow a lateral zigzag, gorillas on Kilimanjaro drop from above without warning.
  • Don't camp at saves. The hazards spawn faster the longer you linger on a screen; momentum kills the run.
  • Voice taunts cost time, not score. The TI Speech Synthesizer monologues add character — they don't dock you. Boot with it enabled if you can.
