Buck Rogers: Planet of Zoom

Sega / Texas Instruments, 1983 — Solid State Software cart. TI conversion of Sega's 1982 pseudo-3D arcade hit. Third-person rear view chasing the horizon: dodge slalom-pole bases and shoot incoming fighters across multiple stages set on different "Zoom" planet biomes.

System help — the TI-99/4A controls controls

Vanishing-point third-person view: your ship slides left/right at the bottom of the screen, the ground scrolls towards you. Stages alternate: low altitude (dodge tall obstacle poles), high altitude (dogfight enemy ships), tunnel (asteroid run). Reach the boss UFO at the end of each cycle.

Getting started

  1. Press 2 at the TI title.
  2. Press Space to launch from the title.

Controls

KeyboardAction
← / →Slide ship left / right
↑ / ↓Climb / dive (between altitude bands)
SpaceFire forward laser

Strategy

  • Read the road. Pole-base obstacles in the low-altitude segments appear small at the horizon — react to the pattern before they grow.
  • Climb dodges most. If a pole approaches with no lateral gap, climbing to the high-altitude band skips it entirely — at the cost of dropping back down to ground enemies later.
  • Boss ship dives. The end-of-stage UFO dives erratically; concentrate fire at where it's heading, not where it is.
  • TI port vs arcade. The arcade ran on Sega's 3D-tech rasteriser; the TI port simplifies to alternating colour bands but preserves the rhythm.
