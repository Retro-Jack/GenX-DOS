Car Wars

Texas Instruments, 1981 — Solid State Software cart. Top-down maze driver: navigate the city grid in your sportscar, dodging the relentless attack of a TANK that patrols the same map. Pure cat-and-mouse on a wall-bounded street network.

System help — the TI-99/4A controls controls

Top-down maze of city blocks. You drive a small red car along the street grid; somewhere on the grid a tank prowls in straight-line bursts, firing shells when it has line-of-sight on you. Collect the flags scattered through the map; the tank speeds up as you collect more. Higher levels add more tanks.

Getting started

  1. Press 2 at the TI title.
  2. Select skill 1-9; press Fire to start.

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Steer the car (turns take effect at intersections)
SpaceAccelerate (hold to speed up)

Strategy

  • Break line-of-sight. The tank only fires when your car is straight ahead of it; turn into a perpendicular street the moment you spot it down a corridor.
  • Flag-grab cadence. Every flag accelerates the tank; pick the next flag based on which corridor offers two perpendicular escape options.
  • Don't dead-end. The grid has T-junctions and dead ends; bait the tank into a dead-end, then U-turn — it can't reverse efficiently.
  • Low skill, more flags. Skill 1 has fewer tanks but the same flag count — a good place to learn the map before pushing higher.
