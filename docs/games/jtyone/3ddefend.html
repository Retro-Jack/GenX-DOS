3D Defender

New Generation, 1981 — M. E. Evans, the creator of 3D Monster Maze. A fast machine-code 3D cockpit shooter: yours is the last craft of a once-noble battle fleet, and you must defend the planet from marauding alien spacecraft before they land.

System help — the Sinclair ZX81 controls

You fly from the cockpit, steering a sight around a 3D sky. Line an alien craft up in your sights and loose your photon torpedoes before it lands — every alien that touches down costs you. A panel of instruments (radar, altimeter, proximity warning, shield, score) helps you hunt them down.

Getting started

  1. The briefing scrolls up first (3D Monster Maze style); press Enter to launch into the cockpit.
  2. Sweep your sight across the sky using the flight controls below; watch the radar and proximity warning for approaching craft.
  3. Fire a torpedo salvo just as an alien slides into your sights.

Controls — a keyboard "joystick"

The keyboard is flown like a joystick using four key zones. One zone alone nudges you a diagonal; press two zones together for a straight move. It's flight-style — the upper rows dive, the middle rows climb.

DirectionKeys (either zone works)
Climb (up)A–G and H–L
Dive (down)Q–T and Y–P
Left (port)Q–T and A–G
Right (starboard)Y–P and H–L
Diagonalany single zone key
Fire torpedoesany number-row key (one press looses a burst of up to 8)

A USB gamepad flies it directly — just push the stick and the craft goes that way (it presses the right pair of keys for you), and any face button fires. Rapid tapping isn't needed; a single press sends a full salvo.

Instruments

  • Forward Radar (×2): shows aliens and meteorites out to two screen widths ahead.
  • Altimeter: your height.
  • Proximity Warning: flags an approaching alien craft — its pointer moves faster as the craft closes.
  • Shield and Score.

Scoring & survival

  • +250 for each alien you destroy; −50 if an alien lands.
  • +50 for flying low enough to tempt a grounded alien back up to attack you.
  • Shield starts at 10 — it drops by 1 each time plasma or a meteorite hits you, and by 2 for a collision or crash landing.

Tactical hints

  • Lead your shots. Fire just before the alien enters your sights — the torpedoes take time to reach a distant craft.
  • Don't linger. Move away smartly after firing so a survivor can't line you up.
  • It ramps up. As your score climbs the sky fills — up to four aliens at once at the higher levels.
