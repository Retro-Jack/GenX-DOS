3D Grand Prix

1983 — first-person driving on the ZX81. The "3D" is the perspective road-trapezoid trick (forerunner of Pole Position on the Atari) rendered in block graphics.

You drive a single-seater along a perspective road. Other cars come at you (or are slower in front of you); steer to overtake, brake before tight corners, and complete laps within the timer. Top-rank a leaderboard at the end of the race.

Getting started

  1. Press 0 at the title to start.
  2. Your car starts at the bottom-centre. The road extends to the horizon.
  3. Accelerate, steer, brake. Each lap completes the timer; checkpoint flags extend the race.

Controls

This game supports a USB gamepad — left/right steers, up accelerates, down brakes (A/B mirror them).

Strategy

  • Brake before corners, not in them. ZX81 perspective rendering is choppy — by the time you see the corner clearly you're already in it.
  • Overtake on straights. Slow cars in front are easy targets; passing in a corner usually means hitting them.
  • The horizon line shifts to signal a coming corner — watch for the road-trapezoid skewing left or right before the bend itself appears.
  • Crash = restart from last checkpoint. Conservative pace beats reckless speed in this title.
