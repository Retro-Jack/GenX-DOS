Night Gunner

Digital Integration, 1983. WWII bomber-gunner sim: ride the tail of an Allied bomber over Europe, shooting incoming Luftwaffe night fighters from a first-person turret view.

You're the rear gunner of a heavy bomber on a night raid. The view is your turret port: stars scroll overhead, searchlights track from below, and Luftwaffe interceptors close from various angles to attack the formation. Your job is simple — aim and fire. Survive the mission and you score by enemies downed.

Getting started

  1. Press a key at the title to start the mission.
  2. The view shows your turret cross-hair centred. Enemy fighter silhouettes approach from the screen edges.
  3. Move the cross-hair onto the silhouette; fire short bursts.
  4. The mission ends with a return-to-base sequence — bonus score for delivery without bomber damage.

Controls

This game supports a USB gamepad — the D-pad or stick moves the sight up/down and traverses left/right, and any face button fires, which also starts the mission at the title.

KeyAction
Top row (1–0)Move the sight up
3rd row (A–L)Move the sight down
2nd row, left half (Q–T)Traverse left
2nd row, right half (Y–P)Traverse right
Space (or Caps Shift)Fire guns / release bombs

Strategy

  • Lead the fighter. Don't aim where they are; aim where they'll be. Burst-fire spreads improve hit probability.
  • Conserve ammo. The turret has a per-mission belt; spray-fire empties it before the second wave.
  • Searchlights help and hurt. Lit silhouettes are easier to track, but the same searchlight tells AAA where your bomber is.
  • Centre-priority targets. Closer fighters threaten the formation; distant ones may peel off before reaching you.
