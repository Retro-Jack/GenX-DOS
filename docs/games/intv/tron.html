Console · Mattel Intellivision

TRON: Deadly Discs

Mattel, 1981 — Steve Roney. The first home video game based on the Tron film.

System help — the Intellivision controls

Tron stands at the centre of an arena floor; identity-disc warriors flicker in from the four door-portals at the corners and try to ring him with throws. Spin to face each one, throw your own disc, and catch the return — discs ricochet around the arena until they hit something. Three doorways close as you score; clear all four for a bonus round; survive the recogniser later.

Getting started

  1. The arena floor is a single screen with four doors at the corners.
  2. Warriors enter and circle Tron, throwing discs at him.
  3. Throw your disc back. Catch the rebound. Survive.

Controls

The Intellivision had no joystick: a 16-direction disc, four side buttons and a 12-key pad. Each row names the original control it stands for. This emulator is keyboard-only — a USB gamepad is not wired to it.

KeyboardAction
↑ ↓ ← →Move Tron around the arena — Disc (16 dir)
CtrlThrow disc up-left — Upper-left button
AltThrow disc up-right — Upper-right button
SpaceThrow disc forward (direction Tron is facing) — Lower side button

Strategy

  • Face before throwing. Move briefly in the direction of an enemy first — Tron's facing matters for the lower fire button.
  • Catch returns. The disc rebounds off the arena walls; positioning to catch it on the return is essential to staying alive.
  • Watch the doorways. Each closed doorway means one fewer spawn point. Closing all four triggers a bonus.
  • Recogniser later. After several rounds the floor-walking Recogniser appears — it must be hit on the legs (high or low pass through), not the body.

Intellivision controls

The unique mapping: the two upper side buttons throw discs diagonally (up-left, up-right) — useful for warriors approaching from above. The lower button(s) throw in your facing direction. This is one of the only carts that uses all three side buttons distinctly.
