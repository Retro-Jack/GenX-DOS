Star Strike

Mattel, 1981 — APh Technological. The Intellivision's answer to the Star Wars Death Star trench run.

A first-person targeting view from the cockpit of a fighter on a strike run. You're flying down a corridor lined with anti-aircraft turrets toward a row of "thermo nuclear missile silos". Hit each of the 5 active silos with a missile from your finite stock; once they're all down, escape before the planet detonates. Anti-aircraft fire hits decrement your shields.

Getting started

  1. Pick a game variant at the title.
  2. Your fighter flies forward on rails; the disc only moves your cursor across the cockpit canopy.
  3. Five silos to destroy; ammunition is limited; shields drain on hits.

Strategy

  • Pick silos first, turrets second. Each silo destroyed is mission progress. Turrets are infinite — only kill the ones firing at you.
  • Lead your shots. The corridor scrolls forward — aim at where a silo will be when your missile arrives, not where it is now.
  • Watch your ammo. Missiles are limited; turrets need only laser fire (free) where possible.
  • The escape after the fifth silo is the trick. Pulling out is timed — keep the cursor centred and don't waste shots.

Intellivision controls

The disc steers the cursor (cockpit-relative); the side button fires whatever weapon is selected at the current target. The keypad overlay distinguishes laser from missile but in practice the cart auto-selects appropriately.
