Console · Magnavox Odyssey²

Atlantis

Imagic, 1982 — Dennis Koble (port of his Atari 2600 original)

System help — the Odyssey² controls

The city of Atlantis sits at the bottom of the screen, guarded by two indestructible sentry posts — one on each side — that fire up into a sky busy with alien Gorgon ships. Each ship type moves at a different speed and altitude; let one reach the lowest band and its deathray drops to level one of the city's six installations. The Domed Palace's force field shields the other four until the dome and palace themselves are destroyed. The game ends when all six sections are levelled.

Getting started

  1. Press a number at the title to choose a variant. Game 1 is the standard speed; later variants ramp the alien speed and add bonus point thresholds.
  2. You command the two sentry posts. Lean the joystick left or right to select a post — it stays selected after you release the stick — then press the action button to fire it.
  3. Hold a Blitz Bomb in reserve: you get one per wave, and it clears the whole sky at once.

Controls

Each row names the control it stands for on the original console. This emulator is keyboard-only — a USB gamepad is not wired to it.

Strategy

  • Prioritise low ships. A bandit-bomber on the bottom band will level an installation this turn — select the nearer post and fire on it before it drops.
  • Pre-select your post. The selected post stays selected after you release the stick, so line targets up on the side you're already aimed at and just tap fire.
  • Save the Blitz Bomb. One per wave — hold it until the sky is crowded or a deathray is about to land, then push forward to wipe the screen.
  • Watch the city. Once all six sections are levelled it's game over, even if you've got plenty of fight left.

Odyssey² controls

Your two sentry posts are fixed — there's no "moving." Lean the joystick left or right to choose which post is active, press the action button (Space) to fire it, and push the joystick forward (↑) to unleash the once-per-wave Blitz Bomb.
