Demon Attack

Imagic, 1982 — Rob Fulop (port of his Atari 2600 original)

Waves of winged demons descend from the icy moon of Krybor. You command a fixed laser cannon at the bottom of the screen, sliding left and right and firing upward. Each wave changes the demons' attack pattern — diving, splitting, or strafing you with energy bombs.

Getting started

  1. Press a number at the title to pick a difficulty.
  2. You start with three lives. Each demon shot or collision costs one life.
  3. Clear a wave to advance; demon types and patterns vary each wave.

Demon types

DemonBehaviour
Stationary squadHover at top, drop bombs. Easy targets but high volume.
Diving demonDrops vertically. Must be tracked.
SplitterHit a splitter and it divides into two faster demons. Time your shots.
StraferSweeps horizontally, firing as it passes.

Strategy

  • One shot at a time. You can only have one laser bolt on screen. Don't fire blindly — wait for a clear shot.
  • Don't shoot splitters early. A splitter dives slowly until shot, then becomes two fast targets. Pick them off when you've cleared the easier demons.
  • Dodge horizontally. Bombs fall straight down — a small slide is enough to avoid most of them.
  • Stay near the centre. The edges leave you no room to dodge a diving demon coming at you from the inside.

Odyssey² controls

Joystick left/right + action key to fire. The on-screen "score" digits are the row of small demons at the top during play — destroying them earns bonus points.
