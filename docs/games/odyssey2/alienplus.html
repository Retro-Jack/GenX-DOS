Console · Magnavox Odyssey²

Alien Invaders - Plus!

Magnavox, 1980 — Ed Averett

System help — the Odyssey² controls

A Space Invaders variant on the Odyssey²: rows of aliens drift sideways and slowly descend while you sweep left and right at the bottom of the screen, firing up. The "Plus" twist is that the front-row aliens — the foot soldiers — periodically swap with one of three mutated commander aliens behind them, who fire back in unpredictable patterns.

Getting started

  1. Select 1- or 2-player at the title screen with the number keys.
  2. Each game is a single wave. Wipe all aliens to win; lose all your shields to lose.
  3. Three shield blocks above you absorb enemy fire — they erode with each hit.

Controls

Each row names the control it stands for on the original console. This emulator is keyboard-only — a USB gamepad is not wired to it.

Strategy

  • Pick off commanders. The three commanders fire back; the foot soldiers don't. Killing a commander makes the wave much safer.
  • Don't camp under your shields. They erode fast under sustained fire. Drift out, shoot, slip back.
  • Edges are safe corridors. The far left and right columns rarely take fire — use them to reposition.
  • Watch the swap. When a commander swaps forward, it changes the firing pattern — re-read the screen before committing to a move.

Odyssey² controls

Joystick left/right slides the gun; the action key fires. The aliens move on a timer regardless of your input — don't wait too long.
