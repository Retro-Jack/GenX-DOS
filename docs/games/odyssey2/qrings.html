Console · Magnavox Odyssey²

Quest for the Rings!

Magnavox, 1981 — Master Strategy Series. Shipped with a fold-out board, plastic monster pieces, and a printed map — the cart provides the action sequences.

System help — the Odyssey² controls

A two-player co-op action-adventure: one player picks Warrior, Wizard, Phantom, or Changeling; the other picks a complementary class. The fold-out board tracks your party's overland progress across the realm of Ringaria, while the cart's action mode handles individual dungeon room encounters — fight monsters, dodge traps, and recover the ten Rings of Power.

Getting started

  1. The fold-out board (originally cardboard, here imagined) drives the metagame. Each player rolls or moves between rooms.
  2. When the board calls for an action sequence, the cart shows the room with monsters drawn from the plastic-piece set.
  3. Players fight monsters until they're cleared or the party retreats. Then back to the board to advance.

Strategy

  • Class composition matters. A Warrior + Wizard is the classic pairing — short-range power with long-range support. A Phantom + Changeling team is stealthier but slower.
  • Watch the monster pieces. Each monster type has its own attack pattern — Trolls charge, Snakes block corridors, Wraiths home in.
  • Retreating is a valid move. If a room overwhelms you, exit before losing both players — the board lets you regroup.
  • This is a co-op game. Without a second player on the second joystick, you're missing half the game.

Odyssey² controls

Both joysticks are used — assign player 2 a controller for the intended co-op experience. The cart's action mode is one piece of a larger board-game system; without the physical board, the metagame structure is improvised.
