Console · Magnavox Odyssey²

Smithereens!

Magnavox, 1982 — Ed Averett. Designed for The Voice peripheral; works without it but the announcer goes silent.

System help — the Odyssey² controls

Two opposing castles on either side of a moat. Each player commands a catapult and an archer; the goal is to lob stones into the other castle and reduce it to rubble before yours falls. The wind blows from left to right at variable speed — you adjust catapult angle to compensate. With The Voice plugged in, a chuckling announcer narrates the carnage.

Getting started

  1. Pick a game variant at the title. Game 1 is one-player vs. the computer; game 2 is two-player; later variants add wind and archer mechanics.
  2. Each castle has multiple wall blocks; chip them away to expose the opponent inside.
  3. Wind direction and speed is shown by a wind indicator at the top — heavy left-to-right means you'll need a steeper shot if you're on the left.

Two-player mode

Player 2 uses joystick 2 with the same controls. In 1-player mode, the computer controls the opposite castle and adjusts difficulty across the game variants.

Strategy

  • Read the wind. The wind indicator is the single most important thing on the screen — a strong tailwind doubles your range.
  • Aim for the top wall. Knocking out the top blocks exposes the archer inside; a body shot ends the game.
  • Don't waste shots on the moat. If the wind is wrong, hold fire and wait for it to shift.
  • Two-player is the intended game. The computer opponent is fine for practice but the design centres on direct human-vs-human aiming duels.

Odyssey² controls

Joystick up/down for angle + action key to fire. The wind randomly reverses between shots — re-check the indicator every time before firing.
