Console · Magnavox Odyssey²

Cosmic Conflict!

Magnavox, 1979 — one of the eight launch titles

System help — the Odyssey² controls

A first-person view from the cockpit of your starship: enemy fighters streak across a scrolling star field, and the radar to your right tracks each one's position relative to you. Centre an enemy in the crosshair, fire, and survive long enough to destroy them all.

Getting started

  1. Press 1 or 2 at the title screen to choose a game variant.
  2. Game 1: one enemy at a time. Game 2: more aggressive enemies. Both end when your shields run out.
  3. The radar (right side of the screen) shows enemies as moving dots. Rotate your ship to bring them into the front viewport.

Controls

Each row names the control it stands for on the original console. This emulator is keyboard-only — a USB gamepad is not wired to it.

← →Yaw left / right — rotate the ship to track enemies on the radar — Joystick

Strategy

  • Watch the radar first. Enemies are easy to miss in the viewport — the radar tells you where to rotate.
  • Don't over-correct. The ship turns smoothly; small taps work better than holding direction.
  • Lasers travel fast but aren't homing. Lead moving targets just slightly.
  • Game 2 is brutal. Enemies fire back and close fast — keep moving and pick targets you can finish quickly.

Odyssey² controls

Each Odyssey² controller has an 8-direction joystick and a single action key. The on-screen keyboard handles game selection at the title screen.
