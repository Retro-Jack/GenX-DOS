K.C. Munchkin!

Magnavox, 1981 — Ed Averett. The Pac-Man-alike that Atari sued over and won — pulled from shelves the year after release.

Steer K.C. (Kid Carrot) around a maze, eating the twelve "munchies" that drift along the corridors. Three colour-coded ghosts chase you. Unlike Pac-Man, the munchies move, the dots aren't pre-placed, and the maze has a designable layout — Magnavox included a maze editor in the cart.

Getting started

  1. Press a number at the title screen to pick a maze (1–3 are preset; 4 is the editable maze).
  2. The 12 munchies start scattered. They drift around the maze and pass each other freely.
  3. Catch all 12 to clear the maze. Ghosts speed up as you eat more.

Maze rules

  • Munchies move — they don't sit waiting. Plan a route that intercepts their drift.
  • The centre cage holds an energiser munchy. Eat it to turn the tables on the ghosts briefly — they flash and can be eaten for bonus points.
  • The wraparound tunnel at the maze edges teleports K.C. (and the ghosts) to the other side.
  • The last munchy gets fast. When only one is left, it accelerates and changes direction more often — chase strategically.

Strategy

  • Eat clusters, not singles. Munchies that drift close together can be picked off in a single sweep — don't chase isolated ones first.
  • Use the centre for the energiser, not as a hideout. The cage is a dead-end with two exits ghosts know how to camp.
  • Ghosts don't reverse. If one is following you down a corridor, a sharp turn into a side branch usually breaks the pursuit.

Odyssey² controls

Joystick only — no action button needed for play. Use the on-screen keyboard to pick a maze at the title.
