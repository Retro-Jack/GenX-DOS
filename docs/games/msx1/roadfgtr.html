Road Fighter

Konami, 1985 — top-down vertical-scrolling arcade racer. Drive a constantly-accelerating Formula car along four stages of public highway, weaving past traffic and dodging oil slicks. The grandfather of OutRun-style "don't hit anything" runners.

No laps, no opponents — just you, the road, and a fuel/distance bar. Two gears (lo / hi) decide top speed; high gear is faster but also harder to thread through traffic without scraping a wall, which costs speed but never lives outright. Hit a car or a slick at top speed: spin, lose chunks of fuel. Reach the goal before fuel hits zero.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space to start; engine ignites immediately.

Strategy

  • Drop to lo-gear before any congestion. Traffic clumps appear at the same screen positions each stage; learn them and downshift two beats early.
  • Walls are not free. Scraping the barrier costs forward momentum and you lose fuel proportional to speed; better than a crash, worse than a clean line.
  • Oil slicks survive one nudge. A glancing tyre over a slick spins you slightly but recoverably; centre-hit at high gear, you're sideways.
  • Bonus car = perfect stage. Clear a stage without hitting a single car or slick and the game awards a bonus 1UP-equivalent.
