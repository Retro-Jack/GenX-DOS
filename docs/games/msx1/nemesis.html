Nemesis

Konami, 1986 — MSX conversion of the Gradius arcade. Vic Viper fights the Bacterion empire through seven horizontally-scrolling stages, building up the famous power-up bar one capsule at a time. The MSX port adds two MSX-exclusive levels not in the arcade original.

System help — the MSX1 controls

Side-scrolling shoot-'em-up with the Gradius power-up bar: shoot orange enemies, collect the capsule they leave behind, that capsule advances a highlight along the bar (Speed → Missile → Double → Laser → Option → ?). Press fire-2 when the highlight is on the upgrade you want. Death sends you back to a checkpoint stripped of upgrades.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title to start.

Strategy

  • Speed → Missile → Double → Laser → Option. The standard MSX1 Nemesis build order: two Speed-ups, then Missile, then Double, then Laser, then two Options. Anything else is an experiment.
  • Don't over-Speed. Three Speeds and you're faster than reflexes — Vic Viper out-runs your own dodges. Two is the sweet spot.
  • Options are the entire game. Once you have two trailing Options, place them above and below Vic Viper; they fire what you fire, tripling your DPS.
  • Checkpoint deaths cascade. Dying after a checkpoint with no power-ups against a swarm usually means death-loop — bomb back to a previous safe spot, farm capsules from the recurring orange enemies.
