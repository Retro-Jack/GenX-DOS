Knightmare

Konami, 1986 — vertical-scrolling fantasy shoot-'em-up. Popolon flies upward through medieval Greek-mythos stages firing arrows at chimerae, gorgons and end-stage gods. The first game in Konami's "Majou Densetsu" trilogy and a candidate for the best MSX1 shooter.

Top-down vertical shooter wearing a fantasy skin: stone walls instead of asteroids, harpies instead of TIE fighters, a Greek-temple boss at every stage end. Power-ups appear from killed sprites and named enemy groups — collect them by name (cross, sword, jewel, etc.) for arrow-spread, speed, shields, sub-weapons.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title.

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Move Popolon in eight directions
SpaceFire forward arrow
L AltSub-weapon (when held) — chain whip, fireball, etc.

Strategy

  • Spread arrow first. Earliest letter-power-up to grab is "A" (arrows) — boosts to triple-spread. Skip the first speed-up if it appears before A; Popolon's base speed is already controllable.
  • Save the cross. "P" gives invulnerability for a short window — hold it and ram a boss for guaranteed damage.
  • Walls don't kill, scraping does. You can hug temple walls for cover, but harpies dive from off-screen along the columns — never park.
  • Boss patterns are scripted. Each temple-end boss runs the same attack loop; survive one full loop without firing to memorise the gaps, then unload.
