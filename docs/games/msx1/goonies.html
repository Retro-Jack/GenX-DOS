The Goonies

Konami, 1986 — single-screen action-puzzle tie-in to Richard Donner's film. Play as Mikey across five stages of the Fratelli pirate caves, rescuing the other Goonies one per level. Side-scrolling platforming wrapped in single-screen room puzzles.

System help — the MSX1 controls

Each stage is a multi-room cave: rooms connect via stage-edge exits and hidden passages. Find the captured Goonie, then escape via the marked exit. Hazards include skull-helmet henchmen, bats, falling rocks, water columns. Items litter the rooms — key, bomb, slingshot — solving the room puzzle usually requires the right one.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title to start Stage 1.

Controls

Strategy

  • Bombs open hidden walls. Each stage has at least one false wall — drop a bomb where the brick pattern subtly differs.
  • Water columns are timing. The geyser pulse is fixed; cross at the lull.
  • Henchmen drop items. Skull-helmets occasionally drop the key you need for that stage; clear them rather than dodging.
  • Different MSX vs NES build. If you remember the Famicom Goonies, the MSX version's layout is wholly different — single-screen rooms instead of side-scrolling stages.
