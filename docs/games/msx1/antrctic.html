Antarctic Adventure

Konami, 1983 — pseudo-3D auto-scrolling penguin runner. Race Penta around a circuit of Antarctic research bases against the clock, hopping seal holes and ice cracks. One of the earliest MSX launch titles and the genre template for Konami's "Penguin Adventure" follow-up.

Diagonal third-person view, ice-blue horizon, scrolling Antarctic flag-pole obstacle course. You guide a penguin running toward each of seven Antarctic stations, jumping seal-blown ice holes and crevasses, and grabbing the occasional flying fish for bonus points. Time runs against you between bases — clear all seven for the loop.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space to start; the run begins immediately.

Controls

KeyAction
← / →Steer Penta left / right across the ice
SpaceJump (over holes, cracks, seals)

Strategy

  • Jump on the upbeat. Holes telegraph as a dark patch about a beat before they reach Penta — jump as the leading edge hits the lower third of the screen.
  • Don't hold left/right. Tap and release; Penta drifts naturally back toward centre. Holding sends you straight into a flagpole.
  • Flying fish are bait-and-switch. A fish low over the ice is bonus points; a fish high overhead means an albatross drop is coming — duck centre-screen and don't jump.
  • Stations beat seals. Reaching a base resets the clock generously; if low on time, don't farm bonus fish — straight-line the run.
