Athletic Land

Konami, 1984 — side-scrolling obstacle-course platformer. Sprint right across a park stitched out of swimming pools, vines, ladders, drum kits and brick walls; one life, one timer per stage.

Earliest of Konami's MSX-cart classics. The hero auto-runs at a brisk jog; you steer the jumps. Every stage is a single horizontal layer of hazards — pools you cross by Tarzan-swinging on dangling vines, ladders that climb to drum-kit platforms, brick walls cleared with running jumps. Touch water or fall in a hole, lose a life.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title to begin Stage 1.

Controls

KeyAction
← / →Run left / right
↑ / ↓Climb ladders / vines
SpaceJump (length depends on run-up)

Strategy

  • Vines are timed pendulums. Grab the vine at the bottom of its swing for max forward distance; grab early and you swing short and drop into the pool.
  • Roll the ladder. When climbing, don't stop at the top — keep ↑ held; the auto-walk continues across the platform without a beat lost.
  • Bricks reward the long jump. Take a brick wall from a full run; standing jumps clear short walls only.
  • Drum platforms bounce. Land squarely and the drum throws you higher than a normal jump — useful for over-tall vines.
