Pippols

Konami, 1985 — horizontal-scrolling fantasy platformer. A pixie-sized hero hops his way across pastel landscapes toward a princess. Sprite-charm Konami at full strength, with one of the most-quoted soundtracks of the early MSX era.

Auto-scrolling-ish side platformer with a slight backtrack tolerance. The hero collects letters that spell a word per stage — gather all letters to ring a bell and clear. Enemies pop out of clouds, mushrooms, hills; one touch is a life. Picking up power-up boots lets you fire small projectiles.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title to start the first stage.

Controls

KeyAction
← / →Walk / run
SpaceJump (longer hop with running speed)
↑ (after boots)Fire projectile upward

Strategy

  • Bell rings only after every letter. Skip one letter and you can't clear the stage; backtrack the moment you notice a gap.
  • Boots are mid-stage spawns. Once you've got them, projectile-tap upward against cloud spawners — they're the most common death.
  • Mushroom-jumps are runways. Bounce off red mushrooms for double-height jumps; they recharge a beat after each use.
  • Don't outrun the scroll. The screen catches up; pushing too far right before the camera drifts can wedge you in an unwinnable corner.
