Console · GCE Vectrex

Star Trek: The Motion Picture

GCE, 1982 — branded port of Sega's 1983 vector arcade "Star Trek: Strategic Operations Simulator." The Vectrex version takes its name and graphics from the 1979 film.

You command the Enterprise. Each sector contains Klingons, starbases, and the central command base. Klingons attack from all sides; shoot them with phasers (forward) and photon torpedoes (in any direction). Refuel and repair at starbases. Survive sector after sector, racking up score.

Getting started

  1. Wait for the Vectrex BIOS intro.
  2. Press a button at the title to start.
  3. Rotate and thrust the Enterprise; fire phasers with A, torpedoes with S.

Controls

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

Strategy

  • Refuel at starbases. Approach the starbase ring; touching it refuels and repairs. Don't crash into the central command.
  • Phasers vs. torpedoes. Phasers are unlimited but forward-only. Torpedoes are scarce but multi-directional — save for Klingons behind you.
  • Don't ram. Touching a Klingon or asteroid = damage. The Enterprise is fragile compared to your tactical needs.
  • Warp away. Button 4 warps you to the next sector — use when surrounded with no fuel.

Vectrex controls

Stick + buttons 1, 2, 4. The plastic overlay (shown via the Overlay button in this emulator) maps colours to each button's function.
