Console · GCE Vectrex

Berzerk

GCE, 1982 — vector port of the 1980 Stern arcade by Alan McNeil. Robots, mazes, electric walls, and Evil Otto.

A humanoid (you) is trapped in a maze. Robots roam each room shooting at you; touch a robot, an electric wall, or a robot bullet — instant death. Shoot all robots in a room for bonus points, or run to an exit. Evil Otto, the indestructible bouncing smiley, appears if you dawdle; he passes through walls and robots alike.

Getting started

  1. Wait for the Vectrex BIOS intro to finish (~4 seconds).
  2. Press a button at the title to start.
  3. Move with arrows; shoot in the direction of last movement with A.

Controls

A USB gamepad works, and this is one of the few machines here where the left stick is the right control rather than the D-pad: the Vectrex controller was genuinely analogue in 1982, and the stick drives those same axes — how far you push it matters, not just which way. The four face buttons are the Vectrex's four: X is button 1, Y is 2, A is 3 and B is 4. The D-pad only stands in on pads that have no stick at all. A gamepad only works in Firefox; Chromium browsers will not hand one to this page.

Strategy

  • "Face" your shot. Berzerk shoots in your last-moved direction. Stop-aim-fire — moving and firing at once fires in your travel direction.
  • Lure robots into walls. Robots aren't smart; lead them along corridors so they touch the electrified walls. You score nothing, but you save bullets.
  • Don't camp. Evil Otto arrives if you stay in a room too long. He cannot be killed, only outrun.
  • Diagonal travel for room exit. Corner-to-corner runs minimise time in any room. Trade kills for door distance.
  • No taunts here. The arcade machine's speech — "Chicken! Fight like a robot!" — needed a dedicated speech board, and this port has none of it. The robots come at you in silence.

Vectrex controls

Stick to move, F (Button 4) to fire. Wait for the intro tune, then press a button to begin.
