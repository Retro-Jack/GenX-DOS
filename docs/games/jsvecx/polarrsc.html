Console · GCE Vectrex

Polar Rescue

GCE, 1983 — original Vectrex submarine combat sim by Tom Sloper. Stalk torpedoes through Arctic waters; rescue trapped men from a sunken pod.

A multi-screen submarine adventure. Navigate the Arctic on a top-down sonar map, dive into side-view torpedo combat against enemy subs, and reach the rescue pod. Limited fuel and torpedoes. The Vectrex's vector graphics show sonar pings, periscope sweeps, and torpedo arcs — atmospheric for 1983.

Getting started

  1. Wait for the Vectrex BIOS intro.
  2. Press a button at the title to start.
  3. Navigate on the sonar map; combat switches to a side-view dive when you encounter an enemy.

Strategy

  • Periscope first. On the map, periscope (button 3) reveals nearby contacts before committing to a dive.
  • Torpedo conservation. Each torpedo counts. Don't fire at long-range targets — wait for a close pass.
  • Fuel = time. Every action burns fuel. Direct course to the rescue pod; engage only essential targets.
  • Rescue pod marker. Sonar pings periodically reveal the pod's location. Get there before fuel runs out.

Vectrex controls

All four buttons in use. The plastic overlay labels each one — this emulator's Overlay button toggles the period-correct overlay graphic.
