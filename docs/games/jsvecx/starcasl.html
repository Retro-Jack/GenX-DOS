Star Castle

GCE, 1983 — vector port of Cinematronics' 1980 arcade by Tim Skelly. Three concentric rotating shield rings around a deadly central cannon.

Three colour-coded shield rings rotate around a central cannon. Shoot holes through each ring's segments; once a hole spans all three rings AND aligns with the cannon, blast the cannon for points. Mines emerge from the centre to track you; rings respawn segments between volleys.

Getting started

  1. Wait for the Vectrex BIOS intro.
  2. Press a button at the title to start.
  3. Rotate ship, thrust, fire at ring segments.

Strategy

  • Triple-aligned hole. The cannon can only be shot through a hole that lines up across all three rings AND across your current angle. Don't fire wildly — chase one alignment.
  • Rings rotate at different speeds. The outer is slowest, inner fastest. Patience: a hole that closes a fraction of a second after you shoot was wasted.
  • Don't chase mines. Mines are killable but worth less time than ring progress. Dodge them with thrust.
  • Keep circling the rings. Star Castle has no hyperspace — when boxed in, rotate and thrust clear before the ring closes on you.
  • Asteroids playstyle. Inertia is forever; tap thrust, don't hold.

Vectrex controls

Stick (or Buttons 1/2) rotates, Button 3 thrusts, Button 4 fires. The Vectrex port preserves the arcade's three-ring geometry exactly.
