Console · GCE Vectrex

Mine Storm

GCE, 1982 — the Vectrex's built-in pack-in game by John Hall. An Asteroids variant with mine types and a bug that hangs the console at wave 13 (the famous "Mine Storm bug").

System help — the Vectrex controls

Your ship faces waves of mines. Some explode into smaller mines on contact (Floating Mines), some chase you (Magnetic), some shoot back (Fireball), some teleport (Magnetic Fireball). The Mine Layer drops new mines mid-wave. Clear the wave to advance. Wave 13 famously crashes the original ROM — replaced by Mine Storm II for refunders.

Getting started

  1. Wait for the Vectrex BIOS intro.
  2. Press a button to begin (Button 1 switches to a 2-player game).
  3. Rotate, thrust, fire. Same fundamentals as Asteroids.

Controls

A USB gamepad works, and this is one of the few machines here where the left stick is the right control rather than the D-pad: the Vectrex controller was genuinely analogue in 1982, and the stick drives those same axes — how far you push it matters, not just which way. The four face buttons are the Vectrex's four: X is button 1, Y is 2, A is 3 and B is 4. The D-pad only stands in on pads that have no stick at all.

Mine types

MineBehaviour
Floating MineDrifts. Splits into smaller mines when hit.
Magnetic MineSlowly tracks your ship.

Mine LayerDrops new mines mid-wave. Kill it fast.
FireballStationary but spits aimed shots.

Strategy

  • Hunt the Mine Layer. If a Mine Layer enters the wave, it'll drop indefinite mines. Drop everything to kill it.
  • Magnetic > Floating priority. Magnetic mines move towards you; Floating drift randomly. Kill chasers first.
  • Stay centred. Wraparound makes hugging the edge dangerous — mines wrap too. Centre dance with brief thrusts.
  • Escape = panic. Button 2 teleports you to a random new spot — last resort, much like Asteroids hyperspace.
  • Wave 13 bug. The original Mine Storm ROM has an infinite-loop bug at wave 13. GCE shipped Mine Storm II cartridges as a free replacement. This emulator's built-in might or might not patch this.

Vectrex controls

Mine Storm is the only game that ships built into the Vectrex BIOS — no cartridge required. Power-on with no cart loaded boots straight into Mine Storm's title screen.
