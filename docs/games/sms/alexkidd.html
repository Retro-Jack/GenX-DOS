Console · Sega Master System

Alex Kidd in Miracle World

Sega, 1986. The Master System's mascot adventure: a Shellcore master in red overalls punches his way across eleven locations of planet Aries to stop Janken the Great.

System help — the Sega Master System controls

Part platformer, part shopping trip, part rock-paper-scissors tournament. Alex punches blocks apart, collects Baum coins from money bags, buys vehicles and tricks in the shops, and settles boss fights the planet Janbarik way — with Janken, the scissors-paper-stone game the villain is literally named after.

Getting started

  1. At the title, press Z (Button 1) to start — or, on a USB pad, the Start button (the SMS had no Start of its own, so the pad's Start fires Button 1).
  2. Punch the colour-odd blocks apart to move through each area; one block colour per location can't be broken (the manual's tip: punch with X to find out which).
  3. Collect the money bags (10U and 20U) and spend them at shops — jump up and touch an item to buy it.

Janken matches — the boss fights

  • Janken's lieutenants challenge you to best-of-three scissors / paper / stone. Point the white arrow with the arrows while the music plays; the round resolves when it stops.
  • Lose two and Alex turns to stone — that's a life. Win two and you move on.
  • Buy the Telepathy Ball when you see one — it shows what your opponent is thinking, which turns the gamble into a formality. (The patterns also never change between games — a notebook works too.)

Strategy

  • Shop early, shop often. The Power Bracelet (100U) fires Shellcore shockwaves but vanishes when you lose a life or change location; Magic Capsule B (120U) is a protective barrier; an extra life is 500U.
  • Boxes aren't all friendly. Money bags and extra lives share box-space with ghosts and skull jitters — the manual's advice on the pink skull box: just jump over it.
  • Nothing bought works underwater, bracelet included. Swim on your own merits.
