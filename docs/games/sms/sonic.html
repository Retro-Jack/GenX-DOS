Console · Sega Master System

Sonic the Hedgehog

Sega / Ancient, 1991. The 8-bit Sonic — not a port but its own game: slower, sneakier, and hiding all six Chaos Emeralds inside the levels themselves.

The Master System's Sonic trades raw speed for exploration. Six zones of three acts, Robotnik at the end of each, and no special stages — the Chaos Emeralds are tucked into the level geometry, waiting for players who look up, down and behind the waterfalls. The ring rules are stricter too: once hit, your scattered rings are gone for good.

Getting started

  1. Press Z or X at the title to start.
  2. Hold rings — one hit without them is a life. There's no re-scooping them here after a hit.
  3. Finish an act with 50+ rings and the pinball bonus stage opens — rings and continues live there.

Strategy

  • Emerald-hunt as you go. All six are in the ordinary acts — ledges above the ceiling line, alcoves under bridges. Missing them costs you the good ending.
  • Treat rings as armour you can't reload. No recollecting after a hit — so bank an act's rings into the 50+ bonus-stage threshold before taking risks.
  • Ten minutes an act. The timer is generous but real — exploration has a deadline.
  • It's an 8-bit original, not a downport. Different zones, different Robotnik fights — knowledge of the Mega Drive game helps less than you'd think.
