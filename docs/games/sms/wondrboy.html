Console · Sega Master System

Wonder Boy

Sega / Westone, 1986. Tom-Tom sprints, skates and hatchet-throws across ten areas of forests, oceans and volcanoes to rescue Tanya from the King.

System help — the Sega Master System controls

A momentum platformer against a ticking clock: your vitality bar drains constantly, and only fruit, milk and mushrooms top it up — so Wonder Boy is played at a run, eating everything in sight. Forty rounds across ten areas, a doll hidden in every one, and a fireball-spitting King waiting at the end of each area.

Getting started

  1. Press Z or X at the title to start.
  2. Grab the first white egg you see — eggs hold the hatchet, skateboard, milk, mushrooms or your guardian Angel. Speckled eggs hold the God of Death — leave them.
  3. Keep eating. Vitality drains all the time; milk refills it completely, fruit and veg add to it.

Strategy

  • The hatchet doesn't persist. Lose a life and you start the round bare-handed — find a new one in an egg before the frogs find you. (Two hatchet hits for jumping frogs and coyotes; the King wants them all in the face.)
  • Collect the dolls. One per round, some invisible until you pass them — you need 36 to be allowed into area 10 at all, and each doubles the round-clear bonus.
  • The skateboard has no brakes. It's fast, it's fun, and it vanishes on the first collision — you survive, it doesn't.
  • The Angel is combat-proof, not gravity-proof. Invincible to enemies while it lasts; cliffs and cloud gaps still kill you.
  • Game over isn't the end — the game offers CONTINUE to resume where you fell. Extra lives at 30,000 / 70,000 / 170,000 points, then every 70,000.
