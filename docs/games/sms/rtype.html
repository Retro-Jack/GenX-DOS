R-Type

Sega / Irem, 1988. The definitive horizontal shooter in a superb 8-bit conversion — the R-9 fighter, the chargeable wave cannon, and the indestructible Droid unit against eight stages of the Bydo Empire.

R-Type is a puzzle wearing a shooter's clothes: every stage is a layout to be learned, and the Droid — the indestructible sphere you dock to the R-9's nose or tail, or send out ahead — is the tool you solve it with. Three ships, eight stages, and a boss at the end of nearly every one.

Getting started

  1. Press Z or X at the title to launch.
  2. Shoot the POW Armor carriers — they drop the Droid gems and weapon units everything depends on.
  3. Learn the charge: tap Z for rapid pulse fire, hold it to bank energy, release for the beam — the longer the hold, the bigger the blast.

Strategy

  • The Droid is armour. It's indestructible and absorbs many shots — dock it on whichever end the bullets are coming from, or park it detached in a doorway to fight for you.
  • Bosses are Droid puzzles. The manual says it plainly: clever Droid use is the key — including against the Bydo itself, whose weakness "no one knows".
  • Extra ships at 50,000 then 150,000 / 250,000 / 400,000 / 600,000. You'll want them all.
  • The manual's own tease: "try running your fingers clockwise or counterclockwise around your D-Button — if it's during the right time and place, there's no telling what might happen!" We leave that one to you.
