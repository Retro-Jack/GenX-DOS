Console · Sega Master System

Shinobi

Sega, 1988. Joe Musashi versus the Ring of Five: shuriken, katana and ninja magic across five missions — a conversion that trades the arcade's one-hit deaths for a proper health gauge.

System help — the Sega Master System controls

The SMS Shinobi is kinder and deeper than the coin-op: you have a life bar, hostage rescues are optional but pay out in weapon upgrades and health, and the bonus stages hand you ninjutsu — screen-clearing magic you'll want banked for the bosses. Walk the two-level stages, crouch-walk under fire, and save the hostages worth saving.

Getting started

  1. Press Z or X at the title to start.
  2. Rescue hostages as you go — certain ones upgrade your shuriken to a gun and your close attack to a katana, others restore or extend the health gauge, and some unlock the bonus stages.
  3. After stages you'll hit the first-person bonus round: shuriken the ninjas as they leap at you — success earns ninjutsu.

Strategy

  • Ninjutsu is a boss coupon. Thunderstorm, tornado or doppelganger — one cast per stage, big damage on bosses, screen-clearing on crowds. Don't waste it on a corridor you could crouch through.
  • Crouch-walk is the answer to gunfire. Most projectiles fly at standing height; the diagonal-down walk slips under them.
  • Falls still kill. The health gauge protects you from hits, not from bottomless holes.
  • Hostages are the upgrade system. The gun and katana come from rescues — skipping hostages means fighting the Ring of Five with starting gear.
