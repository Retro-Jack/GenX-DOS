Jet Set Willy

Tynesoft, 1986 — Matthew Smith's Manic Miner follow-up, BBC port by Chris Robson (a pseudonym for Dave Mann). Sixty rooms in Willy's newly-bought mansion; tidy them all before Maria lets you go to bed.

System help — the BBC Micro controls

Willy threw a wild party. Now the housekeeper Maria refuses to let him sleep until every glass, bottle, and stray item is collected. The mansion is a single huge interconnected maze of platform rooms — find all 83 items (varies by version), then return to the master bedroom and step into bed.

Getting started

  1. Press any key at the title to start.
  2. You begin in the Master Bedroom with Maria guarding the door. Walk left to start exploring.
  3. Step on any flashing object to collect it. The status bar shows your remaining count.

Controls

Three keys run the whole game — walk with Z / X and jump with Return. Timing those jumps to the pixel is the entire challenge.

A USB gamepad plays it as-is — the d-pad walks left/right and A jumps (Willy's jump is Return, the pad's default A button), no remap needed.

Notable rooms

  • The Bathroom — easy starter items, good warm-up.
  • The Wine Cellar / The Off Licence — Willy's drinking habits made literal.
  • The Banyan Tree — vertical screen with falling platforms.
  • We must perform a Quirkafleeg — surreal Spectrum-era cult favourite room name.
  • The Forgotten Abbey — easy to die in mid-fall.
  • The Watch Tower — the BBC version's notorious bug: the original Tynesoft release crashes the moment you enter this screen, which made the game uncompletable (the Attic bug everyone remembers was the Spectrum's).

Strategy

  • No air timer. Unlike Manic Miner, JSW lets you take your time — die to a hazard and you respawn at the room entry but lose a life.
  • Memorise enemy patterns. Most monsters move on a fixed sweep. The trick is finding a safe rhythm window to cross.
  • Item-completion guards rooms. Some areas open only after collecting specific items elsewhere — exploration unlocks more exploration.
  • End in the bedroom. Once every item is gone Maria steps aside; walk into the bed to finish.
  • Snapshot often. Jet Set Willy has no in-game save, so use the save/load buttons at the bottom-left of the browser window — five slots — to bank a hard-won room before a risky jump.
