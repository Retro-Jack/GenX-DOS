Frak!

Aardvark, 1984 — Nick Pelling. A colourful, fiendishly hard platformer starring Trogg the caveman and his trusty yo-yo, famous for the "FRAK!" speech bubble that pops up every time he dies.

Guide Trogg across a single screen of ledges, ladders and hazards to collect the keys and reach the exit, then on to the next. Balloons, daggers and the creatures Poglet and Hib-Hib all want a piece of him — his only defence is the yo-yo, flung out ahead to knock them away. It looks cheerful and plays like a knife-edge: one wrong jump and it's "FRAK!" all over again.

Getting started

  1. Press Return to start (that's the same button the yo-yo uses in play).
  2. Collect every key on the screen — they open the way onward — then step into the exit.
  3. Flick the yo-yo (Return) at anything that moves. Hold it down and Trogg yo-yos further; if it still won't reach, edge a step closer and try again.
  4. Mind the clock: run out of time and night falls. You can play on in the dark, but you can't yo-yo the balloons or daggers any more.

Controls

Frak! uses one fixed key layout. The BBC's * and ? keys aren't where a PC keyboard puts them — this emulator maps the BBC *: key to your PC apostrophe ' and the BBC / ? key to /, so press those for up/down.

Strategy

  • The yo-yo is a spacing tool. It flies out and back on a fixed arc — line enemies up along that arc rather than walking into them. Holding Return extends the throw.
  • Jumps are committed. There's little steering once Trogg leaves a ledge, so set up the launch before you press up — a mistimed hop is the classic "FRAK!" death.
  • Beat the clock. Nightfall doesn't end the game, but losing your yo-yo against balloons and daggers in the dark usually does — clear a screen before the light goes.
  • Snapshot before the nasty bits. Frak! has no in-game save, so use the save / load buttons at the bottom-left of the browser window — five slots — to bank a hard-won screen.
