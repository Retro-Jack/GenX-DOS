Chuckie Egg

A & F Software, 1983 — designed and coded by Nigel Alderton. Eight-screen platformer that became the BBC's flagship action title; the original cassette version is the gold standard.

Henhouse Harry has 8 platforms-and-ladders screens to clear, each littered with eggs and roaming hens. Collect all twelve eggs on a level (and gobble the seed bowls for bonus points) before the timer runs out. Clear all eight and the cycle repeats — only this time a giant duck escapes its cage and stalks you.

Getting started

  1. Press 1 at the title screen for one-player keyboard.
  2. You can redefine the keys at the title, or stick with the defaults below.
  3. Collect every yellow egg on a screen — the door opens and you proceed.

Strategy

  • Sweep eggs in a single loop. Plan an anticlockwise route — backtracking eats the clock.
  • Hens are predictable. They patrol fixed paths until you complete the seed bowls; then they wander. Clear eggs near hen paths first.
  • The duck is your only real enemy. From level 9 onward (the loop) it flies straight at you in open air — stay near ladders so you can drop a level instantly.
  • Don't jump unless you must. A misjudged jump kills more players than the hens; many gaps are bridgeable just by walking off the edge.
