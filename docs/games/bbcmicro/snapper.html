Snapper

Acornsoft, 1982 — written by Christopher Beale. Acornsoft's Pac-Man for the BBC Micro, close enough to the arcade to dodge legal trouble. Loads from disc.

Guide the Snapper around a maze, eating dots and the four ghost-like "Sour Faces" chasing you. Eat the four power-pills in the corners to turn the Sour Faces blue and edible — for a few seconds you hunt them instead of the other way around. Clear every dot to advance to the next, faster level.

Getting started

  1. Snapper opens by asking "Do you want instructions?" — press Y to read them (two screens, any key to page on) or N to go straight to the game. Then any key starts play.
  2. Steer towards dots. The first level is slow; later levels speed up and the Sour Faces leave their pen sooner.
  3. Save the power-pills for when you're cornered, not when the maze is empty around you.

Controls

On a PC keyboard the BBC *: key lands on the apostrophe — this emulator maps that key to ', so it's listed as ' below.

Strategy

  • Pre-plan your power-pill route. Eat all four ghosts on one pill (400 → 800 → 1600 = 3000 bonus) by herding them towards a corner before triggering.
  • Use the wrap-around tunnels. The two side exits ghosts can't enter at full speed — duck through when chased.
  • Bonus fruit doubles late-game scores. It appears mid-level near the centre; grab it before clearing the surrounding dots so you don't have to backtrack.
