Space Invaders

1980 — PETSCII conversion of Taito's 1978 arcade. Five rows, eleven invaders per row, three shields. Same rules, monochrome glyphs.

System help — the Commodore PET controls

A grid of aliens descends a step at a time, firing back. Move left/right and shoot. As the aliens thin out, they speed up — the period-correct difficulty curve from the original arcade hardware. Survive a wave, the next one starts one row closer.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to start the first wave.

Controls

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

KeyboardAction
4Move left
6Move right
AFire beam

Some PET ports use Z/X or ;/' instead; the title screen lists the build's specific mapping.

Strategy

  • Mind the last alien. When only one invader remains it moves at maximum speed — slow you down trying to track, fast it warps off-screen.
  • Shields deteriorate from both sides. Your shots erode them too. Use them as cover but expect them gone by wave 3.
  • Mothership bonus. The drifting UFO pays 50–300 points depending on shots fired before it — pattern-memorise for the 300 every time.
  • One shot at a time. Original arcade rule: you can't fire again until your current shot leaves the screen or hits. Spam-fire wastes nothing but bait yourself to be early.
