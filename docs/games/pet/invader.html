Space Invaders

1980 (unverified) — a machine-code PET version of Taito's 1978 arcade game. Five rows of invaders march down the screen; move your laser base along the bottom and shoot them before they land.

System help — the Commodore PET controls

The invaders march across and down the screen, dropping bombs as they go. Four shields sit between them and your base; they soak up shots from both sides and wear away. The score table also lists a mystery ship, worth an undisclosed score. You have three bases, and the number left is shown at the top of the screen.

The program gives no date or author, so the 1980 in the menu is unconfirmed.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable controls.
  2. The first screen shows how a PET owner could wire up sound effects — press any key to move on.
  3. The title, score table and a demonstration game follow. At PUSH ANY KEY TO START. press a key.

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

Tips

  • Shoot from under the shields. They block bombs coming down as well as your shots going up — fire from beside a shield and step back under it.
  • Watch the bombs, not the invaders. Bases go quickly — dodge first, shoot second.
