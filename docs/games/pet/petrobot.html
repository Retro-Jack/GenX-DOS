Attack of the PETSCII Robots

2021 — David Murray ("The 8-Bit Guy"), free shareware release. The PET was the machine the game was written on. Robots have taken over a human settlement: find the tools to destroy every one of them, then escape by transporter.

System help — the Commodore PET controls

You see the map from above, with your weapon, item, key cards and health down the right-hand side and messages in the information box below. It looks like a shooter, but as the manual says, you will never win by fighting the robots head on. Search crates, desks, cabinets and beds for a pistol, a plasma gun, time bombs, EMPs, magnets, medkits and key cards, then work out how to deal with each robot. The final transporter stays off until every robot is destroyed. This shareware version has two of the full game's nine maps, 01-Research Lab and 02-The Islands.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable controls. The game loads from its disk, which takes a few seconds.
  2. The shareware screen counts down TIMER REACH ZERO — when it reaches 000, press any key.
  3. On the title screen, 8 and 2 move up and down the menu and Return picks:
    • SELECT MAP — switches between the two maps, shown under MAP.
    • DIFFICULTY — each press changes the setting, shown by the robot's face rather than a word. The manual names three: easy, normal and hard, and notes that on hard the Hoverbots attack without being provoked.
    • CONTROLS — KEYBOARD uses the keys below; CUSTOM KEY asks you to press your own key for each action when the game starts.
    • START GAME — loads the map and starts.

Controls

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

The directions after Z and M, and after using a time bomb or magnet, are the walking keys. This emulator plays no sound, so the game's music and effects are silent.

Getting around

  • Transporters — step on one and wait. A flashing circle means it works; an X means it's off. Some just take you elsewhere on the map; the last one ends the map once every robot is gone.
  • Elevators — walk in and wait for the door to close. An ELEVATOR PANEL appears in the information box: left and right choose the floor, and down opens the door, as the panel's own DOWN OPENS DOOR says. With the standard keys that is 4, 6 and 2.
  • Rafts — wait at a dock and an automated raft arrives to take you to another island.
  • Wooden bridges — an explosion on or near one can destroy it and leave the map impossible to finish. If that happens, press Esc and Y and start again.

Tips

  • Search everything. A searched crate shows with its lid off. A large object like a bed or desk only needs searching once, on any tile.
  • Save the plasma gun. The pistol does 1 point of damage a shot, and a Hoverbot takes 10. The plasma gun and time bombs do 11, in a blast five tiles wide that can catch you too.
  • Let the map do the work. Canisters explode when shot, and you can push them into place first. The trash compactor destroys anything that goes in, robots included. You can shoot through windows.
  • Mind the Evilbot. Two hits from it will finish you, and it chases you as soon as it's let out.
  • The manual's first hints: on the Research Lab, search your lander and find the first key card in the big desk in the main lobby; on The Islands, the first key card is in the L-shaped desk in the main building's lobby.
