Home Computer · Commodore PET

Space!

1979 — by Greg Erker, from issue 8 of CURSOR, the PET magazine that came on cassette. You are deep in space with a laser and a sighting device, and a limited supply of energy to hunt down an enemy fleet.

System help — the Commodore PET controls

The screen is the view from your ship: a starfield, your sights in the middle, and enemy ships moving across it. Turn the ship to bring an enemy into the sights and fire the laser. Energy is shown at the bottom with your hits. Everything costs energy — sitting still a little, turning more, and each laser shot a lot — and when it runs low the mission ends and your shooting is rated.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable controls.
  2. Want instructions? — press Y to read them or N to skip. Between pages, press Return.
  3. Want the noises? — press Y or N. This emulator doesn't play PET sound, so either answer is fine.
  4. The mission starts straight away.

Controls

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

KeyboardAction
ShiftFire the laser — either Shift key
8Turn up
2Turn down
4Turn left
6Turn right
7 9 1 3Turn diagonally: up-left, up-right, down-left, down-right

The turning keys are laid out like a compass around 5, on the numeric keypad or the number row. Hold a key to keep turning. The keys move your ship, not the stars — the whole starfield slides the other way.

Energy

ActionEnergy
Start of a mission5000
Each moment, keeping the ship running1
Each moment spent turning6
Each laser shot, hit or miss100

Below 200 the game warns you have only enough energy to return to base, and the mission is over.

Your rating

After the first mission you are simply told you did very well, if you hit anything. From then on your hits earn a rating and some leave:

HitsRatingAward
1–5PassableA weekend pass
6–10FairNothing — the program starts the award line and leaves it blank
11–15GoodA 2-week R&R pass
16–25Very goodA 3-week R&R pass
26–35ExcellentA 4-week R&R pass
36–50SuperbA 5-week R&R pass
Over 50IncredibleThe Terran Medal of Honor

Tips

  • Centre before you shoot. A shot counts only if the enemy is in the centre of the sights or right beside it; the game promises a hit when it sits directly over the circle. A miss still costs 100.
  • Turn in short bursts. Turning burns six times the energy of drifting, so nudge the ship into line rather than chasing.
  • Count your shots. At 100 each, the 5000 tank holds fewer than fifty of them once turning and drift have taken their share.
