ComputerSpace 2001

1981 — PET homage to the 1971 Nutting Associates Computer Space arcade (the first commercial arcade video game ever, predating Pong). Drift through asteroids, rotate and thrust to survive.

System help — the Commodore PET controls

Asteroids-shaped — your ship floats centred in a starfield of large PETSCII asteroids. Rotate, thrust, fire. The asteroids split on hit; clearing the field starts the next wave with more rocks. Newtonian inertia means your ship keeps drifting until you counter-thrust.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to spawn.

Strategy

  • Inertia is the trap. Tap-thrust briefly; never hold. A long burn glues you into a single drift line you can't break out of in time.
  • Aim for centre-mass. A glancing shot may miss — full hits split the asteroid; partial hits do nothing.
  • Stay near centre. Edge-screen wrap-around can dump you next to a rock you can't react to.
  • Hyperspace is a last resort. Computer Space's original hyperspace had a small chance of dropping you on top of an asteroid; PET ports keep that lottery.
