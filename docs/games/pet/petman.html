PET-MAN

1982 (unverified) — a Pac-Man-style maze game created by Paul Gummersall, with coding changes by Jim Butterfield. Eat every dot in the maze while four guards hunt you, and turn the tables with an energizer.

System help — the Commodore PET controls

PET-MAN moves through a maze of dots, and four guards chase him. Eat an energizer (*) and for ten seconds the guards appear in reverse and can be caught instead. Clear every dot and the next round starts, faster. There is only one life: the first time a guard catches you, it's CAPTURE and the game is over.

The program credits its authors but gives no date, so the 1982 in the menu is unconfirmed.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable controls.
  2. DO YOU WANT INSTRUCTIONS — YES OR NO — press Y or N. After the instructions, ARE YOU READY TO PLAY wants Y.
  3. EASY, HARD, OR NO CHANCE (EHN) — press E, H or N. The harder the setting, the more eagerly the guards move.
  4. The maze is drawn and the game begins.

Controls

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

Y / NAt DO YOU WANT TO PLAY AGAIN?

Press a direction once: PET-MAN keeps going that way until he meets a wall or you choose another. A direction into a wall is ignored. The passage through the side of the maze wraps round to the other side.

Scoring

EventPoints
Each dot5
Catching a guard while an energizer lasts100

An energizer lasts ten seconds, and the guards you catch return to the middle of the maze when it wears off. At the end the game shows your score, the high score and how many guards you caught.

Tips

  • You only get one go. With a single life, keep your distance from the guards rather than trusting a narrow escape.
  • Save the energizers for when you're cornered. Four guards bearing down is the time to eat one — and a caught guard is worth twenty dots.
  • Start on Easy. On Easy a guard moves about one turn in ten; on No Chance it's more than every other turn. Easy gives you room to learn the maze.
