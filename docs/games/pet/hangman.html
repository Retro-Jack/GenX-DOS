Personal Computer · Commodore PET

Hangman

1980 — by Raymond Toy, shared through SOFTSWAP, the educational software exchange run by the San Mateo County Office of Education and Computer-Using Educators. Guess the PET's word one letter at a time before the gallows are finished.

System help — the Commodore PET controls

The PET picks a word from a list of 215 and shows a blank for each letter. Every letter you guess is added after YOUR GUESSES:; a right guess fills in each place it appears, and a wrong one adds to the drawing of the gallows. You are allowed ten wrong guesses — the eleventh hangs you. The PET won't give you the same word twice in one session.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable controls.
  2. Do you want instructions (Y/N)? — press Y or N. On the instruction pages, press any key to go on.
  3. At MY WORD IS: start guessing.

Controls

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

KeyboardAction
A – ZGuess that letter
Y / NAt ANOTHER WORD (Y/N)? — play again, or stop

Each guess is a single key — as the game's own instructions put it, do not press the Return key. Guess a letter you've already tried and it just says LETTER REPEATED; it doesn't count against you.

Winning and losing

  • Fill in the whole word and the PET says YOU'VE GOT IT!
  • On the eleventh wrong guess you are hanged: the PET shows MY WORD IS: with the answer, and YOU LOSE!
  • Either way it asks ANOTHER WORD (Y/N)?

Tips

  • Start with the common letters. E, T, A, O, I and N turn up in most words and fill in the shape fastest.
  • Vowels narrow things down. Once you know where the vowels are, the word's pattern usually suggests the rest.
  • The word list isn't simple. It includes words like turpitude, tangential and angstrom — ten wrong guesses go quicker than you'd think.
