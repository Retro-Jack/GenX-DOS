Star Trek

1978 — Version 17, dated 23/07/78, one of the many BASIC descendants of the 1971 Star Trek game. Hunt down every Klingon warship in the galaxy within 40 stardates, using six one-letter commands and a control panel that stays on screen.

System help — the Commodore PET controls

The galaxy is 64 quadrants in an 8×8 grid, and each quadrant is 64 sectors, again 8×8. The left of the screen shows the quadrant you are in — the Enterprise, stars, starbases and any Klingons — and the right shows the stardate, your condition, quadrant and sector, energy, shields, torpedoes and the number of Klingons left. Time passes and energy drains as you travel and fight.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable controls.
  2. Do you need instructions? — type Y or N and press Return. The instructions are several pages; press any key to turn each one.
  3. The game tells you how many Klingons to destroy and how many starbases there are. At Are you ready? type Y and press Return.
  4. At COMMAND? type a command letter and press Return.

Always type something before pressing Return at a prompt. On the PET, Return on its own at a question stops the program and drops you to BASIC's READY. — if that happens, type RUN and press Return to start a new game.

Commands

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported. Only the first letter of a command counts.

TypeAction
NNavigate. At COURSE,DISTANCE type both, separated by a comma — e.g. 90,1 — and press Return. Distance is in quadrants; small values such as .3 move within the quadrant.
TFire a photon torpedo. At COURSE type a direction in degrees.
LLong-range sensors: the eight quadrants around you as three digits each — Klingons, starbases, stars.
MGalaxy map, built from what the long-range sensors have recorded. It only updates when you use L.
SShield control. At UNITS TO SHIELDS type how much energy the shields should hold.
RRedraw the control panel if the screen gets in a mess.

Courses are in degrees, anticlockwise from the right: 0 is right, 90 up, 180 left, 270 down, and any angle from 0 to 359 works.

Winning and losing

  • You start with 3000 energy and 15 torpedoes. Destroy every Klingon within 40 stardates and the game declares YOU'VE WON!!
  • Klingons fire on you in battle; their hits come off your shields and damage the ship's systems. A hit your shields can't take destroys the Enterprise.
  • Run past the 40 stardates and it's TIME'S UP!!! Destroy the last starbase in the galaxy yourself and you're told YOU'RE FIRED!!!

Tips

  • Dock to recover. Move into a sector next to a starbase: energy and torpedoes are refilled, damage is repaired, and while you're docked the starbase's shields protect you. Docking costs a stardate.
  • Scan before you jump. Use L in each new quadrant, then M to plan a route to the Klingons and the nearest starbase.
  • Raise shields before a fight. Shields and movement draw on the same energy, so put enough into the shields to survive a volley without stranding yourself.
  • Mind what's in the line of fire. A torpedo travels in a straight line and stops at the first thing it meets — including a starbase you may need later.
