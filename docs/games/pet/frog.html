Personal Computer · Commodore PET

Frog

1981 (unverified) — by P.J. Fellner. Get your frog across a busy road and a stream into one of the home bays along the top, for one or two players taking turns.

System help — the Commodore PET controls

The frog starts at the bottom of the screen. Cars run along the road in the lower half; the stream in the upper half carries dead snakes and square lilypads. A safe strip across the middle, with FROG spelled out on it, separates the two. Ten home bays line the top, and a timer runs down while you play. You get three frogs.

The program names its author, P.J. Fellner, but gives no year, and no dated listing or catalogue entry for it has turned up — so the 1981 in the menu is unconfirmed.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. The title, credits and instructions play first. The instructions are typed out slowly; press ] to skip ahead — one press can skip more than one page.
  3. At PUSH 1 OR 2 PLAYER BUTTON, press 1 or 2 for the number of players. With two, players swap each time a frog is lost.

Controls

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

Hold a direction for a moment rather than tapping it. The game only sees a key that is down at the instant it checks, so a quick tap can be missed.

Losing a frog

  • Being hit by a car, a dead snake or a square lilypad.
  • Bumping into the wall between two home bays — it still scores 250, but the frog is gone.
  • Letting the timer run out.

Scoring

Each hop1

Time left, on getting home5 per unit

A bonus frog every 10,000 points. The timer starts at 200 for each new frog. Getting home carries the time left over to the next frog with 25 more, and never less than 75.

Tips

  • Line up before the last hop. The walls between the bays cost a frog, so move sideways to face an empty bay before hopping in.
  • The middle strip is safe. Nothing moves along it, so it's the place to wait for a gap in the stream.
  • Speed pays twice. Time left is worth 5 points a unit when you get home, and it carries over to the next frog.
  • Filled bays stay filled. Each player keeps their own; once all ten are full, the game clears them the next time a frog is lost.
