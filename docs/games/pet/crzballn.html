Crazy Balloon

1981 (unverified) — a PET version of Taito's 1980 arcade game. Steer a swaying balloon on a string through a maze of prickly stars to the goal without bursting it.

System help — the Commodore PET controls

The balloon starts at the bottom right of a maze of stars, and the GOAL is on the left. The balloon sways on its string as it goes, so its top and bottom swing from side to side — touch a star with either and it bursts. One star also moves, sliding back and forth along its row, and it bursts the balloon too. The walls of the maze bounce you back. Lines across the maze score points as you pass through them, and the sooner you reach the goal the more it's worth. You have four balloons.

The program names no author and gives no date, so the 1981 in the menu is unconfirmed.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable controls.
  2. DO YOU WANT INSTRUCTIONS? — press Y for two pages of instructions (press any key to turn each), or any other key to start straight away. E ends the program.

Controls

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

The balloon moves only while a key is held down. Don't leave it still: as the game warns, it bursts if it stays put for five seconds or so.

Scoring

EventPoints
Passing through the first kind of line150
Passing through the second kind100
Passing through both on the way to the goal250, plus a 500 bonus at the goal
Reaching the goalA countdown that starts at 1000 and falls the longer you take, never below 100

An extra balloon comes at 4,000 points. The level rises at 4,000, 6,000, 7,000 and 8,000 points, and beyond 9,000 random stars are added to the maze. When your last balloon bursts with a new high score the game asks for your name — type one (up to 12 letters) before pressing Return, because Return on its own stops the program; type RUN to start again if it does.

Tips

  • Mind the sway, not just the balloon. The string swings as well, so leave a clear space either side of a gap before you go through it.
  • Walls are safe, stars are not. Bumping a wall only bounces you back, so hug the walls where the stars are thick.
  • Keep moving. Short steps count as movement and stop the balloon bursting while you line up the next gap.
