Pac-Man

Atarisoft / Commodore licensee, 1983 — the officially-licensed VIC-20 port of Namco's 1980 arcade. Squashed to the VIC-20's 22-column screen but the AI is intact.

Eat the dots, dodge the ghosts, eat the power pellets, eat the ghosts. Standard Pac-Man — the VIC-20's screen is narrower than the original so the maze is reshaped slightly, but Blinky/Pinky/Inky/Clyde all behave per arcade.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title.

Strategy

  • Power pellets aren't free. Trigger when multiple ghosts are close — chasing for one is bad ROI.
  • Pre-buffer corners. Pac turns at junctions zero-cost; press the next direction one tile early.
  • Cruise Elroy. When dots are scarce, Blinky matches your speed — corners become the only escape.
  • VIC-20 sprite limit. Visually busier than expected — you'll see slight flicker at peak ghost density. Pure cosmetic.
