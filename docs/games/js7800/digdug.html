Dig Dug

Atari, 1987 (port of Masanobu Endoh's 1982 Namco arcade). Dig tunnels in a four-layered earth, inflate enemies until they pop, drop rocks to crush them. The granddaddy of "popper-mechanic" arcade games.

Dig tunnels through dirt. Two enemy types: red Pookas (round) and fire-breathing green Fygars. Inflate them with your pump (4 pumps = pop). Drop boulders by digging under them — crushed enemies = bonus points. Clear each round of all monsters. The deeper the layer, the more points each kill is worth.

Getting started

  1. Click RESET (or press Enter) to start.
  2. Move in any direction with arrows; you dig as you move.
  3. Press Z against an adjacent enemy to inflate them (tap repeatedly).

Strategy

  • Rock kills score double. Tunnel under boulders, lure monsters beneath, then dig the supporting pillar. Crushing 4 with one rock = a quarter of the game's score in one move.
  • Fygar's flame. Don't confront a Fygar head-on horizontally — it can breathe fire that kills you. Pump from above or below for safety AND double points.
  • Ghost mode. Enemies trapped in dirt turn into "ghosts" and move through walls towards you. Don't lose track of them — they re-materialise when they reach an open tunnel.
  • Vegetable bonus. A vegetable appears at the centre of each round. Get it for big points (cherry 400 → eggplant 8000).
  • Last enemy escapes. The final enemy walks to the top of the playfield and flees if not killed quickly. Don't dawdle.

Atari 7800 controls

Single button for the pump — Z. X is unused. Click RESET (or press Enter) to start.
