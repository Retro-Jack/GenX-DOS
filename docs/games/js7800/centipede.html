Centipede

Atari, 1986 (7800 launch lineup) — improved port of the 1981 Dona Bailey / Ed Logg arcade. Replaces the 2600's blocky mushrooms with sprites that look like the arcade.

A centipede zigzags down through a field of mushrooms. Hit a middle segment and it splits into two head-led chains. Spider hops, flea drops mushrooms, scorpion poisons. The 7800 ProSystem's MARIA chip handles dozens of sprites at once, so the game looks and runs like the arcade — and supports proper 8-way movement.

Getting started

  1. Press Enter to start.
  2. Move with arrows (8-way), fire continuously with Z.

Strategy

  • Diagonal hits. The 8-way joystick lets you slide diagonally — the 7800 port rewards this over the 2600 version's 4-way limits.
  • Mushroom culling. Each wave starts with the layout you left behind. Sparse field = wider centipede = easier shooting.
  • Spider proximity scoring. Shoot it from one row away for 900 points. Tempting but lethal.
  • Auto-fire only. Hold Z — single shots are wasted frames.
  • Poisoned mushrooms. A centipede crossing one plunges straight down. Either eat it (4 hits) or position to intercept the drop.

Atari 7800 controls

ProLine joystick with auto-fire works perfectly for Centipede. The MARIA chip's sprite count means no flicker, unlike the 2600 port. Press Enter at the title to start.
