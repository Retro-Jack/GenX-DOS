Xevious

Atari, 1986 (port of Masanobu Endoh's 1983 Namco arcade). Vertical-scrolling shoot-em-up that pioneered ground-target bombing — your ship has two weapons: air zapper and ground bomb.

You pilot the Solvalou over the Xevious empire. The air gun (forward) shoots flying enemies; the bomb sight (ground reticule, fixed distance ahead of you) targets bunkers and tanks below. Both fire with separate buttons. Mysterious Andor Genesis mothership appears periodically — destroy its core for huge bonuses.

Getting started

  1. Press Enter to start.
  2. Fly with arrows; air shots Z; ground bombs X.
  3. The ground reticule sits a fixed distance ahead — line it up with a target then drop bombs.

Strategy

  • Bomb the ground constantly. Tanks (Domogram) shoot upward — bomb them on sight. The reticule's fixed distance means you have to lead.
  • Andor Genesis core. The huge floating mothership appears every few stages. Bomb its central core (red) for 5000. Surrounding turrets are decoys.
  • Sol Citadels. Hidden underground cities — bomb specific empty terrain to reveal them. Memorise positions for the bonus.
  • Two-finger rhythm. Air gun (Z) constantly, ground bomb (X) opportunistically. Don't share a thumb on both buttons.
  • Don't camp the bottom. The Solvalou is least useful at the bottom — ground targets escape the reticule before you can hit them. Stay in the upper third.

Atari 7800 controls

The 7800's two-button ProLine is mandatory for Xevious — single-button ports of this game leave bombing crippled. Z for air, X for bombs. Press Enter to start.
