Ms. Pac-Man

Atari, 1986 (port of the 1982 Midway/General Computer arcade — Doug Macrae, Kevin Curran, Steve Golson, Mike Horowitz). The 7800 port is widely considered the best home version short of MAME.

Four mazes (not one), the ghosts' behaviour is no longer fully deterministic, fruit moves around the maze, and warp tunnels work both sides. Eat all the dots in each maze; power pills make ghosts blue and edible. Bonus fruit moves through the maze instead of pausing at the centre.

Getting started

  1. Press Enter to start.
  2. Move with arrows; eat dots; grab power pills when ghosts close in.

Strategy

  • Warp tunnels. Ghosts slow down in tunnels. Lead them into a tunnel before reversing to power-pill.
  • Eat ghosts in order. Each subsequent ghost eaten during one power-pill run scores double (200 → 400 → 800 → 1600). Worth the chase.
  • Don't camp pills. Ms. Pac-Man's AI noticeably "knows" if you stand still — ghosts converge on stationary players.
  • Fruit chase. Bonus fruit travels around the maze rather than pausing. Predict its path through tunnel exits.
  • Intermissions. Cutscenes between rounds (Junior, "The Chase", "Junior") — nothing to do, but they reset your nerves.

Atari 7800 controls

Single-stick. Fire buttons are unused. The 7800 port runs at 60 Hz with full Midway behaviour; press Enter to start.
