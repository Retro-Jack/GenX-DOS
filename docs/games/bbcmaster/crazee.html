Crazee Rider

Superior Software / Acornsoft, 1987 — Kevin Edwards. Behind-the-bike 3D motorbike racing around real-world grand-prix circuits; the BBC Master version adds extra circuits, enhanced graphics and Martin Galway music.

You're one of sixty riders in a motorbike championship raced on real circuits — Brands Hatch, Silverstone, Le Mans, the Nürburgring and more. Finish in the first six on a track to qualify for the next one, and rack up bonus points by knocking other riders off their bikes along the way.

Getting started

  1. At the start line, red, amber and green lights are shown. Wait for the green light before you accelerate — jump the start and you'll be penalised and your bike will stall.
  2. Race to finish in the top six; qualify and the championship moves on to the next circuit.

Controls

The BBC * and ? keys aren't where a PC keyboard puts them — this emulator maps the BBC *: key to your PC apostrophe ' and the / ? key to /, so those are your accelerate and decelerate.

Strategy

  • Knocking a rider off is worth a 200-point bonus — swipe into an opponent at the right moment and they go down.
  • Get the collision wrong and you pay for it. A plain side collision just costs you some speed, but rear-ending a bike slows you considerably and disables your bike for a short period.
  • Don't creep at the lights. A jumped start stalls the bike, and with sixty riders on the grid, that's the whole field gone past before you're moving again.
