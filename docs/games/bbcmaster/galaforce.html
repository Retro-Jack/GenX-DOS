Galaforce

Superior Software, 1986 — Kevin Edwards. A fixed-screen shoot-em-up in the Galaga mould: pilot the Galaforce against swirling alien formations across the Magellanic galaxy.

System help — the BBC Master controls

Your ship roams the lower half of the screen while waves of aliens sweep in along looping flight paths, diving and firing as they pass. Clear each wave to advance; survive long enough and the formations grow denser and faster. Unlike a straight Galaga clone, your ship moves freely in both axes across the play area — so positioning matters as much as trigger timing.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, then press Space to start.
  2. Your ship moves in all four directions in the lower part of the screen — Z/X for sideways, ' / / for up/down.
  3. Hold Return to fire; press S to toggle sound, P to pause.

Strategy

  • Keep moving. Aliens target your position on their diving runs — a stationary ship is an easy hit. Drift constantly and lead them into the gaps.
  • Use the full height. Your ship isn't pinned to the bottom row — rising to meet a wave early can thin it before it fans out.
  • Pick off divers. The aliens that break formation to swoop are the dangerous ones; prioritise them over the ones still looping in.
  • Watch the crossfire. Later waves shoot from multiple angles at once — position so you're only dodging one stream at a time.
