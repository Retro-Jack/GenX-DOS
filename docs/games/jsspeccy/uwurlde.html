Underwurlde

Ultimate Play The Game, 1984 — Tim & Chris Stamper. Sabreman's second adventure: side-view platform exploration with exaggerated bouncy physics.

Sabreman is trapped in the Underwurlde, a vast vertical maze of platforms, ropes, and creatures. There are three exits, each guarded by a different monster: the Wulf, the Witch, and the Vampire. Find a weapon for the right boss and escape via the corresponding gate. The exaggerated bounce physics — Sabreman falls forever without dying, but is launched skyward by enemy contact — is the design twist.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. Sabreman starts inside the Underwurlde. Walk left/right; jump with up; climb ropes with up.
  3. Falling damage doesn't exist — Sabreman bounces. Enemy contact does no damage but launches you randomly skyward.
  4. Pick up an item to throw it; specific items kill specific monsters.

Controls (default)

This game supports a USB gamepad — the D-pad or left stick moves (up jumps), A/Y fire your weapon, B picks up / drops a weapon (and starts at the title), X drops from a rope.

You begin unarmed — Sabreman has lost his sword, so fire does nothing until you pick a weapon up.

Strategy

  • Bounce, don't fight. Most enemies are obstacles, not targets — let them fling you towards a useful exit.
  • Pick the right exit boss. Wulf-killing item ≠ Vampire-killing item. Decide your route early.
  • Map the maze. Hundreds of rooms; the vertical scale is the largest of any Ultimate game.
  • The screen flip points when entering a new room can re-orient you mid-bounce. Learn which rooms have flip-friendly geometries.
