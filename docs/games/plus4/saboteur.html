Saboteur!

Durell, 1985 — Clive Townsend's ninja-infiltration classic, ported from the original ZX Spectrum hit. Land by inflatable, sneak into the warehouse, grab the stolen disk, plant a bomb, escape by helicopter in under nine minutes.

System help — the Commodore Plus/4 controls

Flick-screen stealth-platformer across a multi-level warehouse complex. Patrolling guards and Doberman dogs kill on contact — but you can crouch-kick, throw shuriken, and brawl. Pick up weapons (knives, shuriken, bricks) from desks and crates. The bomb must be planted in the computer room; the helicopter waits on the roof.

Getting started

  1. The .prg auto-loads; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press F1 or fire at the title to start. Choose difficulty (1-9 — start at 1).

Strategy

  • Memorise the layout. No map, no in-game hints. Sketch as you explore on the first lives — death respawns you back at the inflatable.
  • Dogs only respond to crouch-kicks. Standing punches miss; dogs slip under and lunge. Drop low the moment you see one.
  • Throw weapons sparingly. Knives and shuriken are one-shot kills but limited; reserve them for guards in corridors where melee would be suicidal.
  • The clock is brutal. Difficulty 1 gives you nine minutes — plenty if you know the route, almost none if you're exploring. Save-state aggressively until you have the map memorised. Five save slots per game — pick 1–5 from the save or load menu.
