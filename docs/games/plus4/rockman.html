Rockman

Mastertronic, 1985 — Shaun Southern's Boulder Dash clone for the Plus/4. Dig through dirt mazes collecting diamonds while avoiding falling rocks and patrolling fireflies; clear the quota to unlock the exit.

Top-down cave-digging puzzle in 30+ levels. You push through soft dirt; rocks and diamonds rest on dirt and fall when unsupported. Stand under a rock and it crushes you. Collect the per-level diamond quota, then race for the now-open exit before the time runs out. The Plus/4 version's TED palette gives the cave-tiles a richer, less saturated look than the C64 Boulder Dash.

Getting started

  1. The .prg auto-loads; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press L Ctrl at the title to start.

Strategy

  • Plan vertical descents. Once you dig down through dirt, anything above you may fall — never dig under a rock without an escape route.
  • Use rocks as walls. A pushed rock blocks fireflies; a row of rocks can box-in a wandering enemy and let you collect the diamonds it was guarding.
  • Watch the timer. The exit unlocks the moment you hit quota — but you still need to physically reach it. Always know your route home before the last diamond.
  • Diamonds in a column fall together. Hit the bottom one and the whole stack collapses; great for clearing tight pockets in one move.
