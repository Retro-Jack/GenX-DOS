Punchy

Mr. Micro / Anirog, 1985 — Punch-and-Judy seaside-show platformer. Mr. Punch traverses single-screen flick-stages bopping Judy, the Crocodile, the Policeman, and the Devil with his slapstick.

Flick-screen platformer in the Mr. Robot mould (same studio's house engine). Each screen is a single-room puzzle: collect items, work out the safe route, hit the right enemy with the slapstick to clear it. Beach-front aesthetic with Plus/4's pastel TED palette doing the seaside show justice.

Getting started

  1. The .prg auto-loads; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press L Ctrl at the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
← / →Walk left / right
↑Jump / climb ladder
↓Climb down
L CtrlSwing slapstick (hits enemy in front)

Strategy

  • Slapstick has range-of-one. You must be touching-distance to the enemy — wind up just before they walk into reach.
  • Judy can be hit, the Devil can't. Each enemy type has a different "vulnerable" colour or animation frame; pay attention before swinging.
  • Use ladders defensively. A half-climbed ladder is a safe perch — enemies on the platform above and below can't reach you mid-rung.
  • The Crocodile's bite is telegraphed. The jaw opens a frame before snapping; that's your cue to slap.
