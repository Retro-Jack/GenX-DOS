Wizball

Ocean Software (Sensible Software — Jon Hare & Chris Yates), 1987 — surreal side-scrolling shooter where you start as a bouncing ball and gradually unlock movement, pet cat, and chromatic powers. ZZAP! 64 Gold Medal classic.

You control Wizball, an enchanted sphere that initially can only bounce gravity-style. Defeat fountain enemies to release colour droplets — collect them to add to your colour mix (R/G/B), which restores monochrome levels to colour and unlocks abilities (anti-gravity, thrust, cat companion Nifta, smart bomb). Eight worlds, each themed by colour.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press fire at the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Push left / right (early game = momentum only)
Numpad 8Anti-gravity / thrust (once unlocked)
Numpad 0 / 5Fire / launch Nifta the cat (once unlocked)
Up + Down rapidSpin to "select power" (early game pause-and-aim)

Strategy

  • Bounce first, fly later. The first stage is intentionally clumsy — you have to learn ball physics before earning thrust. Use the bounce timing to land collectibles.
  • Match the colour, repair the world. Each level has a target hue (the cauldron at the bottom). Collect droplets to mix R+G+B until you hit it.
  • Nifta the cat collects faster. Once unlocked, Nifta scoops droplets that Wizball can't reach. Send the cat down then circle back to the cauldron.
  • Sensible's soundtrack. Martin Galway's title music is one of the all-time C64 SID showcases — sound on.
  • Hidden 2-player mode. A second joystick controls Nifta directly in co-op. Single player AI does it competently but co-op is faster.
