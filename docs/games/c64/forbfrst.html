Forbidden Forest

Cosmi (Paul Norman), 1983 — archer survival horror against escalating monsters in a parallax-scrolling forest. Notorious for its SID-driven dread soundtrack.

System help — the Commodore 64 controls

You play a longbowman stalking a haunted forest. Each level introduces a new menace — giant spiders, snakes, hangmen, frogs, dragons, the Demogorgon. Aim arrows at the right elevation and lead the target; hits ramp the rage of remaining enemies. Limited arrows per stage; pick up extras from quivers when they spawn.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press F1 at the title to start.

Controls

Strategy

  • Hold to draw. A half-drawn shot drops short; full draw is the only way to hit anything beyond near-range.
  • Spiders — lead high. They weave; aim above where they are now, the parabola brings the arrow down on target.
  • Hangmen — body shot. They twist; centre-mass is the most forgiving target.
  • Demogorgon — backpedal. The boss closes faster than you can outrun forward; reverse and shoot while retreating.
  • Audio is part of the design. The Stewart Charney SID track ramps from ambient drone to klaxon as danger closes — sound on is the intended experience.
