Popcorn

Tandy Corp (Charles Forsythe), 1981 — single-screen "catch the falling thing" arcade game with kernels popping out of a popcorn machine and a chef sliding left/right with a frypan. Catch every kernel; misses cost a life.

System help — the Tandy CoCo controls

The popcorn machine spits kernels at unpredictable arcs across the top of the screen. The chef slides along three pan positions (bottom of screen). Match the kernel's landing column. Higher levels add more poppers and tighter timing windows; bonus pretzels worth more points appear occasionally.

Getting started

  1. Tape auto-loads; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title to start Level 1.

Strategy

  • Track on the rise, not the fall. Read the kernel's arc as it ascends — you'll know where it's coming down a beat earlier.
  • Centre when uncertain. The pan covers more reachable area from middle than from either edge.
  • Pretzel bait. Bonus pretzels deliberately arc near misses — chase them only if no kernel is in danger.
  • Bonus stages reward speed. Between levels: a flurry of pretzels for bonus points only — no life lost if missed.
