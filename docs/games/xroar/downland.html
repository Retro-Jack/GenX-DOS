Downland

Spectral Associates (Michael Aichlmayr), 1983 — single-screen platform-puzzle game. Ten themed caverns, each a single screen of ropes, falling water, jumping rocks and timed keys. The CoCo's signature platformer and a near-mythical example of single-coder polish on the platform.

Each cavern needs three keys collected in time, then the exit door opens. Ropes are the main traversal: climb up, jump across, fall down. Falling water columns kill instantly; bouncing rocks track and crush. Several caverns have a secret door — find it for bonus points and an alternate path.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press fire (L Alt) at the title to start Cavern 1.

Strategy

  • Jumps from ropes are short. Air time from a rope-jump is less than a running jump — line up the next rope before letting go.
  • Water columns are timed. Most water runs in a fixed pulse; cross in the gap, not the splash.
  • Rocks home in. Bouncing rocks track your position lightly — bait one off-screen by walking against a wall, then move past once it overshoots.
  • Cavern 6 is the wall. "The Phantom" cavern with the flashing wall is the spike — practice it in attract mode first.
  • Score chase, not survival. Downland is short (10 caverns, ~30 min) but the score system rewards bonus keys, secret doors and time bonuses.
