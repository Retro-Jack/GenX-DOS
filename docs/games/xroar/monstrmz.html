Monster Maze

Spectral Associates (Wayne Westmoreland & Terry Gilman), 1982 — first-person 3D wireframe maze chase. Find the exit; the maze is plain enough on paper, but a tyrannosaurus prowls the corridors and the only feedback when it's near is the screen flashing "REX LURKS" — gloriously panic-inducing.

System help — the Tandy CoCo controls

Wireframe first-person view down each corridor, four-direction turns (no diagonal moves). Scattered through the maze are exits — the goal is simply to reach one before the T-Rex catches you. The "REX LURKS" warning flashes when the dinosaur is within a square or two; the corridor goes briefly black when it's literally next door.

Getting started

  1. Tape auto-loads; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title to spawn into the maze.

Controls

KeyboardAction
↑Move forward one square
← →Turn left / right 90°
↓About-face (180° turn)

Strategy

  • Move on a beat. The T-Rex's audible footsteps are quarter notes; matching pace means you stay roughly the same distance ahead.
  • "REX LURKS" → never stop. The warning is your cue to commit to a direction; freezing to think is when the dinosaur catches up.
  • Black flash = adjacent. When the screen briefly blacks out, the T-Rex is in a connected corridor — turn away from it next square.
  • Maze is hand-built, not random. Re-runs use the same layout; learn one route and the maze becomes a footrace, not navigation.
