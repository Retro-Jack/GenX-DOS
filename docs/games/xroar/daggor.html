Dungeons of Daggorath

DynaMicro/Tandy, 1982 — first-person 3D wireframe dungeon-crawler with real-time typed commands and an audible heartbeat as your HP gauge. A foundational piece of CRPG history: predates Wizardry's real-time successors and sets the template for the heartbeat-feedback genre.

System help — the Tandy CoCo controls

Five-level wireframe dungeon viewed first-person. Every action — move, turn, attack, pickup — is a typed command in real time. Tire yourself out and the heartbeat speeds up; let the heartbeat hit racing pace and you die from exhaustion. Wizard at the bottom is the win condition.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press any key at the title to begin Level 1.

Controls — typed commands

Type the command and press Enter. Most commands have one-letter abbreviations.

CommandEffect
M (Move)Walk forward one square
TL / TR (Turn)Turn left / right 90°
TU (Turn around)180° turn
A R (Attack Right)Swing right-hand weapon
A L (Attack Left)Swing left-hand weapon
GET / GPick up object underfoot
PUT R / PUT LEquip held item in hand
U (Use)Activate item (torch, scroll, ring)
L (Look)Look down (see what's underfoot)
EX (Examine)Examine an inventory item
R (Rest)Pause to lower heartbeat — risky, monsters still close

Strategy

  • Heartbeat is your HP. Faster pulse = lower stamina. Stand still to recover; running while tired is death.
  • Listen for monsters. Footsteps tell you direction and distance — turn towards the loudest sound before they reach you.
  • Always carry a lit torch. Unlit corridors are pitch black and you blunder into walls (no movement, no damage — but you stay tired).
  • Pace your attacks. Each swing costs a heartbeat-spike; don't spam AR. Time the rhythm.
  • The Wizard is on Level 5. No teleport shortcuts — descend ladder by ladder. The Wizard's room is recognisable by the runic torches.
