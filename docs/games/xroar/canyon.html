Canyon Climber

Datasoft (Steve Bjork), 1982 — three-screen action-platformer. Climb a Grand Canyon-themed cliff in three scenarios: rope-bridge dodge, dynamite plant against mountain goat, eagle's-nest grapple. Cycles back to start at higher speed once cleared.

Stage 1: a rope bridge with archers firing arrows from the cliff side — duck or jump each arrow. Stage 2: a vertical climb past a mountain goat that headbutts you off ledges; plant dynamite at coloured marks to clear the path. Stage 3: scale a cliff face against an eagle dive-bombing the nest. Three lives, no checkpoints.

Getting started

  1. Tape auto-loads; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press the joystick fire button (L Alt) at the title.

Controls

KeyAction
← →Walk left / right
↑ ↓Climb up / down (Stages 2-3); duck under arrows on the bridge (Stage 1)
L Alt / SpaceJump / plant dynamite — the joystick fire button (its action depends on the stage)

Strategy

  • Rope bridge rhythm. The archers fire on a tight loop; learn the cadence and duck the upper line, jump the lower.
  • Dynamite goes at red marks. No effect anywhere else; if you place wrong, you've burned a stick.
  • Mountain goat charges in straight lines. Side-step the lane just before contact — the goat doesn't track laterally mid-charge.
  • Eagle stage is positional. The eagle dives from the same off-screen positions each pass; memorise the three lanes.
  • No checkpoints — lean on the buttons. Three lives and no in-game save, so use the load/save buttons at the bottom-left to snapshot progress between scenarios. Five save slots per game — pick 1–5 from the save or load menu.
