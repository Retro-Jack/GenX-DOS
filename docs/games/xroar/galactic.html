Galactic Attack

The Image Producers for Tandy, 1981 — a laser at the bottom of the screen, eight aliens in formation above it, and a manual that opens with a screenplay about Dr Laserbum.

System help — the Tandy CoCo controls

The aliens arrive in strict formation, sliding right to left across the top of the screen. Then one is hit and the formation breaks: survivors peel off into dive attacks, drop bombs on the way down, and come back for another run if they miss. Your laser sits at the bottom and moves along it. Hit them low and they are worth more — up to sixteen times what a ship in formation scores.

You need a joystick for this one

Galactic Attack reads the joystick and nothing else — the keyboard will not even start it. The manual is blunt about the hardware: "Connect a Joystick to the connection marked LEFT JOYSTK."

Without a gamepad the cursor keys stand in for the stick, with L Alt as the fire button. With a gamepad plugged in, the left stick and A take over. A browser only reveals a pad once a button is pressed on a focused page, so if it is not picked up, click the game area and press again.

Getting started

  1. The cartridge starts on its own; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. At >>PRESS FIRE BUTTON TO BEGIN<<, press fire — L Alt, or A on a pad.

Scoring

TargetPoints
A ship still in formation10
A diving ship, by how low you let it comeup to 160
A bomb shot out of the airnothing — but it cannot hit you either

Clear a whole group before any ship reaches Earth and the aliens answer with a night attack. Survive that without letting a single diver past and everything scored for the rest of it is worth four times as much. Let one through and the next wave drops back to an ordinary one.

Strategy

  • Wait for them to come down. A ship in formation is worth 10 and a low diver up to 160. Shooting the neat rows at the top is the least profitable thing you can do.
  • Shoot the bombs. They score nothing, but a bomb removed is a hit you do not take, and you only have three lasers to lose.
  • Reinforcements arrive on the far side. Lose the laser on the right and its replacement comes in from the left. Expect to be somewhere you did not choose.
  • The night attack is the whole score. Quadruple points make one clean night worth more than several careful ordinary waves, so it is worth taking risks to keep the run intact.
  • They get harder to hit, not just faster. Later waves flutter and dodge the beam while still bombing, so lead them rather than aiming where they are.
