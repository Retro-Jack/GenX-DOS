Galactic Attack

Datasoft (Steve Bjork), 1983 — first-person pseudo-3D space shooter. View from the cockpit of a fighter as alien ships swoop in waves; targeting reticle stays centred, your job is to crab the ship under the reticle on each incoming threat. A CoCo take on the Star Wars / Buck Rogers arcade style.

Star-field horizon, central crosshair, ships appear as dots that grow into recognisable silhouettes as they close. Hold a steady ship and lead the dots into the crosshair. Each ship has a different attack pattern — straight chargers, weavers, and the high-value capital ship that takes multiple hits.

Getting started

  1. Tape auto-loads; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press fire (L Alt) at the title.

Controls

Strategy

  • Lead the weavers. Don't track them with your cursor; pre-position the crosshair where they'll be on the next beat.
  • Capital ships first. They're slower and worth more — clear them while small fighters are still distant.
  • Steady horizon. Wild rolling makes the relative motion of incoming ships unreadable; small corrections only.
  • Range matters. Closer ships are larger and easier to hit; let some come in rather than chasing distant pixels.
