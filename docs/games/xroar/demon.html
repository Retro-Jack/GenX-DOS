Demon Attack

Imagic (Rob Fulop), 1982 — fixed-screen space shooter. Bat-winged demons descend in pairs from the top, alternating high formations with diving attacks. CoCo port of Fulop's classic Atari 2600 title, with the same Galaxian-via-Space-Invaders feel.

You're stationed on the lunar surface, demons descend from above in waves. Each demon takes two hits — the second pass it splits into two faster demons that dive at you. Player ship moves only left/right; demons fire down at varied angles. Survive each wave to advance.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press joystick fire (L Alt) at the title.

Controls

KeyAction
← →Slide left / right
L AltFire (one shot on-screen at a time)

Strategy

  • Kill split demons fast. The two-stage demon mechanic is the wave's whole tension — the second-form demons accelerate to dive attacks; don't let multiples split at once.
  • One-shot on-screen. The single-bullet rule means you have to commit to each shot. Don't shoot through demons you can't hit.
  • Bottom of screen safer than centre. Demon dive trajectories arc through middle altitude — sit low until the last beat, then strafe.
  • Bonus ship every 10,000. The CoCo port keeps the 2600's bonus-ship cadence.
