Poltergeist

Med Systems Software (William F. Denman Jr), 1981 — top-down haunted-house action-adventure. Roam a procedurally-generated mansion, gathering crucifixes to perform the exorcism in the central chamber. Wandering ghosts drain your life on contact; a poltergeist makes furniture fly at random moments.

Top-down room-by-room mansion crawl. Each room has random furniture; furniture occasionally takes flight when the poltergeist is active. Find six crucifixes hidden in the rooms, then return to the central chamber to win. Touch a ghost = lose HP. Find candles for light, holy water as a defensive item.

Getting started

  1. Tape auto-loads; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title; you start in the central chamber.

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Walk (4-direction)

Strategy

  • Map the mansion. Rooms aren't randomised between games once started — pencil a grid so you don't waste lamp-oil retreading corridors.
  • Crucifixes near the centre. Crosses spawn biased to interior rooms; sweep clockwise from the central chamber.
  • Poltergeist windows are audible. The musical sting before flying-furniture warns you — pause at thresholds when you hear it.
  • Holy water is a panic button. Stuns the nearest ghost — save for the last sprint back to the central chamber.
