Attack Force

Big Five Software, 1980 — a top-down arena chase. Eight enemy ships hunt you through a block maze, trying to ram you head-on; out-manoeuvre and shoot them first.

You drive a ship through a scrolling block-grid maze while enemy ramships and flagships converge to ram you. Movement and firing are 4-way and variable-rate — weave through the corridors and fire down them.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  3. Read the controls the game prints on its title screen, then press the key it names to begin (often CLEAR — the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home).

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Move in four directions (variable speed)
SpaceFire
EscBREAK — interrupt / abort to the BASIC READY prompt

Strategy

  • Shoot down the lane. Enemies come straight at you — a well-timed shot kills a rammer before it lands.
  • Don't get pinned. If ships close from two sides at once, slip past one rather than trading the hit.
  • Use the maze. Corners break a rammer's line; round one and fire back as it follows.

The exact keys are shown on the game's title / instruction screen (these older type-in-era games list their controls before play). Read them there, then begin.

The top-left CLEAR button sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key (Home / Delete) — several of these games use CLEAR (to begin a game, or in-play). Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
