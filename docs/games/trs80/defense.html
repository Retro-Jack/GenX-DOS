Defense Command

Big Five Software, 1981 — a Defender-style side-scroller. Patrol a scrolling planet surface and stop alien landers from carrying off your fuel cells.

System help — the TRS-80 Model III controls

Fly left and right over a wrapping landscape. Landers descend to grab fuel cells and haul them away — shoot the landers, catch falling cells, and drop an Anti-Matter bomb when the screen gets swamped.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  3. Read the controls the game prints on its title screen, then press the key it names to begin (often CLEAR — the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home).

Strategy

  • Protect the cells. A lander that reaches the top with a cell becomes a fast mutant — kill landers before they climb.
  • Catch what falls. Shoot a laden lander and catch the dropping cell before it hits the ground.
  • Bomb the swarm, not the single. Save the Anti-Matter bombs for the moments you're genuinely surrounded.

The CLEAR button under the machine sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key (Home / Delete) — several of these games use CLEAR (to begin a game, or in-play). Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
