Home Computer · Tandy TRS-80 Model III

Armored Patrol

Adventure International (Wayne Westmoreland & Terry Gilman), 1981 — a first-person 3-D tank battle in the spirit of Battlezone. Roll across a wireframe field hunting enemy tanks and robots.

You see the field from inside your tank: a vector horizon with enemies as wireframe shapes. The two treads are driven separately (as on the Battlezone twin sticks) — run both to drive straight, one to turn.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  3. Read the controls the game prints on its title screen, then press the key it names to begin (often CLEAR — the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home).

Controls

KeyAction
A / ZLeft tread forward / back

EscBREAK — interrupt / abort to the BASIC READY prompt

Strategy

  • Both treads to charge, one to turn. Drive the treads against each other to spin on the spot and bring a target into your sights.
  • Use the range. Line a tank up dead-centre before firing — angled shots miss at distance.
  • Keep moving after a shot. A stationary tank is an easy return target.

This game prints its own key list on its title / instruction screen — read it there; the keys below are the verified scheme.

The CLEAR button under the machine sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key (Home / Delete) — several of these games use CLEAR (to begin a game, or in-play). Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
