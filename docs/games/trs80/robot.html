Robot Attack

Big Five Software, 1981 — the first TRS-80 game with synthesised speech. Fight through a building of killer robots in the style of Berzerk, blasting them before they corner you.

System help — the TRS-80 Model III controls

Move room to room shooting robots that home in from all sides, while the machine taunts you with digitised speech. You move and shoot with the arrow keys — combine two for diagonals. Keep moving; standing still gets you surrounded.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Move and shoot in eight directions (combine arrows for diagonals)
EscBREAK — interrupt / abort to the BASIC READY prompt

Strategy

  • Never stop. Robots converge on your position — constant movement keeps the angles open.
  • Fire down corridors. Line up rows of robots so one shot does double duty.
  • Mind the walls. Robots (and your own shots) die on the electrified walls — herd them into one.
