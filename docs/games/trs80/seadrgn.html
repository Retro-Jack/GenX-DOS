Sea Dragon

Adventure International (Wayne Westmoreland & Terry Gilman), 1982 — a horizontally-scrolling submarine epic. Dive through undersea caverns past mines, gun turrets and closing walls to reach and destroy the reactor at the end.

Your sub scrolls right through a long, hand-built cavern. Wall-mounted guns, floating mines, sonar-guided torpedoes and tightening passages all stand between you and the reactor core. Mind your air supply.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  3. Read the controls the game prints on its title screen, then press the key it names to begin (often CLEAR — the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home).

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Steer the sub
SpaceFire torpedoes (forward)
EnterFire upward (vertical missiles)
EscBREAK — interrupt / abort to the BASIC READY prompt

Strategy

  • Shoot the gun turrets early. Take them out as they scroll in, before their fire fills the passage.
  • Use both guns. Forward torpedoes (Space) clear the path; the upward shot (Enter) takes ceiling turrets and mines.
  • Watch your air. Surfacing and air pockets keep you alive; running out is as fatal as a mine.

The top-left CLEAR button sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key (Home / Delete) — several of these games use CLEAR (to begin a game, or in-play). Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
