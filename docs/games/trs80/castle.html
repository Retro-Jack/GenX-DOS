Space Castle

Cornsoft Group, 1982, written by Charles Guy — rotate, thrust and shoot your way through three spinning armour shields to the Castle at the centre.

System help — the TRS-80 Model III controls

You are running out of ships, and the only place to get more is the Space Castle. Three spinning walls of armour stand between you and it, so you have to shoot a way through — and the Castle fires back the moment a clear line opens between the two of you. Intelligent mines come out to hunt you down while you work.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  3. Press ? to read the game's own rules screen, then Enter to come back.
  4. Press a digit from 0 to 7 to choose a skill level — that also starts the game.

Strategy

  • Cut one channel, not three. The walls spin independently, so a hole only helps when all three line up — keep widening the same gap rather than starting fresh ones.
  • A clear line works both ways. The moment you can see the Castle, it can see you. Open your channel, then move off the line while you wait for the shot.
  • Thrust in taps. Momentum carries, and drifting into a spinning wall costs a ship — short bursts keep you in control.
  • Deal with the mines first. They chase, they are worth 50 apiece, and trying to out-fly them while lining up a shot is how runs end.

The controls above come from the game's own rules screen, which you can read at any time from the title page with ?.

The CLEAR button under the machine sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key (Home / Delete) — several of these games use CLEAR (to begin a game, or in-play). Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
