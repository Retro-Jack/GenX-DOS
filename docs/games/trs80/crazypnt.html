Crazy Painter

Cornsoft Group, 1982 — a frantic paint-the-floor chase. Cover the whole floor with paint while dodging the creatures that smear it and the steamroller that flattens you.

System help — the TRS-80 Model III controls

Drive your painter across the floor laying down colour. Spilt-paint creatures track through your work and undo it, and a steamroller patrols the edges. Hit a paint puddle to clean your wheels when they gum up.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  3. Read the controls the game prints on its title screen, then press the key it names to begin (often CLEAR — the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home).

Strategy

  • Paint in strips. Sweep methodically so you can see what's left, rather than zig-zagging.
  • Avoid the steamroller. It runs fixed patrols — learn its path and cross behind it.
  • Clean your wheels. Clogged wheels stop painting — dip into a puddle to reset them.

The exact keys are shown on the game's title / instruction screen (these older type-in-era games list their controls before play). Read them there, then begin.

The CLEAR button under the machine sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key (Home / Delete) — several of these games use CLEAR (to begin a game, or in-play). Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
