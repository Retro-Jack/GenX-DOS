MSX — Controls

A standard MSX with cart slot, keyboard and a joystick port. WebMSX maps everything to your PC keyboard out of the box; every game auto-loads from the menu and boots straight to play.

Notes

Every MSX game in this build is a cartridge and auto-boots straight to play. To restart, reload the page.

Press Page Up (STOP) twice quickly to break out of a hung BASIC program.

Mapping follows WebMSX (MIT, ppeccin/WebMSX).
