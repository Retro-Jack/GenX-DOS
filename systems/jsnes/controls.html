NES — Controls

The NES, running in your browser. Released 1983 in Japan as the Famicom; the global cartridge library is ~700 titles, of which a dozen or so genre-definers sit in the bundled menu. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.

Player 1 controller

↑ ↓ ← →D-pad
XA button (primary action — jump / fire in most games)
ZB button (secondary — run / shoot / sub-weapon)

A USB gamepad works automatically — D-pad, A and B as marked, Start on Start and Back on Select.

The save / load controls in the bottom-left corner each open a menu of five save slots — a full machine snapshot per slot, so they work mid-game even in titles with no save of their own. Slots are per-game and survive a reload. To restart, reload the page.

Notes

The browser may briefly mute audio until you click the game area — that's the standard Web Audio autoplay policy, not a bug.
