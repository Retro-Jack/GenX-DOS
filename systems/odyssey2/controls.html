Odyssey² — Controls

This bundle runs in your browser. The Magnavox Odyssey² (1978, "Videopac" in Europe) was unusual for its era: every console had a full 47-key membrane keyboard built in alongside its two joysticks, so some games are typed rather than played on the stick. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.

Notes

The Odyssey² BIOS (o2rom.bin, ©1978 Magnavox/Philips) is bundled with this site — the system can't boot without it.

The save / load controls in the bottom-left corner each open a menu of five save slots — a full machine snapshot per slot, so they work mid-game even in titles with no save of their own. Slots are per-game and survive a reload. To restart, reload the page.

Emulator: libretro-o2em (GPL-2.0+; original o2em by Daniel Boris / Andre de la Rocha) compiled to WebAssembly with a custom SDL2 frontend. Shipped locally from this site; no network calls at runtime.
