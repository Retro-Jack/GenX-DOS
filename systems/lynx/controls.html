Handheld · Atari Lynx (1989)

Atari Lynx — Controls

The Lynx runs on handy (the Atari Lynx libretro core) via EmulatorJS, with the 512-byte Lynx boot ROM bundled. The Lynx was the first colour handheld, with hardware sprite-scaling and rotation that several of these games show off. Click the screen first so the emulator takes keyboard input.

Controls

D-pad on the arrows, the two face buttons on Z / X, and the Lynx's twin Option buttons on Shift / Ctrl:

KeyAtari Lynx
↑ ↓ ← →D-pad
XA button
ZB button
ShiftOption 1
CtrlOption 2
EnterPause

The Lynx had no fixed "up" — it could be flipped, and games used A / B / Option 1 / Option 2 however they liked (fire, jump, weapon-switch, menu…). Each game's notes spell out what they do.
