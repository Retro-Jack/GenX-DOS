Handheld · Atari Lynx (1989)

Atari Lynx — Controls

The Lynx, running in your browser, with the 512-byte Lynx boot ROM bundled. The Lynx was the first colour handheld, with hardware sprite-scaling and rotation that several of these games show off. Click the screen first so the emulator takes keyboard input.

Controls

D-pad on the arrows, the two face buttons on Z / X, and the Lynx's twin Option buttons on Shift / Ctrl:

KeyAtari Lynx
↑ ↓ ← →D-pad
XA button
ZB button
ShiftOption 1
CtrlOption 2
EnterPause

The Lynx had no fixed "up" — it could be flipped, and games used A / B / Option 1 / Option 2 however they liked (fire, jump, weapon-switch, menu…). Each game's notes spell out what they do.

A USB gamepad works automatically — it's auto-mapped to the default scheme above (D-pad, A / B, and the Option buttons).

The save / load controls in the bottom-left corner each open a menu of five save slots — a full machine snapshot per slot, so they work mid-game even in titles with no save of their own. Slots are per-game and survive a reload. To restart, reload the page.

Emulator: handy (libretro core) under EmulatorJS (GPL-3.0); the 512-byte Lynx boot ROM is bundled. Both shipped locally from this site; no network calls at runtime.
