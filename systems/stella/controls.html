Atari 2600 — Controls

The 2600 is a one-button joystick plus a row of console switches. Everything is on the keyboard — click the screen first so it has focus.

Joystick

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Stick direction
SpaceFire button

Console switches

KeyAction
EnterGAME RESET — starts / restarts most games
VGAME SELECT — cycles the game variant

Notes

Most 2600 games (Space Invaders, Asteroids, Centipede, Pitfall) won't begin play until you press Enter (the GAME RESET switch) after the title. Press V (GAME SELECT) at the title to choose a game variant first.

Paddle games (Breakout) steer with the ← / → arrow keys.

The save / load controls in the bottom-left corner each open a menu of five save slots — a full machine snapshot per slot, so they work mid-game even in titles with no save of their own. Slots are per-game and survive a reload. To restart, reload the page.
