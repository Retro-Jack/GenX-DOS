Speed / Bingo Math

Commodore / Bally Midway, 1982 — two educational mini-games on one cartridge. Speed Math: drill arithmetic against a timer. Bingo Math: number-line bingo where rolls are arithmetic problems to solve.

Picks one of two modes at boot. Speed Math throws timed arithmetic problems (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division — configurable). Bingo Math is a 5×5 bingo card; you draw a problem each turn and mark its answer if present. First to a row/column/diagonal wins.

Getting started

  1. Cart autoboots; click the canvas first.
  2. At the start menu, pick Speed or Bingo, then operation, then difficulty.

Notes

  • Bingo Math vs. paper bingo. Same odds, same card geometry; the math layer is the educational substrate.
  • Speed Math difficulty tiers. Easy = single-digit; Medium = two-digit operands; Hard = three-digit with carries / borrows.
  • Multi-player. Both modes support 2 players passing the joystick — Speed alternates rounds, Bingo plays separate cards.
  • One of the few educational MAX carts. Most MAX titles are action games; Speed/Bingo represents the platform's school-market push.
