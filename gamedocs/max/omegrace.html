Omega Race

Midway, 1982 — MAX cart of Midway's 1981 vector arcade. Orbit the inside of a closed rectangle; enemies orbit too; shots bounce off the walls.

A rectangular arena with your ship orbiting the inner perimeter under Newtonian inertia. Rotate to aim, thrust to accelerate. Drone Ships are inert targets; Command Ships shoot back; Death Ships spawn from killed commands and track you; Photon Mines drift toward you and explode on contact. Your shots bounce off the arena edges.

Getting started

  1. Cart autoboots; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to spawn.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Rotate left / right
Numpad 8Thrust
Numpad 0 / 5Fire

Strategy

  • Tap-thrust, never hold. Inertia is the killer — long burns commit you to a drift you can't reverse before colliding.
  • Bounce shots are real flanking. Fire at the wall to hit an enemy orbiting opposite your position.
  • Commands first. Letting commands live escalates drones into death ships fast — kill commands while they're still alone.
  • Mines are bonus targets. Slow, predictable, high-value. Take them when the orbital threat is low.
