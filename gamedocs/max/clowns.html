Clowns

Commodore / Midway, 1982 — the launch cart for the Japan-only MAX Machine. Port of Midway's 1978 arcade. Bounce clowns off a see-saw to pop balloons floating above.

Two clowns on a see-saw at the bottom of the screen. You control the platform sliding left/right; the clown in flight lands on the empty seat, catapulting the other one upward to pop balloons. Three rows of balloons, three colours, three point values. Miss the see-saw and you lose a clown.

Getting started

  1. Cart autoboots — this emulator, in Ultimax mode, loads clowns.crt straight into gameplay.
  2. Click the canvas first to give it keyboard focus.

Strategy

  • Predict the arc. The flying clown follows a fixed parabola — slide the see-saw to the landing point a beat early.
  • Top row first. Higher points and the clown bounces longer per pop — clearing rows top-down is fastest.
  • Don't chase pops. The clown auto-pops every balloon it touches on the way up; aim for clusters, not individuals.
  • Speed escalation. Each cleared screen ramps the see-saw response speed. The "feel" of the controls changes every round.
