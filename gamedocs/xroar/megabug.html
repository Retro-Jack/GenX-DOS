Mega-Bug

Computerware (Bob Withers), 1981 — top-down maze shooter heavily inspired by Centipede. Eight-direction movement, eight-direction fire, segmented bug-trains snake through a maze of mushrooms. The CoCo's first big arcade-style hit.

Centipede-with-mushrooms-laid-out-as-walls. Shoot mushrooms to widen your firing lanes, shoot the bug at its head to kill it in one hit, shoot it mid-body to split it into two faster halves. Other critters spawn — spider (chases you), scorpion (poisons mushrooms making them faster), flea (drops new mushrooms).

Getting started

  1. Cart auto-boots; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press the joystick fire button (this emulator maps to L Alt) at the title.

Strategy

  • Aim for the head. One-hit kill on the leading segment removes the whole bug; body shots multiply your problem.
  • Don't level the field. Some mushrooms are cover — keep two columns thick at the screen edges so the bug bends through chokepoints you can pre-aim.
  • Spider counter-rush. When the spider drops in, charge straight at it firing — point-blank kills before it can zigzag.
  • Poisoned mushrooms accelerate the next bug. Clear scorpion-tainted mushrooms before the next wave starts.
