Polaris

Tandy (Sega licensed port), 1983 — side-view submarine arcade port. Pilot a Polaris submarine: fire missiles upward at surface ships, dodge depth charges falling from above. Same Sega cabinet as the 1980 arcade original, recompiled for the CoCo's PMODE 4 graphics.

Side-view, your sub on the left half of the screen, sea surface across the top, enemy ships and aircraft above the waterline. Missiles fire vertically; depth charges drop in patterns from passing ships. Multiple ship types: destroyers (drop in bursts), aircraft (fast horizontal pass with single bomb), helicopters (loiter and drop).

Getting started

  1. Tape auto-loads; click the canvas first.
  2. Press fire (L Alt) at the title.

Controls

KeyAction
← →Move sub left / right
↑ ↓Dive / surface
L AltFire missile (limited per-screen)

Strategy

  • Strafe-fire on aircraft. Fast horizontal aircraft survive single shots if mis-led — fire as they enter the screen, not when overhead.
  • Dive under depth-charge bursts. Destroyer depth charges drop in tight columns; slide laterally a sub-width to avoid each column.
  • Helicopter timing. Helicopters hover before dropping — count to about a beat after they stop, then strafe.
  • Don't surface unless safe. Surfacing makes you a bigger target; only break the waterline to reposition under low-clearance threats.
