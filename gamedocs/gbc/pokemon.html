Pokémon Red

The game that turned the Game Boy into a phenomenon. Catch, train and battle 151 creatures across the Kanto region on your way to becoming Champion.

A turn-based RPG: pick a starter, criss-cross Kanto catching Pokémon in Poké Balls, level your team, beat the eight Gym Leaders for their badges, foil Team Rocket, and finally take on the Elite Four. Deep, friendly, and enormous for a 1 MB cartridge.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, Enter (Start) → NEW GAME; name yourself and your rival.
  2. Choose a starter from Prof. Oak's lab (Bulbasaur / Charmander / Squirtle), then head north.

Strategy

  • Type matchups win battles. Water > Fire > Grass > Water; learn the chart and you'll out-level nothing-fights with ease.
  • Catch a varied team. Cover types so no Gym wall-checks you — a flyer, a water mon, an electric, etc.
  • Save often (menu → SAVE). Battery save persists in the browser; this is a long game.
  • Stock Poké Balls and Potions before routes and caves — running dry deep in Mt. Moon or Rock Tunnel is a long walk back.
