Handheld · Nintendo Game Boy (1989)

Castlevania: The Adventure

The first Castlevania on a handheld. Christopher Belmont, a hundred years before the NES original, whips his way up Dracula's castle.

A deliberate, methodical platformer across four stages of crumbling battlements, spike pits and collapsing floors. The whip is your only weapon — upgrade it via candle drops, but lose the upgrades when you take a hit. Slower and tougher than its console siblings.

Getting started

  1. Click the screen, press Enter (Start) at the title.
  2. Whip the candles/orbs for whip upgrades and hearts.

Strategy

  • Protect your whip level. The triple-fireball whip is powerful but one hit drops you back to leather — play cautiously once upgraded.
  • Jumps are committal. No mid-air control to speak of; line up before you leap, especially over the disappearing blocks in stage 3.
  • Memorise the gauntlets. The collapsing-floor and rope-climb sections are pattern tests — take them slowly.
  • Save-state before bosses via the EmulatorJS toolbar; this one is old-school punishing.
