Handheld · Nintendo Game Boy Color (2000)

Wario Land 3

A full-colour Game Boy Color platformer and one of the system's best — a sprawling, Metroidvania-flavoured treasure hunt built around Wario's invincible, transformation-based gameplay.

Trapped in a music box, Wario explores its world to collect treasures that unlock new areas and abilities. Wario can't die from enemies — instead they transform him (on fire, zombie, puffed-up, flattened), and you use each transformation to reach new places. Every level is revisited as you gain powers, uncovering its four hidden treasure chests.

Getting started

  1. Click the screen, press Enter (Start), begin a new game.
  2. Enter a level from the overworld map; grab keys to open its grey treasure chests.

Strategy

  • Enemies are tools. Getting set on fire, zombified or flattened is how you reach blocked areas — seek out the transformation you need.
  • Revisit everything. New abilities open earlier levels' locked sections; the four chests per level often need powers from much later.
  • Collect the music coins and golf mini-bits, but treasures drive progress — prioritise keys and chests.
  • Battery save persists in the browser; it's a long, methodical hunt — pace yourself.
