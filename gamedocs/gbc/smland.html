Handheld · Nintendo Game Boy (1989)

Super Mario Land

Mario's handheld debut — a launch title set in Sarasaland, with submarine and aeroplane shooter stages and a slightly different physics feel to the NES games.

Guide Mario across four worlds (12 levels) to rescue Princess Daisy from Tatanga. Run, jump and fireball your way past Sarasaland's odd enemies; two levels swap platforming for an auto-scrolling Marine Pop submarine or Sky Pop plane shoot-'em-up.

Getting started

  1. Click the screen, press Enter (Start) at the title.
  2. Reach the level-end gate; the upper exit warps you to a bonus.

Strategy

  • Grab the Superball flower. Its ball bounces at angles and collects coins — far more useful than the NES fireball.
  • Hit the top exit. Each level's gate has an upper door (reach it via the lift) for a bonus mini-game.
  • The shooter stages (worlds 2-3 and 4-3) reward steady fire — learn the boss patterns at the end.
  • Momentum matters — Mario's a touch floatier here; hold Z for the running jumps over wide gaps.
