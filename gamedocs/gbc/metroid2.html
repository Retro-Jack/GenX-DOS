Handheld · Nintendo Game Boy (1991)

Metroid II: Return of Samus

Samus descends into the Metroids' home planet SR388 to wipe out the species. A sprawling, atmospheric exploration game squeezed onto the Game Boy.

No map and no hand-holding: spelunk the tunnels of SR388, hunting down Metroids one at a time (a counter on-screen tracks how many remain). Each clutch killed lowers the lava and opens the way deeper. Find missile tanks, energy tanks and the classic suit upgrades — Morph Ball, bombs, Spider Ball, Screw Attack — to reach the Queen.

Getting started

  1. Click the screen, press Enter (Start) at the title.
  2. Kill every Metroid in an area to drain the acid blocking the path down.

Strategy

  • Map as you go. There's no in-game map — pen and paper (or a side note) saves a lot of backtracking.
  • Bomb every suspicious wall/floor. Morph-Ball bombs reveal hidden passages and tanks everywhere.
  • Save at the recharge stations (and use the save / load buttons before each Metroid fight — the Alpha/Gamma/Zeta/Omega forms hit hard).
  • Spider Ball is the key traversal upgrade — cling to walls and ceilings to reach otherwise-impossible ledges.
