Handheld · Nintendo Game Boy (1989)

Tetris

The pack-in that sold the Game Boy. Alexey Pajitnov's falling-block puzzle, the version millions learned the Korobeiniki theme from.

Stack the falling tetrominoes into complete horizontal lines to clear them; clear four at once (a "Tetris") for the big score. The board fills as you climb levels and the pieces drop faster — survive as long as you can.

Getting started

  1. Click the screen, press Enter (Start) past the title.
  2. Pick A-TYPE (endless) or B-TYPE (clear 25 lines), then a starting level/height.

Controls

KeyAction
← →Move piece
↓Soft drop (speed the piece down)
X (A) / Z (B)Rotate the piece
EnterSTART / pause

Strategy

  • Keep the stack flat. Avoid deep single-width wells except one column you're saving for a long bar.
  • Hunt the Tetris. Four lines cleared together score far more than four singles — leave that one column open.
  • Soft-drop, don't slam. The GB version has no hard drop; tap ↓ to place quickly but keep control.
  • High starting level nets more points per line if you can handle the speed.
