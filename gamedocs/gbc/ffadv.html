Final Fantasy Adventure

Square, 1991. The first game in the Mana series (a Final Fantasy spin-off) — a real-time, Zelda-style action-RPG with charge-up combat, swappable weapons, magic and stat levelling, on a quest to reach the Tree of Mana.

A boy and a girl escape the Dark Lord's dungeon and set out to protect the Tree of Mana. Combat is real-time and top-down like Zelda, but with RPG bones: you hold the attack button to fill a power gauge for a heavy strike, pick which weapon to swing (each also clears different obstacles — the axe fells trees, the sickle cuts brush, the chain crosses gaps), cast magic, and pour level-up points into the stats you choose. Chocobo rides, companions and a battery save round it out.

Getting started

  1. Press Enter (START) at the title and begin a new game.
  2. Move with the D-pad; attack with X. The power gauge at the bottom refills after each swing — strike when it reads MAX for a much stronger hit.
  3. Press Enter (START) for the Item / Magic / Equip / Ask menu to equip a weapon and ready magic, then use it with Z. V (SELECT) saves.

Strategy

  • Time the MAX Attack. The power gauge refills after each swing; waiting for it to read MAX before you strike does far more damage — vital against bosses (a higher Will stat refills it faster).
  • Match weapon to obstacle. Axe fells trees, sickle cuts grass, the morning-star/chain crosses gaps — and each weapon levels up with use, so favour the one you're building.
  • Choose stats on level-up. Stamina (HP), Power (attack), Wisdom (white magic), Will (black magic) — a balanced spread carries you through the mid-game.
  • Keep healing magic ready. Cure and Heal are lifelines; top up MP at inns before a dungeon.
  • Save often. The cartridge has a battery save (this emulator keeps it) — save before bosses and long dungeon dives.
