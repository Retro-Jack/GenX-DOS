Handheld · Nintendo Game Boy (1989)

Gargoyle's Quest

Capcom, 1990. A Ghosts 'n Goblins spin-off that makes the villain the hero: you play Firebrand, the Red Arremer demon, across an action-platformer laced with RPG-style overworld exploration.

The Ghoul Realm is under attack, and Firebrand sets out to save it. Between fast side-scrolling battle stages you roam a top-down overworld — visiting towns, talking to NPCs, and hunting the vials and items that extend your wing power and firepower. Firebrand's signature move is flight: a jump can become a hover, and later upgrades keep you aloft longer, which is the key to reaching places a normal platformer hero couldn't.

Getting started

  1. Click the screen, press Enter (START) at the title.
  2. Explore the overworld map into towns and battle scenes; press X to talk / confirm.
  3. In an action stage: jump with X, hold X to flap and hover, and shoot with Z.

Strategy

  • Hover is your main tool. Hold X after a jump to flap and drift — it crosses gaps and hazards no ground jump can. Early flight time is short; extend it with wing-power vials.
  • Cling to walls. Firebrand grabs vertical surfaces and can jump off them — use walls to climb shafts and reposition mid-fight.
  • Collect upgrades or stall out. Vials and items from battle scenes and NPCs raise your flight time, fireball strength and life bar; later regions are impassable without them.
  • Talk to everyone. The overworld is half the game — townsfolk hand out keys, passwords and directions, and some paths open only after a specific conversation or item.
  • Mind the passwords. Progress is kept by password rather than battery — note them down, or lean on this emulator's save / load slots (five per game) between stages.
