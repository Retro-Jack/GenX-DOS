Handheld · Nintendo Game Boy (1992)

Kirby's Dream Land

The debut of Kirby (and HAL/Sakurai's pink hero). A gentle, charming platformer — no copy abilities yet, just inhale, float and spit.

Float through five short stages of Dream Land to recover the stolen food and Sparkling Stars from King Dedede. Kirby can fly indefinitely by puffing up, swallow enemies and spit them back as projectiles. Famously beginner-friendly — but a hidden hard mode awaits those who finish it.

Getting started

  1. Click the screen, press Enter (Start) at the title.
  2. Hold ↑ to puff up and fly; press Z to inhale enemies.

Strategy

  • Flight trivialises most hazards — but flying releases your air as a weak puff, so land to inhale a real projectile.
  • Swallow, don't just spit. Pressing ↓ with an enemy in your mouth swallows it; spitting it (a star) is your main attack.
  • Hunt the spicy curry and mints for temporary fire-breath and air refills.
  • After the credits, a button code unlocks a tougher remix — try it once you know the stages.
