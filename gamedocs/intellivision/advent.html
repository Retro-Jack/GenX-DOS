Advanced Dungeons & Dragons: Cloudy Mountain

Mattel, 1982 — designed by Tom Loughry. The first home video game licensed by TSR — sold both as Adventure and AD&D internationally.

An overworld map of forests, rivers, and mountains hides two halves of a magic crown atop Cloudy Mountain at the centre. Cross the map dodging tornadoes; enter each mountain to navigate randomly-laid dungeon screens of caves, monsters, and treasures. Find arrows in the caves to shoot bats, spiders, and dragons. Recover both halves of the crown to win.

Getting started

  1. The overworld shows your party (a single explorer) at the south edge of the map.
  2. Walk to a mountain entrance to dive into a cave system; mountains contain randomised tunnel layouts.
  3. Pick up arrows in the caves — they're your only weapon. Reach the crown half and exit safely.

Hazards

SnakeHides in cave corners — visible only when adjacent.
DemonLurks in deepest tunnels — only the centre arrows hurt it.
Dragon (Cloudy Mountain only)Multi-screen boss. Multiple arrows; aim carefully.
Tornado (overworld)Wandering wind — death by contact. Track its path before crossing open ground.

Strategy

  • Pick up every arrow. Arrows are finite per cave and the dragon needs several.
  • Map your route on the overworld. Tornadoes path predictably — wait one cycle before crossing.
  • Listen for the sound effects. The game tells you what's in the next room via stings — bats whir, spiders hiss, dragons roar from a distance.
  • The crown halves are in two different mountains. Plan to visit both Cloudy peaks — the easier south mountains usually give you the first half.

Intellivision controls

The disc is 16-direction; arrows fire in the disc's last direction. The three side buttons are functionally identical (the two upper buttons share a wire with the lower buttons). The keypad is unused during cave play but the printed overlay is mostly map decoration.
