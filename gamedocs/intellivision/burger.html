BurgerTime

Mattel, 1982 — port of Data East's 1982 arcade hit (originally titled "Hamburger" in Japan)

Chef Peter Pepper walks ladders and platforms over giant hamburger ingredients. Walk across a bun, lettuce, patty, or top to make each layer fall one level; build a complete burger by sending all parts down to the trays at the bottom. Mr. Hot Dog, Mr. Pickle, and Mr. Egg chase you; salt-shaker pickups let you stun them briefly.

Getting started

  1. Pick a game variant at the title.
  2. Walk across every section of every ingredient — top buns, lettuce, patty, tomato, bottom buns — to drop them all into the tray.
  3. Three lives. Touching an enemy ends a life.

Strategy

  • Crush enemies with ingredients. A falling ingredient takes out any enemy standing on it — line up your walks to drop layers onto pursuers for bonus points and a clear corridor.
  • Conserve salt. You start with a small stack; each use is a one-shot stun. Save it for late-level swarms.
  • Cascade kills. Dropping a stack of multiple ingredients in sequence is the score multiplier — chain them when possible.
  • Watch the ladders. An enemy climbing a ladder from below can corner you on a platform — change levels early.

Intellivision controls

Standard 16-direction disc; the side button throws salt. The keypad overlay shows the enemy bestiary but isn't used during play.
