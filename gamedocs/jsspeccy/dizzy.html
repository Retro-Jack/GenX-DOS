Dizzy — The Ultimate Cartoon Adventure

Codemasters, 1987 — the Oliver Twins. First in the long-running Yolkfolk series; arcade adventure with inventory puzzles, a £1.99 budget price, and an egg as the protagonist.

Dizzy, an anthropomorphic egg, hunts the wizard Zaks who has cursed his hometown. The world is a single connected map of single-screen rooms — collect items, combine them with the environment in the right order, and reach Zaks's lair for the showdown.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. Dizzy starts at the bridge. Walk left or right, jump gaps, fall (he survives any fall) and explore.
  3. Pick up an object by walking over it. Carry up to 3 items at once.
  4. Drop or use items via the inventory; specific items unlock specific rooms.

Controls (default)

This game supports a USB gamepad — left/right on the d-pad or stick walks, A jumps and B opens the inventory. Start begins a game at the title.

KeyAction
ZWalk left
XWalk right
SpaceJump (Dizzy rolls; distance scales with run speed)
EnterInventory — pick up / drop / use

Inventory puzzles (early game)

  • Bottle from the kitchen → fill at the well → use on the dragon's fire.
  • Iron crown → wear into the magical mirror room (other crowns won't do).
  • Cherry → feed to the bird; the bird drops the worm which you need elsewhere.
  • Spellbook → only readable after you light the right candle.

Strategy

  • 3-item limit is the puzzle. Plan which 3 items you need in any zone; drop unused ones in marked safe rooms so you can find them later.
  • Dizzy survives any fall. Falling damage is zero; use vertical drops as fast travel.
  • Map the world. ~50 screens; sketch the connections — some only-one-way drops mean you can't backtrack.
  • Zaks fight is reflex-only. All inventory work happens before; the boss is straightforward jumping.
