Jet Set Willy

Software Projects, 1984 — Matthew Smith. Sixty rooms in Willy's newly-bought mansion; tidy them all before Maria lets you go to bed. The Spectrum original is the canonical version.

Willy threw a wild party. Now the housekeeper Maria refuses to let him sleep until every glass, bottle, and stray item is collected. The mansion is a single huge interconnected maze — find all 83 items, then return to the master bedroom and step into bed.

Getting started

  1. Press any key at the title.
  2. You begin in the Master Bedroom with Maria guarding the door. Walk left to start exploring.
  3. Step on any flashing object to collect it.

Notable rooms

  • The Bathroom — easy starter items.
  • The Off Licence / The Wine Cellar — Willy's drinking habits made literal.
  • The Banyan Tree — vertical screen with falling platforms.
  • We must perform a Quirkafleeg — surreal cult-favourite room name.
  • The Forgotten Abbey — easy to die mid-fall.
  • The Attic — famously bugged on the original Speccy release (rooms randomly destabilised); patched in later editions.

Strategy

  • No air timer. Unlike Manic Miner, take your time — but dying respawns you at the room entry, costing a life.
  • Memorise enemy patterns. Most monsters move on a fixed sweep. Find a safe rhythm window.
  • Item completion guards rooms. Some areas only open after collecting specific items elsewhere.
  • End in the bedroom. Once every item is gone, Maria steps aside; walk into the bed to finish.
