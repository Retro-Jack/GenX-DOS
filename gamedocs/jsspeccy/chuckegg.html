Chuckie Egg

A & F Software, 1983 — Nigel Alderton. Spectrum port of the BBC original; one of the platform's earliest classics. Eight screens that loop with a giant duck.

Henhouse Harry runs eight platform-and-ladder screens collecting eggs while hens patrol fixed routes. Get all 12 eggs and the door opens. Clear all eight screens and a giant duck escapes its cage, joining you on the loop.

Getting started

  1. Press 1 at the title for one-player keyboard.
  2. Press R at the title to redefine keys, or keep the defaults.
  3. Collect every yellow egg; the door opens and you proceed.

Controls (default)

This game supports a USB gamepad — the D-pad or left stick runs and climbs, and any face button jumps. Pick the one-player game at the title on the keyboard, and keep its default keys — the pad presses those.

Strategy

  • Anti-clockwise sweep. Plan a single loop — backtracking eats the clock.
  • Hens are predictable until all seed bowls are eaten. Clear hen-adjacent eggs first.
  • The duck = late-game enemy. Stay near ladders so you can drop a floor instantly.
  • Spectrum vs BBC. Slightly slower scroll, simpler audio, but same level layouts.
