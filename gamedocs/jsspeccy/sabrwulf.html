Sabre Wulf

Ultimate Play The Game, 1984 — Tim & Chris Stamper. Top-down jungle maze: Sabreman's first adventure, hunting the four Amulet pieces while every monster known to colourful Speccy art bears down on him.

Sabreman starts at the centre of a 256-screen jungle maze. Four amulet pieces are hidden in the maze; collect all four to escape. Hordes of native fauna — orchids, hippos, rhinos, the titular Sabre Wulf himself — chase you across rooms. You have a sabre for short-range combat and have to keep moving constantly.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. Sabreman appears mid-maze. Move in 8 directions through arched exits.
  3. Pick up amulet pieces by walking over them.
  4. The Sabre Wulf himself appears periodically — much faster than other enemies. Run or use a flower.

Strategy

  • Keep moving. Sabreman is faster than most enemies. Standing still = surrounded.
  • The Sabre Wulf is unkillable. Use a yellow flower or just run to the next screen — he doesn't follow through doorways.
  • Map the maze. 256 rooms with no in-game map. Note distinctive landmarks (statues, river edges) per cluster.
  • Flowers respawn slowly. Don't waste yellow on minor threats.
