Atic Atac

Ultimate Play The Game, 1983 — Tim & Chris Stamper. Top-down dungeon adventure: choose a Knight, Wizard, or Serf and escape the haunted manor with the ACG (Achievement Cup of Gold).

You're trapped in a multi-storey manor packed with ghosts, ghouls, and mummies. Find the three pieces of the Golden Key (their hiding spots differ per character class), assemble them, and escape through the main door. The food roast at the bottom of the screen is your health — every monster hit chews it away.

Getting started

  1. Tape auto-loads. At the title press K Knight / W Wizard / S Serf.
  2. You start in a fixed room; explore by walking through doors.
  3. Pick up food (chicken legs etc) to refill the roast. Drop or use items via the inventory.

Strategy

  • Map every room. The manor has 159 rooms across multiple floors — pen-and-paper grid is essential. Some doors only open from one side.
  • The trapdoor in some rooms drops you a floor — useful shortcut or unwanted detour depending on direction.
  • Eat conservatively. The roast respawns slowly; eating just before a monster fight wastes recovery.
  • Key fragments are class-specific. Knight pieces in one cluster, Wizard in another — the manor's layout doesn't change but the keys do.
