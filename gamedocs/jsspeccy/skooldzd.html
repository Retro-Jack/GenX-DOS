Skool Daze

Microsphere, 1984 — David S. Reidy. A schoolboy's life sim — six classrooms, four teachers, three rival pupils, and a single goal: steal your school report from the safe before the term ends.

Eric must steal his school report from the head's office safe. Combination is split across four teachers — each knows one digit. Hit a teacher with the right object at the right time and they'll mutter their digit. Mix that with avoiding detention, getting to lessons on time, and pranking your three school-mate rivals (Boy Wander, Einstein, Angelface).

Getting started

  1. Press Enter at the title to start.
  2. You start outside the school gates. Run inside, follow the timetable on-screen, and react to events.
  3. Pick up objects (catapult, water pistol, pea-shooter) by stepping on them.
  4. Hit a teacher's head to make them mutter — note the digit; combine four digits for the safe.

Controls (default)

This game supports a USB gamepad — left/right on the D-pad or stick walks, up/down take the stairs, A jumps, B punches, X fires the catapult, Y sits / stands and LB writes on a blackboard.

Strategy

  • Lines = clock. Detention isn't game over but it costs you a whole lesson — and one of your few chances to corner a teacher.
  • Stink bomb the head's office. Mr Wacker leaves; you can sneak past faster.
  • Catapult is the best digit-extractor. Range lets you hit a teacher mid-lesson without getting caught.
  • The combination order matters. Once you have all four digits, enter them at the safe in the head's office in the correct order to unlock and grab the report.
