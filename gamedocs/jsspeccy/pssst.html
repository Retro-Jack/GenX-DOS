Ultimate Play The Game, 1983 — Tim & Chris Stamper. Robbie the Robot defends a single Thyrgodian Megga Chrysanthemum plant from insects, with the right spray for each bug.

Robbie has a flower in a pot, centred at the bottom of the screen. Bugs descend in waves — caterpillars, spiders, hornets, more. Each insect is killed by one specific can in your shed (top of screen): wasps need Hwasp, slugs need Lhasa, and so on. Pick the wrong can and you waste shots; pick the right one and the insect dies in one hit.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. Robbie sits below the plant. Move left/right; press up to climb to the shed shelf and grab a can.
  3. Spray bugs by aiming up (the spray fires in the direction Robbie faces) while moving.
  4. Each wave brings different insects — quickly identify which can is needed.

Strategy

  • Switch cans early. Don't wait until you can identify the bug — read the wave on entry, swap can before it reaches Robbie.
  • The plant takes hits. If a bug touches the flower it loses petals; lose all petals and the round ends.
  • Climb sparingly. Going to the shed leaves the plant unguarded for a few seconds — risky against fast waves.
  • Score scales with combo. Killing several bugs without missing multiplies the per-hit score.
