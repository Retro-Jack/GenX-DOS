King Kong 2: Ikari no Megaton Punch

Konami, 1986 — top-down action-adventure tie-in to the John Guillermin film "King Kong Lives" (1986). You play Kong navigating a sprawling, scrolling jungle/cave/temple map looking for Lady Kong, fighting tanks and helicopters with megaton punches.

Eight-direction top-down scroll, in the Konami Maze of Galious / Vampire Killer family. Find power-ups (palmettos, fruits) for health and special attacks; smash through military hardware that the army keeps sending to recapture you. Boss fights at chosen story points trigger split-screen "punch-out" sub-games.

Getting started

  1. Cart auto-boots; click the canvas first.
  2. Press Space at the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Move Kong in eight directions
SpacePunch (megaton attack — straight-line)
L AltUse held item (rock, bomb)

Strategy

  • Punch in cardinal directions only. Diagonals don't fire — line up the enemy to N/S/E/W before the swing.
  • Eat fruit on sight. Health drips constantly even without damage; fruit pickups are essentially the timer.
  • Boss sub-games use sticks differently. Punch-out fights switch to a side-view face-to-face brawl — rapid alternating ←/→ taps build a charge, fire releases it.
  • Story sequence is hard-gated. Some sections won't progress until you find a specific item; if a passage shimmers but blocks you, you don't have the right key yet.
