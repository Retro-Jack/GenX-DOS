Home Computer · MSX2

Aleste

Compile, 1988 — vertical-scrolling shoot-'em-up; the first game in Compile's long-running Aleste series (later Power Strike on Master System). Six stages of intense bullet-and-formation action with the most varied weapon system on MSX2.

The Raidant fighter flies upward against the Dia 51 organism. Power capsules cycle through eight selectable weapons: Multi (spread), Diagonal, Round (orbiting), Buster (heavy), Ring (back-cover), Sword (shield), Napalm (ground), Charge (laser-rapid). Pick the weapon with a button-tap before grabbing; the wrong choice can leave you under-armed for a boss's pattern.

Getting started

  1. Disk auto-boots; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title to start Area 1.

Controls

Strategy

  • Multi or Diagonal early. Stages 1-2's loose formations reward spread; switch only when the bosses' patterns demand pinpoint Buster.
  • Ring beats turret rings. Stage 3's turret rings tracking the centre-bottom are murderous — the Ring weapon's back-arc keeps Raidant alive while you concentrate forward fire.
  • Sword is a panic button. Costs the weapon slot but gives a spinning shield; one-frame death cancellations.
  • Slowdown isn't lag — it's content. Compile coded slowdown as a feature; when the V9938 chokes, your reaction window expands. Use it.
