Bubble Bobble

Taito, 1987 (MSX port of the 1986 arcade). The co-op bubble-'em-up: Bub and Bob, two bubble dragons, trap monsters in bubbles and pop them across 100 single-screen rounds to rescue their girlfriends from the Cave of Monsters.

Each round is one screen. You attack by spitting a bubble to trap a monster, then jumping into that bubble to burst it — turning the monster into fruit to collect. Clear every monster on the screen to drop to the next round. Bubbles also float, so you can ride your own up to high ledges, and popping several at once rains down higher-value treats. One quirk of the MSX version: you jump with a button, not by pushing up on the stick.

Getting started

  1. The cart autoboots to the title; click the canvas first, then press Space to start.
  2. Trap a monster in a bubble with Space, then jump (L Alt) into the bubble to pop it and grab the fruit.
  3. Remember: jump is L Alt (not up on the stick) — the MSX oddity that catches everyone out.

Strategy

  • Pop bubbles quickly. A trapped monster escapes if you leave it too long — and comes back enraged, faster and pink. Burst it before it breaks free.
  • Chain your pops. Jumping through a row of bubbled monsters bursts them together for far bigger fruit and point values.
  • Don't dawdle. Linger on a round and the invincible whale Baron von Blubba appears to hunt you — clear the screen with some pace.
  • Ride your bubbles. Jump onto your own floating bubbles to reach high platforms and hidden bonus items.
  • EXTEND and two-player. Collect the letters E-X-T-E-N-D across a round for a free life — and note the true ending needs both Bub and Bob; solo play gets the lesser one.
