Handheld · Sega Game Gear (1993)

Dr. Robotnik's Mean Bean Machine

Sega's Western reskin of Puyo Puyo — the brilliant chain-reaction puzzler, dressed up with Robotnik and the Sonic cartoon's robots.

Beans drop in pairs into your well. Connect four or more of the same colour to pop them — and the real game is the chain: arrange your stack so one pop drops beans into another match, and another, sending a flood of garbage "refugee" beans onto your opponent. Out-chain the AI down a ladder of Robotnik's henchmen.

Getting started

  1. Click the screen, press Enter (Start); pick Scenario (1P ladder) or Exercise.
  2. Match four same-colour beans to pop; bury opponents in garbage to win.

Strategy

  • Build chains, don't just clear. Stack colours so a single pop cascades into several — chains are what bury the opponent.
  • Leave a "trigger" column. Set up a tall multi-step chain, then drop the one bean that sets it all off at once.
  • Clear incoming garbage (clear beans next to it to remove it) before it crowds your setup.
  • Keep one side low so you always have room to manoeuvre under pressure.
