Handheld · Sega Game Gear (1993)

Mortal Kombat

The blood-soaked arcade fighter, shrunk onto the Game Gear — and unlike the Nintendo ports, the Sega version kept the gore (with the famous code).

Pick one of the seven warriors and fight up the tournament ladder against the rest, then the bosses Goro and Shang Tsung. Best-of-three rounds; whittle the opponent's health bar with strikes and special moves, and finish a dazed loser with a Fatality. A faithful — if necessarily compressed — handheld take on the original.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, press Enter (Start); choose your fighter.
  2. Win two rounds to advance up the ladder.

Strategy

  • Learn one special per fighter. A reliable projectile or teleport (Scorpion's spear, Sub-Zero's freeze) carries you a long way.
  • Block on reaction. Hold Enter (Start) to block — there's no dedicated block button on the two-button pad; jumping in blind against the CPU gets punished.
  • Sweep and zone rather than brawling toe-to-toe — chip from range, then capitalise.
  • Finish with a Fatality when the round-winning blow leaves them swaying — input the character's code quickly.
