Handheld · Sega Game Gear (1993)

Land of Illusion Starring Mickey Mouse

The sequel to Castle of Illusion and one of the best-looking, best-playing platformers on the Game Gear — a sprawling, slightly Metroidvania-flavoured Mickey adventure.

A magic crystal has drained the colour and joy from the land, and Mickey sets out to restore it. Bounce on enemies, swing your way across gaps, and gather abilities (a magic rope, a shrinking potion, swimming) that open up earlier areas — there's gentle backtracking and a hub-like world map between the platforming stages.

Getting started

  1. Click the screen, press Enter (Start); begin a new game.
  2. From the map, enter a stage; clear it to open the next path.

Strategy

  • Bounce, don't barge. Most enemies are beaten by landing on them; time the hop rather than walking into them.
  • Backtrack with new abilities. The rope and potion open paths in stages you've already visited — revisit for extra hearts and secrets.
  • Collect hearts to extend your life bar; the later bosses hit harder than the early platforming suggests.
  • Save-state before bosses — continues are limited. Five save slots per game — pick 1–5 from the save or load menu.
