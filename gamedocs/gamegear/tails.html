Tails Adventure

Not a Sonic speed-run — a slow, exploratory, Metroidvania-style adventure starring Tails solo, with item-gated progress and a surprising amount of depth for the Game Gear.

The Battle Kukku army has invaded Cocoa Island, and Tails has to stop them alone. Explore interconnected stages, find and equip gadgets (bombs, the Remote Robot, a hammer, more) that unlock new routes, and revisit earlier areas as your toolkit grows. Tails can fly for a short burst and carry items he picks up — it's about exploration and gear, not raw speed.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, press Enter (Start); begin a new game.
  2. From the map, enter a stage; equip items via the Start menu before exploring.

Strategy

  • Equip for the job. You carry a limited set of items at once — swap loadout (Start menu) to suit the stage; bombs open walls, the robot reaches switches.
  • Backtrack constantly. New gadgets open old areas — Cocoa Island is one connected map, not a level list.
  • Fly sparingly. Tails' flight is a short burst, not infinite — use it to cross gaps, then land.
  • Battery save persists in the browser; save before tackling a new region.
