Home Computer · Commodore 16

Tower of Evil

Creative Sparks / Sparklers, 1984 — vertical-climb platformer up a haunted tower. Each floor introduces new traps; reach the top to confront the wizard.

Single-screen floors stacked vertically. Move + jump between ledges; each floor has a unique hazard pattern (rolling stones, skulls, snake pits, magic bolts). Find the floor's exit (ladder, doorway, magic portal) to ascend. Limited lives; reaching the wizard at the top wins.

Getting started

  1. Disk autoboots; click the canvas first.
  2. Press fire at the title to start.

Controls

Strategy

  • Each floor is a memorisation puzzle. The hazard pattern is fixed per floor; failing twice on the same floor means the memory's not in yet, not bad luck.
  • Jumps lock direction. Plan landing before leaving the ground; you can't reverse mid-air.
  • The exit isn't always obvious. Some floors need you to interact with an object (lever, key) before the upward path opens.
  • Bolts arc. The wizard's magic bolts on later floors follow a predictable arc — duck under, don't try to outrun.
