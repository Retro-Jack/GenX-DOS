Berks

CRL Group (Andrew Spencer), 1985 — single-screen squash-the-blob arcade puzzler. Roam a maze stomping bouncing "Berks" — squashed Berks split into smaller, faster Berks. Clear the level by reducing them to crumbs.

Maze-runner with a Centipede-via-Bubble Bobble feel. The Berks bounce around the screen; jumping on top splits them into smaller versions, then again into crumbs that disappear. Touch a Berk from the side and you die. Cleared screens advance to faster spawn waves.

Getting started

  1. Disk autoboots; click the canvas first.
  2. Press fire at the title to start.

Strategy

  • Stomp from above only. Any contact other than top-down kills you; never approach a Berk at its own height.
  • Crumb the smallest first. Big Berks split into mediums; mediums split into smalls; smalls disappear. The screen gets more crowded before it gets emptier.
  • Use the ladders defensively. Ladders are escape routes — climb out of a chase, drop back in at a clean angle.
  • Andrew Spencer signature. Same author later wrote Mr. Puniverse — both lean on the platform-stomp idea.
