Atlantis

Imagic, 1981 — Dennis Koble's classic, ported from the 2600 original

The same Atlantis defended on the Odyssey²: a city of seven sectors on the seabed, three artillery emplacements on its rooftop, and waves of Gorgon ships overhead. Each gun aims at a fixed angle; you only choose which one fires. The Intellivision port adds detail in the city graphics and a smoother enemy formation pattern, but the soul of the game is unchanged.

Getting started

  1. Pick a game variant at the title using the keypad.
  2. Your three guns sit on top of the city — left (angled up-right), centre (straight up), right (angled up-left).
  3. Atlantis falls when all seven city sectors are destroyed.

Enemy ships

TypeBehaviour
PatrolSlow, top band — easy points
CruiserMid altitude, mid speed
Bandit-bomberFast, low altitude — destroys a city sector if it dives
Death RayLowest altitude pass — high points, brief window

Strategy

  • Hit the low fliers first. Patrol scouts can wait; a bandit-bomber on the lowest band is one move from destroying a sector.
  • Centre is most accurate. Save the centre gun for fast-moving mid-altitude targets.
  • Don't fire blindly. Each shot has cool-down — empty volleys waste opportunity.
  • The Death Ray pass is a points bonanza. If you can hit it, do — but don't sacrifice a city sector to try.

Intellivision controls

This cart uses the disc as a 3-position gun selector — pressing left/centre/right fires that gun. You don't aim by moving; you select by direction.
