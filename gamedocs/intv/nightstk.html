Night Stalker

Mattel, 1982 — Steve Montero. One of the Intellivision's signature "twin-stick" precursors.

Your character runs around a dark maze with limited ammo. A central bunker is your gun supply — return to it to reload, but only when you can. Robots patrol the maze and shoot back; bats and spiders attack from the shadows. Wave-by-wave robots get tougher and faster. Score-attack survival is the game.

Getting started

  1. Pick a game variant at the title.
  2. Your character starts in the centre bunker with a full clip.
  3. Shoot robots before they shoot you; return to the bunker to reload when empty.

Strategy

  • Track ammo. The screen indicator shows shots remaining — plan a return trip before the magazine empties.
  • Shoot bats fast. Bats are unpredictable and obstruct line-of-sight to other targets — clear them when they cross.
  • Don't camp the bunker. Robots converge on the gun pile when they see you reloading.
  • Reds take three hits. Don't try to face a red robot empty — disengage, reload, then re-engage.

Intellivision controls

The disc moves; firing direction is the last-moved direction. Like Berzerk, you have to "face" your shot by moving first, then tap fire while standing still — moving and firing simultaneously fires in your current heading.
