B-17 Bomber

Mattel, 1981 — designed for the IntelliVoice speech-synthesis module. Plays without it, but loses the crew's spoken radio chatter.

A WWII bombing-run simulator: you pilot a B-17 over occupied Europe in three view modes — navigation map, top-down pilot/gunner view, and bombsight. Plot a route to your target city, dodge flak and Luftwaffe fighters, drop bombs on the target, and fly home before you run out of fuel or ammo. The IntelliVoice add-on gave the bombardier and navigator spoken lines through the TV speaker.

Getting started

  1. The map view shows occupied Europe with target cities and your refuel base.
  2. Switch to pilot view for the actual flight; use the gunner view to shoot fighters.
  3. Bombsight view drops bombs on the target city — get the crosshair on the target as it passes underneath.

Mission flow

  1. Pick a target city from the map. Note the distance — fuel is finite.
  2. Climb in pilot view to cruising altitude; navigate by compass.
  3. Enemy fighters appear — switch to gunner view to shoot them down.
  4. Over the target, switch to bombsight; line up the crosshair and release.
  5. Return to base before fuel runs out.

Strategy

  • Listen to the crew (with IntelliVoice). The bombardier announces target alignment; the navigator calls heading corrections.
  • Fly above the flak. Altitude reduces ground fire damage. Trade speed for height when approaching targets.
  • Gunner before bombsight. A fighter on your tail mid-bombing-run kills the mission. Engage threats from the gunner view first.
  • Track fuel. The trip back is the same distance — never overcommit to a deep target without reserves.

Intellivision controls

The IntelliVoice add-on isn't emulated in many cores — sound effects play, but the spoken crew lines are silent. The cart still functions; view-switching is handled through the keypad overlay.
