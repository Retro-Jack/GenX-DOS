Lock 'N' Chase

Mattel, 1982 — port of Data East's 1981 arcade maze game (sometimes called "Lock 'n' Chase" with the apostrophe placements varying by region)

A maze-runner with a defensive twist: you're a cat-burglar grabbing coins through a bank vault while detectives chase you. At intersections you can briefly slam down a door — corridors temporarily seal, splitting the pursuit. Collect all coins, grab the bonus money bag, and clear the level before the doors run out.

Getting started

  1. Pick a game variant at the title.
  2. The maze is filled with coins. Run through to collect them.
  3. Press the side button at an intersection to drop a door segment — sealing a corridor for a few seconds.

Strategy

  • Doors are finite. You can't slam infinitely — each level gives a small allotment. Save them for tight pursuits.
  • Door behind, not in front. Drop a door immediately behind you as a detective approaches; never block your own forward path.
  • Money bag bonus. The bonus bag appears mid-level — grab it before the detectives reach the same intersection.
  • Corner cuts beat straights. The detectives mirror your moves on long corridors; sharp turns into side branches break pursuit.

Intellivision controls

The disc only reads cardinal directions in the maze. The side button drops a door at the next intersection you cross — not where you currently stand.
