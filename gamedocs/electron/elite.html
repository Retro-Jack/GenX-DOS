Elite

Acornsoft, 1985 — David Braben & Ian Bell, Electron port by Andy Onions. 2 048 procedurally-generated planets across 8 galaxies in a machine with no Mode 7 — a feat of compression.

You start in Lave with 100 credits, a Cobra Mk III, a pulse laser, and a rating of "Harmless". Trade goods between systems, fight (or flee) the pirates that ambush you, dock at space stations, upgrade your ship. The Electron version drops one cockpit colour and a few visual flourishes but keeps the same universe and core loop as the BBC original.

Getting started

  1. At the title press Space, then 2 to start a new commander (or 1 to load).
  2. You launch from inside Lave station — match the rotation of the stars before flying out, then hit J to in-system jump.
  3. Press Alt+7 for the market (buy Food on Lave), Alt+5/Alt+4 for the charts, Alt+1 to set a target, H to hyperspace.
  4. On arrival the station orbits the planet — line up with the rotating slot to dock.

Trading basics

  • Agricultural → Industrial → back. Food/Furs/Textiles on agricultural worlds, sell at industrial; Computers/Machinery on the return.
  • Stay legal at first. Narcotics, Slaves, and Firearms get you fined; profitable only with police-evading kit.
  • Fuel scoops let you skim a star for free hyperspace fuel — carefully.
  • Don't fight Thargoids unprovoked. The first one ends most early commanders' careers.

Electron vs BBC version

The core Elite universe is identical. The Electron's single-ULA hardware means slightly slower frame rate during dense space combat and one fewer colour in the cockpit overlay. Save files are interchangeable between BBC and Electron tape versions.
