Home Computer · Acorn Electron

Hopper

Acornsoft, 1983 — written by Andrew Bray. Acornsoft's Frogger; the Electron version pares back the BBC original's colours but keeps the timing intact.

Hop your frog across a four-lane road, then across a river of logs and turtles, into one of five lily-pad slots on the far bank. Fill all five slots to clear the level. Each level adds traffic speed and shorter log gaps.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. Hop forward into the first traffic lane; let the next lane pass, then continue.
  3. On the river, ride floating logs and turtle backs. Falling in the water = a life.
  4. Land in an empty lily-pad slot. Slot filled — back to the start to do it again.

Strategy

  • Hop in rhythm. Each hop is a fixed grid step — predict where the lane gap will be one beat ahead.
  • Turtles sink. Don't ride them long; jump off before they dive under.
  • The diving frog appearing in slots blocks them — wait for it to leave.
  • Outer slots first. Easier to reach without a sideways-then-forward double-hop; save the centre for last.
