Home Computer · Acorn Electron

Chuckie Egg

A & F Software, 1984 — Nigel Alderton's Electron conversion of the BBC Micro original. Eight screens of platforms-and-ladders that became a defining title across all of A&F's hosts.

Henhouse Harry runs eight screens of platforms, eggs, and patrolling hens. Collect all twelve eggs on a level before the timer runs out, then the door opens to the next. Clear all eight and the cycle loops — but a hungry duck now stalks you in open air.

Getting started

  1. Press 1 at the title for one-player keyboard.
  2. Press Caps Lock at the player-select screen to redefine keys, or keep the defaults.
  3. Collect every yellow egg on the level — the door opens and you proceed.

Strategy

  • Sweep eggs in a single loop. Plan an anticlockwise route — backtracking burns the clock.
  • Hens follow fixed paths until you collect all 5 seed bowls; then they wander. Clear hen-adjacent eggs first.
  • The duck is the late-game enemy. From level 9 it flies straight at you in open air. Stay near ladders so you can drop a level instantly.
  • Avoid risky jumps. Many gaps are walkable — Harry steps off ledges safely. Test before you commit a jump.
