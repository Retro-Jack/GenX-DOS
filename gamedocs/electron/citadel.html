Citadel

Superior Software, 1985 — Michael Jakobsen. Electron port of the BBC arcade-adventure-platformer; ~100 interconnected rooms.

Find the citadel hidden somewhere in the maze, recover the magical artefacts, and escape. Rooms hold keys, food, scrolls, and helpful objects — alongside creatures that drain energy on contact. Match the right object to the right obstacle; the puzzle is half platforming, half adventure inventory.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. You start outside the castle. Walk through the door on the right and collect everything you can pick up.
  3. You carry one object at a time; drop the current item by walking over a new one — choose carefully.

Strategy

  • Map as you go. Citadel has no in-game map — pen-and-paper grid is the original walkthrough.
  • Energy is your only timer. No clock; monster hits chip the bar. Always know where the nearest apple is.
  • Object swaps cost progress. Drop the key and walk away, the room's enemies usually take it back. Carry it to the door.
  • Sealed rooms are clues. If you've cleared everywhere reachable and a room remains shut, you're missing an object — not a jump.
