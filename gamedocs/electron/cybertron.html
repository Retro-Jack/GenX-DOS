Cybertron Mission

Micro Power, 1984. Top-down corridor-shooter through a 64-room cybernetic complex; one of Micro Power's flagship Electron titles.

You're inside a hostile robot complex with a single mission: find and destroy the reactor core. The map is a 64-room grid (8×8). Each room has corridors, doors that need keycards, robot guards, and energy cells. Collect what you need to survive the deeper rooms; the reactor is buried in the most heavily defended part of the grid.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. You enter the complex from the edge. Move with the cursor keys (Z/X for left/right, : / / for up/down).
  3. Shoot robots with Return. Walk over energy cells to refill power.
  4. Pick up colour-coded keycards by walking over them. Each opens a same-colour door.

Strategy

  • Map every room. The grid is huge; pencil-and-paper or constant M checks are mandatory.
  • Energy management. Every shot drains the bar; topping up at cells is more important than killing every robot.
  • Keycards don't stack. You carry a limited set — drop one to pick another up, so plan colours before backtracking.
  • The reactor is in the centre. Most playthroughs spiral inward; deeper rooms have faster robots and tighter corridors.
