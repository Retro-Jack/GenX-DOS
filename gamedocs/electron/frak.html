Frak!

Aardvark Software, 1984 — Nick Pelling. Caveman platformer with a yo-yo for a weapon; the speech-bubble exclamations ("Frak!", "Aiee!") were a BBC/Electron icon.

Trogg the caveman traverses a sideways-scrolling prehistoric land of platforms, chasms, and hostile alien wildlife. His only weapon is a yo-yo with stunning range. Cross each screen to the right, mind the gaps, and don't let the natives catch you.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. Walk Trogg right with X. Jump gaps with Return — hold direction while jumping for distance.
  3. Press Shift to throw the yo-yo. It returns automatically — wait for it before throwing again.
  4. Reach the right edge of the screen to scroll to the next section.

Hazards

  • Aliens — flying and walking enemies; stun with the yo-yo before they touch you.
  • Chasms — fall off and lose a life. Jump distance scales with running speed.
  • Spike pits — instant death; can't be jumped onto safely.
  • Falling rocks — telegraphed by shadow; step left or right.

Strategy

  • Yo-yo cooldown is real. A missed throw leaves you defenceless for a beat — make the first one count.
  • Run before long jumps. Trogg's standing jump only clears short gaps; build up momentum.
  • Look up the screen first. Some platforms above are reachable; map exits aren't always at floor level.
  • The "Frak!" speech bubble appears on death — there's no health bar, every hit is fatal.
