Repton 2

Superior Software, 1985 — Tim Tyler. Sequel to Repton: 16 interconnected screens form a single continuous world, plus time-travel and puzzle-piece collection across the whole map.

Repton burrows through dirt collecting diamonds and avoiding boulders, eggs (which hatch into monsters), and a new cast of hazards. The breakthrough over the original: screens connect into a single world via exits, and you collect puzzle pieces that assemble a picture of Repton on a meta-screen — you only finish by completing the picture, not by clearing diamonds alone.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to begin.
  2. Move with the cursor keys; dig dirt by walking through it.
  3. Each screen has diamonds, puzzle pieces, and an exit (or several) to adjoining screens.
  4. Visit the meta-screen to see which puzzle pieces you've collected — the assembled picture is the win condition.

Strategy

  • The picture is the goal. Diamonds give score, but puzzle pieces complete the game — prioritise them.
  • Plan diamond order, not the route. Dug dirt is permanent — wrong order can seal a piece off.
  • Time capsules are reset buttons. Stuck on a screen? Find a time capsule to undo the last puzzle-piece pickup and try a different solution.
  • Screens connect. Repton 2's exits weren't in the original — keep a map of which screen connects to which, and which side.
