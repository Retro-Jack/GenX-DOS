Repton 2

Superior Software, 1985 — Tim Tyler. The Repton sequel drops separate levels for one vast interconnected world linked by transporters — and with no save, it must be completed in a single sitting, which makes it the hardest Repton of all.

Repton burrows through dirt collecting diamonds — some sealed in safes that a key releases — while guiding wall-following spirits into cages to turn them into diamonds, and dodging falling boulders, eggs that hatch into monsters, skulls, and meteors that drop from exposed roof. The leap over the original: there are no separate levels — transporters link every screen into one continuous world — and scattered through it are jigsaw pieces. Collect them all and the picture completes with "Repton 2 is ended".

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to begin.
  2. Move with Z/X (left/right) and '// (up/down); dig dirt by walking through it.
  3. Step into transporters to cross between screens — it's one connected world, completable (with some restrictions) in any order.
  4. Collect every jigsaw piece to win — and remember there's no save: Repton 2 has to be finished in a single sitting.

Strategy

  • The jigsaw is the goal. Diamonds are score; collecting every jigsaw piece is what ends the game — prioritise them.
  • No save, one sitting. The original Repton 2 can't be saved — and this bundle has no save either — so it's all or nothing in a single run. That, plus the long object list, is what makes it the hardest Repton.
  • Turn spirits into diamonds. Herd wall-following spirits into cages — it clears them and creates diamonds you need.
  • Plan the diamond order. Dug dirt is permanent; the wrong order can seal off a piece or a key.
  • Map the transporters. With no levels, note which transporter leads where so you don't lose your bearings.
