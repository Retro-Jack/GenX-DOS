Console · Nintendo Entertainment System

Contra

Konami, 1988 (NA) — port of the 1987 arcade run-and-gun. Bill & Lance, two commandos vs. the Red Falcon alien invasion. Three lives; one hit, you die.

Eight stages alternating side-scrolling outdoor levels and pseudo-3D corridor levels. Power-ups upgrade your default rifle to spread, machine gun, laser, flame, or fire-arrow. The NES port adds an exclusive split-screen 2-player mode.

Getting started

  1. Press START at the title — leave on "1 PLAYER GAME" or pick "2 PLAYER GAME" for co-op.
  2. Run right (or up, in corridor levels). Pickups float in shootable falcons or are inside boxes.
  3. Reach the boss at the end of each stage.

Strategy

  • Spread is king. If you have to pick one weapon for the run, take Spread. Skip drops that downgrade it.
  • The Konami Code. ↑↑↓↓←→←→ B A at the title screen grants 30 lives (or 99 in some versions). Iconic, also necessary for casual runs.
  • Corridor levels. The two pseudo-3D base levels (1, 3) — keep moving forward, shoot the central reactor, exit fast. Don't pause; the camera scrolls regardless.
  • Boss patterns. All bosses have clear weak-points (turrets, core orbs). Approach from a corner of the screen they can't reach.
  • Waterfall (Stage 2). Jump-shoot up the waterfall — the alien hands grab you off the side, so stay centred.

NES controls

Default keyboard mapping for this bundle: arrows = D-Pad, Z = B, X = A, ENTER = START, V = SELECT.
