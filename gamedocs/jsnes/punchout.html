Mike Tyson's Punch-Out!!

Nintendo R&D3, 1987 (NA) — directed by Genyo Takeda. Pattern-recognition boxing; learn each opponent's tells.

Little Mac fights through three circuit titles (Minor, Major, World) of 14 opponents, ending with Mike Tyson himself. Each fighter has a strict tell-then-attack rhythm — block, dodge, or duck the tell, then counter into their face for a star (special punch).

Getting started

  1. Press START at the title to enter a password or "BEGIN" for Glass Joe.
  2. Watch the opponent's eyes, gloves, and shoulders — every attack has a frame-or-two pre-animation.
  3. Counter exactly on the recovery frame to score a star; use stars (B + UP) for one-hit knockdowns.

Strategy

  • Star punches. Earned by countering on the exact recovery frame; spent via START. Three stars max; lose them if you take a hit.
  • Pause-buffer. Pausing right as you throw a punch in some fights stalls the opponent — useful for stamina recovery.
  • Heart counter. Bottom-left hearts deplete when you block, miss, or get hit at the wrong moment. Empty = Mac can't throw punches until a successful dodge.
  • Three knockdowns per round = TKO. Most fighters can be TKO'd in round 1 with star management.
  • Continue codes. Famous one: 007 373 5963 jumps straight to Mike Tyson. Use for credit-feeding sessions.

NES controls

Default keyboard mapping for this bundle: arrows = D-pad, Z = B, X = A, ENTER = START, V = SELECT.
