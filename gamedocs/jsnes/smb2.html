Super Mario Bros. 2

Nintendo R&D4, 1988 (NA) — a Western re-skin of Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic (FDS, 1987). Mario, Luigi, Toad, and Princess Peach uproot vegetables and lob them at enemies in the dream world of Subcon.

Unlike SMB1/3, you don't stomp enemies — you pluck items out of the ground (vegetables, POW blocks, shells, bombs) and throw them. Four playable characters with different stats: Mario is balanced, Luigi jumps highest, Toad is fastest, Peach floats. Pick the right one for each level.

Getting started

  1. Press START at the title to pick a character per level.
  2. Hold DOWN on a vegetable or carpet patch to crouch and grab; press B to throw.
  3. Reach the door at the end of each sub-stage; defeat the boss at the end of each world.

Strategy

  • Charge jumps. Hold DOWN until your character flashes, then release A for a super-jump that clears most ceilings.
  • POW blocks shake the screen. Picking one up and throwing it stuns every enemy currently on-screen — save them for crowd moments.
  • Sub-space coins. Throwing a Magic Potion creates a temporary door; entering it flips you into sub-space where you can grab coins for the slot machine at level end.
  • Don't stomp — you'll get hit. Landing on an enemy lets you pick them up like a vegetable, but you take damage if you walk into them on the ground.
  • Birdo bosses. Catch the eggs she spits and throw them back — three hits ends the fight.

NES controls

Both NES face buttons are in play: A jumps, B doubles as pickup/throw/run depending on context. The default keyboard mapping for this bundle: arrows = D-Pad, Z = A, X = B, ENTER = START, SHIFT = SELECT.
