Final Fantasy

Square, 1990 (NA) — designed by Hironobu Sakaguchi. Origin of the long-running JRPG series; menu-driven turn-based combat; party-of-four with classes you pick at the start.

Four Light Warriors restore the four elemental Orbs (Earth, Fire, Water, Air). Form a party of four from six classes, grind XP and gold, sail across continents, descend the final temple to defeat Chaos. Permadeath until you visit a Clinic; vanilla NES item economy is brutal compared to remakes.

Getting started

  1. Title → NEW GAME → name and class each of the four party members.
  2. Talk to the King of Coneria, get the bridge built. Head north-east to Pravoka.
  3. Save at every overworld inn — the cartridge battery saves anywhere using the SAVE option, but only via the menu.

Strategy

  • Classic balanced party. Fighter / Thief / White Mage / Black Mage covers tank, speed, heals, nukes. Beginners can sub Red Mage for Thief.
  • Fighter / Fighter / White / Black is the cheese — two tanks, plenty of damage, fewest deaths.
  • FAST + RUSE. Cast on the front line before bosses (Astos, Pirate, Chaos) — both stack. RUSE is the underrated dodge buff.
  • Class promotion (Bahamut). Get the tail from the Cardia Islands cave (mid-game), bring it to Bahamut in the Cardia Cave for the upgrade. Skip this and bosses become uneliable.
  • NES bugs. The original has known broken spells (Temper, Saber, Lock, Lok2, Xfer, Sabr1 etc. don't function). Don't waste spell slots on them.
  • Save before EVERY dungeon. No checkpoints inside; party wipe sends you back to title.

NES controls

Default keyboard mapping for this bundle: arrows = D-pad, Z = B, X = A, ENTER = START (opens main menu), V = SELECT.
