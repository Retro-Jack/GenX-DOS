Console · Nintendo Entertainment System

Mega Man 2

Capcom, 1989 (NA) — directed by Akira Kitamura. The series-defining entry: 8 robot masters in any order, weapon-weakness rock-paper-scissors, six Wily stages.

Pick a robot master from the stage select. Beat them, take their weapon, use it against the next one. Two difficulty levels (NA-exclusive: pick "Difficult" for the Japanese-original balance, "Normal" halves damage). After all 8 masters, Wily Castle is six stages of escalation.

Getting started

  1. At the password screen press START to skip to stage select.
  2. Pick a stage — Metal Man is the conventional first pick (his weapon trivializes most of the rest).
  3. Defeat the boss with Mega Buster (or the boss's weakness if you have it).

Strategy

  • Recommended order. Metal → Wood → Air → Crash → Flash → Quick → Heat → Bubble. Or just Metal first and Metal Blade everything else.
  • Energy Tanks. Up to four E-Tanks carry over between stages — refill HP from the menu. Farm hidden ones (Flash Man, Heat Man stages).
  • Items 1/2/3. Beat Heat Man for Item 1 (platform jumper), Air for Item 2 (jet sled), Flash for Item 3 (climbing platform). Wily Stage 1 needs Item 2; Stage 4 needs Item 1.
  • Wily Stage 4 bosses. The Boss Rush — re-fight all 8 masters in capsule order; weapons carry their stockpile, so manage ammo at the password screen.
  • Bubble Lead on Alien. Wily's final form (the alien) is only damaged by Bubble Lead. Save 14+ shots for it.

NES controls

Default keyboard mapping for this bundle: arrows = D-Pad, Z = B, X = A, ENTER = START, V = SELECT (opens weapon menu).
