The Legend of Zelda

Nintendo R&D4 (Shigeru Miyamoto & Takashi Tezuka), 1987 (NA). The open-world action-adventure that started it all: roam Hyrule, clear eight labyrinths, gather the Triforce of Wisdom and rescue Princess Zelda from Ganon.

Link begins on an open overworld with nothing but the promise of a sword. Explore in any direction — burn bushes, bomb suspicious walls and push blocks to uncover caves, rupees and heart containers. Eight labyrinths each hold a fragment of the Triforce of Wisdom and a key item; collect all eight, then climb Death Mountain to Ganon. The cartridge has a battery, so your quest saves between sessions (this emulator keeps that save too).

Getting started

  1. Press Enter (START), register a name, and pick a save slot.
  2. Get the sword first. Enter the cave straight north of where you start — the old man hands you the wooden sword. Don't wander Hyrule unarmed.
  3. Attack with X (sword); use your selected item with Z; open the subscreen to equip items and see the map with Enter.

Strategy

  • Full hearts = sword beam. At full health the sword fires a projectile; take damage and you lose it — a big reason to keep topped up and to hunt heart containers.
  • Bomb and burn everything suspicious. Many dungeon entrances, shops and secrets are hidden behind bombable walls or burnable bushes — probe dead-ends and lone trees.
  • Equip via the subscreen. The sword is always on X; bombs, boomerang, candle, bow and the rest are selected on the Enter subscreen and used with Z.
  • Key items open the map. Early labyrinths hand you the bow, raft, ladder and recorder — the raft and ladder reach places you can't otherwise, and the recorder warps you and drains Digdogger.
  • Buy a shield and a blue candle early. A bigger shield blocks more, and the candle lights dark rooms and burns bushes — both from overworld shops (bring rupees).
