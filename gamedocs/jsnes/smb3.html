Console · Nintendo Entertainment System

Super Mario Bros. 3

Nintendo R&D4, 1990 (NA) — directed by Shigeru Miyamoto & Takashi Tezuka. Eight kingdom-themed worlds; overworld map; suit-based power-ups; the high-water mark of NES platforming.

Bowser's seven Koopaling kids have stolen the kingdoms' wands. Walk a per-world map, pick levels in order or branch through pipes, P-Wings, and Hammer Bros. encounters. Save power-ups in inventory for later levels. New suits: Super Leaf (raccoon → flight), Frog Suit, Tanooki, Hammer Bro.

Getting started

  1. Title → 1 PLAYER GAME. The overworld map opens at World 1-1.
  2. Beat each level on the path. The Hammer Bro. icon roams and forces a battle if you cross its tile.
  3. Each World ends with an airship (Koopaling fight) or X-castle. Recover the wand to free that world's king.

Strategy

  • P-Meter for flight. Sprint (hold B) on flat ground until the P-meter (bottom of screen) fills, then tap A repeatedly to gain height. Critical for hidden coin clouds and World 3-9-style flight levels.
  • Inventory stacking. Beat Toad Houses (overworld) for free power-ups. Save P-Wings and Frog Suits for tough levels (W4 castles, W8).
  • White Mushroom Houses. Find them by getting all coins / killing all enemies on certain levels. They give Anchor / Music Box / P-Wing.
  • Warp Whistles. Three exist in W1-3 (fortress, behind back-wall), W1-3 again behind hidden white block, W2 pyramid. Up to three worlds skipped.
  • Koopaling boss pattern. Each fires magic from a wand; hop the magic, then triple-stomp them. The cabin walls trap the magic so it bounces — anticipate.
  • Bowser at 8-Bowser. Let him jump twice on his floor — the third bash breaks through, and he falls. Don't fight him head-on.

NES controls

Default keyboard mapping for this bundle: arrows = D-Pad, Z = B, X = A, ENTER = START, V = SELECT (use inventory).
