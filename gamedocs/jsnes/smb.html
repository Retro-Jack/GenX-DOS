Super Mario Bros.

Nintendo R&D4, 1985 — directed by Shigeru Miyamoto. The pack-in title that defined the side-scrolling platformer template.

Run right, jump on Goombas, eat mushrooms to grow, fire-flowers to shoot. Eight worlds of four sub-stages each (1-1 through 8-4); rescue Princess Toadstool from Bowser. Lose all lives, you're booted to the title — there's no continue from the menu, but a hidden code lets you continue from the last world reached.

Getting started

  1. Press START at the title — leave the cursor on "1 PLAYER GAME".
  2. Walk right with the D-pad; A jumps. Land on enemies to defeat them. Hit `?` blocks from below.
  3. Reach the flagpole at the end of each sub-stage. Castles (X-4) end with Bowser; defeat him by reaching the axe behind him, not by jumping over a fireball.

Strategy

  • Warp Zones. 1-2's ceiling has pipes leading to worlds 2/3/4. 4-2 has a hidden vine and ceiling-walk to worlds 5/6/7/8. World 8 has no warps.
  • Run-jump distance. Hold B before A — the running speed adds significant range; cold jumps don't clear most gaps.
  • Hammer Bros. Duck between hammer arcs (Super/Fire form), then dash through. Don't try to stomp them from above.
  • Bowser at 8-4. Either ten fireballs hit him, or grab the axe behind him — the axe drops the bridge regardless of his HP.
  • Continue code. At Game Over screen, hold A then press START to continue from the same world (NA only).
