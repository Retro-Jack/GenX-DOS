Mega Man

Capcom, 1987 — designed by Akira Kitamura. The debut of the Blue Bomber: beat six Robot Masters in any order you like, steal each one's weapon, then storm Dr. Wily's fortress.

Dr. Wily has turned six industrial robots against the city. You pick them off from a stage-select screen in whatever order you fancy — and every Robot Master you beat hands over his signature weapon, which another master is weak to. Puzzle out the weakness chain, arm up, then take on Wily's multi-stage castle. There are no passwords in the first game, so it's meant as one sitting.

Getting started

  1. Press Enter (START) at the title, then pick a stage from the select screen.
  2. Jump with X, fire the Mega Buster with Z.
  3. Beat a Robot Master to gain his weapon; switch weapons in the pause menu (Enter).

Strategy

  • Beat them in weakness order. Each master folds fast to the previous one's weapon: Bomb Man → Guts Man → Cut Man → Elec Man → Ice Man → Fire Man. Start Bomb Man with the Buster and roll the chain from there.
  • Get the Magnet Beam. It's hidden in Elec Man's stage — it builds temporary platforms you'll need to cross the gaps in Wily's castle. Don't head to the fortress without it.
  • Yellow Devil? Use Thunder Beam. The first Wily boss is brutal head-on, but Elec Man's Thunder Beam shreds it — tap fire as it reassembles.
  • No password — lean on save states. The original has no save; use this emulator's save / load slots between Robot Masters so a death doesn't cost the whole run.
  • Conserve special weapons. Energy is limited and doesn't refill on death — use the Buster for chip damage and save the boss-killer weapon for the boss.
