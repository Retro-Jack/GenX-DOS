Kid Icarus

Nintendo R&D1, 1987 (NA) — built on the Metroid engine by the same team. You're Pit, an angel shooting his way up out of the Underworld to rescue the goddess Palutena from Medusa.

Pit climbs from the depths toward the Palace in the Sky. The early stages scroll straight up — and the screen only scrolls up, so dropping off the bottom is instant death — while each world ends in a horizontally-scrolling fortress. Pit's bow fires straight ahead; enemies and blocks drop hearts, the game's currency, which you spend in shops and clinics hidden behind doors. Gather the three sacred treasures and reach Medusa.

Getting started

  1. Press Enter (START) at the title.
  2. Climb with the D-pad, jump with X, fire arrows with Z.
  3. Don't fall. Vertical stages never scroll back down — drop off the bottom of the screen and you die. Climb deliberately.
  4. Duck into doors: clinics restore health, shops sell items, training chambers hand out upgrades — all paid for in hearts.

Strategy

  • Hoard hearts. They're your currency — enemies and broken blocks drop them, and everything behind a door (health, items, upgrades) costs them. Don't leave a stage cash-poor.
  • Use the clinics. Health doesn't regenerate; a clinic behind a door tops your hearts-container up for a fee — worth it before a fortress.
  • Beware Eggplant Wizards. Their shot turns Pit into a legless eggplant that can't attack until a hospital cures him — avoid the projectile rather than trade fire.
  • Don't dawdle near Reapers. If a Reaper spots you it panics and summons a swarm of fast Reapettes; take them from range or slip past their sight-line.
  • Fortresses are mazes. Each world ends in a fortress — grab the map and pencil to chart it, find the key, and beat the boss guarding the treasure.
