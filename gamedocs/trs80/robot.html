Home Computer · Tandy TRS-80 Model I

Robot Attack

Big Five Software, 1981 — the first TRS-80 arcade game with synthesised speech. Fight through a building of killer robots, blasting them before they corner you.

Move room to room shooting robots that home in from all sides. The machine taunts you with digitised speech as you go. Keep moving and keep firing — standing still gets you surrounded.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the screen once so the keyboard has focus.
  3. Press CLEAR (the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home) to start play.

Strategy

    Never stop. Robots converge on your position — constant movement keeps the angles open. Fire down corridors. Line up rows of robots so one shot does double duty. Watch your back. They come from behind as readily as ahead; sweep the room, don’t tunnel-vision.

Most Big Five / Adventure International games print their own key list on the title or instruction screen — when in doubt, read it there before pressing CLEAR.

The on-screen CLEAR button (top-left) sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key; on the keyboard that's Home or Delete. Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
