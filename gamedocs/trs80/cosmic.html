Home Computer · Tandy TRS-80 Model I

Cosmic Fighter

Big Five Software, 1980 — wave-based space shooter. Survive ranks of descending aliens, then face the lone surviving ship as it tries to dock with its mother craft.

Columns of aliens drift down the screen firing as they come. Thin them out wave by wave; the final alien makes a run to dock and escape — stop it for the bonus.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the screen once so the keyboard has focus.
  3. Press CLEAR (the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home) to start play.

Strategy

    Work the edges. Aliens at the flanks reach you first — clear them so you have room to dodge. Save a clear lane. Keep one column open so you always have somewhere to retreat. Chase the last ship. The escaping straggler is worth a bonus if you catch it before it docks.

Most Big Five / Adventure International games print their own key list on the title or instruction screen — when in doubt, read it there before pressing CLEAR.

The on-screen CLEAR button (top-left) sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key; on the keyboard that's Home or Delete. Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
