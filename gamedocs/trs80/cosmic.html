Cosmic Fighter

Big Five Software, 1980 — a wave-based space shooter. Survive ranks of descending aliens, then dock your fighter with the mother ship between rounds.

Columns of aliens drift down the screen firing as they come. Thin them out wave by wave; between waves your fighter rises to dock with the space station — line it up and press the dock key.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the screen once so the keyboard has focus.
  3. Read the controls the game prints on its title screen, then press the key it names to begin (often CLEAR — the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home).

Controls

KeyAction
← →Move ship left / right
SpaceFire
DDock with the space station (between waves)
EscBREAK — interrupt / abort to the BASIC READY prompt

Strategy

  • Work the edges. Aliens at the flanks reach you first — clear them so you have room to dodge.
  • Line up the dock. Centre the fighter under the station before pressing D or you'll miss the docking run.
  • Save a clear lane. Keep one column open so you always have somewhere to retreat.

The exact keys are shown on the game's title / instruction screen (these older type-in-era games list their controls before play). Read them there, then begin.

The top-left CLEAR button sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key (Home / Delete) — several of these games use CLEAR (to begin a game, or in-play). Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
