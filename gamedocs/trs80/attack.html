Home Computer · Tandy TRS-80 Model I

Attack Force

Big Five Software, 1980 — top-down arena chase. Eight enemy ships hunt you through a scrolling lane, trying to ram you head-on; turn the tables and shoot them first.

You pilot a ship along a horizontal lane while enemy craft converge from both ends, lining up to ram you. Fire ahead and weave so the ramming ships overshoot.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the screen once so the keyboard has focus.
  3. Press CLEAR (the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home) to start play.

Strategy

    Shoot down the lane. Enemies come straight at you — a well-timed shot kills the rammer before it lands. Don’t get pinned. If ships close from both sides at once, slip past one rather than trading the hit. Clear waves fast. The longer a wave lives, the more crowded the lane gets.

Most Big Five / Adventure International games print their own key list on the title or instruction screen — when in doubt, read it there before pressing CLEAR.

The on-screen CLEAR button (top-left) sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key; on the keyboard that's Home or Delete. Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
