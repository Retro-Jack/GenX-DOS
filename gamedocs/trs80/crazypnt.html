Crazy Painter

Big Five Software, 1982 — a frantic paint-the-floor chase. Cover the whole floor with paint while dodging the creatures that smear it and the steamroller that flattens you.

Drive your painter across the floor laying down colour. Spilt-paint creatures track through your work and undo it, and a steamroller patrols the edges. Hit a paint puddle to clean your wheels when they gum up.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the screen once so the keyboard has focus.
  3. Press CLEAR (the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home) to start play.

Strategy

    Paint in strips. Sweep methodically so you can see what’s left, rather than zig-zagging. Avoid the steamroller. It runs fixed patrols — learn its path and cross behind it. Clean your wheels. Clogged wheels stop painting — dip into a puddle to reset them.

Most Big Five / Adventure International games print their own key list on the title or instruction screen — when in doubt, read it there before pressing CLEAR.

The on-screen CLEAR button (top-left) sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key; on the keyboard that's Home or Delete. Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
