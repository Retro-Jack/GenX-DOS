Home Computer · Tandy TRS-80 Model I

Armored Patrol

Adventure International (Wayne Westmoreland & Terry Gilman), 1981 — a first-person 3-D tank battle in the spirit of Battlezone. Roll across a wireframe battlefield hunting enemy tanks and robots.

You see the field from inside your tank: a vector horizon with enemies as wireframe shapes. Rotate to bring a target into your sights, close the range, and fire before it fires back.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the screen once so the keyboard has focus.
  3. Press CLEAR (the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home) to start play.

Strategy

    Turn, don’t chase. Rotating to face a target is faster than driving around it. Use the range. Line a tank up dead-centre before firing — angled shots miss at distance. Keep moving after a shot. A stationary tank is an easy return target.

Most Big Five / Adventure International games print their own key list on the title or instruction screen — when in doubt, read it there before pressing CLEAR.

The on-screen CLEAR button (top-left) sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key; on the keyboard that's Home or Delete. Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
