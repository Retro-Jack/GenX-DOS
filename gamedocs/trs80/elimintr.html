Home Computer · Tandy TRS-80 Model I

Eliminator

Adventure International (Wayne Westmoreland & Terry Gilman), 1981 — open-space combat where you defend against waves of attackers with free Asteroids-style flight.

Your ship drifts and turns in open space as waves of enemy craft sweep in. Rotate to face threats, thrust to reposition, and fire — inertia means you’re always gliding, so plan your turns early.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the screen once so the keyboard has focus.
  3. Press CLEAR (the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home) to start play.

Strategy

    Face the wave. Point at the densest cluster and let them fly into your fire. Glide, don’t race. Momentum carries you — small thrust corrections beat long burns. Hold the centre. Drifting to a screen edge cuts off half your escape routes.

Most Big Five / Adventure International games print their own key list on the title or instruction screen — when in doubt, read it there before pressing CLEAR.

The on-screen CLEAR button (top-left) sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key; on the keyboard that's Home or Delete. Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
