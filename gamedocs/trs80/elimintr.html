Home Computer · Tandy TRS-80 Model I

Eliminator

Adventure International (Wayne Westmoreland & Terry Gilman), 1981 — open-space combat where you defend against waves of attackers with free Asteroids-style flight.

Your ship drifts and turns in open space as waves of enemy craft sweep in. Steer with the arrow keys, fire with Space, and keep a smart bomb and a hyperspace jump in reserve for when you're swarmed.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the screen once so the keyboard has focus.
  3. Read the controls the game prints on its title screen, then press the key it names to begin (often CLEAR — the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home).

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Steer the ship
SpaceFire
EnterSmart bomb
ClearHyperspace
EscBREAK — interrupt / abort to the BASIC READY prompt

Strategy

  • Face the wave. Point at the densest cluster and let them fly into your fire.
  • Bomb when surrounded. The smart bomb (Enter) is for the moment you're boxed in, not for stragglers.
  • Hyperspace to escape. Clear jumps you out of a corner — risky, but better than a certain hit.

The top-left CLEAR button sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key (Home / Delete) — several of these games use CLEAR (to begin a game, or in-play). Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
