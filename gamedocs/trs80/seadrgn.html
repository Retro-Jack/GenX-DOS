Home Computer · Tandy TRS-80 Model I

Sea Dragon

Big Five Software, 1982 — a horizontally-scrolling submarine epic. Dive through undersea caverns past mines, gun turrets and closing walls to reach and destroy the reactor at the end.

Your sub scrolls right through a long, hand-built cavern. Wall-mounted guns, floating mines, sonar-guided torpedoes and tightening passages all stand between you and the reactor core. Mind your air supply.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the screen once so the keyboard has focus.
  3. Press CLEAR (the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home) to start play.

Strategy

    Shoot the gun turrets early. Take them out as they scroll in, before their fire fills the passage. Thread, don’t barge. The narrowing caverns punish speed — ease through tight gaps. Watch your air. Surfacing and air pockets keep you alive; running out is as fatal as a mine.

Most Big Five / Adventure International games print their own key list on the title or instruction screen — when in doubt, read it there before pressing CLEAR.

The on-screen CLEAR button (top-left) sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key; on the keyboard that's Home or Delete. Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
