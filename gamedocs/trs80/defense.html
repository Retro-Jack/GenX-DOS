Home Computer · Tandy TRS-80 Model I

Defense Command

Big Five Software, 1981 — a Defender-style side-scroller. Patrol a scrolling planet surface and stop alien landers from carrying off your fuel cells.

Fly left and right over a wrapping landscape. Landers descend to grab fuel cells and haul them to the top of the screen — shoot the landers, catch falling cells, and use a smart bomb when the screen gets swamped.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the screen once so the keyboard has focus.
  3. Press CLEAR (the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home) to start play.

Strategy

    Protect the cells. A lander that reaches the top with a cell becomes a fast mutant — kill landers before they climb. Catch what falls. Shoot a laden lander and catch the dropping cell before it hits the ground. Bomb the swarm, not the single. Save smart bombs for moments you’re genuinely surrounded.

Most Big Five / Adventure International games print their own key list on the title or instruction screen — when in doubt, read it there before pressing CLEAR.

The on-screen CLEAR button (top-left) sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key; on the keyboard that's Home or Delete. Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
