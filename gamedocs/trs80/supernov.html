Super Nova

Big Five Software, 1980 — a faithful Asteroids for the TRS-80, popular enough that its key layout became a TRS-80 standard. Rotate, thrust and blast drifting rocks while a hunting saucer takes pot-shots.

Your ship floats in open space. Big rocks split into mediums, mediums into smalls, smalls into dust. Manage your momentum — a free-floating ship keeps drifting — and clear the field before the saucer lines you up.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  3. Read the controls the game prints on its title screen, then press the key it names to begin (often CLEAR — the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home).

Controls

KeyAction
R T O PRotate, thrust and fire (the four Asteroids controls — the title screen shows which is which)
SpaceHyperspace (random jump)
EscBREAK — interrupt / abort to the BASIC READY prompt

Strategy

  • Tap thrust. Short bursts only — a long burn leaves you skating into rocks you can't avoid.
  • Leave one rock. Holding the field at a single small rock lets you reposition before the next wave.
  • Hyperspace is a last resort. It can drop you on top of a rock — use it only when a collision is otherwise certain.

This game prints its own key list on its title / instruction screen — read it there; the keys below are the verified scheme.

The top-left CLEAR button sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key (Home / Delete) — several of these games use CLEAR (to begin a game, or in-play). Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
