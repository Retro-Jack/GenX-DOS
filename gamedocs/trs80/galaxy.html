Galaxy Invasion

Big Five Software, 1980 — a fast TRS-80 take on Namco’s Galaxian. A formation of aliens hangs overhead; flagships peel off and dive at your laser base, firing as they swoop. Clear the formation to advance.

Your base sits at the bottom of the screen and slides left and right. Aliens break from the formation in ones and twos and dive — lead them as they come, and pick off the diving flagships for the big points.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the screen once so the keyboard has focus.
  3. Press CLEAR (the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home) to start play.

Strategy

    Hunt the divers. Aliens are only dangerous when they leave the formation — concentrate fire on whatever is swooping. Flagships pay. The escorted command ships are worth the most; bag them mid-dive. Keep moving. A stationary base is an easy target for the angled shots that come down with each dive.

Most Big Five / Adventure International games print their own key list on the title or instruction screen — when in doubt, read it there before pressing CLEAR.

The on-screen CLEAR button (top-left) sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key; on the keyboard that's Home or Delete. Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
