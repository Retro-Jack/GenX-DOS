Galaxy Invasion

Big Five Software, 1980 — a fast TRS-80 take on Namco's Galaxian. A formation of aliens hangs overhead; flagships peel off and dive at your laser base, firing as they swoop.

Your base slides along the bottom of the screen. Aliens break from the formation in ones and twos and dive — lead them as they come, and pick off the diving flagships for the big points. You can only have one shot in the air at a time.

Getting started

  1. The game auto-loads and jumps straight to its title screen.
  2. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  3. Read the controls the game prints on its title screen, then press the key it names to begin (often CLEAR — the on-screen CLEAR button, or Home).

Controls

KeyAction
← →Move your laser base left / right
SpaceFire upward (one shot on screen at a time)
EscBREAK — interrupt / abort to the BASIC READY prompt

Strategy

  • Hunt the divers. Aliens are only dangerous once they leave the formation — concentrate fire on whatever is swooping.
  • Flagships pay. The escorted command ships are worth the most; bag them mid-dive.
  • One shot out. You can't fire again until your shot clears — make each one count and don't waste it on empty sky.

The exact keys are shown on the game's title / instruction screen (these older type-in-era games list their controls before play). Read them there, then begin.

The top-left CLEAR button sends the TRS-80 CLEAR key (Home / Delete) — several of these games use CLEAR (to begin a game, or in-play). Esc is the TRS-80 BREAK key.
