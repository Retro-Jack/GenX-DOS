Space Invaders

Atari, 1980 — home port of Tomohiro Nishikado's 1978 Taito arcade. The Atari 8-bit version was one of the launch titles that proved home computers could play arcade games at home.

Five rows of invaders descend toward your laser cannon at the bottom of the screen. Shoot them all before they reach the ground; dodge their bombs by ducking behind the four shields. Each wave starts lower and faster. UFOs cross the top occasionally for bonus points.

Getting started

  1. Press F4 Start at the title screen.
  2. Press F3 Select to cycle game variant (1-player / 2-player / shield count).
  3. Move left/right with the joystick, fire with L Ctrl.

Strategy

  • Outside columns first. Killing the rightmost or leftmost columns gives you escape lanes when the swarm crowds.
  • Save the shields. Fire through your own shield only when desperate — every shot eats the protection you'll need later.
  • UFO timing. The UFO's score is fixed by your shot count; experienced players time the 23rd shot for the 300-point UFO.
  • Speed up as numbers fall. The fewer invaders left, the faster they move — the last one is a sprinter.
  • Last invader sweep. When only one is left, follow it across the screen rather than trying to intercept.

Atari 400 controls

Single joystick + fire. The Atari 8-bit version added colour to the original monochrome arcade and tightened sprite control via the dedicated GTIA chip.
