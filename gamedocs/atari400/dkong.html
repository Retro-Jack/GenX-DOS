Donkey Kong

Atari (Atarisoft), 1983 — home port of Shigeru Miyamoto's 1981 Nintendo arcade. Jumpman (later named Mario) climbs four screens to rescue Pauline from the giant ape Donkey Kong. The Atari 8-bit cart version includes all four arcade stages — most home ports of the era cut at least one.

Climb girders, jump barrels and fireballs, smash with a hammer when you find one. Reach the top to rescue Pauline; Donkey Kong grabs her again and you climb a new stage. Four stages in rotation: Barrels, Pies/Cement Factory, Elevators, Rivets.

Getting started

  1. Press F4 Start.
  2. Walk left/right with the joystick.
  3. Climb ladders with ↑/↓.
  4. Jump with L Ctrl.

Strategy

  • Hammer chains. Smash two barrels back-to-back without dropping the hammer for the 800-point bonus chain.
  • Don't jump straight up. Jumping vertically misses barrels rolling past; jump forward (toward the barrel) to clear it.
  • The Rivets stage. Removing the highest rivet first opens the top platform; killing all six wins the round.
  • Climb only when clear. A barrel rolling down a ladder kills you mid-climb. Wait for clear gaps.
  • The kill screen is real. Like the arcade, the Atari version's score wraps at million-scale and the timer becomes unbeatable around level 22.
