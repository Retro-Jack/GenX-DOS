Carnival

Coleco, 1982 — port of Sega's 1980 arcade shooter

Three rows of targets drift across a shooting-gallery screen — ducks, rabbits, owls, and bonus letters. A rifle at the bottom slides left and right and fires upward. Each shot costs one bullet from your finite stock; running out is game over. Bonus rounds, bear targets, and a duck that turns into a vampire keep the screen busy.

Getting started

  1. At the title, press 1 on the keypad for one-player or 2 for two-player.
  2. The bullet counter shows your remaining ammo — hit targets to refill, but don't burn shots on the moving owls.
  3. Spell out BONUS by shooting the letters as they drift across to trigger a bonus round.

Strategy

  • Shoot ducks first. A duck that reaches the bottom row of the gallery turns into a swooping vampire that flies down and eats bullets from your stock.
  • Don't waste bullets on owls. Owls are worth fewer points than the bullet they cost; let them pass.
  • Spell BONUS in order. The letters must be hit in B-O-N-U-S sequence — out-of-order hits don't count.
  • Bear round. Hit the bear repeatedly during its appearance for big points; if you miss too many he leaves angry.

ColecoVision controls

The standard ColecoVision controller has a joystick + 2 side-fire buttons (left/right) + a 12-key keypad. Carnival uses just the joystick and the left fire button during play; the keypad selects the game variant at the title.
