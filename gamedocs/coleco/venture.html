Console · ColecoVision

Venture

Coleco, 1982 — port of Exidy's 1981 arcade dungeon crawler

A top-down dungeon crawler: you are Winky, a smiley-face adventurer with a bow. The overworld view shows three dungeon levels — pick a room, dive in, grab the treasure, and shoot the monsters that live there. The monsters reset each time you re-enter; the treasures don't. Stay too long and the Hallmonsters appear and chase you, untouched by your arrows.

Getting started

  1. Press 1 or 2 on the keypad at the title.
  2. The overworld shows a hallway with multiple room doors. Walk into a door to enter that room.
  3. Once inside, kill or evade monsters, grab the treasure, and exit the same door.

Rooms

Each dungeon level has 8 rooms with themed monsters and treasures:

RoomNotes
Goblin / WallmanBeginner rooms — slow monsters, easy treasures
Cyclops / Two-HeadedMid-tier — must be killed before grabbing the treasure
Dragon / SkeletonLate rooms — the dragon respawns; skeletons are mobile
GenieMagic-using room boss

Strategy

  • Hallmonsters are inevitable. A counter starts the second you enter a room — work fast or be eaten by the un-killable Hallmonster.
  • Arrows travel until they hit a wall. Line up shots through doorways for safe long-range kills.
  • Treasure first, monsters second. Sometimes grabbing the loot and running pays better than killing the room clean.
  • Dead monsters don't disappear. Bodies block your path — kills in narrow rooms can trap you.

ColecoVision controls

Joystick + left fire button. Arrow direction is whatever joystick direction you moved last — releasing the joystick locks your facing.
