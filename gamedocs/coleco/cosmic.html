Cosmic Avenger

Coleco, 1982 — port of Universal's 1981 arcade scrolling shooter

A horizontally-scrolling shoot-'em-up: your starfighter flies right over alien-occupied terrain, weaving between mountains and underground tunnels. Bomb ground installations, shoot incoming aerial enemies, and dive into caverns where the playfield narrows. Survive the cycle and the speed ramps up.

Getting started

  1. At the title, press 1 or 2 on the keypad to select 1- or 2-player.
  2. Your fighter starts in the air with three lives. Crashing into terrain or being hit costs one life.
  3. Two weapons: a forward laser (air targets) and downward bombs (ground installations).

Strategy

  • Ground targets pay more. Aerial enemies are point-fillers; the dug-in installations are the score multipliers. Don't burn through a cavern without bombing it.
  • Mind the terrain. The mountains scroll predictably but the underground tunnels are tight. Hold position briefly before committing to a dive.
  • Vertical movement is slow. Pre-align with incoming threats rather than reacting late.
  • Speed ramps with each cycle. The first lap is forgiving; subsequent passes demand more anticipation.

ColecoVision controls

Standard ColecoVision controller. Both side-fire buttons are used here — keep your thumb on the joystick, index on the left button, middle on the right.
