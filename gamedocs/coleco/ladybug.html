Lady Bug

Coleco, 1982 — port of Universal's 1981 arcade maze game

A maze game with a clever twist: the maze itself can be rearranged. You control Lady Bug, who runs through a hedge labyrinth eating dots and dodging Skull Spiders. Hitting a turnstile pivots a maze wall — use this to redirect chasing enemies into dead ends, or unblock your escape route.

Getting started

  1. At the title, press 1 or 2 on the keypad.
  2. Each maze has dots, hearts, and letters (E-X-T-R-A and S-P-E-C-I-A-L) to collect.
  3. Eat all the dots to clear the maze. Hearts give powerups; letter bonuses spell out extra-life thresholds.

Strategy

  • Turnstiles are weapons, not obstacles. A spider chasing you up a corridor can be cut off by spinning a turnstile behind you.
  • Eat the hearts last. Save them for crisis moments when you're boxed in.
  • Centre vegetables decay. They start as the high-point item and degrade — eat early or skip.
  • Plan letter collection. SPECIAL letters spawn at fixed positions; don't waste a move dodging when you could collect.

ColecoVision controls

Joystick only — no fire button needed. Lady Bug always moves in the joystick direction at constant speed; releasing the stick stops her.
