Q*bert

Coleco, 1983 — port of Gottlieb's 1982 isometric arcade hit

Q*bert hops up and down an isometric pyramid of cubes, turning each cube's top to a target colour. The catch: the joystick controls feel rotated. "Up" is up-and-to-the-right; "left" is down-and-to-the-left. Coily the snake chases you down the pyramid; smaller hazards bounce around the edges. Fall off and you die unless you land on a flying disc.

Getting started

  1. Press 1 or 2 on the keypad at the title.
  2. Q*bert starts on top of the pyramid. Hop to each cube to change its colour.
  3. When every cube matches the target colour shown at the top of the screen, the level clears.

Diagonal mapping is the entire trick — the screen is isometric but the joystick is four-way. Practice this.

Hazards

EnemyBehaviour
Coily (snake)Hatches as a purple ball, falls down the pyramid, then transforms. Coily chases by mirroring your moves — lure him off the edge with a flying disc.
Ugg / Wrong-WayClimb diagonally from the sides — only dangerous if Q*bert is on the same diagonal.
Slick / SamGreen creatures that revert your colour changes. Catch them by landing on the same cube.
Flying discsOn the side edges — hop onto one and Q*bert flies back to the top. Coily follows and falls.

Strategy

  • Lure Coily. The classic move: position one square away from a disc, hop onto the disc as Coily reaches you, and watch him fall.
  • Catch Slick and Sam. They give points and reset the cubes they touch — landing on them is worth the trip.
  • Plan colour changes. Later levels need two hops on the same cube — landing once paints it intermediate; landing twice undoes the change. Memorise the cycle.
  • Use the diagonal pause. Q*bert can pause briefly on a cube before the next hop — use the gap to dodge sideways.

ColecoVision controls

Joystick only. The rotated diagonal is what makes Q*bert hard — the right answer is to think in isometric directions, not screen directions.
