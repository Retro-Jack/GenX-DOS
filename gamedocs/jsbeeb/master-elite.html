Elite (Master version)

Acornsoft / Imagineer, 1986 — David Braben & Ian Bell. Master-enhanced Elite: more colours in the cockpit, smoother wireframe rendering, the full mission chain on a double-sided disc.

The Master release of Elite is the most polished 8-bit version. Same universe — 2 048 systems across 8 galaxies, same Cobra Mk III, same trading/combat loop — but the Master 128's extra RAM and 65C12 speed let the cockpit show more colour and the wireframes draw faster. This build (the flicker-free reconstruction from bbcelite.com) ships as a double-sided disc image (`.dsd`); `*DRIVE 2` in the !BOOT loads side 1 of the disc.

Getting started

  1. At the title press Space, then 2 to start a new commander.
  2. Launch from Lave station — match the rotation, fly out, J to in-system jump to the planet.
  3. Buy Food (cheap on Lave), F7 market, F4/F5 charts, F1 set target, H hyperspace.

Master version differences

  • Flicker-free wireframes. The 65C12's extra speed lets the renderer double-buffer cleanly — no scanline tearing in dense combat.
  • Extra cockpit colours. The 128K RAM allows palette switching mid-frame; the dashboard and chart screens use more hues than the BBC original.
  • Disc-driven mission chain. Constrictor, Cougar, Thargoid mothership missions are all on the disc — no separate "Mission Disc" sale.
  • Save/Load to disc. Faster than the tape commander files; survives between sessions cleanly.

Strategy basics

  • Agricultural → Industrial → back. Standard trade route.
  • Don't go illegal early. Narcotics/Slaves get you fined; profitable only with police-evading kit.
  • Fuel scoops for free star-skim fuel.
  • Run from Thargoids until you're at "Dangerous" or better.
