Stryker's Run

Superior Software, 1986 — Chris Roberts (later of Wing Commander, Star Citizen). Side-scrolling action: Lieutenant John Stryker must deliver a peace treaty 30 km across enemy lines, on foot or by hijacked jetpack.

You play Stryker, a Volgan deserter racing to bring peace plans to the Western alliance. The land rolls past in a smooth side-scroll: enemy infantry, tanks, and aircraft try to stop you. Pick up rifles and grenades, hijack a jetpack from a downed pilot, and run/fly to the next checkpoint before reinforcements arrive.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to begin.
  2. Stryker starts on the right; the land scrolls left as he runs.
  3. Walk over a downed enemy to pick up his weapon. Walk over a parachuting pilot to grab his jetpack.
  4. Reach the marker at the left edge of each segment for the next checkpoint.

Strategy

  • The jetpack changes everything. First playthroughs run on foot; experienced players grab the jetpack early and skip half the infantry encounters.
  • Crouch to take cover. Tank shells fly head-height — duck behind a hillock and they miss.
  • Grenades for tanks. Rifles don't dent armour; lob a grenade and run.
  • Master vs BBC version. The Master enhancement uses the 65C12 CPU's extra speed to add smoother sprite motion and a broader colour palette during the day/dusk transitions.
