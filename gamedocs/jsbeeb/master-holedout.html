Holed Out

4th Dimension, 1989 — Mark Reid. Widely cited as the best 8-bit golf simulator; rich club selection, true 3D ball physics, four full 18-hole courses.

Play a full round of golf on one of four courses. The view shows the fairway from behind the ball, with wind direction, distance to the pin, and club selection on the HUD. Aim with the cursor, choose a club, then nail the swing timing — overshoot the power meter and you slice; undershoot and you drop it short.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to enter the course menu.
  2. Choose course (1–4) and 1- to 4-player game.
  3. For each shot: aim with Z/X, pick club with ://, then press Return to start the swing.
  4. Swing meter: press Return at the top for max power, then again at the bottom of the downswing for accuracy.

The swing timer

  • Press 1 — start the swing; the power bar begins filling up.
  • Press 2 — peak power. Time at the very top for max distance; below the peak loses yards.
  • Press 3 — accuracy. Time at the centre line for straight; left of centre slices, right hooks.

Strategy

  • Wind matters more than you think. A 10mph headwind cuts 15-20 yards off a driver. Adjust club selection up by one.
  • Punch shot with a 5-iron beats a fairway wood under tree branches — accept the distance loss for safe positioning.
  • Putting is its own mini-game. The grid showing green slope is a hint, not a guarantee; downhill putts carry further than the bar suggests.
  • Save the round to disc between holes — Holed Out supports mid-round save/restore.
