Repton 3

Superior Software, 1986 — Tim Tyler & Matthew Atkinson. The series peak: 24 screens, four themed worlds (Around the World, Prehistoric, Toys, Future Shock), and a level editor.

Repton's third outing keeps the boulder-pushing, diamond-collecting core but adds time bombs, transporters, and crowns to collect alongside the diamonds. Each themed world has six screens to clear; clear all 24 to win. A built-in editor lets you design your own screens.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to enter the world-select menu.
  2. Choose A–D for the four worlds; each world's screens unlock in sequence.
  3. Collect every diamond AND every crown, then defuse the time bomb to clear the screen.

Strategy

  • Defuse the bomb last. The bomb only appears once every diamond is collected — you can't pre-empt the timer.
  • Crowns drive the route. Plan the path so every crown is reachable; they're often boxed in by boulders.
  • Fungus is a soft timer. The longer you take, the more area it covers — sometimes you can leave it alone, sometimes it'll eat your route.
  • Editor mode (option from main menu) loads BBC screens too — community ROMs of fan-made Repton 3 packs run on this same engine.
