Home Computer · BBC Master 128

Firetrack

Electric Dreams, 1987 — Nick Pelling (author of Frak!). Vertically-scrolling shoot-em-up over an enemy stronghold; technical showcase for the Master's smooth-scrolling capabilities.

Your fighter screams down a vertical corridor over alien terrain, dodging gun emplacements, tanks, and waves of homing aircraft. Each stage ends at a boss formation. Smooth scrolling at 50 fps in the Master version — Pelling was demonstrating what the new hardware could do.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. Your ship hovers at mid-screen, terrain scrolling toward you from the top.
  3. Move with the cursor keys (Z/X for sideways, : / / for up/down within the screen).
  4. Hold Return to fire constantly — there's no fire limit.

Strategy

  • Stay at the bottom. The screen scrolls forward; sitting near the bottom gives you more reaction time on enemy spawns.
  • Power-up tokens drop from killed turrets — collect for spread-shot upgrades.
  • Boss patterns are fixed. Memorise the safe corner for each end-of-stage formation.
  • Smart bombs for the boss. Save the limited supply; they clear the screen instantly and break the boss's pattern.
