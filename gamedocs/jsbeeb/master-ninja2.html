Last Ninja 2

Superior Software, 1990 — System 3 / Mark Cale (BBC port by Dave Lawrence). Isometric action-adventure across six levels of modern New York; the BBC Master version is the latest, most polished port of the C64 original.

Armakuni, the last ninja, has been transported through time to 1990s New York. He must navigate six levels — Central Park, Wall Street, the Sewers, the Mansion, the Office, and the Final Battle — solving inventory-based puzzles and fighting Kunitoki's henchmen with bare hands or improvised weapons. Each room is an isometric tableau with multiple objects to pick up, throw, or use.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to begin.
  2. Armakuni starts in Central Park. Walk in any of 8 directions with the keys below.
  3. Pick up an object by walking over it; the inventory icon updates.
  4. Combat: face an enemy and tap the punch key; weapons appear in inventory when picked up.

The six levels

  1. Central Park — tutorial setting; nun-chucks under a bench.
  2. The Sewers — Bonus Round: rats, sewer guards, a key for the next level.
  3. Wall Street — daytime brawl; office workers ignore you, hit-men don't.
  4. The Mansion — Kunitoki's hideout; secret passages and trap rooms.
  5. The Office — top of the mansion; multiple desks hide keys and switches.
  6. The Final Battle — Kunitoki himself, sword vs sword.

Strategy

  • Pick up everything. Useless-looking items (a stick, an empty bottle) often unlock the next room.
  • Face the camera. Isometric controls are confusing — pick a single rotation convention (e.g., : = up-right always) and stick to it.
  • Combat opens with a kick. The high punch finishes; the kick stuns. Two kicks then one punch beats most early enemies.
  • Save before the Final Battle. Kunitoki has a one-shot fatal strike; learning his pattern usually costs a few attempts.
