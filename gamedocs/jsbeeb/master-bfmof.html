By Fair Means or Foul

Superior Software, 1988. Side-view boxing where the title is the gameplay: throw illegal punches when the ref isn't looking.

Standard boxing — three rounds, knock-downs and points — but you can headbutt, kidney-punch, rabbit-punch, and bite. The catch: the ref is watching. Time your dirty work for when his back is turned or you're disqualified. Win cleanly for the title shot, win foul for the headlines.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. Choose 1-player vs the championship ladder, or 2-player local versus.
  3. Round 1 starts; watch the ref's gaze indicator at the top of the screen before throwing illegal moves.

The ref system

  • A small ref-icon at the top of the screen turns to face the action periodically.
  • Illegal moves when the ref is looking → warning. Three warnings → disqualified.
  • Illegal moves when the ref looks away → free damage, no penalty.
  • Heavy clean punches alert the ref to look your way faster.

Strategy

  • Mix clean and dirty. Pure clean = AI out-scores you on points. Pure foul = disqualified.
  • Watch the gaze indicator more than the opponent. Two clean punches, gaze rotates, slip in a kidney shot, recover.
  • Headbutts are the highest illegal damage but the longest animation — only safe when the ref is fully turned.
  • The opponent fouls too. Block low when their hands disappear off-screen; that's the rabbit-punch wind-up.
