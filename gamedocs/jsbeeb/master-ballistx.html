Ballistix

Superior Software, 1990 — port of the Psyclapse / Psygnosis 1989 title. Future-sport: 1v1 score-against-goal across 100+ playfields with deflectors, accelerators, and bonus tiles.

Two players (or one vs AI) face off across a vertical playfield with goals at top and bottom. Balls fire constantly down the middle; you nudge them with directional taps to deflect into the opponent's goal. The playfields add bumpers, splitters, accelerators, anti-grav zones — every level changes the geometry of how a ball moves.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title.
  2. Choose 1P vs CPU or 2P at the next menu.
  3. Balls fall from the top continuously. Tap arrow keys to nudge a ball sideways — you don't grab them, you tap them while in motion.
  4. First to 10 (configurable) wins the match. Win to advance to the next playfield.

Strategy

  • Bonus tiles compound. Worth more than scored goals on some playfields — feed balls through them on your half.
  • Accelerators are double-edged. They send the ball both ways depending on direction of travel; learn each playfield's flow.
  • Defensive nudges matter. Tapping down to slow a ball lets you redirect it next frame.
  • Multiball is chaos. When a splitter triggers, prioritise the ball closest to your goal — sacrifice the rest.
