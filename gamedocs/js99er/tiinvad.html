TI Invaders

Texas Instruments, 1981 — Solid State Software cart. TI's licensed Space Invaders port, faithful enough to feel like the arcade. The 4A's TMS9918A sprite hardware lets the alien grid descend at smooth rates and the player's laser base moves with proper one-pixel granularity.

Five rows of eleven aliens marching back and forth, descending each pass; your laser base slides along the bottom firing one shot at a time. Four bunkers degrade with each hit, friendly or hostile. A mystery ship occasionally arcs across the top for bonus points. Clear a wave, the next starts a row lower and a step faster.

Getting started

  1. Cart auto-boots; press 2 at the TI title for "TI INVADERS".
  2. Choose 1 or 2 player at the cart menu.

Controls

KeyAction
← / →Move laser base left / right
SpaceFire (one shot on-screen at a time)
RPause / resume during play

Strategy

  • One bullet rule. You can't fire again until your current shot leaves the screen — commit only to hits you can confirm.
  • Bunker pockets. Punch a hole in the lower face of a bunker for a peek-out firing slot; the alien grid scrolls past predictably.
  • Mystery ship pattern. Bonus UFO points are determined by your shot count when it appears — practice runs settle on a specific shot number that yields the 300-point reward.
  • Last alien is fastest. Each wave's final alien moves at sprint speed; don't camp middle screen.
