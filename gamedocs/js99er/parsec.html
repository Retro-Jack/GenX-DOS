Parsec

Texas Instruments (Jim Dramis, Paul Urbanus), 1982 — Solid State Software cart. The platform's signature game and the most cited TI-99/4A title. Side-scrolling shoot-'em-up with synthesised voice ("Press Fire to begin"), gradient skies, ten alien types and the famously cruel fuel-refuel sequence.

You fly the LIFT (Lambda Interstellar Fighter, type 32) horizontally through ten ship-type waves: Swoopers, Urbites, Saucers, Salty Splatters, Dramites, Killer Satellites, Cyranoids, Bynites, LTAs and finally the Asteroid Belt. Between waves you refuel by threading the LIFT through a moving fuel-line — too slow or off-centre and the ship overheats and explodes. Watch the speed indicator.

Getting started

  1. Press 2 at the TI title for Parsec.
  2. Press Fire (Space) at "PRESS FIRE TO BEGIN" — voice optional.

Strategy

  • Manage laser temperature. Rapid fire overheats the laser; the cart's voice will tell you ("LIFT laser overheating!"). Pace shots between targets.
  • Refuel timing. The fuel tunnel scrolls into view at a fixed speed; slow to about 50% before the tunnel mouth and centre the LIFT — Speed governs descent into the tunnel as well as forward motion.
  • Asteroids are unkillable. Wave 10 — you can't shoot through asteroids; thread the gaps using altitude alone.
  • Mind the laser heat. Holding fire overheats the laser and blows your ship up — fire in controlled bursts, and beware it heats faster each wave.
  • Voice synthesis is the cart's gimmick. The TI Speech Synthesizer peripheral is emulated in software; toggle in settings if you want to hear "Press Fire to begin", "Bonus Lambda" and the death announcements.
