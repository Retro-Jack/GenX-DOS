Tombstone City: 21st Century

Texas Instruments (John Plaster), 1981 — Solid State Software cart. Cowboy-shooter on a grid: defend Tombstone, Arizona from morgs (alien tumbleweeds) that breed wherever two existing morgs sit on either side of an empty tile. Single-screen, real-time, eight-direction movement.

The screen is a grid of stylised town tiles — cactuses scattered across an open desert. Morgs (the tumbling green aliens) appear at the edges and reproduce: any empty tile with two morgs as horizontal/vertical neighbours becomes a new morg next turn. You shoot from your cowboy sprite; clear every morg to advance. Get crushed in a morg sandwich and you die.

Getting started

  1. Cart auto-boots; press 2 at the TI title.
  2. Choose skill level 1-9 at the cart menu.

Strategy

  • Break breeding lines. The morgs reproduce only between two existing morgs along a row/column — kill any morg that completes a "sandwich" pair before its next turn.
  • Cactus walls block bullets. They also block morg movement; use them as defensive bastions, but don't get cornered.
  • Don't fire blind. Each shot reveals you; if a morg is on the same row two tiles away, you're crushed next turn.
  • Bonus skill 9 spawns extra morgs immediately — for high-score runs, take 1-3 first.
