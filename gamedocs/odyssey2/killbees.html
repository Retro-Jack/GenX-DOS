Console · Magnavox Odyssey²

Killer Bees!

Magnavox, 1983 — Ed Averett. Magnavox's last first-party release; among the largest carts on the system at 8 KB.

You don't steer one fighter — you steer a swarm. The screen shows three astronauts moving through a corridor; your bees follow your joystick as a cloud. Sting astronauts to take control of them; the controlled astronauts attack the others. The game becomes a positional puzzle of which fighter to commandeer and when to let them go.

Getting started

  1. Pick a game variant at the title with a number key.
  2. The swarm responds to the joystick as a single body — it doesn't disperse.
  3. When the swarm overlaps an astronaut, the astronaut turns and starts attacking the others.

Strategy

  • Take the strongest first. A controlled astronaut keeps fighting until killed — pick the one most likely to take out two others.
  • Let controlled astronauts work. Once you've taken one, fly the swarm to a safe spot — it can't sting again until the current host falls.
  • Watch the spray. Astronauts have a spray weapon that disperses the swarm — fly around the spray cone, not through it.
  • The swarm is fragile. Take too much spray and bees die; lose enough and the game ends.

Odyssey² controls

Joystick steers the swarm; action key concentrates it for a sting. The on-screen keyboard handles game selection at the title.
