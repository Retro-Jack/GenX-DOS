Console · Magnavox Odyssey²

UFO!

Magnavox, 1981 — Ed Averett

You pilot a single fighter against waves of UFOs that swarm in from all sides of a top-down playfield. The mothership lurks at the top of the screen, periodically dropping mines and warping smaller UFOs into the arena. Shoot fast, dodge faster, and don't let the mothership escape.

Getting started

  1. Press a number at the title to pick a difficulty. Higher numbers mean more UFOs and faster mothership patterns.
  2. Your fighter starts in the centre of the playfield and can rotate in 8 directions.
  3. Each wave ends when the mothership is destroyed — or when your fighter is.

Strategy

  • Keep moving. A stationary fighter is an easy target — UFOs lead their shots.
  • Aim for the mothership first. The smaller UFOs keep spawning until you kill it. Most of your firepower should go upward.
  • Use the edges. Wrapping around the playfield edges can break a tight pursuit, but watch for mines drifting from the top.
  • Don't chase isolated UFOs. Engage clusters that block your shot at the mothership; let stragglers come to you.

Odyssey² controls

Joystick + single action key. The fighter's bullets travel in your last-moved direction — release the stick to lock in a firing angle, then tap fire.
