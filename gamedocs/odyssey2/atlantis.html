Console · Magnavox Odyssey²

Atlantis

Imagic, 1982 — Dennis Koble (port of his Atari 2600 original)

The lost city of Atlantis sits on the seabed at the bottom of the screen, defended by three artillery emplacements that fire upward into a sky busy with alien Gorgon ships. Each ship type moves at a different speed and altitude; let any of them reach the lowest band of sky and they drop down to obliterate one of the city's seven sectors. Hold them off as long as you can.

Getting started

  1. Press a number at the title to choose a variant. Game 1 is the standard speed; later variants ramp the alien speed and add bonus point thresholds.
  2. Your three gun positions (left, centre, right) fire automatically — you only pick which gun fires next.
  3. The centre gun aims straight up; the side guns angle outward. Together they cover almost the whole sky.

Controls

InputAction
←Fire the left gun (angled up-right)
↑Fire the centre gun (straight up)
→Fire the right gun (angled up-left)

Enemy ships

TypeBehaviour
Patrol scoutSlow, top altitude band. Easy points.
CruiserMid speed, mid altitude. Numerous.
Bandit-bomberFast, low altitude. Must be hit before it drops.
Death rayPeriodic special pass at the lowest band — high points, fleeting window.

Strategy

  • Prioritise low ships. A bandit-bomber on the bottom band will destroy a city sector this turn. Centre gun first, then the others.
  • The centre gun is most accurate. Use it for fast targets in the middle of the sky; side guns for the edges.
  • Sacrifice scouts. Letting a slow top-band scout go to take out a cruiser is usually the right call.
  • Watch the city. Once Atlantis falls (all seven sectors destroyed), it's game over even if you've got plenty of ammo left.

Odyssey² controls

Each joystick direction selects a gun; no fire button is needed — pressing the direction fires that gun. There's no "moving" — your guns are fixed defensive emplacements.
