Console · Magnavox Odyssey²

Pick Axe Pete!

Magnavox, 1982 — Ed Averett

Pete is a miner trapped on a three-level platform stage with a stash of pickaxes and a single key door per floor. Rocks roll down ladders and bounce across platforms; Pete dodges, climbs, and swings his pick to smash them. Pick up the key, unlock the door, advance to the next level.

Getting started

  1. Press a number at the title to choose a difficulty (game variant). Higher numbers spawn rocks faster.
  2. Pete starts on the bottom platform. Ladders connect to the middle and top floors.
  3. Each level needs you to grab a key and reach the door before the rocks overwhelm you.

Stage objects

ObjectEffect
Rolling rockDrops down a ladder, then rolls along a platform until it falls off or hits Pete
Pickaxe pileTop platform — walk over it to top up your pick stash
KeySpawns somewhere on the level — touch to collect
DoorLocked until you grab the key; touch with key in hand to advance

Strategy

  • Stock up on picks first. Run to the top platform's pickaxe pile before you start engaging rocks — running out of picks mid-floor is fatal.
  • Smash, don't dodge, on narrow platforms. Dodging works on a long run but a rock on a short platform will corner you.
  • Climb between rocks, not under them. A rock that drops onto a ladder while you're climbing kills you. Wait for it to pass.
  • The key always lights up. If you can't see it, look for the flashing object — it stands out from rocks even at the same height.

Odyssey² controls

Joystick for movement + action key for the pickaxe. The on-screen keyboard handles game variant selection at the title.
