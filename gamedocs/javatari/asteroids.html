Asteroids

Atari, 1981 — port of the 1979 vector arcade by Lyle Rains, Ed Logg, Dominic Walsh. The 2600 version uses a bank-switching trick (Atari's first 8KB cart) to fit the rotation animation.

Your ship floats in a wraparound asteroid field. Shoot asteroids — each large one breaks into 2 mediums, each medium into 2 smalls. Clear all rocks to advance to the next wave with more, faster ones. Flying saucers appear at random and shoot at you. Hyperspace teleports you to a random spot (sometimes safe, sometimes worse).

Getting started

  1. Click GAME SELECT to cycle game variant.
  2. Click GAME RESET to start.
  3. Rotate with ←/→, thrust with ↑, fire with Space.

Strategy

  • Tap thrust. Don't hold — short taps adjust position, long thrusts send you careening into the rock you were aiming at.
  • Centre control. Drift in tight circles near screen-centre. Asteroids wrap around the playfield; the centre gives you maximum reaction time.
  • Hyperspace only as last resort. ~1 in 6 chance you re-emerge inside a rock = instant death. Better to thrust through the gap if there is one.
  • Big small-saucer payouts. Small saucers shoot accurately and are worth 1000 — kill them fast, but expect to die trying.
  • Wave 1 lurking. A favourite trick: leave one small asteroid alive on wave 1 and farm saucers indefinitely. The 2600 version has a max-wave loop, so this is the actual high-score path.

Atari 2600 controls

Two-button single-stick, modified for Asteroids: stick handles rotation + thrust + hyperspace, button fires. GAME RESET is needed to start play.
