Home Computer · Commodore 128 (VDC 80-col)

Invaders

Homebrew Space Invaders written for the C128's 80-column VDC mode — descending alien ranks, a lone laser base, and a difficulty you can dial up or down on the fly. The wide 80-col screen gives the alien grid plenty of room to march.

⚠ Accuracy not guaranteed. This is an obscure / homebrew title with no published manual, so these controls couldn't be checked against an authoritative source. Watch the title screen — it may list the keys in-game.

Boots straight into VDC 80-column mode. Slide your base along the bottom and pick off the alien rows before they reach you — the classic arcade format, with a speed control so you can tune the pace to taste.

Getting started

  1. The .prg autoboots into VDC 80-col — click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press F (Fire) at the title screen to start.

Controls

All keyboard — the title screen suggests using 1 and 2 on the numeric keypad so your firing hand stays free.

KeyAction
FFire (also starts the game)
1Move base left (numpad 1)
2Move base right (numpad 2)
PPause
Speed up / slow downDedicated keys raise or lower the game speed (shown on the title screen)

Strategy

  • Thin the edges first. Clearing the outermost columns buys room to dodge as the swarm speeds up on each descent.
  • Use the speed control. Slow it down to learn the patterns, then crank it up once you're confident — the score rewards the faster pace.
  • Numpad grip. Driving with 1/2 on the numpad keeps F under your other hand for rapid fire.
  • Pause is your friend. P freezes a hairy wave so you can reset your nerves before the aliens drop another row.
