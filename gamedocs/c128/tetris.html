Home Computer · Commodore 128 (VDC 80-col)

Tetris 128

Homebrew, early 1990s — a Tetris implementation written for the C128's VDC 80-column screen. The wide canvas leaves room for the well, the next-piece preview, score, level, and lines all on screen at once without the 40-col scrunch.

Standard Tetris rules: tetrominoes fall, fill rows to clear them, level speed climbs every 10 lines. The 80-col VDC display means the well is rendered at a more pleasant aspect ratio than the C64 ports, and the side panels actually fit.

Getting started

  1. The .d64 autoboots into VDC 80-col; click the canvas first.
  2. Pick a starting level (0-9) with ← / →, hit L Ctrl (fire) to start.

Strategy

  • Keep the right column open. Old-school Tetris doctrine: leave the rightmost column clear so a vertical I-piece can clear four lines at once (tetris bonus).
  • Don't stack flat. Build a slight slope so misplaced pieces have somewhere to drift; flat stacks force exact placements.
  • Soft-drop vs hard-drop. Soft drop earns one point per row; hard drop two per row. On level 0-3, soft-drop everything for grinding score.
  • Speed jumps at level 10. Around level 10 the fall rate becomes faster than most reaction times — the score grind is in 0-9.
