The Last V8

Mastertronic, 1985 — Steven Boyett and Mev Dinc's post-apocalyptic chase. Year 2008: nuclear war has wiped out civilisation; your radioactive V8 is the last working car. Outrun the falling debris from a damaged sky to reach the safe zone.

Side-on auto-scrolling racer. The road scrolls past at fixed speed; debris pillars and rocks drop from above and crater the surface in front of you. Touch nothing, reach the bunker. Top-down split-window shows fuel + temperature gauges (the V8 overheats if pushed hard). Levels are short — survival times of a minute or two — but punishing.

Getting started

  1. The .d64 autoboots; click the canvas first.
  2. Press L Ctrl (fire) at the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
↑Accelerate (heats engine)
↓Brake
← / →Swerve lane (no full steering — just two lateral nudges)
L CtrlJump (clears low debris; consumes fuel)

Strategy

  • Look ahead, not at the bonnet. Debris shadows on the road tell you where impacts will land — by the time the rock is on-screen, it's too late.
  • Heat is the silent killer. The temperature gauge climbs faster than fuel falls; periodic braking is the only way to survive long stretches.
  • Jump conservatively. Each jump burns fuel and locks your lane in mid-air; reserve for unavoidable craters.
  • The bunker entrance is on the right. Each level ends at a fixed marker — don't burn yourself out racing past it.
