The Rocky Horror Show

CRL Group, 1985 — Ian Ellery's official tie-in to the cult musical. Brad has been turned to stone in Frank N. Furter's castle; play Janet (or Brad on the flip-side disk) and rebuild him by collecting his body parts scattered across the mansion before the alien rocket launches.

Flick-screen mansion-explorer with each room a single-screen puzzle. Pick up Brad's pieces (head, arms, legs, torso) one at a time and reassemble on the de Medusa device. Frank, Riff Raff, Magenta, and Columbia patrol — they steal whatever piece you're carrying if they touch you. Time-limited: the Transylvanians' rocket lifts off at midnight regardless.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press L Ctrl (fire) at the title to start.

Controls

← / →Walk left / right (Numpad 4/6 also work)

Strategy

  • Map first, scavenge second. The mansion is roughly 5×5 rooms with several locked doors; sketch the layout on your first life so you know where each body part is when speed matters later.
  • Hand off via the de Medusa device. You can't carry two pieces; deliver each one to the central device before grabbing the next.
  • The Transylvanians follow predictable beats. Each character roams a fixed beat — Frank in the lab, Riff Raff in the corridors. Time crossings between their loops rather than running.
  • The clock is unforgiving. Without a route plan, the rocket leaves at midnight whether you've reassembled Brad or not — game over.
