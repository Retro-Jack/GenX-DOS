Wumpus 2.0

Homebrew, 2023 — a modern C128 take on Gregory Yob's 1973 cave-deduction classic, written for the VDC 80-column screen. The wider display lets the cave map and the room descriptions share a screen.

Boots into VDC 80-col. You're in a network of 20 caves arranged as a dodecahedron, each room connected to three others. Somewhere in the maze lives the Wumpus; nearby rooms have bats (random teleport) and pits (instant death). Sensory hints tell you what's adjacent — "I smell a Wumpus", "I feel a draft", "I hear bats" — but never which exact tunnel. Deduce the Wumpus's room and fire your one arrow.

Getting started

  1. Runs in VDC 80-column mode. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Enter at the title; the cave map prints with your starting room.

Controls

Typed-command parser. You're prompted between "Move or Shoot?" and a tunnel number:

InputEffect
MMove — followed by a tunnel number
SShoot — followed by 1-5 tunnel numbers for a crooked arrow path
<tunnel #>Enter the adjacent room number when prompted

Strategy

  • Track sensed warnings on paper. Each room you visit reports adjacent hazards. Pencil them on a map of the cave and you can triangulate the Wumpus, bats, and pit locations.
  • Bats are reversible, pits aren't. Walking into a bat room teleports you — survivable. Walking into a pit ends the game.
  • One arrow. Shoot only when triangulation is certain — a missed shot wakes the Wumpus, who relocates and may kill you in the next move.
  • The Wumpus sleeps until disturbed. Repeatedly entering Wumpus-adjacent rooms is safe; entering his room directly is not. You can scout without dying.
