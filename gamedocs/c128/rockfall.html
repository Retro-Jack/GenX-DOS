Home Computer · Commodore 128 (VDC 80-col)

Rockfall 128

Homebrew, 2023 — a Boulder Dash-inspired puzzle game written for C128 native mode with the VDC 80-column screen. Modern C128 scene production, runs entirely on real hardware too.

Top-down cave digger in the Boulder Dash mould. You push through dirt; boulders and diamonds rest on dirt and fall when unsupported. Stand under a falling rock = death. Collect the per-level diamond quota; the exit opens, race for it. The 80-col VDC gives a much wider playfield than the C64 Boulder Dash — more cave per screen, fewer flick-screen panic moments.

Getting started

  1. The .prg auto-loads via this emulator's autostart; click the canvas first.
  2. Pick a level from the menu, press L Ctrl (fire) to start.

Strategy

  • Plan vertical descents. Digging straight down means anything above can fall. Tunnel sideways first to clear escape routes, then commit downward.
  • Boulders block monsters. Pushed boulders make excellent walls — corner a wandering enemy with one, harvest its diamonds.
  • Diamonds in a stack fall together. Knock the bottom one out and the whole column collapses; great for clearing tight clusters in one move.
  • Map the exit before the quota. The exit opens the moment quota hits; you don't want to be on the wrong side of the cave when it does.
