Star Fleet I: The War Begins!

Interstel / Cygnus, 1987 — Trevor C. Sorensen's text-and-graphics space combat strategy. Command a Federation-style battlecruiser hunting Krellan and Zaldron ships across a 192-quadrant galaxy. Rank up from Cadet to Fleet Admiral by completing missions of increasing scale.

An 80-column Commodore 128 title — boots into the C128's VDC chip (8563), giving crisp wide screen for the densely typed tactical readouts. Turn-based commands typed at a parser prompt; combat resolves in short real-time bursts. Save your captain between sessions to carry rank forward (the .d64 is read-only in the browser, so use the save / load buttons). Five save slots per game — pick 1–5 from the save or load menu.

Getting started

  1. The .d64 autoboots into the VDC 80-column screen; click the canvas first.
  2. Type your captain name when prompted, pick a mission difficulty, hit Enter.

Controls

Text parser — every action is a typed command at the Command? prompt. The common ones:

CommandEffect
LRSLong-Range Sensors — show 9-quadrant region
SRSShort-Range Sensors — show current quadrant
NAV <course> <warp>Navigate to a quadrant (course 0-360, warp 0-12)
PHA <energy>Fire phasers at the highlighted target
TOR <course>Fire torpedoes
SHI U / SHI DRaise / lower shields
DOCDock with a starbase (must be adjacent)
HELPShow the full command list

Strategy

  • Dock between fights. Starbases refill energy, torpedoes, and repair damage. Plot the nearest base before every engagement.
  • Manage shield energy. Shields drain energy reserves; keep them down between fights to conserve, raise the moment a contact appears on LRS.
  • Phasers scale with energy. Dumping 1500 energy into a phaser shot one-shots most Krellans; conserve torpedoes for cloaked Zaldron destroyers.
  • The galaxy is small but dense. 192 quadrants; the mission gives a star-date deadline — plot routes, don't wander.
