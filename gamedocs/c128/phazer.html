Home Computer · Commodore 128 (VDC 80-col)

Phazer

Homebrew, 2023 — a modern C128 native-mode arcade title written for the VDC 80-column screen by the active homebrew scene that still ships C128-exclusive software on physical disks and CSDb.

⚠ Accuracy not guaranteed. This is an obscure / homebrew title with no published manual, so these controls couldn't be checked against an authoritative source.

Boots into VDC 80-col mode. Fixed-screen shoot-'em-up: ship-grid layout, you sit at the bottom and waves of enemies sweep across the wide 80-column playfield. The 80-col VDC gives more horizontal real estate than the C64's 40 columns, so dodge lanes feel less cramped than equivalent C64 ports.

Getting started

  1. The .prg auto-loads via this emulator's auto-start; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press L Ctrl (fire) at the title to start.

Strategy

  • Use the width. 80-col playfield is twice the lateral range of C64 versions of similar games — don't cling to one corner, you have room to outrun sweeps.
  • Identify the priority enemy. Each wave usually has one fast/diving enemy worth killing first; the rest follow predictable patterns.
  • Spread shots only on dense waves. If a power-up gives a spread/triple shot, keep moving — stationary spread covers nothing.
