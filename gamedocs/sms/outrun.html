Console · Sega Master System

Out Run

Sega, 1987. The arcade legend at home: a Ferrari, an open road, three songs on the radio and five finish lines — you pick the route at every fork.

A checkpoint chase across a branching map: every run starts at Coconut Beach and forks left or right at the end of each scene, five scenes deep, toward one of five goals — Vineyard, Death Valley, Desolation Hill, Autobahn or Lakeside. Beat each scene's clock and the spare seconds carry forward; miss it and the race is over on the spot.

Getting started

  1. First, choose your radio station with ←/→ and confirm with Z or X: Magical Sound Shower (left), Passing Breeze (default) or Splash Wave (right).
  2. Wait for the green light, then hold X and go.
  3. Start in low gear, wind it up, then shift high on the straight.

Yes, the gears are that way round — down for high, up for low, exactly as the manual demands.

Strategy

  • Low gear is a cornering tool. It holds up to 170 km/h and hugs the road — downshift into the curve, upshift out of it, straight from the manual's own driving school.
  • Lift off before you brake. Releasing the accelerator slows you smoothly; the manual advises quick taps of the brake only for emergencies — slamming it means a dead stop and lost momentum.
  • Bank time early. Spare checkpoint seconds are added to the next scene's limit — a fast first scene buys slack for the hard ones. The goal-line bonus is your remaining time × one million points.
  • The forks are the game. Five goals, twenty-five scenes, and each junction commits you — after a game over, the course map shows how far your line got.
