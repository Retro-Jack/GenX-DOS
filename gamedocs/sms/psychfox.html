Psycho Fox

Sega / Vic Tokai, 1989. A momentum platformer with a Shinto twist: the Psycho Stick shapeshifts you between fox, hippo, monkey and tiger — and Birdfly rides your fist.

Psycho Fox runs on physics — real acceleration, slopes that launch you, and a punch that doubles as a boomerang when Birdfly's aboard. The trick is form-switching: each animal solves different terrain, and the Psycho Stick (used from the pause inventory) is how you swap.

Getting started

  1. Press Z or X at the title to start.
  2. Punch the eggs — they hold items, Psycho Sticks and Birdfly.
  3. Press Enter (Pause) to open the inventory: pick an item with ←/→ and use it with X — that's how the Psycho Stick transforms you.

Controls

Strategy

  • Birdfly is a free hit. While he's on your fist he's both a boomerang punch and armour — take damage and he flies off instead of you dying. Re-find him fast.
  • Momentum is the real enemy. The physics reward long committed runs and punish hesitation — most pits claim players mid-dither, not mid-sprint.
  • Match form to terrain. Blocks in the way want the hippo; high ledges want the monkey; long flat stretches belong to the tiger.
