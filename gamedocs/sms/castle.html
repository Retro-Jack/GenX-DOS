Console · Sega Master System

Castle of Illusion Starring Mickey Mouse

Sega, 1990. Mickey storms the witch Mizrabel's castle to rescue Minnie — five worlds of illusion behind five doors, seven Gems of the Rainbow to claim.

One of the Master System's prettiest platformers: every castle door opens into a themed labyrinth — an enchanted forest, a toyland, a dessert factory, a giant's library, a clock tower — each guarded by a Master of Illusion holding a gem. Bounce on the bad guys, throw what isn't nailed down, and mind the clock.

Getting started

  1. Press Z or X through the story to the Game Selection screen, choose with ↑/↓, and press either button to start: Practice (three levels, three gems) or Normal (all five worlds, seven gems).
  2. Press ↑ at a castle door to enter it.
  3. Watch the meters: power stars drain when Mickey's hit, the timer costs a try at zero, and three lost tries end the game — though Mickey can continue up to nine times.

Strategy

  • Bounce everything. The manual's first hint — the bounce attack kills, breaks treasure chests, and doubles as transport: bouncing off enemies' heads reaches high ledges and crosses wide pits.
  • Cakes are power, ears are lives. Big cake refills two power stars, small one star; Mouse Ears grant an extra try, as does every 10,000 points.
  • Sliding is steerable. On a slope, press away from the slide to control it — and a slope slide into a mid-air direction press plus Z is a proper attack.
  • The time bonus is real money. Remaining time converts to points at each level's end, and points convert to tries. Dawdling costs twice.
