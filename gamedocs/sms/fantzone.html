Console · Sega Master System

Fantasy Zone

Sega, 1986. The original "cute-em-up": Opa-Opa the sentient spaceship shoots, bombs and *goes shopping* through eight pastel rounds.

A shooter where the scrolling obeys you: each round is a loop you fly left or right around, hunting its bases — kill them all and the boss appears. Enemies drop coins, and coins matter, because Fantasy Zone's real weapon system is a shop in a balloon. The eighth round is the genre's original boss rush: every previous boss again, then the finale.

Getting started

  1. Press Z or X at the title to start.
  2. Sweep the round in either direction and destroy all the bases — the radar-style layout loops, so pick a direction and clear as you go.
  3. Catch the coins everything drops, and board the SHOP balloon when it floats by.

Strategy

  • The shop is the power curve. Engines for speed, weapon and bomb upgrades, extra lives — but every purchase raises that item's next price, so buy what you'll actually use.
  • Death is expensive. Losing a life strips your upgrades — the rebuild costs more than the life did. Fly like you're carrying money, because you are.
  • Timed weapons, permanent engines. The flashy weapons run on timers; engine upgrades stay. When the guns lapse mid-boss, remember you still have bombs.
  • Save coins for round 8. The boss rush is where lives and firepower get spent — arriving rich is arriving prepared.
