Console · Atari 2600

Centipede

Atari, 1983 — Tom Loughry's 2600 port of Dona Bailey & Ed Logg's 1981 arcade. The arcade's trackball is replaced by a 4-way joystick; you control a wand-wielding "shooter" at the bottom of a mushroom field.

A centipede snakes down through a field of mushrooms. Shoot each segment — hit a middle one and the centipede splits into two head-led halves. Spider hops around eating mushrooms (and you). Flea drops new mushrooms from the top. Scorpion poisons mushrooms — a centipede that touches one drops straight down. Clear all segments to advance the wave.

Getting started

  1. Press V (Select) for variant.
  2. Press Enter (Reset) to start.
  3. Move the wand in the bottom band with arrows; Space fires upward.

Strategy

  • Always shoot from beneath the centipede head. Heads pass through mushrooms only by veering down. Lining up under a head one row up = guaranteed hit, no split.
  • Keep firing. Auto-fire (held Space) is the only sane play. Single shots waste time.
  • Cull mushrooms. Too many mushrooms compress the centipede into vertical chains that drop fast. Keep the lower third sparse.
  • Sniper the spider. Approach it when it pauses (it lurks ~1/3 from the bottom). Touching it = death; killing it close = 900 pts.
  • Poisoned mushroom = drop-attack. A centipede crossing a poisoned mushroom plunges straight at the bottom. Either eat the mushroom yourself or position to intercept the dropping head.

Atari 2600 controls

The 2600 port replaces the arcade trackball with a 4-way joystick — diagonals are harder. Most players slow down with frequent stops to aim precisely. Enter (Reset) starts the game.
