Space Invaders

Atari, 1980 — Rick Maurer's port of Tomohiro Nishikado's 1978 arcade. The game that quadrupled 2600 sales: the first arcade-to-console license.

A grid of 36 invaders descends in a V-pattern. Shoot them all before they reach the bottom of the screen. Four bunkers shield you (and erode as both sides shoot through them). Mystery saucer flies overhead for bonus points. 112 variant modes — pick mode 1 for the canonical Atari port.

Getting started

  1. Press V (Select) to cycle game mode (1 = classic; 112 in total).
  2. Press Enter (Reset) to start.
  3. Move left/right with the joystick, fire with Space. One bullet on-screen at a time.

Strategy

  • Bottom row first. Each row scored higher, but the bottom row is the most dangerous (closest to you). Always clear column-wise from the bottom up.
  • End columns. Eliminating the leftmost and rightmost columns shrinks the grid's horizontal movement → slower march down.
  • Bunker tactics. Don't shoot through your own bunker for a clear lane — the bunker degrades with each shot through it.
  • Mystery saucer. The UFO at top scores 100-300 points. Time your shot — the score depends on number of shots fired so far. 23 shots = guaranteed 300 in some variants.
  • Reflex test at end. The last invader speeds up rapidly. Position-shoot rather than chase — pick a column it'll cross and wait.

Atari 2600 controls

Single-stick + fire. Enter (Reset) to start, V (Select) to change variant (112 modes), Space fires.
