Home Computer · Commodore Plus/4

Popeye

Parker Brothers / Macmillan, 1984 — home conversion of Nintendo's 1982 arcade. Catch Olive's falling hearts (then musical notes, then "HELP" letters) across three platform-and-ladder stages while dodging Bluto and the Sea Hag's vulture.

Three rotating stages, each a single multi-floor screen. Olive Oyl tosses items from her top platform; Popeye must catch them before they fall off the screen. Complete the stage when all items are collected. Bluto stalks Popeye on Olive's level and can climb to your floor; the Sea Hag throws bottles you can punch with the spinach can.

  2. Press L Ctrl (fire) at the title to begin.

Strategy

  • Catch hearts in pairs. Olive tosses two at a time; position under her so both land in your sprite — missing one doesn't kill you but wastes the toss.
  • Eat the spinach can early on Bluto-heavy screens. Spinach lets you punch Bluto off the platform for big points and a brief safe window — save it for the moment he's closing in, not the moment you spawn.
  • Use the see-saw on stage 2. Bouncing on the see-saw flips you up a level instantly — faster than climbing the ladder Bluto's parked on.
  • The vulture is predictable. The Sea Hag's bottle and the vulture both follow set arcs; punch the bottles for points, duck the vulture by climbing.
