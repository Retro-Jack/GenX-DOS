Citadel

Martech, 1985 — Patrick Buckland's flick-screen platform adventure. You're a wizard-in-training exploring a 100-room castle; collect spell components, evade monsters, and find your way to the throne-room exit.

Flick-screen exploration across a roughly 10×10 grid of rooms. Each room is a fixed-platform layout with patrolling skeletons, ghosts, or spiders. Pick up keys, scrolls, and potions; some doors only open with the right item, and some monsters are only beatable after you've found a specific weapon. The TED-colour palette gives every wing of the castle a distinct hue.

Strategy

  • Map the castle as you go. 100 flick-screens with no in-game map; pen-and-paper (or scribbled mental landmarks like "blue room with three columns") is essential.
  • Pick up everything. Some items look useless on the screen you find them but unlock a door several rooms away. There's no inventory weight limit worth worrying about early.
  • Skeletons respawn but ghosts don't. Killing a ghost permanently clears it from that room — prioritise ghosts in rooms you'll cross often.
  • Save state at branch points. Citadel has no in-game save; lean on the save / load buttons when you're about to commit to an unexplored wing.
