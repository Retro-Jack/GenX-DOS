Home Computer · Commodore Plus/4

Trailblazer

Gremlin Graphics, 1986 — Mr. Chip Software's into-the-screen ball-roller. Bounce a football down a tile-strip suspended in space, hitting speed-up/slow-down tiles and dodging the gaps. Time-trial mode against the clock; head-to-head split-screen against a friend.

Forward-scrolling 3D-perspective course made of multi-coloured chequer tiles. Each tile colour does something — red = gap (don't fall in), green = slow, yellow = boost, white = jump. Steer left/right to pick lanes; press jump to hop hazards. Time-trial: beat the level's par. Two-player: first across the finish wins. The Plus/4 TED chip's smooth horizontal-scroll is the conversion's highlight.

Getting started

  1. The .prg auto-loads; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Select 1P TIME TRIAL or 2P at the title (joystick down + fire).

Controls

KeyAction
← / →Steer ball lane
↑Jump (clears a single gap tile)
L CtrlBoost on jump (extends arc)

Strategy

  • Read three tiles ahead. The perspective means immediate tiles are already committed; your steering only affects what's coming, not what's under you.
  • Yellow tiles are a trap when stacked. Two consecutive boost tiles fling you off the steerable speed range — pair them with green to bleed off velocity.
  • Jump-and-boost clears double gaps. Hold fire as you press up to extend the jump arc over two consecutive red tiles.
  • The course wraps around par + 5%. Time-trial failure resets you, not the level; learning a course only costs the failed clock, not the whole run.
