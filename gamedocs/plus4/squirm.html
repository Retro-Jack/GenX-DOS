Squirm

Anirog / Mr. Micro, 1985 — Centipede-by-way-of-Snake action puzzler. You control a worm that grows as it eats; navigate a maze of mushrooms while a hostile centipede descends, segmenting whenever it hits a wall.

Single-screen arcade arena, played as a worm rather than a centipede gun. Eat food pellets to score and grow; collide with mushrooms or the descending centipede to lose a segment. Centipede splits when shot or wall-blocked. Survive long enough and faster waves spawn.

Getting started

Strategy

  • Don't trail through your own body. Snake rules: turning back on yourself ends the run instantly.
  • Mushrooms are walls AND points. Centipede splits when it hits one — herding the centipede into mushroom clusters maximises kills per shot.
  • Stay in open lanes when the centipede is fast. Cornering yourself near the bottom row gives you no room to dodge when the centipede reaches the player level.
  • The first wave is the easiest test. Use it to get a feel for turn radius; later waves add spiders and faster centipede speeds.
