Mercenary: Escape from Targ

Novagen, 1986 — Paul Woakes' first-person wireframe-vector open-world classic, ported to the Plus/4 from its Atari 8-bit and C64 origins. Crash-land on Targ, scavenge a city of glowing 3D buildings, hire to either side of an ongoing alien war, and find a way off the planet.

Real-time first-person vector graphics. You can walk, drive, fly. Buildings and ships are drawn as wireframe polygons; doors are touch-to-enter. Pilot the recovered ship through canyons, dock with structures, trade items, and negotiate with the warring Mechanoids and Palyars. A genuine open-world game years ahead of its time — and Plus/4 hardware just barely keeps up.

Getting started

  1. The .prg auto-loads; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Wait for the intro then press L Ctrl to begin.

Strategy

  • Talk to the computer. Benson, your onboard AI, has hints — listen to the text scrolling at the bottom every time you enter a new location.
  • Map the city. Buildings are numbered; jot down which number holds what. Without a map the city becomes a maze of identical wireframe pyramids.
  • Pick a side, then play both. Mechanoids and Palyars both pay for jobs; nothing stops you from working for one and selling the goods to the other.
  • Save aggressively. Wireframe collisions on the Plus/4 can be unforgiving; the save / load buttons are your friend in the canyons. Five save slots per game — pick 1–5 from the save or load menu.
