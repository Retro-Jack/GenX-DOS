Kikstart

Mastertronic, 1985 — Shaun Southern's side-on motorcycle obstacle course (the Plus/4 conversion of his Kwik Snax / Lotus Esprit pipeline before they hit the big time). Eight stages of jumping logs, popping wheelies over oil slicks, and beating the clock.

Side-scrolling moto-trials. The course scrolls right at a fixed pace; your job is to ride over a steady stream of obstacles — barrels, ramps, rolling tires, water — without crashing. Three lives; complete the stage to advance. Mastertronic's £1.99 budget price meant tight code but very little hand-holding.

Getting started

  1. The .prg auto-loads; click the canvas first.
  2. Press L Ctrl at the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
↑Jump
↓Wheelie (front wheel up — clears low obstacles)
← / →Slow down / speed up
L CtrlPause / unpause

Strategy

  • Wheelie over short stuff, jump over tall stuff. Wheelies are faster to recover from than jumps — but won't clear a ramp or a log pile. Read each obstacle's height before committing.
  • Slow down before water. Hitting water at full speed almost always wipes out; tap left to drop a gear before the splash.
  • The course is deterministic. Each stage has a fixed obstacle layout; once you memorise the sequence, it becomes a rhythm game.
  • Don't bother trying to brake mid-jump. Bike physics commit on take-off; correct on the previous obstacle, not the current one.
