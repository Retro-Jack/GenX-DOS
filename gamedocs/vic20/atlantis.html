Atlantis

Imagic, 1983 — VIC-20 port of Dennis Koble's Atari 2600 classic. Defend the underwater city from waves of Gorgon ships crossing the screen at three different altitudes.

Atlantis sits at the bottom of the screen with six landmarks and two side cannons + one centre cannon. Gorgon ships fly across at three altitudes — high (fast, score), middle, and low (lethal — they fire downward at your city). Shoot them before they reach the right edge; when too many landmarks fall, Atlantis is destroyed.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to start the first wave.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 0 / 5Fire centre cannon (longest range, narrowest arc)
Numpad 4Fire left cannon (angled)
Numpad 6Fire right cannon (angled)

Strategy

  • Centre cannon for high altitude. The high-altitude ships are fastest and worth the most points — only the centre cannon's range reaches them.
  • Side cannons for low-altitude defence. Low-altitude bombers are the real threat — drop them on entry with whichever side cannon faces them.
  • Don't chase the high ships. If you commit centre to a high-altitude target while a low bomber drops, you'll lose a landmark.
  • The Acroplex bonus. Atari 2600 lore says shooting the right ship gives a flying Acroplex to escape — VIC-20 version omits this; just play for landmark survival.
