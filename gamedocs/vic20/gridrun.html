Gridrunner

Jeff Minter / Llamasoft, 1982 — the game that launched Llamasoft and built Minter's reputation. Centipede crossed with paranoia. The grid is the battlefield.

A grid fills the playfield. A centipede-like "snake" segments down it. Droids drop bombs at random intervals. A vertical and a horizontal "X-Y" zapper sweep the edges. You shoot up while moving along the bottom row. Survive a level, the next one ramps the snake speed and adds segments.

Getting started

  1. Game auto-boots; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to spawn.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Move left / right along the bottom row
Numpad 0 / 5Fire (auto-aims up)

Threats

ThreatBehaviour
Snake / centipedeSlithers down the grid; each segment is a separate hit; tail-segment dies, head replaces it
Droid bombsFall in straight lines from the top — predictable, but constant
X zapperHorizontal laser sweeps the bottom row — your row
Y zapperVertical laser sweeps a column at random intervals

Strategy

  • Pacing the X zapper. It crosses on a fixed clock — learn the cycle, never get caught at full stop.
  • Snake from the tail. Shoot the rear segment; head segments respawn but tail kills are the only progress.
  • Y zapper telegraphs. A flash precedes the strike — react on the flash, not the laser.
  • Minter style. Score-loop game; difficulty plateaus at ~level 20 in a way that rewards grind. The wave-30+ run is the speedrun community's metric.
