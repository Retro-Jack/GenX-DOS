Avenger

Commodore, 1981 — one of the VIC-20 launch cartridges. The platform's officially-blessed Space Invaders clone, written by Commodore's in-house team.

Twenty-two invaders in three rows descend a step at a time, firing back. You move left/right at the bottom of the screen with three shields, and your laser auto-aims forward. Clear the wave to advance — the aliens come back faster and closer.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots — VICE autostarts the cart into the title.
  2. Click the canvas first to give it keyboard focus.
  3. Press a key past the title; you'll spawn at the bottom-centre.

Controls (joystick on numpad)

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Move left / right
Numpad 0 / 5Fire

VICE is configured with the unified GenX-DOS keyboard joystick on the numpad; alphanumeric keys still type as themselves for the BASIC prompt.

Strategy

  • One shot at a time. Like the arcade, you can't fire again until your previous laser clears the screen — pace your shots.
  • Last alien sprints. The final invader of a wave moves twice as fast — track it across the screen, don't try to corner it.
  • Shields wear down both ways. Your own shots erode the shields from below — keep them as cover but don't waste them.
  • UFO bonus. The drifting saucer gives 50–300 points depending on shots already fired this wave; pattern-memorise the 300.
