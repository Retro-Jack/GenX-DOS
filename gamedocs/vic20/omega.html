Omega Race

Commodore, 1982 — port of Midway's 1981 vector arcade. You orbit a closed rectangular arena; enemy ships orbit too, and you all share the same wraparound geometry. Inertia is the puzzle.

Your ship moves with Newtonian momentum around the inside of a rectangle. Thrust to accelerate, rotate to aim. Enemies (Drone Ships, Command Ships, Death Ships, Photon Mines) all behave differently and the arena edges bounce both you and shots. Survive a wave to advance.

Getting started

  1. Game auto-boots; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to spawn.

Strategy

  • Inertia kills more than enemies. Tap-thrust; never hold. A drift that crosses the arena is a drift you can't reverse in time.
  • Use the edges. Your shots bounce off the rectangle once before dissipating — flank enemies orbiting opposite you.
  • Kill commands first. Letting commands live escalates the wave from drones to death ships fast.
  • Mines are bonus, not threat. They're slow; close in, kill the high-value mine, then deal with the orbiters.
