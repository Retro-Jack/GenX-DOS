Gorf

Commodore, 1982 — port of Midway's 1981 arcade. Four distinct missions — Astro Battles, Laser Attack, Galaxians, Space Warp — fought back-to-back. Survive all four to face the Flag Ship and start the loop one rank higher.

An anthology shooter pretending to be one game. Mission 1 plays like Space Invaders; mission 2 throws Laser fighters at you; mission 3 is Galaxians divebombing; mission 4 sends ships flying out of a centre warp. Clear all four to promote through the ranks: Space Cadet → Space Captain → Space Colonel → Space General → Space Warrior → Space Avenger.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots; click the canvas first for keyboard focus.
  2. Press a key past the title; mission 1 begins immediately.

Controls (joystick on numpad)

Mission-by-mission

MissionTactic
1 — Astro BattlesShoot through the shield centre to make a kill-tunnel; pick the row top-to-bottom
2 — Laser AttackLasers shoot first — strafe constantly, never linger under one
3 — GalaxiansDivebombers swoop in pairs; lead the dive, don't track the bomber
4 — Space WarpShips funnel out of the centre — stand off-axis, pick them at the edge
Boss — Flag ShipShoot the central core through gaps in its rotating barrier

Strategy

  • Promotion = harder loop. Higher rank = faster enemies, smaller windows; the run-game is points-per-loop, not progress.
  • Speech-chip taunts (from the arcade) are stripped on VIC-20 — but the gameplay timing is faithful.
  • Your laser is slow. Lead all moving targets; don't shoot where they are.
