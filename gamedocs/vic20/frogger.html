Home Computer · Commodore VIC-20

Frogger

Parker Bros. / Sierra On-Line, 1983 — officially licensed VIC-20 port of Konami's 1981 arcade. Five lanes of traffic, five lanes of river, five home pads.

Cross the highway, ride the river, fill all five home pads. Cars and trucks kill on touch; water kills unless you ride a log or turtle; the timer drains while you stall. Standard Frogger rules with the colour palette VIC-20 was famous for — vivid blues and greens despite the 22-column screen.

Getting started

  1. Game auto-boots; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to spawn at the bottom.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 8Hop forward (north)
Numpad 2Hop back (south)
Numpad 4Hop left

Each hop is a discrete one-tile move. The frog snaps to the grid on logs and turtles.

Strategy

  • Diving turtles. Submerge on a fixed beat. Watch one cycle from the verge before committing to a turtle ride.
  • Outer pads first. Centre pads are easy; tackling the outer two under time pressure is the usual death.
  • Crocodile pads. Two of the five pads occasionally turn into crocodiles — eyes open before the final hop.
  • The VIC-20's wider sprites change collision feel vs. arcade — cars are slightly more forgiving on the hop window.
