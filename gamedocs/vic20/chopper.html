Choplifter

Creative Software, 1982 — port of Dan Gorlin's 1982 Apple ][ classic. Hostage-rescue helicopter game; fly across enemy lines, pick up POWs, fly them back to base.

Sixty-four hostages stranded across four barracks east of your base. Tanks, jets, and a satellite drone try to stop you. Land near a barracks, hostages run out and climb aboard (max 16 at a time), then fly home. Lose all hostages or get destroyed — game over.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to take off from base.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Fly left / right
Numpad 8Climb / lift
Numpad 2Descend / land
Numpad 0 / 5Fire
Tap direction twiceRotate helicopter — face left, right, or forward (down-firing)

Strategy

  • The rotation matters. Choplifter's defining mechanic — face left/right to fire that way, face down to shoot the ground. Tanks need ground-fire; jets need side-fire.
  • Land close, but not on hostages. Touching a hostage with the skids kills them. Hover over, then settle gently.
  • Sixteen at a time. Don't be greedy — clear a barracks (4 runs of 16) before moving east to the next one.
  • The drone is persistent. A homing satellite that ignores your fire pattern; outrun it east-or-west, don't engage.
