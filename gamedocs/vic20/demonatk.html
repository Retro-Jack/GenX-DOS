Demon Attack

Imagic, 1983 — VIC-20 port of Rob Fulop's 1982 Atari 2600 masterpiece. Demonic birds dive at your laser base from the sky in escalating waves of geometric pattern shifts.

Three demons descend from the top, weaving in S-shapes. Shoot them; new waves get faster patterns, split into pairs on hit, drop bombs, then drop divebombing pairs. Each surviving wave you advance; lose all three bases and the demons claim the moon.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to spawn the laser base.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Move base left / right
Numpad 0 / 5Fire laser (auto-aims up)

Wave progression

WaveWhat changes
1–3Single demons, gentle S-pattern
4–6Demons split into two on first hit; pairs cross-pattern
7–10Demons drop bombs straight down at your base position
11+Tracker demons home in on the laser base

Strategy

  • Pattern lock. Demons sway in fixed sinusoids — predict the next position, fire ahead, don't react.
  • Split demons are double points. Don't fear the hit-then-split — each half pays full score on the kill.
  • Bombs are line-of-sight. Stay under a demon only long enough to fire; drift left/right between shots.
  • Three bases per credit. Lose all three and the run ends; pace your aggression vs. lives remaining.
