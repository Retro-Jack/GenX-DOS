Home Computer · Commodore VIC-20

Radar Rat Race

Commodore, 1981 — VIC-20 launch cart. Inspired by Sega's 1980 Rally-X maze-driver. You're a mouse collecting cheese in a maze while cats and a radar-tracked rat hunt you.

Top-down maze view of the whole playfield (no scrolling — the VIC-20's screen IS the level). Collect every wedge of cheese to clear the round; the cats patrol fixed routes; the rat homes in on your radar signature. Touch any of them and you lose a life.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots; click the canvas first for keyboard focus.
  2. Press a key past the title to spawn.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 8 2 4 6Move up / down / left / right
Numpad 0Drop cheese (decoy — slows the rat briefly)

Strategy

  • Plan a route, not a reaction. Cats are predictable — pick a sweep that doesn't intersect any patrol.
  • Use the rat against the cats. The rat tracks YOU, ignores cats. Sometimes you can lead it into a cat's path, taking one out.
  • Cheese drops are limited. Save them for the final sprint when one cat is between you and the last wedge.
  • Wrap-around corridors. The left and right maze edges connect — a short escape if a cat closes from one side.
