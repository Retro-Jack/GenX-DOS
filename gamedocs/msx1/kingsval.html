King's Valley

Konami, 1985 — single-screen Egyptian pyramid puzzle-platformer. Vick the explorer raids King Tut's pyramid, one chamber per screen, collecting jewels while dodging mummies who patrol the platforms.

Each level is a single screen of stone platforms connected by ropes, ladders and ledges. Collect all jewels to open the exit door. Mummies walk fixed routes — one touch and you die. Vick carries a chisel he can throw forward to dispatch a mummy if he picks one up; otherwise it's pure pathing.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title to begin.

Controls

KeyAction
← / →Walk left / right
↑ / ↓Climb up / down ropes & ladders
SpaceThrow chisel (if held) — kills a mummy in its lane

Strategy

  • Mummy lanes are fixed. Each mummy walks a single platform end-to-end; memorise the cadence and slip behind as it turns.
  • Save the chisel for chokepoints. Don't burn it on the first mummy; use it on the mummy guarding the last jewel or the exit door.
  • Ropes break the chase. Mummies don't follow you onto a rope — climb mid-rope, the mummy walks past, drop back down behind it.
  • Level editor included. The cart's level-edit mode is on the title screen — Konami shipped a built-in editor on an MSX cart in 1985, which was unusual.
