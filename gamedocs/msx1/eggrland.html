Home Computer · MSX1

Eggerland Mystery

HAL Laboratory, 1985 — single-screen block-pushing puzzle game; the direct ancestor of Adventures of Lolo (NES, 1989). Play as Lolo navigating a castle's worth of one-screen rooms, pushing emerald-framers and avoiding sleeping monsters.

Each room is a single screen of tile grid. Collect every heart-framer in the room and the treasure chest opens — touch it to advance. Heart-framers sit in patterns that force you to push emerald-frames as walls, blockers, or stepping stones. Most monsters are dormant until you grab the last heart-framer; then they wake up and chase you to the chest.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title; first room loads immediately.

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Walk Lolo one tile per tap
Direction (into block)Push an emerald-framer one tile
SpaceFire magic shot (when collected — turns monsters into eggs)

Strategy

  • Plan the last heart-framer. Picking it up wakes monsters; make sure your route to the chest is open before grabbing the final framer.
  • Egg-and-push. A magic shot turns a monster into an egg you can shove like an emerald-frame. Eggs slide until they hit a wall — useful for blocking corridors.
  • Eggs hatch. Don't dawdle; the monster rehydrates after several beats and resumes chasing.
  • Direct precursor to Adventures of Lolo. If you remember Lolo's NES outings, mechanics map directly — same one-block-push puzzle vocabulary.
