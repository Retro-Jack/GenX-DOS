Yie Ar Kung-Fu

Konami, 1985 — side-on single-screen 1v1 martial-arts arcade fighter. Oolong battles a procession of named opponents — Buchu, Star, Nuncha, Pole, Feedle, Chain — each with a distinct weapon and timing. The proto-template for the entire fighting-game genre.

Two-bar life meter, single round, no rounds-of-three: KO the opponent or be KO'd. Eight-direction stick decides stance + kick height; fire decides whether the input is a punch or a kick. The MSX port is one of the smoother conversions — Buchu hits hard but the timing is honest.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press Space at the title; Buchu is opponent #1.

Strategy

  • Each enemy has a tell. Buchu hops before his belly-slam; Nuncha winds nunchucks before swing; Pole telegraphs the low staff sweep. Read the tell, counter low/high accordingly.
  • Jumping kicks beat ground attacks. A diagonal jumping kick crosses up most opponents; land behind them for a free standing strike.
  • Don't out-range Pole and Chain. Their weapons reach further than your fist; close to grappling range immediately or stay outside their swing arc.
  • Star throws stars in arcs. Time crouches by sound — duck the high arc, jump the low one; same input on Star's signature double-throw kills both volleys.
