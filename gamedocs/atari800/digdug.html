Dig Dug

Atari, 1983 — home port of Masahisa Ikeda's 1982 Namco arcade. Dig tunnels through stratified earth and dispatch monsters by inflating them or dropping rocks on them.

You're an underground miner. Dig through dirt to reach the Pookas (round red goggle-monsters) and Fygars (green fire-breathing dragons). Kill them by pumping them full of air until they explode, or by dropping boulders. Clear each cave to advance.

Getting started

  1. Press F4 Start.
  2. Dig with the joystick — you tunnel as you walk.
  3. Fire to pump enemies; hold to inflate until they burst.
  4. Tunnel under boulders to dislodge them.

Strategy

  • Boulders pay big. Setting up a multi-enemy boulder kill is the fastest way to a high score.
  • Don't over-pump. An inflated enemy slowly deflates and re-emerges if you stop. Pump in short bursts when you want to stun, full bursts when you want to kill.
  • Fygar fire arc. Fygar breathes fire 2 tiles ahead. Approach from the back; never from the front.
  • Disembodied enemies. When monsters can't reach you through tunnels, they turn into "ghost" eyes that drift through dirt — they can't hurt you when ghost-form but materialise when they reach an existing tunnel.
  • Bonus veg. A cherry / pineapple / etc. appears at the centre of the screen for a short window — 400 to 4000 points depending on level.

Atari 400 controls

Cart-based (cartType 2 in our bundle). Joystick + fire — the pump is the fire button held down.
