Archon: The Light and the Dark

Free Fall Associates / Electronic Arts, 1983 — Jon Freeman, Anne Westfall, Paul Reiche III. Chess where landing on the same square triggers real-time arcade combat. One of EA's first six "we see farther" launch titles.

Two opposing armies, Light and Dark, fight over a chess-like 9x9 board. Each piece (knight, archer, dragon, phoenix, sorceress, djinni, etc.) moves like a chess piece and fights with unique attacks. The squares cycle between dark and light — colour at the moment of combat boosts the matching side.

Getting started

  1. Disk auto-boots to the title screen. Press F4 Start.
  2. Pick 1-player (vs AI) or 2-player. Light moves first.
  3. Move the on-board cursor to your piece, fire to select. Cursor moves again to the destination square; fire to commit.
  4. If the target square has an enemy, the screen switches to the arcade combat arena.

Combat arena

MechanicEffect
Square colourCombat takes place on the board square's current cycle — light pieces fight better on light, dark on dark
CooldownEach attack has a recovery time; rapid-fire pieces fire often but weakly
Movement obstaclesRandom rocks appear on the arena floor — block projectiles, take damage to clear
HealthWounded pieces don't auto-heal; spend a turn on a power point to recover

Power points (5 squares)

Five squares grant special abilities. Control all five and you win outright (alternate to total elimination).

Strategy

  • Match piece to colour. Don't initiate a fight on the wrong-colour square unless you must — wait one or two turns for the cycle to drift.
  • Heal injured pieces. A wounded dragon is still a dragon. Park it on a power point and skip its move to bring HP back.
  • Sorceress / Wizard spells. Each unique spell can be cast only once per game. Save Shift Time, Heal, and Teleport for crisis moments.
  • Imprison vs. eliminate. Some pieces are key to mobility — capturing the enemy's mover puts pressure on their power-point race.
  • Power-point control wins. Eliminating every enemy piece is the slow path; holding the five power points is the fast one.

Atari 800XL controls

Joystick + fire for everything. Two joysticks recommended in 2-player — Light uses port 1, Dark uses port 2.
