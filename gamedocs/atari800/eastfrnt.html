Eastern Front 1941

Atari Program Exchange, 1981 — Chris Crawford. The first scrollable-map wargame on a home computer; APX's all-time best-seller. You command the Wehrmacht against the Soviet Union from June 1941 to spring 1942.

A turn-based wargame on a real geographic map. Each turn is a week. You issue movement and attack orders to German divisions and Romanian/Hungarian corps; the Soviet AI moves at end-of-turn. Win by capturing Moscow, Leningrad, and the southern oilfields — or by inflicting unsustainable Soviet losses.

Getting started

  1. Press F4 Start at the title screen. Choose level 1 (Beginner) for your first game.
  2. The map auto-centres on a German unit. Move the cursor (joystick or arrow keys) over a unit and press fire to select it.
  3. With a unit selected, push the joystick toward the destination hex — an arrow appears showing the order. Release fire when the line points where you want.
  4. Press F4 Start again to end your turn and run the week.

Strategy

  • Encircle, don't chase. Armour out front, infantry sealing the pocket. A surrounded Soviet division dies in supply; a chased one retreats and recovers.
  • Three axes. Army Group North → Leningrad, Centre → Moscow, South → Kiev/Rostov. Stretch too thin and the AI counter-attacks the gap.
  • Watch the calendar. Mud (October) halves movement. Winter (December–February) destroys German infantry not in cities. Push hard summer through autumn.
  • Romanian / Hungarian corps are fragile. Use them to hold flanks behind a river, never as the spearhead.
  • Don't garrison every captured hex. The AI doesn't usually counter-attack into empty territory; pull rear divisions forward.

Atari 800XL controls

Joystick + START + Reset. The map scrolls in 4-way directions; the cursor stays roughly centred while the world moves around it. End-turn is a single button press.
