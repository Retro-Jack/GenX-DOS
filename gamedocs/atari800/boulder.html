Boulder Dash

First Star Software, 1984 — Peter Liepa, Chris Gray. Originally written for the Atari 8-bit before being ported everywhere. The Rockford-the-miner puzzle/action template that spawned hundreds of imitators.

Dig through caves of dirt to collect a target number of diamonds, then escape through the exit door. Boulders fall when undermined and crush enemies (and you). Fireflies and butterflies chase or explode. 16 caves, each with 5 difficulty levels.

Getting started

  1. Press F4 Start at the title screen.
  2. Choose difficulty 1–5 and starting cave (A, E, I, M).
  3. Dig with the joystick. Collect diamonds until the door flashes; reach the door before time runs out.

Strategy

  • Plan boulder paths. Boulders roll off other boulders into open dirt — one wrong dig can bury the diamond you needed.
  • Crush butterflies for huge gains. A single butterfly under a falling boulder yields 9 diamonds — find chambers where this is set up.
  • Magic walls have a timer. Once active, they only convert for a few seconds — feed boulders fast.
  • Trap amoebas early. Wall off the amoeba so it can't grow — when it hits the cell limit, the whole mass turns into diamonds.
  • Never dig sideways under a boulder line. The whole column drops on you.
  • Grab to defuse. Hold L Ctrl + direction to pick up a diamond next to a falling-boulder setup without disturbing the boulder.

Atari 800XL controls

Single joystick + fire (fire = grab). The Atari original ran at 60 Hz with smooth scrolling — later ports tried but rarely matched the feel of this version.
