Rescue on Fractalus!

Lucasfilm Games, 1985 — David Fox, Loren Carpenter. Lucasfilm's first published game (with Ballblazer). Used Loren Carpenter's recursive fractal terrain algorithm (the same technique that drew the Genesis planet in Star Trek II) to render real-time 3D mountains on an 8-bit machine.

You pilot a Valkyrie scout-fighter over the procedurally-generated mountains of Fractalus, rescuing downed pilots while dodging enemy saucers and gun emplacements. Land beside a pilot, wait for them to run to your ship, take off, and ferry them to the mothership.

Getting started

  1. Press F4 Start, choose a difficulty level (1–16+).
  2. You launch from the mothership. Navigate by the long-range scanner pip toward a stranded pilot.
  3. Approach low and slow; the Valkyrie auto-lands when you press L Ctrl + ↓.
  4. Wait for the pilot to knock at the airlock, then press 0 (or fire) to open.
  5. Take off, return to the mothership when rescue quota is met.

Strategy

  • Fly low. Mountain peaks block scanner; fly between them or you'll over-shoot the pilot.
  • Engine off to land. Press 2 just before touchdown — momentum carries you to a stop.
  • Shields cost energy. Use them only when a saucer dives in or under flak fire.
  • Don't trust the knock. Late levels include alien fake-pilots who knock at the airlock and then attack. Wait for the visor-down silhouette before opening.
  • Mothership window. Return when your rescue quota is filled; the mothership only stays for a limited window before warping.
  • Torpedoes for saucers and flak. Aim slightly above the target — the trajectory dips.

Atari 800XL controls

Joystick + fire + 0/1/2/9 keyboard. The Atari original ran the fractal renderer faster than the later C64 port — landings feel more responsive on this hardware.
