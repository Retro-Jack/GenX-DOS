Pac-Man

Atari, 1982 — home port of Toru Iwatani's 1980 Namco arcade. The Atari 8-bit version retained the four-ghost maze and authentic intermission cutscenes (unlike the infamous 2600 version released the same year).

Navigate the maze, eat all 240 dots and 4 power pills to clear the level. Avoid the four ghosts unless you've grabbed a power pill — then chase and eat them for big points. Fruit appears at the centre periodically for bonus.

Getting started

  1. Press F4 Start.
  2. Move Pac-Man with the joystick (he keeps moving in the last direction until you turn).
  3. Eat all dots and pills to advance.

Strategy

  • Eat power pills with three ghosts nearby. The four-in-a-row chain pays 200+400+800+1600 = 3000 points.
  • Hug Pinky on corners. She's predictable — turning sharp at the corner just before Pinky's ambush spot wastes her trajectory.
  • Side tunnels. Ghosts slow down in the wrap-around tunnels; you don't. Use them as escape routes when cornered.
  • Fruit windows. Cherries appear after ~70 dots and ~170 dots — and only stay ~10 seconds. Grab if safe; skip if it puts you in a ghost's path.
  • Eyes-only ghosts are harmless. A "eaten" ghost returns to the ghost-house as floating eyes — you can pass through them.

Atari 400 controls

Joystick — no fire button. Note the Atari 8-bit version maintained the ghost AI integrity (Inky's flanker math works correctly), unlike many home ports of the era.
