Portable Computer · Tandy TRS-80 Model 100

Road Rally

A twitchy timed drive from David Busch's 25 Games for your TRS-80 Model 100 (TAB Books, 1984) — one of the few real action games on the machine.

The road scrolls down at you, twisting left and right, and your car holds a single row at the top. Nudge left and right to stay on the tarmac — clip the verge and you crash. There's no finish line; the goal is simply to survive as long as you can and beat your best time.

Getting started

  1. The chequered-flag banner appears; answer Instructions? (Y/N).
  2. Pick a difficulty from 1 (hard) to 9 (easy) — the number sets how wide the road is, so 1 is a tightrope and 9 is a boulevard.
  3. Keep the car on the road as it winds. Drift onto the verge and it's game over.

Controls

KeyAction
← Left arrowSteer left
→ Right arrowSteer right

The road drifts a little every frame on its own — you're constantly correcting, not just reacting to corners.

Crashing

Touch the edge of the road and the car crashes with a burst of static. The game then tells you how long you lasted, in minutes and seconds, and offers another run — the score to beat is simply your own survival time.
