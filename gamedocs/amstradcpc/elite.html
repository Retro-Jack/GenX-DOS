Elite

Firebird, 1985 — David Braben & Ian Bell. The open-universe space-trader template that every later game in the genre rebuilds from. Eight galaxies, 256 planets each, all procedurally generated.

Start with a Cobra Mk III, 100 credits, and the rank "Harmless". Buy low, sell high, fight off pirates, dock at stations, work up through the combat ratings. Reaching Elite is a long-haul commitment — most players measure it in weeks of real time.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots — bundle pre-types run"elite.
  2. Click the canvas first to give it keyboard focus.
  3. Press any key past the title; you'll launch from Lave.

Strategy

  • Trade routes first. Industrial systems buy food and minerals; agricultural systems buy machinery and computers. Two-leg run, fixed profit.
  • Save before docking. Manual docking is unforgiving — the docking computer (buy ASAP) is worth every credit.
  • Don't sell the slave trade. The Galactic Police remember; you'll be marked Fugitive across the system.
  • Asteroid mining is steady low-risk income — pick the inner rocky planets, target asteroids with the laser, scoop the splinters.
