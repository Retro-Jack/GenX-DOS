Knight Lore

Ultimate Play the Game, 1984 — Tim and Chris Stamper. The genre-defining Filmation isometric engine; Sabreman is cursed and must find ingredients for the antidote across the wizard Melkhior's castle.

Forty days, forty nights — and forty interconnected isometric rooms. Sabreman shifts between man and werewolf at every sundown; the wolf form can survive things Sabreman can't and vice-versa. Find the wizard, drop the right object into his cauldron, repeat.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots — bundle pre-types run"knight.
  2. Click the canvas first to give it keyboard focus.
  3. Press any key at the title to begin.

Strategy

  • Werewolf form is stronger but heavier. Jumps are shorter; some platforms can only be reached as Sabreman.
  • Map as you go. Rooms wrap unexpectedly — note ingredient locations relative to the cauldron room.
  • Push the right object. The cauldron tells you what it wants; dropping the wrong item wastes a precious carry slot.
  • Energy regenerates standing still. Tuck into a corner away from hazards if you need to recover.
