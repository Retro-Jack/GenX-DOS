Bruce Lee

US Gold / Datasoft, 1984 — Ron Fortier. Bruce fights through a wizard's castle collecting lanterns while a sumo and a ninja close in.

Twenty rooms, twenty lantern chains. Each lantern Bruce destroys puts him a step closer to the wizard. The Green Yamo (sumo) is slow and lethal; the Ninja is fast and unpredictable. Stay above them, kick when you can.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots — bundle pre-types run"bruce.
  2. Click the canvas first to give it keyboard focus.
  3. Choose 1 or 2 player at the title.

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Move / climb
SpacePunch / kick (direction + jump)
↑ + SpaceFlying kick

Strategy

  • Flying kick is the punish. Both enemies stagger if hit mid-air — chain a jump-kick to break tight patrols.
  • The Yamo's reach is short. If you crouch or duck him at the right pixel, he can't connect.
  • The Ninja teleports. Don't tunnel-vision on him; lanterns first, fight only when cornered.
  • Spike floors and exploding bushes are deterministic. Memorise the safe pattern per room.
