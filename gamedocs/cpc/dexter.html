Get Dexter

ERE Informatique, 1986 — Rémi Herbulot & Michel Rho. The French isometric standout that proved continental developers could match Ultimate at their own game. Released abroad as "Crafton & Xunk".

You play Crafton, a robot in a hostile lab, trying to free the human Xunk. Forty-odd isometric rooms with proper puzzle logic — pick objects up, combine them, drop them on switches, dodge the patrol robots. Crafton can also become invisible briefly to slip past detection.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots — bundle pre-types run"disc.
  2. Click the canvas first to give it keyboard focus.
  3. Press any key past the title to take control.

Controls

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Move (isometric — diagonals on a true grid)
SpacePick up / drop object under Crafton
EnterActivate / use carried object
IToggle invisibility (drains energy)

Strategy

  • Map first. Get Dexter's room layout is genuinely puzzle-shaped; brute-force exploration burns the energy budget.
  • Invisibility is a resource. Save it for guard rooms with no cover, not corridors you could time-tread.
  • Object combinations matter. Some doors open only when a specific item is on the trigger plate — note item locations as you find them.
  • Lifts and teleporters reset your facing — re-orient before moving or you'll walk into a patrol.
