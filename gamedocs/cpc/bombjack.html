Bomb Jack

Elite Systems, 1986 — conversion of the 1984 Tehkan arcade. Jack bounces around five landmarks defusing 24 bombs per round while enemies turn into floating menaces.

Five scrolling backdrops — Sphinx, Greek temple, castle, neon city, prison. Each holds 24 bombs. Light bombs (with a red flame) in sequence for a 5× combo bonus. Collect "P" power-pills to freeze enemies briefly.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots — bundle pre-types run"bomb.
  2. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  3. Press a key at the title to start.

Controls

This game is keyboard-driven — its controls live on the CPC's own keys, so a gamepad doesn't apply here.

KeyAction
← →Move
SpaceJump / glide (hold to extend hang-time)

Bonus letters & pickups

LetterEffect
BBonus round / extra points
EExtra life
PPower-pill — enemies become bonus coins for a few seconds
SSpecial — full screen of bonus coins

Strategy

  • Chain the lit bombs. The next-in-sequence always lights when you defuse one — every 24-bomb perfect chain scores a massive bonus.
  • Glide, don't jump. Holding Space gives Jack a parachute fall — much safer than committing to a tall arc.
  • Bird-enemy paths are deterministic. Each round has fixed safe routes — die a few times, learn them, then run the loop.
  • Score-loop is the long game. The game wraps at round 6 with harder enemy density; play for points, not progress.
