Roland on the Run

Amsoft, 1984 — the launch-era platformer starring Amstrad's mascot Roland the Rat. Five levels of run-and-jump avoiding the Cat.

Roland is loose in the Greater Sewer System and the Cat is chasing him. Run along the platforms, dodge the Cat and the falling debris, collect cheese for points, and reach the exit on each of the five levels.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots — the bundle pre-types run"ontherun.
  2. Click the canvas first to give it keyboard focus.
  3. Press a key at the title screen to start.

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Move / climb
SpaceJump
EscPause / quit to title

Strategy

  • Cheese first. Most pieces are deliberately on routes that risk a Cat encounter — read the patrol path before committing.
  • Jump is short. Stand on the edge of a platform to maximise reach; mid-platform jumps undershoot.
  • The Cat won't climb every ladder. Use vertical splits to break line-of-sight.
