3D Starstrike

Realtime Games, 1985 (1984 original) — Ian Oliver, Andrew Onions & Graeme Baird. Wireframe 3D combat in the mould of the Star Wars arcade cabinet: fighter waves, surface defences, then the trench.

A vector-graphics assault run in three acts — dogfight the enemy fighters in space, strafe the battle station's surface installations, then drop into the trench and put your shot into the reactor before your shields give out. Then it all starts again, faster.

Getting started

  1. The disc autoboots straight into the game's title screen. Click the canvas first to give it keyboard focus.
  2. Pick the joystick option — in this bundle that's the way to play (see controls below).

Controls

This game supports a USB gamepad — a first for the CPC bundle. Choose the joystick option at the title and the pad's d-pad or left stick steers, with any face button as fire. No pad? The cursor keys and Space drive the same joystick.

InputAction
↑ ↓ ← → (or pad stick / d-pad)Steer your sights
Space (or any pad face button)Fire lasers

The title screen also offers keyboard control options, but the game never displays their key list — the joystick option above is the documented, tested way in.

Strategy

  • Shields are the score multiplier of survival. Every hit you take is shield gone, and there's no topping up mid-wave — dodge first, shoot second.
  • Shoot the incoming fire. Enemy shots can be blasted out of the air — in the thick of a wave, defence is your best offence.
  • The trench is a memory game. Barriers come in patterns; hold your line, weave the gaps, and don't fire the reactor shot early.
