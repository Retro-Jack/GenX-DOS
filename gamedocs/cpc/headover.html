Head over Heels

Ocean, 1987 — Jon Ritman & Bernie Drummond. Filmation II isometric — the genre's high-water mark. Two characters with complementary abilities, separated and reunited across 300+ rooms of seven planets.

Head can jump high and shoot doughnuts; Heels runs fast and carries objects in a tray. Together (when joined: Head riding Heels) you get both. The game opens with them separated in Castle Blacktooth — find each other, then liberate each of the five enemy planets by recovering the crown.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots — bundle pre-types run"head.
  2. Click the canvas first to give it keyboard focus.
  3. Press any key at the title screen to play.

Strategy

  • Join up early. The combined Head-over-Heels has both abilities and access to many rooms neither can clear alone.
  • Doughnuts stun, not kill. Enemies un-stun after a few seconds; use the window to push past or solve the room.
  • Springs and fish. Springs reset stage hazards; fish (carriable food) raise the lives counter.
  • Save the planets in difficulty order. Penitentiary first, then Egyptus / Safari / Book World / Moonbase. Blacktooth is the wrap-up.
