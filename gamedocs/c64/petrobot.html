Attack of the PETSCII Robots

8-Bit Productions (David Murray, "The 8-Bit Guy"), 2021 — a top-down action-strategy game built entirely from PETSCII-style character graphics. Infiltrate a robot-occupied settlement, destroy every robot, and reach the transporter to escape.

Each level is a single human settlement seen from above. Robots have taken it over; your job is to wipe them out and leave. The catch is that it plays more like a puzzle than a shooter — you rarely have the firepower to win a straight fight, so you search the rooms for weapons, keys and tools, then pick the right one for each robot and obstacle. The exit transporter only powers up once every robot is destroyed.

Getting started

  1. Starts in Color PETSCII mode. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. From the main menu start a new game; the CONTROLS option lets you pick between two keysets (keyset 2 swaps the move/fire hands).
  3. This is the free shareware build — it includes 2 of the full game's 13 levels (the rest, plus cheat codes and SNES-pad support, are in the paid version).

Strategy

  • Think first, shoot second. You can't out-gun the robots head-on — most are beaten with the right tool or by avoiding them, not by trading fire.
  • Search everything. Z on crates, lockers and furniture turns up weapons, ammo, keys and items — you'll be under-equipped if you skip rooms.
  • Match the tool to the problem. Cycle weapons and items with F1/F3; some robots only fall to a specific weapon, and locked doors need the matching key.
  • Clear the whole settlement. The transporter pad stays dead until the last robot is gone — only then does it activate, and stepping onto it ends the level.
  • Use the map. F7 shows the layout so you can plan a route and spot rooms you haven't searched yet.
