Elite

Firebird / Acornsoft (Ian Bell & David Braben), 1985 (C64 port from the 1984 BBC original) — open-ended space trading and combat. Eight galaxies of 256 procedurally-generated planets each.

Trade goods between systems for profit. Defend yourself against pirates. Earn the "Elite" combat rank by 6,400 kills. The C64 port adds colour over the BBC original; the wireframe Cobra Mk III + economy + hyperspace + docking-computer loop is preserved.

Getting started

  1. Disk autoboots — multi-load, give it a moment.
  2. At the title press F1; pick "Begin new commander" for a fresh game.

Strategy

  • Start with food + textiles. Buy at agricultural worlds (Lave), sell at industrial worlds. The early profit margin is the loop that funds your first laser upgrade.
  • Avoid Anarchy systems early. Pirate density is overwhelming until you have at least a beam laser and military shields.
  • Docking is the deadly bit. The rotating Coriolis station entry slot demands you match rotation before crossing the threshold. Save up for the docking computer (£15,000).
  • Jameson is the default name. Saving a commander overrides; you start as Jameson with 100 Cr.
  • The Thargoid encounter is real. Random hyperspace witchspace traps drop you out among alien fighters. ECM + missile + run.
