Hunchback

Ocean Software (port of the 1983 Century Electronics arcade), 1983 — Quasimodo runs castle battlements, jumps gaps, dodges arrows and rolling balls, racing a left-to-right timer to reach Esmeralda.

Single-screen battlements scroll past as you run right. Each screen has a fixed obstacle pattern (pit, arrow archer, swinging rope, charging knight). Reach the right edge to advance. The timer at the top counts down a knight's relentless pursuit — slow runs lose.

Getting started

  1. Tape image — type LOAD at the BASIC prompt then start the boot sequence; or wait for autoload.
  2. Click the canvas first so VICE receives input.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Run left / right
Numpad 0 / 5Jump

Strategy

  • Memorise the pattern, then commit. Each obstacle screen is identical run-to-run; the difficulty is reflex execution, not adaptation.
  • Jump arrows late. The arrow archer fires at a fixed cadence; jump when the arrow is already past mid-screen.
  • Rope swings have rhythm. Time your jump to grab the rope at the apex of its swing, not the bottom.
  • The chasing knight is the real clock. Pause too long and he catches you — there's no safe space to wait out a level.
