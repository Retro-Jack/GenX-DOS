Home Computer · Commodore 64

Ghosts 'n Goblins

Elite Systems (port of the 1985 Capcom arcade), 1986 — Sir Arthur the knight rescues Princess Prin Prin across six demon-infested levels. Two hits and you're dead (one strips your armour to boxers).

Side-scrolling platformer. Zombies, plant-men, demons, and red ogres swarm; pick up weapons from defeated zombies (lance, dagger, torch, axe, shield) — only one at a time. Reach the end-of-level boss within the timer. The ending is famously cruel: completing the game once means starting over at higher difficulty before the "real" ending.

Getting started

  1. Disk auto-boots; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press fire at the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Walk left / right
Numpad 8Jump (hold direction during jump to drift)
Numpad 2Crouch (aim weapon low while standing still)
Numpad 0 / 5Throw weapon

Strategy

  • Lance is the best weapon. Straight horizontal throw, fast cadence. Dagger has lower range; torch arcs and burns; axe arcs and hits short. Drop other pickups by walking past — only swap if forced.
  • Jump arcs lock. Once you commit to a jump, you can only drift slightly. Plan landings before you leave the ground.
  • Red devils are the level-end threat. They lunge and teleport. Crouch + spam the lance; their hit-box favours low shots.
  • Cyclops at the boss room. Two-hit kill if you stand at his feet and rapid-throw; he can't reach low.
  • The "real" ending requires two loops. Beating the game once reveals "this is a trap devised by Satan, do it again with the Cross" — second loop is the canonical finish.
