Home Computer · Commodore 64

Boulder Dash

First Star Software (Peter Liepa & Chris Gray), 1984 — dig through dirt as Rockford collecting diamonds, dodging falling boulders and firefly/butterfly enemies, racing a per-cave timer to reach the exit.

Each cave is a grid of dirt, boulders, diamonds, and enemies. Tunnel through dirt; boulders fall when unsupported and crush Rockford if he's beneath; fireflies and butterflies explode on contact. Collect the target diamond count to open the exit, then reach it before time runs out. 20 caves total (A–P + four bonus rooms).

Getting started

  1. Disk autoboots; click the canvas first.
  2. Press the spacebar (or numpad 0) at the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 8 2 4 6Move / dig in that direction
Numpad 0 + directionPush boulder, or dig without committing to move
F1Pause
F3Restart current cave

Strategy

  • Boulders fall predictably. Diagonal slides happen when a boulder lands on another rounded object — diamonds also slide. Plan tunnels so the boulder cascade clears, not blocks.
  • Butterflies become diamonds. A butterfly trapped against dirt or a boulder explodes into a 3×3 diamond pattern — usually the cleanest source of bulk diamonds.
  • Don't stand under a column of boulders. Tunnelling sideways under a stack drops them all at once; lethal.
  • Fireflies are deadlier than butterflies. Both explode on contact but fireflies actively patrol; butterflies just drift wall-following.
  • Read the cave before digging. Caves are deterministic — every run starts identical. Plan a route to the exit before you commit the first move.
