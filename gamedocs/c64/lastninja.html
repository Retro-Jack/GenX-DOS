The Last Ninja

System 3 (Mark Cale et al.), 1987 — isometric action-adventure across feudal Japan. Armakuni the ninja avenges his slain clan against the shogun Kunitoki. Six themed levels (Wilderness, Wastelands, Palace Gardens, Inner Sanctum, Palace, Inner Palace).

Detailed isometric rooms (the 3D art reused famously by the sequels) with hand-to-hand combat, weapon pickups (nunchaku, shuriken, sword, staff), and puzzle/lever interactions. Stamina drains in combat; collect food/water for health. Many rooms hide items behind cosmetic-looking bushes or under stones.

Getting started

  1. Disk autoboots; click the canvas first.
  2. Press fire at the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6 / 8 / 2Walk in isometric directions (NE/NW/SE/SW relative to view)
Numpad 0 / 5Action button (jump in walk mode, strike in combat mode)
SpaceToggle walk / combat mode
Fire + direction (combat)Different strikes — high/low/punch depending on direction
IInventory (cycle through carried items / select weapon)

Strategy

  • Search everything. Bushes, pots, stones, "decorative" objects — many hide keys, scrolls, weapons.
  • Save before duels. Each enemy is a separate fight; dying restarts the room. Multi-screen runs need careful pacing.
  • Toggle modes deliberately. Walk mode for moving + jumping gaps; combat mode for strikes. Forgetting to switch costs lives.
  • Don't skip the dialogue/cutscenes. The Buddhist Monk and the Master sequences gate items you'll need later.
  • Ben Daglish's score. The opening title track is a SID showpiece — sound on is the intended experience.
