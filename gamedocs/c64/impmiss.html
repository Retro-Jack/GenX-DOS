Home Computer · Commodore 64

Impossible Mission

Epyx (Dennis Caswell), 1984 — explore Elvin Atombender's underground complex, search furniture for password fragments, solve the puzzle, stop the doomsday device. 6-hour countdown is the meta-timer.

A 32-room subterranean lair. Roll-jump-search past patrolling robots in each room; furniture conceals puzzle pieces, "snooze" codes that disable robots briefly, and "lift reset" codes. Collected pieces visit the puzzle screen, where four assemble into each of the 9 punch-card puzzles that spell out Elvin's password. Get all 9 before the clock runs out.

"Another visitor. Stay a while. STAY FOREVER!" — the opening sample is one of the earliest digitized speech moments on the C64.

Getting started

  1. Disk autoboots; click the canvas first.
  2. Press F1 or fire at the title to start.

Controls

KeyAction
Numpad 4 / 6Run left / right
Numpad 8 / 2Use lifts (up / down)
Numpad 0 / 5Jump / somersault (with direction)
F1 / F3Puzzle screen — flip the selected piece vertically / horizontally
F5Puzzle screen — change the piece's colour to match

Strategy

  • Search everything. Every chair, sofa, computer hides something — even if "empty" the search isn't a waste; you can't predict which holds the puzzle pieces.
  • Save reset codes for lifts. If you fall into a pit, a reset code rebuilds the lift. Burn snoozes on robots in pinch rooms instead.
  • Search uses 10 seconds. Time is the real currency. Cancel a search early if a robot's about to walk in.
  • Four pieces per punch-card. Each completed puzzle is four pieces assembled into a solid colour-matched rectangle; flip and recolour them to fit. Nine finished puzzles spell out a password word.
  • Falls aren't always fatal. Off the edge of a platform without a lift = dead. Off a platform that lands on another platform = fine.
