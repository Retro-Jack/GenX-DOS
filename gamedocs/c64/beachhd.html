Beach Head

Access Software (Roger and Bruce Carver), 1983 — multi-stage WWII-themed Pacific assault. Five mini-games chained: torpedo-run, aerial dogfight, naval gun duel, landing-craft beach run, tank-vs-tank.

Each stage uses a different control scheme but the same joystick. Clear all five and you loop back at higher difficulty. Survive-as-many-men is the score driver more than raw points; men lost in early stages can't help in the beach assault.

Getting started

  1. Disk autoboots — click the canvas first so this emulator receives input.
  2. Press F1 at the title to start.

Strategy

  • Torpedo stage — go right. The convoy is dense to the right; left mostly wastes torpedoes. Don't fire until under 600 yards.
  • Dogfight — climb first. Altitude advantage in the dive-pursuit makes the planes easier to catch.
  • Naval duel — bracket then halve. First shot long, second short, third splits the difference. Adjust elevation in small increments.
  • Beach run — hug the cliffs. The mortar pattern misses the cliff edges; staying centre is suicide.
  • Tank stage — read the smoke. Enemy tanks telegraph their shots; sidestep one tank-width left or right when the muzzle lights.
