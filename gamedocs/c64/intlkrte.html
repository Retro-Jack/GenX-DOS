International Karate

System 3 (Archer Maclean), 1986 — two-fighter karate bouts judged by a referee on points. Land clean techniques (full-points) to win two from three exchanges; tournament progresses through changing locations.

Side-on tournament fighter. Joystick directions chain with the fire button to produce different strikes (kicks, punches, sweeps). A clean hit triggers the ref's "Ippon!" (full point) verdict; partial hits score half. Bonus rounds between bouts: break falling boards / dodge thrown daggers.

Getting started

  1. Disk autoboots; click the canvas first.
  2. Press fire at the title to start a tournament.

Strategy

  • Range, then commit. Roundhouse is the best opener — long reach, fast recovery.
  • Sweep low when they jump. Crouched sweep hits the recovery frames.
  • Flying kick is high-risk. Telegraphed; opponent crouches and you sail over for a point against you.
  • Bonus rounds matter. The board-break / dagger-dodge bonus stages award full points — don't sleepwalk them.
  • Archer Maclean signature. Same author later did the Jimmy White's Snooker physics — this game's wide animation set is a clue to the style.
