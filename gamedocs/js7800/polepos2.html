Pole Position II

Atari, 1986 (port of the 1983 Namco/Atari arcade — Toru Iwatani). The pseudo-3D F1 racer that defined the genre. Four tracks; qualify on time, then race.

Choose one of four circuits (Fuji, Test, Suzuka, Seaside) at the title. Qualify a lap inside the time limit to enter the race. Race 8 laps against AI opponents, avoiding crashes (any wall hit explodes your car). The 7800 port faithfully reproduces the arcade scaling and AI.

Getting started

  1. Press F3 to enter the menu.
  2. Use F2 Select to choose track and gear (auto vs. manual).
  3. Qualify a lap inside the time limit; then race.

Strategy

  • Low gear off the line. If you select manual, start in low (Z) and shift to high (X) once revs peak. Holding accelerate at start in high gives slow acceleration.
  • Brake into corners. The 7800 port punishes mid-corner braking — bleed speed early, then accelerate through the apex.
  • Watch the horizon. The road's curve is telegraphed by the angle of the upcoming road — start steering before the curve enters your sprite zone.
  • Don't tap the wall. Any wall contact = full explosion → 3-second restart. Treat the road edges as instant-death.
  • Slipstream. Tucked behind an AI car you gain speed; pass on the inside of corners.

Atari 7800 controls

ProLine joystick — two buttons used for low/high gear in manual mode, otherwise unused. Press F3 to advance through the menu and start qualifying.
