Asteroids

Atari, 1986 (7800 launch lineup) — General Computer Corp's enhanced port of the 1979 Lyle Rains / Ed Logg vector arcade. Switches between the original mode and "New Style" (raster sprites + bonus features).

Same rules as the 2600 version, with crisper MARIA graphics and an enhanced mode. Rotate, thrust, shoot rocks until they're all gone, then a tougher wave. Two saucer types take pot-shots. Hyperspace teleports randomly. The 7800 version adds Multiplier and Shields powerups in the New Style mode.

Getting started

  1. Press Enter to start.
  2. Shift Select switch toggles game variant (classic vs. new style, easy vs. hard, 1P vs. 2P).
  3. Rotate with ←/→, thrust with ↑, hyperspace ↓, fire Z.

New Style additions

  • Shields. Hold X for brief invulnerability. Drains rapidly — emergency only.
  • Score multiplier. Collect green crystals dropped from broken asteroids — up to ×6.
  • Coloured rocks. Different colours = different point values; spinners worth 1000+.

Strategy

  • Centre dance. Wraparound makes screen-edge play safe, but the centre lets you exit any rock collision in two directions.
  • Small saucer kill = 1000. Late-wave small saucers shoot accurately. Worth the risk for the multiplier.
  • Don't over-thrust. Inertia is forever — taps, not holds.
  • Hyperspace ~1/6 self-kill. Use only when surrounded with no thrust path.
  • New Style is more fun. The original mode is a nostalgia run; the enhanced one is the better game.

Atari 7800 controls

ProLine joystick (two-button) — Z fire, X shields (in New Style). Press Enter at the title screen to start play.
