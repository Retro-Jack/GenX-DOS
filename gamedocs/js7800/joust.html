Joust

Atari, 1986 (port of the 1982 Williams arcade by John Newcomer). The first two-player co-op arcade game and the inventor of the "flap to fly" mechanic.

You're a knight riding an ostrich (P1) or stork (P2). Flap your wings to stay aloft. Hit enemy buzzard-riders with your lance — if you're higher than they are on collision, you win the joust; equal height = bounce; lower = you die. Killed riders become eggs that hatch new (faster) enemies if not collected.

Getting started

  1. Press Enter to start.
  2. Tap Z to flap (each tap = one wing-beat = lift).
  3. Steer with ←/→. Charge enemies high; collect their eggs fast.

Strategy

  • Flap rhythm. Don't hold Z. Tap-tap-tap for steady altitude; one strong burst to gain height.
  • Bottom platform = death. The lava pit's troll grabs anything stalled near the bottom. Stay airborne over lava.
  • Egg sweep. When you kill a rider, they leave a stationary egg. Eggs hatch into FASTER enemies after a few seconds. Race to collect.
  • Pterodactyl head-on. The big bird is invincible from sides/back. Only a direct lance-on-beak collision kills it. Wait for it to approach, charge dead-on.
  • Survival wave. Wave 5, 10, 15... include "Survival" rounds — clear all enemies for a 3000 bonus.

Atari 7800 controls

One-button. Z is the flap; X is unused. Two-player simultaneous co-op uses a second ProLine — plug in a second gamepad and it's auto-assigned to Player 2. Press Enter (Reset) at the title to start.
