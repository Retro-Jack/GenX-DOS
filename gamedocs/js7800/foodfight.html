Food Fight

Atari, 1987 (port of the 1983 arcade by Dennis Koble). Charley Chuck runs through a kitchen-floor playfield trying to reach a melting ice-cream cone before it puddles. Four chefs (Angelo, Zorba, Jacques, Oscar) throw food at him; Charley can throw food back.

Each level is a square arena dotted with piles of food. Pick up food (by walking onto a pile) and throw it (any direction) at the chefs. Reach the ice-cream cone at the far side before it fully melts (timer). Survive long enough on lower levels for tougher chef behaviour and pickier food piles.

Getting started

  1. Press Enter to start.
  2. Walk Charley to a food pile to pick up; press Z + a direction to throw.
  3. Reach the cone before the timer melts it.

Strategy

  • Hit chefs for score. Killing a chef = 200 pts × (chefs alive). All four chefs on one level alive at the start; chain-kill them for the multiplier.
  • The hole trick. Walking into a hole spawns a fresh chef as you fall in. Use it to dump unwanted positions, but you lose a life.
  • Food rains. When chefs run out of nearby food to throw, food bombs rain from above. Eight Up arrows = an X-bomb pile that can clear half the screen.
  • Ice-cream timer. Even if you've killed all chefs, the cone keeps melting. Don't sit there throwing food forever — finish the level.
  • Score-chase. Letting chefs kill each other (with thrown food) is the actual high-score path — they're worth nothing if you let them die natural, but 200 × your chain otherwise.

Atari 7800 controls

The 7800 port preserves the arcade's "hold a direction while pressing fire to throw that way" — it's an 8-way directional throw. X is unused. Press Enter to start.
