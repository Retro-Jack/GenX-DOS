Galaga

Atari, 1987 (port of Shigeru Yokoyama's 1981 Namco arcade — successor to Galaxian). Fixed-shooter masterpiece with the dual-fighter capture mechanic and Challenging Stage bonus rounds.

Enemies fly in formation at the top of the screen, then peel off to dive-bomb you. The Boss Galaga can capture your ship with a tractor beam — rescue it later with a successful pass to double your firepower. Every fourth stage is a Challenging Stage: zero risk, all bonus points.

Getting started

  1. Press Enter to start.
  2. Move left/right; Z fires.
  3. Two bullets on-screen at once. Shoot the formation as it enters; pick off divers as they come.

Strategy

  • Don't shoot the tractor beam. Let the Boss Galaga capture a ship deliberately. When that Boss returns later, kill it from the side — your captured ship floats free; collect it for a side-by-side dual fighter (double firepower).
  • Two-bullet limit. The 2-shot cap is the game's pace governor. Aim, don't spray.
  • Formation-attack scoring. Killing a Boss + both escorts gives 1600 — much higher than waiting for them in formation. Diving-Boss = big payoff window.
  • Challenging Stages. Every 4th stage. No firing back, you can't die. Hit all 40 = "PERFECT" bonus (10,000 pts).
  • Don't shoot when dual. A dual fighter is wider — easier hit. Stay mobile and pick fights tactically.

Atari 7800 controls

One button — Z. X is unused. The 7800 port preserves the arcade's 2-bullet limit and the dual-fighter mechanic. Press Enter to start.
