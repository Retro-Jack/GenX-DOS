Desert Falcon

Atari, 1987. Isometric shoot-'em-up: you're a falcon skimming an Egyptian desert, firing arrows at snakes and scorpions and collecting hieroglyphs for power-ups on the long flight to the Sphinx.

The desert scrolls past in a 3D isometric view and you fly the falcon over it — climbing, diving and swerving to line up shots and snatch treasures. Ground hazards (cacti, sphinxes, snapping enemies) can be flown over; low-lying treasures have to be dropped onto. Collect three matching hieroglyphs and you earn a temporary super-power. Survive the gauntlet and the level ends at a boss.

Getting started

  1. Press Enter (Reset) to start.
  2. Steer with ← / →. Altitude is flight-stick style: pull back (↓) to climb, push forward (↑) to drop and land.
  3. Fire arrows with Z — a direct hit destroys an enemy.

Strategy

  • Altitude is your dodge. Most ground hazards can't touch you if you're flying — climb (↓) to sail over snakes and cacti, then drop only when it's safe.
  • Mind the flight-stick feel. Pulling back to climb catches everyone out at first; get the down-is-up reflex sorted before you worry about scoring.
  • Match three hieroglyphs. Collecting three of the same glyph grants a temporary super-power — the big scoring and survival swing. Learn which glyphs are about and grab the set.
  • Arrows fly forward. Line enemies up ahead of you rather than trying to snap-shoot sideways; you can't fire behind.
  • Hop onto treasure. Low treasures are collected by landing or hopping over them — descend (↑) to scoop them, then climb straight back out of danger.

Atari 7800 controls

ProLine joystick — steer left / right, pull back (↓) to climb and push forward (↑) to land, and fire arrows with the left button (Z). Press Enter (Reset) at the title to start; Shift (Select) cycles the game variation.
