Parodius: The Octopus Saves the Earth

Konami, 1988 — horizontal shoot-'em-up; the original "Parodius" before the arcade sequel. Self-parody of Gradius/Nemesis with the same power-up bar engine but moai heads replaced by paper-cut-out moai, a flying octopus pilot, and stages built around cabaret dancers and pirate-ship Konami's Game.

Same engine as Nemesis (the player's first ship was Vic Viper; here it's Tako the octopus by default, with Penta-the-penguin and Belial co-stars in later versions). Power-up bar: Speed → Missile → Double → Laser → Option → ?. Same checkpoint-death system. Joke around the seriousness — the music is mahjong jingles and cancan parodies.

Getting started

  1. Cart/Disk auto-boots; click the canvas first.
  2. Select a character at the title screen, press Space to start.

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Move ship/octopus in eight directions
SpaceFire main weapon + missile
ShiftActivate highlighted power-up

Strategy

  • Standard Gradius build. Two Speed-ups → Missile → Double → Laser → two Options. Override only if a stage's mid-boss is biased to a different weapon.
  • Bell power-ups appear. Shooting a bell cycles its colour — collect a green bell for shield, red for big laser, blue for line-of-bells smart-bomb (Parodius's signature mechanic, kept from arcade).
  • Octopus moves differently. Tako has wider hitbox but a strong default shot — choose Penta or Vic Viper for tighter dodging.
  • Stage 4 is the trap. Pirate ship stage scrolls vertically mid-section; remember to re-centre after the rotation cuts.
