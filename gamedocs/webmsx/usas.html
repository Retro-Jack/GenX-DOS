The Treasure of Usas

Konami, 1987 — side-scrolling action-platformer with light Metroidvania structure. Treasure hunters Wit and Cles explore six lost ancient temples (Inca, Maya, Egyptian, Greek, Indus, Babylonian) hunting the legendary Usas treasure. Switch characters on the fly for different abilities and reach.

Each temple is a multi-screen maze of platforms, ladders, locked doors and enemies. Wit has a longer jump; Cles has a stronger shot. Coins from kills buy hearts (health), keys, and ability upgrades from in-temple shops. Reach the end-of-temple boss for the treasure piece; six pieces win the game.

Getting started

  1. Cart auto-boots; click the canvas first.
  2. Press Space at the title to start in the first temple.

Controls

KeyAction
← / →Walk
↑ / ↓Climb ladders / ropes; enter doors
SpaceJump (longer with running speed)
ShiftFire weapon
TabSwitch character (Wit ↔ Cles)

Strategy

  • Switch by ability gap. Wit's long jump crosses traps Cles can't; Cles's heavy shot kills tougher enemies in fewer hits. Tab as part of normal movement.
  • Buy hearts early. Each shop sells hearts at the same price; stockpile before tough bosses, not during.
  • Temples are non-linear. Some treasures require revisiting earlier temples with a key bought late — don't sweat 100% on first run-through.
  • The unused character heals. The character not currently in play regenerates HP slowly — swap to the wounded one for safe-room recovery.
