Metal Gear

Konami, 1987 — Hideo Kojima's directorial debut and the original stealth-action game. Solid Snake infiltrates Outer Heaven to destroy the bipedal nuclear walker codenamed Metal Gear. Top-down screens, line-of-sight enemy cones (well, narrow lines at this point), radio codecs, item radio puzzles, mandatory weapons-only-when-found design.

Top-down flick-screen action-adventure. Each screen has guards on patrol routes; if a guard sees Snake, an alert spawns reinforcements until Snake leaves the building or hides. Weapons and items pile up in a switchable inventory; some areas require specific items (gas mask for the toxic-smoke corridor, body armour for the laser room, etc.).

Getting started

  1. Cart auto-boots; click the canvas first.
  2. Press Space at the title; Snake enters the jungle perimeter.

Strategy

  • Punches don't alert. Three quick punches from behind silently KO a guard — silenced pistol is for ranged needs only.
  • Each room rewards re-entry. Leave a screen and the guards reset to patrol routes — useful for resetting an alert mid-mission.
  • Cardboard box is a teleport. Riding a truck under a tarp moves Snake between major areas — saves backtracking.
  • Radio frequencies are intel. The frequencies in the manual aren't optional — calling specific contacts is the only way to learn boss patterns and unlock late-game scenes.
  • Original MSX2 build. The NES "Metal Gear" port was butchered (Kojima disowned it); this is the canonical one.
