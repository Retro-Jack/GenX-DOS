SD Snatcher

Konami, 1990 — chibi-style first-person turn-based RPG re-imagining of "Snatcher" (Hideo Kojima's 1988 cyberpunk adventure). Compresses the Blade Runner-tinged Snatcher story into a Dragon Quest-style city RPG with super-deformed sprites, gun-customisation, and Konami sound-chip music ports of the original's noir soundtrack.

Move Gillian Seed around Neo Kobe City in first-person town navigation; random encounters drop into turn-based gun-targeting combat. Choose which body part to shoot (arms, legs, head, torso) — different parts have different HP and effects when destroyed. Buy gun parts (barrels, sights, ammo) at gun shops; the game has more shopping than fighting.

Getting started

  1. Disk-set auto-boots; click the canvas first. (The bundle ships SD Snatcher as a 4-disk set; WebMSX auto-mounts them.)
  2. Press Space at the title to begin a new game.

Strategy

  • Body-part targeting wins fights. Shoot arms first on humanoid Snatchers — disarms them, sometimes outright wins; head shots are high-risk, high-reward.
  • Save before every gun-shop visit. Upgrade choices commit money; if the next dungeon needs a different barrel, you can't easily roll back.
  • Talk to everyone twice. Konami's RPG dialogue cycles — repeat-talk often unlocks a second hint after a story flag.
  • Distinct from the parent Snatcher. The original Snatcher (also on MSX2, also Konami) is a graphic-adventure with voice acting; SD Snatcher is the RPG retelling with combat and chibi art. Same plot, completely different play.
