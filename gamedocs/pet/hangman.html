Hangman

Raymond Toy, 1980 — the canonical PET BASIC port of the schoolyard guessing game. PETSCII gallows, single-letter guesses, eight wrong before the figure is complete.

The PET picks a word from its internal list. You guess letters one at a time; correct letters appear in their positions; wrong letters add a piece to the gallows figure. Eight wrong guesses = game over. Solve the word before the final piece.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots; click the canvas first for keyboard focus.
  2. Press a key past the title to start.
  3. Type a letter (A–Z) and press Return. Repeat until you win or hang.

Controls

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

KeyAction
A – ZGuess that letter
ReturnSubmit the guess
Word guess (if prompted)Type the whole word for one big risk/reward

Strategy

  • ETAOIN SHRDLU. The 12 most common English letters — guess them roughly in order; almost every word has at least one in it.
  • Vowels first. Three or four vowels narrow the word skeleton dramatically.
  • Don't guess the same letter twice. The PET will accept it and waste a turn on most versions.
  • Word lists are short. Re-playing the same build, you'll start to recognise words from 2-3 letters; that's the BASIC dictionary, not luck.
