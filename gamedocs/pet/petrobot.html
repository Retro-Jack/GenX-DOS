PETSCII Robots (Shareware)

David Murray ("The 8-Bit Guy"), 2022 — a modern, commercial-grade strategy/survival game written for the 1979 PET 4032, proving the platform still has games being written for it 45 years post-launch.

The bundle loads the PET in 4032 mode (BASIC 4, the per-game override in games.json) because the game needs the 4032's character ROM for its custom tileset. Three shareware levels — find weapons, recover the keycard, escape the level before the robot patrols catch you. Items combine; doors lock and unlock; movement is grid-based.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots — VICE attaches the .d64 and runs the BASIC loader. Expect ~30 seconds of authentic 1541 drive chunking; it's not frozen.
  2. Click the canvas first to give it keyboard focus.
  3. Press a key past the title to start at Level A.

Strategy

  • Scout before you fight. The map (M) shows the level — plan a route that minimises adjacent robot encounters.
  • Robots are dumb but durable. They patrol fixed routes and follow on line-of-sight; break LOS with corners.
  • Save ammo for the patrolling robots. Stationary defence robots can be magneted-funneled or avoided.
  • The keycard is on a specific robot (or in a specific cabinet). Each level's location is fixed — first playthrough is exploration, replays are speedruns.
  • Three levels in the shareware — the commercial release adds 30+ more, sold direct from the8bitguy.com.
