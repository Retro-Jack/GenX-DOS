Home Computer · Commodore PET

Crazy Balloon

1981 — PET port of Taito's 1980 maze arcade. Guide a balloon through a maze of pins without popping. Reach the flag for the bonus and next level.

A maze of pins, spikes, and moving needles. You're a balloon — touch anything sharp and you pop. Steer toward the exit flag. Each level adds more hazards; some levels move the maze elements while you're navigating.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots; click the canvas first.
  2. Press a key past the title to spawn the balloon at the start.

Controls

KeyAction
W A S DMove (up / left / down / right)
or I J K LSame on the right hand

PET ports of Crazy Balloon use one of the two standard PETSCII keyboard layouts; the title screen shows the build's specific keymap.

Strategy

  • Slow movement only. A balloon drifts — corrections take time. Plan two tiles ahead, not one.
  • Hug the safe-side wall. Maze-runner rule: pick a wall, follow it, you'll always reach the exit eventually (in static mazes).
  • Don't backtrack through moving needles. The needle returns on the same pattern; once you've crossed, the safe window has closed.
  • Bonus flag matters. Speed bonuses scale; sit-and-think levels score lower even with no deaths.
