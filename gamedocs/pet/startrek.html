Star Trek

1977 — the BASIC space-strategy classic. Mike Mayfield wrote the original on a Sigma 7 in 1971; it spread through HP and DEC minicomputers as Super Star Trek before landing on the PET. Command the Enterprise and clear the galaxy of Klingons before the star date runs out.

The galaxy is an 8×8 grid of quadrants, each itself an 8×8 grid of sectors. You start with a fixed number of star dates and a galaxy seeded with Klingon warships, starbases (for refit) and stars (obstacles). You win by destroying every Klingon before time expires; you lose if the clock runs out, your energy is exhausted, or the Enterprise is destroyed. Everything is driven by three-letter typed commands.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Read the mission briefing (Klingon count + star date limit), then press Enter past it.
  3. At the COMMAND? prompt type a three-letter command and press Enter.

Commands

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

TypeAction
NAVNavigate — enter course (1–9, compass dial) then warp factor
SRSShort-range scan — map of the current quadrant
LRSLong-range scan — the three quadrants around you
PHAPhasers — enter energy to fire; split across all enemies in range
TORPhoton torpedo — enter a course; travels in a straight line
SHEShields — transfer energy in or out
DAMDamage control report
COMLibrary computer (galaxy map, status, course calculator)
XXXResign the command

Course is a compass dial: 1 is east, then anticlockwise — 3 north, 5 west, 7 south, back to 9 (= 1). Decimals work, so 1.5 is north-east. Warp 1 crosses one quadrant.

Strategy

  • Scan before you move. Run LRS every quadrant — the digits report Klingons / starbases / stars so you can plot a path that avoids being outnumbered.
  • Shields up before a fight, down to manoeuvre. Klingons hit your shields each turn; but raising shields and firing both drain the same energy pool, so drop them to navigate or recharge.
  • Torpedoes are free but aimed. They cost no energy but need an exact course and a clear line — a star in the way absorbs the hit. Phasers always connect but split their damage across every enemy present.
  • Dock for a full refit. Sit adjacent to a starbase and shields restore, energy refills, and torpedoes reload — the only way to recover, so know where the nearest base is.
  • Watch the star date. Every warp and every repair burns time. Plan the shortest tour that still lets you refit; dawdling loses the game as surely as a destroyed ship.
