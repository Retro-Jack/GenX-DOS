Lunar Lander

1979 — the text/PETSCII version of the iconic 1969 Jim Storer FOCAL game. You burn fuel to slow a descent and try to touch down at safe velocity.

No graphics, no joystick — every second of the descent you type a fuel-burn rate (pounds of fuel per second), the PET integrates the physics, and reports altitude, velocity, and remaining fuel. Land at < 1 m/s for a safe touchdown.

Getting started

  1. Game autoboots; click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to begin the descent.
  3. At each second, type a burn rate (lb/s) and press Return.

Numbers that matter

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

QuantityTypical
Starting altitude120 miles (or 12,000 ft depending on build)
Starting velocity1 mile / hr downward
Starting fuel16,000 lb
Lunar gravity~1.62 m/s² (5.32 ft/s²)
Safe touchdown< 1 m/s (~3 ft/s)
Engine thrustEach lb/s burn produces a fixed thrust over its second

Strategy

  • Burn nothing early. Free-fall while you're high — gravity is weak; burning fuel at altitude is wasted.
  • Big slow burn near the end. The famous "suicide burn" — full thrust just before contact, killing all velocity in the last few seconds.
  • Don't redline. If you ask for more fuel/sec than the tank holds, the simulation runs out mid-step and you crash.
  • Replay value is the physics. The game is short; the appeal is dialling in the precise burn profile.
