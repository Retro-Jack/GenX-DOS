Pac-Man

1982 — PETSCII clone of Namco's 1980 arcade. Eat the dots, dodge the ghosts, eat the power pellets, chase the ghosts.

Standard Pac-Man maze rules but with PETSCII glyphs and monochrome rendering. Four ghosts patrol; four power pellets flip them blue for a few seconds. Clear all dots to advance.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls.
  2. Press a key past the title to start.

Controls

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

Ghost behaviour (true to arcade)

GhostAI tendency
Blinky (red)Direct chase — always targets your current tile
Pinky (pink)Ambush — targets four tiles ahead of you
Inky (cyan)Flanking — geometry-based pincer with Blinky
Clyde (orange)Random when close, retreats when too near you

Strategy

  • Power pellets aren't free. Trigger them only when at least two ghosts are within chase distance — chasing for one is bad ROI on the timer.
  • Corners are slow. Pac turns at junctions in zero time but the ghosts do too — pre-buffer the input one tile early on tight chases.
  • The fruit bonus appears twice per level. Worth the side-quest in the early levels, not worth dying for after wave 5.
  • Cruise elroy. When dots are scarce, Blinky speeds up to your speed — corners become your only escape.
