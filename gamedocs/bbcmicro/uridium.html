Uridium

Hewson, 1986 — Andrew Braybrook (BBC conversion by Dominic Robinson). Hardware-pushing horizontal scroller across the surface of 15 alien dreadnoughts; the BBC version is the rarer port.

You're the Manta — a single fighter sent against an Alien fleet of fifteen super-tankers. Each level: skim above the dreadnought's hull, dogfight enemy fighters, dodge surface installations, then dive into the landing strip at the end and out-manoeuvre the rotating reactor pads. Survive to attack the next ship.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. Your Manta accelerates automatically; you steer left/right and thrust forward/back to control your scroll position.
  3. Shoot fighters and ground installations for score. Reach the end of the dreadnought (or the landing pad in the centre) to trigger the landing sequence.

Dreadnought hazards

Enemy fightersSix types per ship; some chase, some sweep, some line up shots.
Gun turretShoot it from above; bonus if you destroy all on a ship.
Landing stripCentre of each dreadnought — appears after enough kills. Slow down and align to land.

Strategy

  • The flip is your best move. Pressing flip mid-screen lets you turn and re-strafe the section behind you — essential for collecting all surface guns.
  • Altitude bands matter. The Manta has two heights — low (skimming) and high (clearance). Stay high near masts, low for ground gun shots.
  • Landing is its own mini-game. When the strip appears, decelerate and weave between the moving safe-zones. Score bonus depends on landing precision.
  • BBC port is harder than the C64. Fewer colours, slower scrolling — but the dreadnoughts are the same layout, so YouTube C64 playthroughs help.
