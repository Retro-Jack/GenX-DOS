Home Computer · BBC Micro

Citadel

Superior Software, 1985 — Michael Jakobsen. Sprawling arcade-adventure-platformer with ~100 interconnected rooms; one of the BBC's best-loved exploration games.

Find the citadel hidden somewhere in the maze, recover the magical artefacts, and escape. Rooms hold keys, food, scrolls, and helpful objects — and creatures that drain energy on contact. Find the right object for the right obstacle: the puzzle is half platforming, half adventure inventory.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to begin.
  2. You start outside the castle. Walk through the door on the right and collect everything you can pick up — most early rooms have a single key item.
  3. You carry one object at a time; drop the current item by walking over a new one. Choose carefully.

Strategy

  • Map as you go. Citadel is huge and has no in-game map. A pen and paper grid is the original walkthrough.
  • Energy is the only timer. No clock — but every monster hit chips away at the bar. Always know where the nearest apple is.
  • Object swaps cost progress. If you drop the key and walk away, the room's enemies usually take it back. Carry the key all the way to the door.
  • Dead-end rooms are clues. If you've cleared everywhere reachable and a room remains sealed, you're missing an object — not a jump.
