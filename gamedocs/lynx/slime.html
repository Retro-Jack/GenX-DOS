Todd's Adventures in Slime World

A gooey run-and-explore platformer — squelch through caverns of slime, blast slime creatures with your slime gun, and find the way out (without getting completely covered).

Todd parachutes into a sprawling slime-filled cavern system and has to find the exit. Splatter enemies with your slime gun, swim through pools, leap chasms, and grab gear — but watch the goo: get too coated and Todd slows to a crawl, so hose yourself off at clean-water springs. Several game modes from quick exits to full exploration.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls; pick a mode (e.g. Exit for a quick run, Adventure for the big map).
  2. Head for the exit, blasting slime monsters and rinsing off goo as you go.

Strategy

  • Stay clean. Slime build-up bogs Todd down — duck into clean springs to wash off before it's critical.
  • Pick up better weapons (bombs, stronger guns) — the basic slime shot struggles with bigger creatures.
  • Explore for hidden gear in Adventure mode; many caverns have side pockets with power-ups.
  • Momentum jumps: run-up to clear the wider slime chasms; standing jumps fall short.
