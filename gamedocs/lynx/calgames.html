Handheld · Atari Lynx (1989)

California Games

The Lynx's pack-in launch title — the colourful Epyx sports compilation that showed off everything the first colour handheld could do.

A set of laid-back "extreme sports" events: half-pipe skateboarding, BMX, surfing, foot bag (hacky sack), and flying disk. Pick an event (or compete through all of them for a medal), nail tricks for points, and don't wipe out.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls; at the menu pick Compete (all events) or a single event.
  2. Each event explains its own scoring — trick variety and clean landings win.

Controls

Per-event, but generally: X (A, pad A) / Z (B, pad B) perform tricks/jumps, the arrows (pad D-pad) steer and angle your trick, Enter (pad Start) pauses.

Strategy

  • Half-pipe: build air off each wall and rotate (hold a direction) for aerials — bigger spins, more points, but land level.
  • BMX: jump the ramps and tuck/spin mid-air; watch your speed into each obstacle.
  • Surfing: ride the curl and cut back up the wave for tricks; bail and the shark's waiting.
  • Foot bag & disk are timing-and-positioning games — small, precise inputs beat mashing.
