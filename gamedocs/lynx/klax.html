Handheld · Atari Lynx (1989)

Klax

Atari's "it is the nineties and there is time for Klax" puzzler — catch falling coloured tiles and stack them into lines. A perfect handheld pick-up-and-play.

Tiles tumble down a conveyor toward you. Slide your paddle to catch them, hold up to five in your stack, and drop them into the well below to form Klaxes — rows of three or more same-coloured tiles, horizontal, vertical or diagonal. Each wave has a goal (so many Klaxes, or a points target); miss too many tiles and you lose a life.

Getting started

  1. Click the screen, start; each wave states its objective.
  2. Catch tiles on the paddle, then drop them into the well to build matching lines.

Controls

KeyAction
← →Move the paddle across the conveyor
X (A) / ↓Drop a tile from your stack into the well
↑Flip a tile back up onto the conveyor (to re-catch later)
EnterPause

Strategy

  • Diagonals score big. A diagonal Klax (and bigger lines) beats plain horizontals — set them up deliberately.
  • Don't overfill your stack. Five tiles max; flip one back up the conveyor if you're stuck rather than dropping a mismatch.
  • Plan two tiles ahead. You can see what's coming — position the paddle early.
  • Clear the bottom row to stop the well filling; a full well ends the wave badly.
