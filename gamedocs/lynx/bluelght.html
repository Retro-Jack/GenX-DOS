Handheld · Atari Lynx (1989)

Blue Lightning

The Lynx's flashiest launch showpiece — an into-the-screen jet-combat game in the After Burner mould, built to flaunt the hardware's sprite-scaling.

Pilot a fighter jet through a series of behind-the-plane missions: dogfight enemy aircraft, bomb ground targets and bridges, weave through canyons and mountains, and down each mission's boss. Enemies and scenery scale toward you at speed — the demo that sold a thousand Lynxes in shop windows.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, start the first mission; you're already airborne.
  2. Steer to keep targets in your sights, fire, and pull up before the terrain.

Strategy

  • Save the afterburner. Option 1 gives a 10-second top-speed burst — hold it for escaping a swarm or chasing down a fleeing target, not for routine cruising.
  • Cannon for aircraft, guided missiles for the tough targets — switching at the right moment is the whole game.
  • Remember the inverted stick. Up dives and down climbs — the classic flight-sim convention trips up first-timers right into the mountains.
  • Hug the terrain low for ground runs, but pull up early; the scaling makes mountains arrive fast.
  • Learn each mission's boss pattern — they take sustained fire and punish a static approach.
