Handheld · Atari Lynx (1989)

Gauntlet: The Third Encounter

The Lynx's take on Atari's arcade dungeon-crawler — top-down, monster-flooded mazes, with a quirky set of character classes unique to this version.

Pick a hero and descend through level after level of maze, blasting endless spawning monsters, smashing the generators that spew them, grabbing food (health), keys, potions and treasure, and finding the exit down. Health drains constantly — keep moving, keep eating, and don't get cornered.

Getting started

  1. Click the screen; choose a character class at the start.
  2. Find and reach the down-exit on each floor; shoot generators to stem the monster tide.

Strategy

  • Kill the generators first. Monsters spawn forever until you destroy the generator pumping them out.
  • Health is a clock. It ticks down on its own; grab every food item and don't dawdle.
  • Save potions for swarms — the screen-clearing magic is precious; don't waste it on a stray ghost.
  • Don't get pinned. Keep an escape route; being surrounded drains you fast even with a strong class.
