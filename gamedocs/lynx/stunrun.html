Handheld · Atari Lynx (1991)

S.T.U.N. Runner

Atari's arcade tube-racer on the Lynx — pilot a rocket-bike through neon tunnels at insane speed, another showcase for the handheld's scaling hardware.

Blast through enclosed tube tracks against the clock, hitting boost pads to break the speed barrier, shooting or dodging obstacles and enemy vehicles, jumping ramps, and reaching the checkpoint before time runs out. Pure speed and reflexes — the walls curve around you as the track scales past.

Getting started

  1. Click the game area to enable sound and controls, start; you're dropped onto the first track with a countdown.
  2. Steer to follow the tube, grab boosts, and reach the next checkpoint to extend your time.

Strategy

  • Chase every boost pad. Linking boosts to stay over the speed barrier is how you make the checkpoints.
  • Ride the walls. Cornering high on the curved tube carries more speed than the centre line.
  • Save the shockwave for when the track is genuinely blocked — it clears the path but you want it ready.
  • Memorise hazard placement; at full speed there's no time to react blind, only to remember.
