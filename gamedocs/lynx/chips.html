Handheld · Atari Lynx (1989)

Chip's Challenge

A Lynx original — the tile-based puzzle classic that later became famous on Windows started life here as a launch title.

Guide Chip through 100+ single-screen puzzle rooms in the Bit Busters clubhouse. Collect the required number of computer chips to open the exit, while navigating locked doors (colour-coded keys), water, fire, ice, bombs, monsters and movable blocks. Pure logic — no reflexes, just planning.

Getting started

  1. Click the screen, start a new game; each level shows how many chips you need.
  2. Walk over chips to collect them, keys to pick them up, the exit (chip socket) to finish.

Controls

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Move Chip (one tile per press)

Strategy

  • Look before you step. Many tiles are one-way, ice (you slide till you hit something), or force-floors — plan the whole route.
  • Push blocks into water to make bridges; you can only push, never pull.
  • Keys match doors by colour and are consumed — don't waste a key on a door you didn't need to open.
  • Some levels are timed — a clock means efficiency matters; save-state before experimenting.
