Palace of Magic

Superior Software, 1987 — Martyn R. Howard. A flick-screen platform-adventure in the mould of the earlier Citadel — shrunk to miniature by the wizard Caldeti, you explore a sprawling palace, church, woods and dungeon to find the objects that restore your size and the teleporter home.

This is exploration as much as platforming: over a hundred interconnected screens hold keys, spells and objects, and progress is gated on carrying the right thing to the right place. You hold only one object at a time, so much of the game is working out what to fetch, from where, and in what order — bribing wizards, unlocking doors and slipping past patrolling nasties along the way. Fall damage and drifting enemies punish sloppy jumps, so read each screen before you leap.

Getting started

  1. At the controls screen, press Space to continue (or Return to skip the loading screen).
  2. You carry one object at a time — press Space over an item to pick it up, and again to drop it where it's needed.
  3. Move with Z/X, look up/down with ' / /, and jump with Return. Line up jumps carefully — a bad landing costs a life.

Strategy

  • One object at a time. Everything hinges on carrying the right item to the right screen — when stuck, you're usually holding the wrong thing or need to fetch something first.
  • Map as you go. The palace loops back on itself; note which locked doors and blocked routes you've seen so you know where a newly-found key belongs.
  • Bribe the wizards. Some guardians block the way until handed a specific object — that's the puzzle, not a fight.
  • Respect the drop. Falling too far kills you; take the longer platform route rather than gambling on a big descent.
  • Time the patrols. Enemies drift on fixed paths — watch a screen for a moment and cross on the gap rather than rushing in.
