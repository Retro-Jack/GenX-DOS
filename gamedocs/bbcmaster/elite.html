Elite (Master version)

Acornsoft, 1986 — David Braben & Ian Bell. Master-enhanced Elite: flicker-free ships, finer scoring and improved sound, with built-in missions on a double-sided disc.

The Master release is a refined 8-bit Elite. Same universe — 2,048 systems across 8 galaxies, same Cobra Mk III, same trading/combat loop — but it inherits the flicker-free ship-drawing algorithm from the Apple II version (released earlier the same year) and adds finer scoring, reworked sound and a volume control. This build is reconstructed from the documented source at bbcelite.com and ships as a double-sided disc image (`.dsd`); `*DRIVE 2` in the !BOOT loads side 1 of the disc.

Getting started

  1. At the title press Space. At the Load New Commander (Y/N)? prompt, press N for the default Commander Jameson. (Y opens the disc-access menu, but disc saves don't persist in this build — use the load/save buttons at the bottom-left of the browser window.)
  2. Launch from Lave station — match the rotation, fly out, J to in-system jump to the planet.
  3. Buy Food (cheap on Lave); F7 market, F4/F5 charts to pick a destination, H hyperspace.

Saving your progress: the disc is read-only in the browser, so an in-game Save Commander won't survive closing the tab — use the GenX save / load buttons to keep your commander (ship, cash, rating) between sessions. There is only one save slot — it's the best we could do.

Master version differences

  • Flicker-free ships. The Master uses the improved ship-drawing algorithm from the Apple II version (released earlier the same year) — far less flicker than the cassette/disc BBC builds.
  • Finer kill scoring. Kills are scored by ship type and can be fractional, instead of the original's flat one-kill-per-ship.
  • Coloured laser sights & energy-bomb storm. The laser crosshairs change colour with the laser type, and the energy bomb sets off a lightning-storm effect.
  • Updated sound + volume control. Reworked sound effects with an in-game volume control — a real bonus on original hardware.
  • Trumble mission dropped. The Trumble code is still present but inactive; that mission was cut when the 6502 Second Processor and C64 versions were merged into the Master build (no sprite support). The Constrictor and Thargoid missions remain.

Strategy basics

  • Agricultural → Industrial → back. Standard trade route.
  • Don't go illegal early. Narcotics/Slaves get you fined; profitable only with police-evading kit.
  • Fuel scoops for free star-skim fuel.
  • Run from Thargoids until you're at "Dangerous" or better.
