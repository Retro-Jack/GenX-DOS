Crazee Rider

Superior Software, 1987 — Stewart Stewart-Smith. Side-on motorbike racer across multiple stages; arcade-style chase against a rolling clock.

You ride a 250cc bike along a side-scrolling track, dodging cars, oil slicks, fences, and other obstacles. Each stage ends at a checkpoint; you must reach it before the timer runs out, or it's game over. Crash and the bike resets — but the clock keeps running.

Getting started

  1. Press Space at the title to start.
  2. The bike accelerates automatically; you steer up and down between lanes and modulate speed.
  3. Slipstreaming a slow car gives a brief speed boost — but stay in the same lane too long and you'll rear-end it.

Strategy

  • The wheelie is your shortcut. Low obstacles like crates or hedgehogs can be wheelie'd over; cars and trucks can't.
  • Brake into corners, accelerate out. Sounds obvious — the AI cars don't, which makes them easy passing targets mid-corner.
  • Checkpoint times tighten each stage. Stage 1 has slack; by stage 4 every second matters.
  • Oil slicks auto-slide you across lanes; don't fight it, just steer back after the slip.
