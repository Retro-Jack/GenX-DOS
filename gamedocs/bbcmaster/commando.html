Home Computer · BBC Master 128

Commando

Elite Systems, 1985 — BBC conversion by Trevor Harwood, from Capcom's 1985 arcade original. Vertical-scrolling run-and-gun: you're "Super Joe", one soldier fighting up through enemy lines to the fortress with an M60 and a fistful of grenades.

The battlefield scrolls upward and you run into it, alone against the rebel army. Your M60 fires straight up the screen; your grenades lob further, arcing over trenches, walls and lakes to reach what bullets can't. Enemy fire comes from every direction — soldiers pour out of caves, strongholds and troop carriers, and mortars rain down. You start with six grenades and top up by clearing outposts. Keep pushing forward; there is no retreat.

Getting started

  1. Page through the instructions to the title, then press Return (Fire) to start.
  2. Super Joe faces up the screen. Fire with Return; move him around the advancing battlefield with the direction keys below.
  3. Lob a grenade with Shift to clear obstacles, trenches and tightly-packed enemies your bullets can't reach.

Controls

The BBC * and ? keys aren't where a PC keyboard puts them — this emulator maps the BBC *: key to your PC apostrophe ' and the / ? key to /, so those are your up/down.

Strategy

  • Grenades reach what bullets can't. Your gun only fires up the screen; the grenade arcs over trenches, walls and clustered enemies. Save a couple for the emplacements you can't shoot past.
  • Top up your grenades. You only carry a handful — clear outposts and pick up the boxes they drop, or you'll be caught empty at the choke-points.
  • Keep moving up. The screen scrolls with you and enemies spawn behind as well as ahead. Linger and you get surrounded; there is no retreat.
  • Read the terrain. Trenches, cliffs and lakes block your path and channel you into kill-zones — grenade a gap or skirt the edge rather than charging the middle.
  • Strafe, don't stand. Enemy bullets come from all sides; a moving target survives, a stationary one doesn't.
