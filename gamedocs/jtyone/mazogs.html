Mazogs

Bug-Byte, 1981 — Don Priestley. Real-time top-down maze with treasure to grab, exits to find, and the maze-dwelling Mazogs to fight or flee.

You're dropped into a procedurally-generated maze with hidden treasure and patrolling Mazogs (monsters). Find the treasure, fight off any Mazogs you can't avoid, and reach the exit. The maze is randomised every game — no two playthroughs the same.

Getting started

  1. Press a key at the title to start.
  2. You appear at one corner of the maze; the exit is at the opposite corner.
  3. Move with the cursor keys; treasure pickup is automatic on contact.
  4. Bump into a Mazog while carrying the sword and you fight it — keep pushing into it to win.

Strategy

  • Right-hand rule. Stick to one wall and you'll traverse most random mazes without dead-ending.
  • Mazogs are slower than you. Outrun rather than fight unless you're cornered.
  • Treasure-vs-exit tradeoff. Pure exit run scores low; clearing treasure deepens the playthrough but exposes you to more Mazog encounters.
  • Random seed means strategy is general — no memorisable maze layout.
