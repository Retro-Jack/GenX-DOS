Maze Death Race

1983. Top-down race-and-shoot: drive through a maze while enemy cars hunt you. Score by destroying rivals and reaching the maze exit before time runs out.

A top-down maze viewed from above. You're one car; several others patrol the corridors. Fire forward to destroy them; collide head-on without firing and you both die. Reach the exit (often marked with a flag) within the time limit to clear the maze and progress.

Getting started

  1. Press 0 at the title to start.
  2. Your car appears at one corner. Drive in 4 directions through the maze corridors.
  3. Fire forward with the shoot key to destroy enemy cars ahead of you.
  4. Reach the exit corner within the time limit.

Controls

This game supports a USB gamepad — the D-pad or stick drives, any face button fires — the same button (0) also begins a game at the title.

Strategy

  • Shoot from corners. Coming around a bend with your bullet already in flight kills the patrolling AI before they react.
  • Don't ram unfired. Head-on collision kills both cars; only worthwhile when you're last-life vs their last car.
  • Edge lanes are safer than middle. AI patrols concentrate centrally on most layouts.
  • Time bonus vs score. Rush for time bonus, or clean up rival cars for kill score — rarely both.
