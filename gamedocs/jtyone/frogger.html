Frogger

1982. Unofficial Frogger conversion for the ZX81 in chunky block graphics — one of several near-identical ports from different small publishers.

Hop your frog across a four-lane road, then across a river of logs and turtles, into one of five lily-pad slots. Fill all five slots to clear the level. Each level adds traffic speed and shorter log gaps. The ZX81 version uses character-cell block graphics and runs at the platform's flicker-prone refresh rate.

Getting started

  1. Press any key at the title to start.
  2. Hop forward into the first lane between cars.
  3. On the river, ride logs and turtle backs to the far bank. Fall in the water = lose a frog.
  4. Land in an empty lily-pad slot. Fill all five to complete the row.

Controls

This game supports a USB gamepad — the D-pad or stick hops in all four directions, any face button hops forward.

Strategy

  • Hop in rhythm. Each hop is a fixed grid step — predict where the lane gap will be one beat ahead.
  • Turtles sink. Don't ride them long.
  • Outer slots first. Easier to reach without diagonal-style detours.
  • ZX81 flicker means the rendering can hide an oncoming car for one frame — assume the worst at lane boundaries.
