Galaxians

Artic Computing, 1982. Galaxian-style fixed shooter; aliens swoop from the formation in dive-bomb arcs while you shoot from the bottom of the screen.

A grid of alien ships hovers at the top of the screen. They take turns peeling off in dive-bomb attacks toward your ship at the bottom. Shoot them while they're vulnerable in formation, or dodge the divers and pick them off as they swoop. Clear the formation to advance to the next, faster wave.

Getting started

  1. Press 0 at the title to start.
  2. Your ship sits at the bottom-centre. Move left/right; fire to launch a single bullet at a time.
  3. Aliens dive in 1, 2, or 3-ship groups. Track the lead ship and shoot before they reach you.

Controls

This game supports a USB gamepad — left/right on the d-pad or stick moves your ship, and any face button fires. Start presses 0, which also starts a game at the title.

Strategy

  • Wait for divers. Drones in formation score 30 — diving they're worth 60. Same shot, double points.
  • Flagship convoys are the prize. Resist shooting the escorts until the flagship enters its dive — kill order: flagship first, escorts second.
  • Single-bullet rule. Your bullet has to fully clear the screen before you can fire again. Don't waste shots.
  • Edges are dangerous. A diver pinning you against a screen edge eats your dodge room — stay near centre and only sidestep at the last second.
