Console · GCE Vectrex

Rip-Off

GCE, 1982 — port of Tim Skelly's 1980 Cinematronics arcade. The first co-op vector arcade.

You're a tank guarding a stash of eight fuel canisters at the centre of the screen. Pirate ships swoop in from all sides trying to steal one canister at a time and tow it offscreen. Shoot the pirates or chase down stolen canisters and shoot the tow-ship to release the cargo. Lose all 8 canisters = game over.

Getting started

  1. Wait for the Vectrex BIOS intro.
  2. Press a button at the title to start.
  3. Steer your tank; A fires.

Strategy

  • Defend, don't pursue. The pirates spawn from the edge. Stay between them and the canisters; let them come to you.
  • Wedge formation. In 2-player mode, partners cover opposite sides; in solo, sit at one canister cluster and pivot.
  • Tow disruption. A pirate towing a canister is slower than free pirates. Catch them after the grab — shoot tow-ship, canister floats free.
  • Don't waste the bonus row. Higher-numbered waves give multiplier; if you can survive long enough to hit the score-cap loop, the game restarts at full canisters.

Vectrex controls

Stick + button. Solo and 2-player; in 2-player, P2 plugs into the second controller port (not currently keyboard-mapped in JSVecX).
