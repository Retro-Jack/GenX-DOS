Console · GCE Vectrex

Pole Position

GCE, 1983 — vector adaptation of Namco's 1982 raster arcade. The Vectrex's only racing game.

Qualify a lap under the time limit, then race against 8 AI cars on the Fuji circuit. Vector-drawn road perspective makes the track feel different from the arcade raster version — flatter, more geometric.

Getting started

  1. Wait for the Vectrex BIOS intro.
  2. Press a button at the title to start.
  3. Qualify in 73 seconds (default). Then race 4 laps.

Strategy

  • Start in low gear. Shift to high gear only when revs peak — pulling away in high gear is sluggish.
  • Lift off the gas early. There's no brake — releasing the gas slows you; vector geometry makes corners crisp but harsh, so you can't smear into a turn.
  • Slipstream. Tuck behind an AI car for a few seconds of speed boost, then swing wide to pass.
  • Wall = explosion. Any wall touch resets you in flames. Don't kiss the edge.

Vectrex controls

Stick steers; Buttons 3/4 are the gas (hold to accelerate, release to slow), Buttons 1/2 select low / high gear.
