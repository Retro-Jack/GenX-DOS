Console · GCE Vectrex

Spike

GCE, 1983 — original Vectrex platformer by Craig Lambert. The only console game of its era with speech synthesis built into the cartridge.

Spike, a yellow figure, must rescue his girlfriend Molly from Spud. Climb ladders, jump across platforms, dodge pits, throw rocks at Spud. Cinema-style intros and outros — and the cartridge actually speaks ("Hi Mom!", "Oh no!", "All Right!") via its onboard speech chip.

Getting started

  1. Wait for the Vectrex BIOS intro.
  2. Press A at the title.
  3. Climb, jump, throw rocks. Reach Molly at the top.

Strategy

  • Plan jumps from corners. Spike's jump arc is fixed; mid-air control is minimal. Stand at the edge before pressing jump.
  • Rocks vs. Spud. Spud appears on the upper levels. Three rocks knock him off the platform.
  • Ladder safety. Climbing ladders takes time; check for falling rocks/Spud before starting up.
  • Speech is cosmetic. Button 2 ("Speak") triggers stored speech samples — no gameplay effect, just charming.

Vectrex controls

All four buttons in use; the Vectrex speech chip in the Spike cartridge (one of only two games ever to ship with one — the other being "Pole Position" expansion module) provides voiced quips.
