Console · GCE Vectrex

Scramble

GCE, 1982 — vector port of the 1981 Konami arcade by Konami's Akira Hashimoto. Horizontal-scrolling shooter with terrain and fuel management.

A ship flies right through six themed segments: mountains, UFOs, fireballs, asteroids, urban, base. Bomb ground targets (especially fuel tanks — they refill your dwindling fuel meter). Shoot rockets and UFOs out of the air. Hit terrain or run out of fuel = death.

Getting started

  1. Wait for the Vectrex BIOS intro.
  2. Press A at the title.
  3. Steer with arrows; A fires forward, S drops bombs.

Strategy

  • Bomb every fuel tank. Each refill is 100 pts AND fills the meter. Fuel runs out faster than enemies kill you.
  • Lead the bomb. Bombs arc down-forward — release them just past the target.
  • Section 4 is hardest. Asteroids leave one tile of clear at a time. Vertical position is everything.
  • Base run. The rocket at the base must be bombed (laser doesn't kill it). One shot, one chance — slow your approach.
  • Loop bonus. Completing the run loops you back to section 1 with a difficulty bump. The Vectrex version preserves the arcade loop.

Vectrex controls

Stick + buttons 1 (laser) and 2 (bomb). Wait for the BIOS intro before pressing buttons.
