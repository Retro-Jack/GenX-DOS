Console · GCE Vectrex

Solar Quest

GCE, 1982 — vector port of Cinematronics' 1981 arcade. Asteroids-style shooter with humans to rescue and a sun to dodge.

Your ship rotates and thrusts in space. Hostile ships, mines, and asteroids litter each wave. A central sun pulls you in if you drift too close — touch it, you die. Astronauts float in space; collect them for bonus points and an extra weapon: the smart bomb.

Getting started

  1. Wait for the Vectrex BIOS intro.
  2. Press a button at the title to start.
  3. Rotate, thrust, fire — exactly like Asteroids, plus a sun and rescuable humans.

This emulator is keyboard-only — USB gamepads aren't supported.

Strategy

  • Don't orbit the sun. It pulls you in with increasing force. Stay in the outer half of the playfield.
  • Astronauts = bombs. Each rescued human awards a smart bomb. Smart bombs clear the screen instantly — save for crowded moments.
  • Thrust into rescues. Astronauts drift slowly; thrust to intercept, easing off before touching.
  • Don't shoot astronauts. Friendly fire = lost human + lost bomb.
  • Late-wave mines. Stationary mines clutter the field by wave 4+. Memorise their spawn points; shoot at the start of each wave.

Vectrex controls

Stick rotates; D thrusts, F fires, S smart-bombs, A transports.
